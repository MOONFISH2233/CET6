原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Nationally, one in six children miss 15 or more days of school in a year. Education officials have deplored all this missed instruction.

从全国范围来看，每六名儿童中就有一名每年缺课15天或以上。教育官员对如此大量的教学缺失表示了强烈不满。

P2

These chronically absent students suffer academically because of all the classroom instruction they miss out on. In 2015, the U.S. secretary of education responded to this crisis, urging communities to support every student to attend every day and be successful in school. His open letter stated that missing 10% of school days in a year for any reason—excused or unexcused—is a primary cause of low academic achievement.

这些长期缺课的学生由于错过了大量课堂教学，在学业上受到影响。2015年，美国教育部长回应了这一危机，敦促各社区支持每一名学生每天到校，并在学校取得成功。他在一封公开信中指出，无论出于何种原因——获准缺勤或未经批准缺勤——每年缺课达到在校日的10%，都是学业成绩低下的主要原因。

P3

Worrying about whether children attend school makes sense. After all, if students don't show up, teachers can't teach them.

担心儿童是否到校上课是有道理的。毕竟，如果学生不来，教师就无法教他们。

P4

But what if America's attendance crisis is about much more than students missing class? What if, instead, it is a reflection of family and community crises these students face—such as being ejected from the family apartment, fearing for their safety in their neighborhood or suffering an illness?

但是，如果美国的出勤危机远不只是学生缺课呢？如果它实际上反映的是这些学生所面临的家庭和社区危机呢？例如，他们被赶出家庭公寓、担心所在社区的人身安全，或正在遭受疾病困扰。

P5

As social scientists we investigated how excused and unexcused absences relate to children's academic achievement.

作为社会科学家，我们研究了获准缺勤和未经批准缺勤与儿童学业成绩之间的关系。

P6

We find that absences excused by a parent do little to harm children's learning. In fact, children with no unexcused absences—but 15 to 18 excused absences—have test scores equal to their peers who have no absences.

我们发现，家长批准的缺勤对儿童的学习几乎没有不良影响。事实上，那些没有未经批准缺勤、但有15至18次获准缺勤的儿童，其考试成绩与没有缺勤记录的同龄人相同。

P7

Meanwhile, the average child with even just one unexcused absence does much worse academically than peers with none.

与此同时，即使只有一次未经批准缺勤的普通儿童，其学业表现也远不如没有未经批准缺勤的同龄人。

P8

We believe unexcused absence is a strong signal of the many challenges children and families face, including economic and medical hardships. Unexcused absences can be a powerful signal of how those out-of-school challenges affect children's academic progress.

我们认为，未经批准缺勤是儿童和家庭面临诸多挑战的强烈信号，其中包括经济困难和医疗困境。未经批准缺勤可以有力地反映出那些发生在校外的挑战如何影响儿童的学业进步。

P9

Our evidence suggests unexcused absences are problematic, but for a different reason than people often think. Absence from school, and especially unexcused absence, matters mainly as a signal of many crises children and their families may be facing. It matters less as a cause of lower student achievement due to missed instruction.

我们的证据表明，未经批准缺勤确实存在问题，但原因不同于人们通常所认为的那样。缺课，尤其是未经批准缺勤，主要是儿童及其家庭可能正在面临诸多危机的一种信号。与由于错过教学而导致学生成绩下降相比，缺课作为成绩下降原因的重要性较低。

P10

How we choose to think of school absences matters for educational policy. School attendance policies typically hold schools and families accountable for the days children miss, regardless of whether they were excused or unexcused absences.

我们如何看待学生缺课，会影响教育政策的制定。学校出勤政策通常会让学校和家庭对儿童缺课的天数负责，而不论这些缺勤属于获准缺勤还是未经批准缺勤。

P11

These policies assume that missing school for any reason harms children academically because they are missing classroom instruction. They also assume that schools will be able to effectively intervene by reducing student absences. We find neither to be the case.

这些政策认为，学生无论出于何种原因缺课，都会因为错过课堂教学而在学业上受到损害。它们还认为，学校能够通过减少学生缺勤来进行有效干预。我们发现，事实并非如此。

P12

As a result, these attendance policies end up disproportionately punishing families dealing with out-of-school crises in their lives and pressuring schools who serve them to get students to school more often.

因此，这些出勤政策最终会对那些正在应对生活中校外危机的家庭施加不成比例的惩罚，并向为这些家庭提供服务的学校施压，要求它们让学生更加频繁地到校。

P13

We instead suggest using unexcused absence from school as a signal to channel resources to the children and families who need them most.

相反，我们建议将学生未经批准缺勤作为一种信号，把资源引导给最需要帮助的儿童和家庭。

解析逐题详解

46

What does the U.S. secretary of education say in his open letter?

美国教育部长在他的公开信中说了什么?

  • A)It is of vital importance to respond promptly to the school absence crisis.及时应对学校缺勤危机至关重要。
  • B)The academic performance of chronically absent students is deplorable.长期缺勤学生的学业表现令人担忧。
  • C)Low academic achievement is mainly attributed to school absences.学业成绩低下主要归因于学校缺勤。
  • D)The effect of school absences on American education is worrisome.学校缺勤对美国教育的影响令人担忧。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 过度推断公开信里没有任何关于 "必须迅速回应" 的话——urging communities to support every student 是劝学校社区支持学生出勤，不是对 respond promptly 的呼吁；A 项把作者（urging）的动作改写成信件内容，且加了原文没有的 "vital importance / promptly" 程度词。
B) 偷换概念deplored 在原文里描述的是 Education officials 对 "all this missed instruction" 的态度，B 项把它偷换成了 "chronically absent students 的 academic performance is deplorable"——主语从 "缺课现象" 偷换成 "学生的学业表现"，evaluatee 错位。
C) ✓ 正确C 项把 missing 10% of school days … is a primary cause of low academic achievement 的主动因果倒装为 "低学业主要归因于缺课" 的被动因果，与预判 "缺课是学业差的主因" 完全吻合。
  • missing 10% of school days in a year for any reason↔school absences
  • is a primary cause of low academic achievement↔Low academic achievement is mainly attributed to
D) 张冠李戴公开信原文用 primary cause 的客观因果定性，并未对 "school absences 对美国教育的影响" 作 worrisome 这种情感色彩评价——D 项把 Worrying 段的句首词搬到这里，但那是作者对 "担心缺勤" 的转述，并非教育部长公开信的内容。
P2

His open letter stated that missing 10% of school days in a year for any reason—excused or unexcused—is a primary cause of low academic achievement.

他的公开信指出，一年缺课 10%——无论是否有故——是低学业成就的一个主要原因。

47

What do the authors find about school absences?

作者对学校缺勤有什么发现?

  • A)Excused school absences have little impact on children's learning.请假缺勤对儿童学习影响很小。
  • B)There is little difference between unexcused and excused absences.无故缺勤和请假缺勤之间几乎没有差别。
  • C)Excused absences lead to comparatively better school performance.请假缺勤导致相对更好的学业表现。
  • D)Unexcused absences are a big challenge to both schools and families.无故缺勤对学校和家庭都是一个巨大挑战。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确A 项是 P6 第一句的字面同义改写——主语 excused absences、谓语 do little to harm 全部一一对应，无任何添加或扭曲，与预判完全吻合。
  • absences excused by a parent↔Excused school absences
  • do little to harm children's learning↔have little impact on children's learning
B) 与原文相反原文 the average child with even just one unexcused absence does much worse academically than peers with none 明确指出 unexcused 与 excused 差异很大——B 项 little difference 与原文 much worse 直接相反。
C) 过度推断原文只说 excused absences do little to harm、有 excused 缺勤的孩子 have test scores equal to their peers who have no absences——是 "equal to"（相当），不是 comparatively better（相对更好）。"持平" 被悄悄抬升为 "更好"。
D) 答非所问Unexcused absences are a big challenge to both schools and families 谈的是 unexcused 对学校和家庭构成挑战——题干 Q47 问的是 "authors 关于 school absences 的发现"，作者的核心发现是 excused 不损学（P6），而非 unexcused 对学校家庭有多挑战。话题偏离。
P6

We find that absences excused by a parent do little to harm children's learning.

我们发现，由家长请假的 excused absences 对孩子的学习几乎没有伤害。

48

What do the authors believe concerning unexcused school absences?

作者对无故缺勤持什么看法?

  • A)They are likely to cause a decrease in students' academic achievements due to missed instruction.由于错过了教学,它们可能导致学生学业成绩下降。
  • B)They point directly to many of the out-of-school challenges confronting children and their families.它们直接指向儿童及其家庭面临的许多校外挑战。
  • C)They are matters the American government typically ignores when formulating educational policies.在制定教育政策时,美国政府通常会忽视这些问题。
  • D)They give a clear signal to children and their families of the crises they are likely to face in the future.它们向儿童及其家庭发出了未来可能面临危机的明确信号。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反They are likely to cause a decrease in students' academic achievements due to missed instruction 是作者明确反对的观点——It matters less as a cause of lower student achievement due to missed instruction 中 "matters less as a cause" 直接否定了 "缺课通过 missed instruction 导致学业下降" 这一因果。A 项把作者反对的观点扣给作者本人。
B) ✓ 正确B 项把 is a strong signal of 改写为 point directly to——"信号" 与 "指向" 是六级常见的隐喻互换，与预判 "无故缺勤指向校外挑战" 完全吻合。
  • is a strong signal of↔point directly to
  • the many challenges children and families face↔many of the out-of-school challenges confronting children and their families
C) 无中生有原文未说政府 typically ignores unexcused absences——恰恰相反，P10 说政策 hold schools and families accountable for the days children miss，是 "管太严" 而非 "忽视"。C 项凭空捏造 "政府忽视" 这一事实。
D) 偷换概念原文 is a strong signal of the many challenges children and families face 用一般现在时 face，指 "当下面对" 的挑战；D 项改成 the crises they are likely to face in the future 把时间挪到未来——把 "反映当下危机" 偷换成 "预测未来危机"。
P8

We believe unexcused absence is a strong signal of the many challenges children and families face, including economic and medical hardships.

我们认为，无故缺勤是孩子和家庭所面临诸多挑战的强烈信号，包括经济与医疗方面的困难。

49

What is the assumption underlying education policies in the U.S.?

美国教育政策所基于的假设是什么?

  • A)Children's academic performance depends on reducing the number of absences.儿童的学业表现取决于减少缺勤次数。
  • B)Schools can boost children's academic performance by effective intervention.学校可以通过有效干预提高儿童的学业表现。
  • C)Schools as well as families should be held responsible for out-of-school crises.学校和家庭都应对校外危机负责。
  • D)Children's academic performance is closely related to the quality of instruction.儿童的学业表现与教学质量密切相关。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念原文 missing school for any reason harms children academically 是 "缺课损学业"——A 项 Children's academic performance depends on reducing the number of absences 把它倒过来说 "学业取决于减少缺勤"，表面相近但因果主语错位：原文主语是 "缺课"，A 项主语变成 "学业"，且 depends on 把单向因果偷换为 "取决于" 这一更强的依存关系。
B) ✓ 正确B 项把第二个 assume（学校能有效干预）与前一句的因果（缺课损学业）合起来——干预 → 减少缺勤 → 提升学业，是政策预设的完整逻辑，与预判吻合。
  • schools will be able to effectively intervene↔Schools can boost children's academic performance by effective intervention
  • by reducing student absences + These policies assume that missing school for any reason harms children academically↔boost children's academic performance
C) 张冠李戴原文 hold schools and families accountable for the days children miss 说政策让学校和家庭对 "缺课天数" 负责，而不是对 out-of-school crises 负责——"out-of-school crises" 是 out-of-school challenges 里的概念，C 项把它扣到了 "政策让学校家庭负责的对象" 头上，移花接木。
D) 无中生有原文从头到尾在讨论 attendance / absences / missed instruction（错过的教学），从未讨论 the quality of instruction（教学质量）——D 项凭空引入 "教学质量" 概念，与原文核心论域无关。
P11

They also assume that schools will be able to effectively intervene by reducing student absences.

这些政策还预设，学校能通过减少学生缺勤来有效进行干预。

50

What do the authors suggest doing regarding school absences?

作者建议如何处理学校缺勤问题?

  • A)Identifying their underlying causes.找出其根本原因。
  • B)Reframing school attendance policies.重新制定学校出勤政策。
  • C)Directing resources to helping needy children.将资源用于帮助有需要的儿童。
  • D)Pressuring schools to reduce unexcused ones.向学校施压以减少无故缺勤。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 答非所问原文 P13 的建议是 "把 unexcused absence 当信号 → 把资源调配给需要者"，不是 Identifying their underlying causes（找缺勤的根本原因）——作者已经在前文给出原因（家庭/社区危机），末段不再建议 "找原因"，而是 "用信号导资源"。
B) 过度推断原文 P10–P12 批评了现行政策，但 We instead suggest 的建议内容是 "用 unexcused absence 当信号导资源"，而不是 Reframing school attendance policies（重构考勤政策）——作者直接给出替代做法，没把 "重构政策" 作为建议本身提出。
C) ✓ 正确C 项把 channel resources to 直接对应 Directing resources to，把 who need them most 压缩为 needy——两步同义改写都是六级高频考法，与预判完全吻合。
  • channel resources to↔Directing resources to
  • the children and families who need them most↔needy children
D) 与原文相反原文 P12 明确指出现行政策 pressuring schools who serve them to get students to school more often 是负面后果，作者是 "批评施压"——D 项 Pressuring schools to reduce unexcused ones 反而把 "施压学校" 作为作者的建议，与原文立场完全相反。
P13

We instead suggest using unexcused absence from school as a signal to channel resources to the children and families who need them most.

我们反而建议：把无故缺勤作为信号，把资源调配给最需要这些资源的孩子和家庭。
