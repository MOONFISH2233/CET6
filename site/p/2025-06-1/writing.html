范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 162 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

As requirements for job applications are getting increasingly higher, college students ought to be better prepared for their future career. In an era when employers demand not only a diploma but also practical competence, waiting until graduation to think about work is simply too late.

There are concrete steps every student can take. To begin with, obtaining relevant certificates and mastering professional skills builds a solid foundation. Last summer, for instance, I taught myself data analysis and earned a certificate, which later helped me stand out in an internship interview. What is more, internships offer irreplaceable experience: by working in a real company, students learn how a workplace actually operates and discover which career truly suits them. Such preparation turns abstract knowledge into employable ability.

In conclusion, the fiercer competition becomes, the more valuable early preparation is. Therefore, college students should seize every chance to enrich their skills and experience, so that when opportunity knocks, they are ready to open the door.
