原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

After earning a bachelor's degree, I was determined to do what I love. I headed straight to graduate school to investigate the social problems that fascinated me.

获得学士学位后，我下定决心去做自己热爱的事情。我径直进入研究生院，研究那些令我着迷的社会问题。

P2

For almost a decade, I told everyone I encountered that they should do the same. "Follow your passion," I counseled. "You can figure out the employment stuff later."

将近十年的时间里，我告诉我遇到的每一个人，他们也应该这样做。“追随你的热情，”我劝告道。“就业方面的事情以后再想办法解决。”

P3

It wasn't until I began to research this widely accepted career advice that I understood how problematic it really was.

直到我开始研究这一广为接受的职业建议，我才明白它实际上存在多么严重的问题。

P4

As a sociologist, I interviewed college students and professional workers to learn what it really meant to pursue their dreams, which I will refer to here as the passion principle. I was stunned by what I found out about this principle in the research for my new book.

作为一名社会学家，我采访了大学生和职场人士，以了解追逐梦想究竟意味着什么；在本文中，我将其称为“热情原则”。在为我的新书进行研究时，我对自己发现的有关这一原则的情况感到震惊。

P5

Surveys show the American public has long held the passion principle in high regard as a career decision-making priority. And its popularity is even stronger among those facing job instability.

调查显示，美国公众长期以来一直高度重视“热情原则”，将其视为职业决策中的优先事项。而在那些面临就业不稳定的人群中，这一原则更受欢迎。

P6

Advocates of the passion principle found it compelling because they believed that following one's passion can provide workers with both the motivation necessary to work hard and a place to find fulfillment.

“热情原则”的倡导者认为它很有说服力，因为他们相信，追随自己的热情既能为劳动者提供努力工作的必要动力，也能为他们提供获得满足感的途径。

P7

Yet, what I found is that following one's passion does not necessarily lead to fulfillment, but is one of the most powerful cultural forces perpetuating overwork. I also found that promoting the pursuit of one's passion helps perpetuate social inequalities due to the fact that not everyone has the same economic resources to allow them to pursue their passion with ease.

然而，我发现，追随自己的热情并不一定会带来满足感，反而是延续过度工作的最强大文化力量之一。我还发现，鼓励人们追随自己的热情，会助长社会不平等，因为并不是每个人都拥有同样的经济资源，能够轻松地追逐自己的热情。

P8

While the passion principle is broadly popular, not everyone has the necessary resources to turn their passion into a stable, good-paying job. Passion-seekers from wealthy families are better able to wait until a job they are passionate about comes along without worrying about student loans in the meantime. They are also better situated to take unpaid internships to get their foot in the door while their parents pay their rent. And they often have access to parents' social networks to help them find jobs. Surveys revealed that working-class and first-generation college graduates, regardless of their career field, are more likely than their wealthier peers to end up in low-paying unskilled jobs when they pursue their passion.

尽管“热情原则”广受欢迎，但并不是每个人都拥有将自己的热情转化为稳定且薪酬丰厚工作的必要资源。来自富裕家庭的热情追逐者更有能力等待自己热爱的工作出现，而不必担心这期间的学生贷款。他们也更有条件接受无薪实习，以便在父母为其支付房租的同时进入这个行业。此外，他们通常还能利用父母的社交网络来寻找工作。调查显示，无论所从事的职业领域如何，工薪阶层家庭出身的大学毕业生和第一代大学毕业生，在追随自己的热情时，比家庭更富裕的同龄人更有可能最终从事低薪的非技术性工作。

P9

Colleges, workplaces and career counselors who promote the "follow your passion" path for everyone, without leveling the playing field, help perpetuate socioeconomic inequalities among career aspirants.

那些向所有人宣传“追随你的热情”这条道路，却不努力创造公平竞争环境的高校、工作场所和职业顾问，助长了职业追求者之间的社会经济不平等。

P10

It's not just well-off passion-seekers who benefit from the passion principle. Employers of passionate workers do, too. Potential employers showed greater interest in passionate applicants in part because they believed the applicants would work hard at their jobs without expecting an increase in pay. They even sacrifice a good salary, job stability and leisure time to work in a job they love.

从“热情原则”中获益的不仅仅是经济条件优越的热情追逐者，热情洋溢的员工的雇主也同样受益。潜在雇主对充满热情的求职者表现出更大的兴趣，部分原因在于他们认为这些求职者会努力工作，却不会要求加薪。他们甚至会牺牲丰厚的薪水、工作的稳定性和闲暇时间，去从事一份自己热爱的工作。

解析逐题详解

51

What did the author advise people do for almost a decade?

作者在近十年里建议人们做什么?

  • A)Figure out what is the most fascinating job.弄清楚什么是最令人着迷的工作。
  • B)Follow widely accepted career counsel.遵循广泛接受的职业建议。
  • C)Pursue their careers with passion.满怀热情地追求自己的职业。
  • D)Do whatever they are zealous for.做任何他们热衷的事情。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 偷换概念Figure out what is the most fascinating job 把 fascinated 这个形容作者自己研究方向的词嫁接到 job 上，原文 P2 是作者劝别人 follow passion，不是劝别人去找最有趣的工作。
B) 张冠李戴Follow widely accepted career counsel 把作者自己当年发出的建议描述为去跟随别人的建议，但原文 I counseled 表明作者本人就是 counsel 的发出者。
C) 偷换概念Pursue their careers with passion 是『用激情去追求事业』，原文是『追随你的激情』，二者并不等价：前者是把激情当工作态度，后者是把激情当工作方向。
D) ✓ 正确预判答案是『追随激情/做自己热爱的事』，Do whatever they are zealous for 用 zealous for 同义替换 passion，正中预判。
  • Follow your passion↔Do whatever they are zealous for
P2

For almost a decade, I told everyone I encountered that they should do the same. "Follow your passion," I counseled. "You can figure out the employment stuff later."

在将近十年的时间里，我告诉我遇到的每一个人：他们应该和我做一样的事。我劝告说：「追随你的激情」「就业那些事儿你以后再琢磨」。

52

How did the author feel about the passion principle through his research?

作者通过研究对热情原则有何感受?

  • A)He was astonished by its consequences.他对其后果感到震惊。
  • B)He was further convinced of its soundness.他进一步确信了其合理性。
  • C)He was actually right to follow it through.他贯彻执行它实际上是正确的。
  • D)He was struck by its broad popularity.他对其广泛流行感到震撼。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判是『被研究发现震惊』，astonished by its consequences 把震惊源具体落到 consequences 上，与原文 P7 起列举的负面后果一致。
  • I was stunned by what I found out↔He was astonished by its consequences
B) 与原文相反He was further convinced of its soundness 说作者更加确信激情原则的正确性，但原文 how problematic it really was 与 stunned 均显示作者发现了它的问题，立场是反向的。
C) 与原文相反He was actually right to follow it through 说作者证实自己当初跟随激情是对的，但原文 does not necessarily lead to fulfillment 表明作者得出的是『追随激情未必带来满足』的反思结论，并非自我肯定。
D) 答非所问He was struck by its broad popularity 把震惊对象错挂到『大众接受度』上，但题干问的是 through his research，原文 P5 谈大众接受度但用的是中性动词 hold ... in high regard，作者并未对此 struck。
P4

I was stunned by what I found out about this principle in the research for my new book.

在为新书做的研究中，我被自己关于这一原则的发现震惊了。

53

What is important to turning one's passion into a stable, good-paying job?

将热情转化为稳定、高薪工作的关键是什么?

  • A)Willingness to take unpaid internships and low-paying jobs.愿意接受无薪实习和低薪工作。
  • B)Full academic preparedness and sound career counseling.充分的学业准备和合理的职业咨询。
  • C)Hard work and sacrifice of leisure time.努力工作和牺牲休闲时间。
  • D)Financial backing and social connections.经济支持和社会关系。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 偷换概念Willingness to take unpaid internships and low-paying jobs 把『愿意做无薪实习和低薪工作』当成转化成功的关键，但原文里无薪实习是富裕者的专利（while their parents pay their rent），而低薪非技能岗位则是工薪阶层 end up in low-paying unskilled jobs 的不利结局，二者都不是『成功条件』。
B) 无中生有Full academic preparedness and sound career counseling 提到的『学业准备充分』在 P8 中根本没有任何线索；P9 虽出现 career counselors 但是被批评的对象、不是关键条件。
C) 张冠李戴Hard work and sacrifice of leisure time 在原文中是 P10 雇主对激情员工的期待与员工实际付出的代价，与题 53 问的『把激情变成稳定高薪』的关键条件无关。
D) ✓ 正确预判要点是经济和人脉两个维度，Financial backing and social connections 一项即覆盖两条，是与原文论据结构最完整匹配的概括。
  • wait until a job they are passionate about comes along without worrying about student loans↔Financial backing
  • access to parents' social networks to help them find jobs↔social connections
P8

Passion-seekers from wealthy families are better able to wait until a job they are passionate about comes along without worrying about student loans in the meantime. They are also better situated to take unpaid internships to get their foot in the door while their parents pay their rent. And they often have access to parents' social networks to help them find jobs.

出身富裕家庭的激情追随者更有能力等到自己真正热爱的工作出现，同时不必担心助学贷款；他们也更有条件接受无薪实习以打开职业大门，因为有父母替他们付房租；而且，他们往往可以借助父母的社会人脉帮自己找工作。

54

What happens when everyone is encouraged to follow their passion?

当每个人都被鼓励追随自己的热情时会发生什么?

  • A)Many more career aspirants end up unemployed.更多的职业追求者最终失业。
  • B)People are less concerned with socioeconomic inequality.人们不太关心社会经济不平等。
  • C)Socioeconomic inequality is likely to persist.社会经济不平等可能持续存在。
  • D)Career counselors are going to lose credibility.职业顾问将失去公信力。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 无中生有Many more career aspirants end up unemployed 中的『失业』在原文中没有出现，P8 只说工薪阶层会落入 low-paying unskilled jobs（低薪非技能岗），那是有工作而非失业。
B) 与原文相反People are less concerned with socioeconomic inequality 说人们对不平等的关注度下降，原文 P9 说的是不平等本身被延续，并不涉及关注度的变化，且 less 反向于作者的批评立场。
C) ✓ 正确预判结果是『不平等被延续』，C 用 persist 同义、用 is likely to 对应 help 的中等程度，结构性等值，是 54 的最佳概括。
  • help perpetuate socioeconomic inequalities↔Socioeconomic inequality is likely to persist
D) 过度推断Career counselors are going to lose credibility 推断职业顾问会失去公信力，原文 P9 只是批评他们推行『跟随激情』而不拉平起跑线，并未说他们会丧失公信力。
P9

Colleges, workplaces and career counselors who promote the "follow your passion" path for everyone, without leveling the playing field, help perpetuate socioeconomic inequalities among career aspirants.

那些向所有人推行『跟随激情』路径却不去拉平起跑线的高校、用人单位与职业顾问，正在加剧职业追求者之间的社会经济不平等。

55

What does the author say about employers of passionate workers?

作者对雇用充满热情的员工的雇主有何看法?

  • A)They provide these workers with job stability and a good salary.他们为这些员工提供工作稳定性和良好的薪水。
  • B)They exploit these workers' passion to benefit themselves.他们利用这些员工的热情为自己谋利。
  • C)They level the playing field for these workers to reach their goals.他们为这些员工实现目标创造公平的竞争环境。
  • D)They encourage these workers to realize their aspirations.他们鼓励这些员工实现自己的抱负。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反They provide these workers with job stability and a good salary 说雇主给激情员工稳定和高薪，但 They even sacrifice a good salary, job stability 明示员工是在牺牲稳定和高薪、不是获得它。
B) ✓ 正确预判定性为『雇主占便宜』，exploit 准确对应『利用他人 passion 为自己获利』，是对 P10 整段事实最贴近的概括。
  • Employers of passionate workers do, too↔They exploit these workers' passion to benefit themselves
  • work hard at their jobs without expecting an increase in pay↔exploit these workers' passion
C) 张冠李戴They level the playing field for these workers to reach their goals 把『拉平起跑线』归给雇主，但原文 without leveling the playing field 表明这事根本没人做，更不是雇主在做。
D) 偷换概念They encourage these workers to realize their aspirations 用『鼓励员工实现抱负』的正面表述粉饰雇主行为，但 P10 揭示的恰是雇主借此让员工 work hard ... without expecting an increase in pay，本质是利用而非成就员工。
P10

Employers of passionate workers do, too. Potential employers showed greater interest in passionate applicants in part because they believed the applicants would work hard at their jobs without expecting an increase in pay.

雇用激情员工的雇主也同样受益。潜在雇主对激情型应聘者更感兴趣，部分原因是他们相信这些应聘者会卖力工作而不会指望涨薪。
