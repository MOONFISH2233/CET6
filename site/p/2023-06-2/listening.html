Modern humans arose in Africa about 200,000 years ago. They then spread across Europe and Asia sometime after 60,000 years ago. This is the "Out of Africa" model, as it's commonly known. In the 1990s, the hypothesis found widespread acceptance. But this popular idea is in need of revision, particularly given the number of important findings across Asia over the past few decades.

现代人类在大约20万年前起源于非洲。随后，他们在距今6万年后的某个时间扩散到了欧洲和亚洲。这就是人们通常所说的“走出非洲”模型。在20世纪90年代，这一假设得到了广泛接受。但鉴于过去几十年间在整个亚洲取得的一系列重要发现，这一流行观点有必要进行修改。

2

For instance, many new human fossils found, particularly in China, are now dated as older than 60,000 years. This calls into question the idea that modern humans migrated out of Africa only 60,000 years ago. Take the recent discovery of two modern human teeth found in the Luna cave in China's Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region. When my research team and I dated these teeth, we found they were more than 70,000 years old—a situation clearly impossible if modern humans moved out of Africa only 60,000 years ago.

例如，许多新发现的人类化石（特别是在中国发现的）现在的测年结果都早于6万年前。这使人们对现代人类仅在6万年前才迁出非洲的观点提出了质疑。以最近在中国广西壮族自治区陆那洞发现的两颗现代人类牙齿为例。当我和我的研究团队对这两颗牙齿进行测年时，我们发现它们有超过7万年的历史——如果现代人类仅在6万年前才走出非洲，这种情况显然是不可能的。

3

So with such findings, what happened exactly? Where does the most current data suggest we came from?

那么，面对这些发现，究竟发生了什么？最新的数据表明我们来自何方？

4

The first question we should ask is why did modern humans leave Africa to begin with? If a population is perfectly adapted to a particular environment and has access to an abundance of resources, then there really is no reason to move or change. For instance, some monkeys in Africa have a set of teeth that has hardly changed over the course of millions of years, indicating that they found a place that has worked for them.

我们首先应该问的问题是：现代人类最初为什么离开非洲？如果一个群体完全适应了特定环境并且拥有丰富的资源，那么确实没有理由迁移或改变。例如，非洲的一些猴子的牙齿在数百万年间几乎没有任何变化，这表明它们找到了适合自己的生存之地。

5

So what happened with humans? Some researchers have suggested that population density increased to the point where smaller human groups were forced to explore new lands. Other researchers have suggested that due to major environmental events in East Africa, humans were prompted to find greener pastures. Yet another explanation could simply be that early modern human hunters were following the large animals that they relied on, and so they moved out of Africa without realizing that they were actually moving from one continent to another.

那么人类发生了什么？一些研究人员提出，人口密度增加到了迫使较小的人类群体不得不去探索新土地的程度。另一些研究人员则认为，由于东非发生的重大环境事件，促使人类去寻找更适宜生存的环境。还有一种解释可能仅仅是，早期的现代人类猎人跟随着他们所依赖的大型动物，因此在没有意识到自己实际上正在从一个大洲迁移到另一个大洲的情况下就移出了非洲。

6

Humans need daily access to reliable fresh water, which appears to be absent from many coastal areas. This brings us to another question: by which route did modern humans move out of Africa? No clear route across the Mediterranean from North Africa appears to be present, so the earlier dispersals out of Africa and into Europe and Asia might have been across the Arabian Peninsula.

人类每天都需要获得可靠的淡水，而许多沿海地区似乎缺乏淡水。这就带出了另一个问题：现代人类是通过哪条路线迁出非洲的？从北非穿过地中海的路线似乎并不存在明确的路径，因此早期走出非洲进入欧洲和亚洲的扩散可能是穿过了阿拉伯半岛。

【题目】 Why does the woman wish to study architecture?

这位女士为什么希望学习建筑学?

  • A)She is attracted to the beauty of modern buildings.她被现代建筑的美感所吸引。
  • B)She is preoccupied with her dream to be an architect.她一心想着成为建筑师的梦想。
  • C)She is influenced by her father who teaches architecture.她受到教授建筑学的父亲的影响。
  • D)She is drawn to its integration of design and engineering.她被建筑学中设计与工程的结合所吸引。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 原词陷阱 录音确有 I'm attracted to，A 项 She is attracted to the beauty of modern buildings. 借用了 attracted 这个原词，但她被吸引的是"设计与工程的结合"，不是"现代建筑的美"——美这一点录音从未说，属听到 attracted 就想选的陷阱。
B) 原词陷阱 preoccupied 一词录音里出现在教授口中 you were preoccupied with all the lofty mathematics，指她曾为数学发愁，根本不是"痴迷当建筑师的梦想"。B 项 She is preoccupied with her dream to be an architect. 盗了 preoccupied 又改了对象，与问"动机"无关。
C) 未提及 C 项 She is influenced by her father who teaches architecture. 牵出一个"教建筑的父亲"，但整段录音从未提到她父亲，更没说受其影响——是预读时就警惕的"凭空多出一个人物"的诱选。

In particular, I'm attracted to the marriage of design and engineering.

尤其是，我被设计与工程的结合所吸引。

如果男士得到这份工作,他会做什么?

  • A)Serve as a personal assistant.担任私人助理。
  • B)Play a key role in Real Madrid.在皇家马德里扮演关键角色。
  • C)Run common daily chores for the woman.为女士处理日常琐事。
  • D)Help promote Mr. Sanchez's public profile.帮助提升桑切斯先生的公众形象。
解析
A（细节题）
B) 过度推断 Play a key role in Real Madrid. 借了第7、8段反复出现的 Real Madrid，把"跑腿助理"夸成"在皇马担任关键角色"。录音清楚说他要做的是 run common everyday chores（跑日常琐事），与"key role"完全相反，是过度拔高。
