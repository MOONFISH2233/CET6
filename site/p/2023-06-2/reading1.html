原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

How on earth did we come to this? We protect our children obsessively from every harm; we scrutinize every carer, teacher or doctor with whom they come into contact. Yet despite all this, one group, which in no way has their best interests at heart, has almost unconstrained access.

我们究竟是如何走到这一步的？我们近乎偏执地保护孩子免受一切伤害；我们审查与他们接触的每一位看护人、教师或医生。然而，尽管做到了这一切，一个绝非一心为了孩子最大利益着想的群体，却几乎可以无拘无束地接触到他们。

P2

We seem to take it for granted that advertisers and marketers are allowed to condition even the youngest children. Before children have even developed a proper sense of their own identity, or learned to handle money, they're encouraged to associate status and self-worth with stuff, and to look to external things such as fame and wealth for validation. We're turning out little consumers rather than young citizens who will value themselves for what they contribute to the society in which they live.

我们似乎理所当然地认为，广告商和营销人员被允许去塑造哪怕是最年幼的孩子。在孩子们甚至还没建立起对自我身份的正确认知，或学会管理金钱之前，他们就被鼓励将社会地位和自我价值与物质挂钩，并寻求名声和财富等外在事物来证明自己。我们正在培养微型消费者，而不是那些会因自己对所生活的社会做出贡献而珍视自身价值的青年公民。

P3

We've reached this point so gradually that many of us have never questioned it. It's crept up on us in the 60 years since advertisers started to target the young and found that they could recruit them to a commercial assault on their parents. We've come to know it as "pester power" or the ability of children to pressure parents to make certain purchases.

我们走到这一步是如此循序渐进，以至于我们当中的许多人从未对此提出过质疑。在广告商开始针对年轻人、并发现可以招募他们对其父母发起商业冲击以来的60年里，这一切已经悄然潜入我们的生活。我们逐渐将其称为“缠人力量”（pester power），即儿童施压父母做出某些购买行为的能力。

P4

Many psychologists, child development experts and educators point to research suggesting that this emerging cradle-to-grave consumerism is contributing to growing rates of low self-esteem, depression and other forms of mental illness.

许多心理学家、儿童发展专家和教育工作者指出，研究表明这种兴起的“从摇篮到坟墓”的消费主义正在导致自卑、抑郁和其他形式心理疾病的发生率不断上升。

P5

Not all psychologists agree. There're plenty working hand in glove with a ￡12bn-a-year industry that has turned the manipulation of adult emotions and desires into an art form—often literally. It's also one that's forever developing new ways to persuade our children to desire material possessions, and because of advertisements' viral effect they only need to infect a few to reach the many. Advertising and marketing can serve a useful purpose for children. Marketing may help socialize children as consumers, inform them about products, and help them carve out unique identities as they reach adulthood.

并非所有心理学家都持赞同意见。有许多心理学家与这个年产值达120亿英镑的产业密切合作，该产业已将对成年人情感和欲望的操纵变成了一门艺术形式——往往字面意义上确实如此。该产业还在不断开发新方法来劝诱我们的孩子渴望物质财产，并且由于广告的病毒式效应，他们只需要感染少数人就能传播给大众。广告和营销对儿童也能起到有益的作用。营销可能有助于促进儿童作为消费者的社会化，向他们提供产品信息，并帮助他们在迈入成年期时树立独特的自我身份。

P6

Then, should we ban all advertising aimed at young children? I say yes.

那么，我们是否应该禁止所有针对幼童的广告？我的答案是肯定的。

P7

Of course there'll be plenty of objections to an outright ban on advertising to the under-11s. There'll be those who argue that would be a breach of freedom of speech and infringe the rights of corporations to brainwash little children into demanding their products.

当然，全面禁止面向11岁以下儿童的广告必然会引发大量反对意见。有些人会辩称，这将是对言论自由的侵犯，并侵犯了企业通过给幼童洗脑来让他们索要其产品的权利。

P8

Most parents hate what advertising does to their children, but we do have the power to end it and let our children grow up free from many of the pressures of consumerism until they're old enough to make their own decisions. And though advertising is only part of an all-pervasive(无处不在的) marketing culture we need to make a start somewhere. Let's ban all advertising targeting children of primary school age and younger now.

大多数父母都憎恨广告对其孩子产生的影响，但我们确实有能力终结它，让我们的孩子在成长过程中免受消费主义诸多压力的困扰，直到他们大到足以做出自己的决定。尽管广告只是无处不在的营销文化的一部分，但我们需要从某个地方开始。让我们现在就禁止所有针对小学及以下年龄儿童的广告。

解析逐题详解

46

Which group of people does the author say has almost unrestricted access to children?

作者说哪一群体几乎可以不受限制地接触儿童?

  • A)Advertisers.广告商。
  • B)Carers.看护者。
  • C)Teachers.教师。
  • D)Doctors.医生。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判的「对孩子不友好却不受限制的群体」在 P2 被明示为广告商和营销者，与选项 Advertisers 直接对应。
  • advertisers and marketers are allowed to condition even the youngest children↔Advertisers
B) 与原文相反Carers 属于 every carer, teacher or doctor with whom they come into contact 中被严格审查的一方，是「保护孩子」的角色，与「几乎不受限地接触孩子」的群体相反。
C) 与原文相反Teachers 同样位于 every carer, teacher or doctor 列表中，是被审查的对象，不是「几乎不受限地接触孩子」的群体。
D) 与原文相反Doctors 也是 every carer, teacher or doctor 列表中的被审查对象，原文未把医生列为「几乎不受限接触孩子」的群体。
P1

Yet despite all this, one group, which in no way has their best interests at heart, has almost unconstrained access.

然而尽管如此，有一个群体，他们丝毫不把孩子的最大利益放在心上，却几乎不受限制地接触着孩子。

47

What kind of people should we enable children to become according to the author?

根据作者的观点,我们应该让儿童成为什么样的人?

  • A)Those who look to fame and wealth for external and ultimate validation.那些寻求名利以获得外在和终极认可的人。
  • B)Those who value themselves because of their contribution to society.那些因为对社会的贡献而重视自己的人。
  • C)Those who associate self-worth with the ability to handle money.那些将自我价值与处理金钱的能力联系起来的人。
  • D)Those who have developed a proper sense of their own identity.那些已经形成了对自身身份认同的恰当意识的人。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反Those who look to fame and wealth for external and ultimate validation 对应 to look to external things such as fame and wealth for validation，是作者批判而非倡导的形象。
B) ✓ 正确选项 B 在主语「Those who」、动词「value themselves」、原因状语三处与定位句逐项对应，是作者正向倡导项的同义改写。
  • young citizens who will value themselves for what they contribute to the society in which they live↔Those who value themselves because of their contribution to society
C) 偷换概念Those who associate self-worth with the ability to handle money 把 learned to handle money 这个被作者列出的「尚未具备」的前提，偷换成孩子应该成为的目标。
D) 偷换概念Those who have developed a proper sense of their own identity 同样取自 Before children have even developed a proper sense of their own identity，仅是作者列出的孩子尚未具备的前提，并非最终倡导项。
P2

We're turning out little consumers rather than young citizens who will value themselves for what they contribute to the society in which they live.

我们正在培养小消费者，而不是会因自己对所在社会的贡献而看重自身价值的年轻公民。

48

Many child development experts and educators call attention to research that suggests _____.

许多儿童发展专家和教育工作者呼吁关注研究,这些研究表明_____。

  • A)life-long consumerism is causing more and more cases of psychological problems.终身消费主义正在导致越来越多的心理问题病例。
  • B)increasing commercialization of education is eroding many children's self-esteem.教育日益商业化正在侵蚀许多儿童的自尊。
  • C)the growing desire for wealth is contributing to a rising rate of depression.对财富日益增长的渴望正在导致抑郁症发病率上升。
  • D)the craving for purchasing material things is nurtured throughout one's life.对购买物质产品的渴望在人的一生中被培养起来。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确选项 A 的主语「终生消费主义」、谓语「正在导致」、宾语「越来越多心理问题」与定位句三项要点逐一同义对应，是最精确的复述。
  • this emerging cradle-to-grave consumerism↔life-long consumerism
  • growing rates of low self-esteem, depression and other forms of mental illness↔more and more cases of psychological problems
  • is contributing to↔is causing
B) 无中生有increasing commercialization of education 在原文中完全没有提及，作者批评的是面向孩子的广告与营销，不是教育自身的商业化。
C) 以偏概全the growing desire for wealth 只是原文 fame and wealth 的一半；a rising rate of depression 也只是 low self-esteem, depression and other forms of mental illness 的一项。两处都只取了原文的局部。
D) 答非所问the craving for purchasing material things is nurtured throughout one's life 说的是「物欲是如何被培养起来的」，而题干要求复述「研究表明的因果结论」——D 项把因果关系换成了「物欲是如何形成」，与题干追问方向不一致。
P4

Many psychologists, child development experts and educators point to research suggesting that this emerging cradle-to-grave consumerism is contributing to growing rates of low self-esteem, depression and other forms of mental illness.

许多心理学家、儿童发展专家和教育者援引研究指出：这种正在出现的「从摇篮到坟墓」的消费主义，正在导致低自尊、抑郁和其他形式心理疾病的发生率不断上升。

49

What does the author imply about the impact of advertising?

关于广告的影响,作者暗示了什么?

  • A)It is actually infectious to many rather than a few.它实际上会传染给许多人而不是少数人。
  • B)It is rooted in our desire for material possessions.它植根于我们对物质财产的欲望。
  • C)It is comparable to that of virus.它可与病毒的影响相比。
  • D)It is literally limited to children.它实际上仅限于儿童。
解析
A（推断题）
A) ✓ 正确由 infect a few to reach the many 一步推出「广告其实波及多数而非少数」，正好是 imply 题所要的「一步推断」型答案。
  • they only need to infect a few to reach the many↔It is actually infectious to many rather than a few
  • they only need to infect a few to reach the many↔rather than a few
B) 与原文相反rooted in our desire for material possessions 把因果反过来——原文是「广告诱使孩子产生物欲」（persuade our children to desire material possessions），不是「广告源于物欲」。
C) 过度推断comparable to that of virus 已经超出原文 viral effect 的比喻：原文只说「影响具有病毒式扩散效应」，并没断言「广告影响可与病毒影响相提并论」。两者程度差异明显。
D) 与原文相反literally limited to children 把广告影响范围缩限到「仅限于儿童」，而原文 infect a few to reach the many 恰恰强调影响会扩散到「多数」，并非「仅限于儿童」。
P5

It's also one that's forever developing new ways to persuade our children to desire material possessions, and because of advertisements' viral effect they only need to infect a few to reach the many.

它（广告业）也是这样一个行业：不断开发新方法来诱使我们的孩子渴望物质；而由于广告的病毒式效应，他们只需感染少数即可触及多数。

50

What is the opponents' argument against a complete ban on advertising to young children?

反对者针对完全禁止向幼儿做广告提出了什么论点?

  • A)It would deprive them of the chance to learn about products.这会剥夺他们了解产品的机会。
  • B)It would render them unable to carve out unique identities.这会使他们无法塑造独特的身份认同。
  • C)It would breach their freedom as would-be consumers.这会侵犯他们作为潜在消费者的自由。
  • D)It would violate the rights endowed upon advertisers.这会侵犯赋予广告商的权利。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴deprive them of the chance to learn about products 取自 Marketing may help socialize children as consumers, inform them about products，是作者描述广告「益处」的内容，并非 P7 中的反方论据。
B) 张冠李戴render them unable to carve out unique identities 同样取自 help them carve out unique identities as they reach adulthood，是广告作为「益处」的功能描述，非 P7 中反方对禁令的反对理由。
C) 偷换概念breach their freedom as would-be consumers 中 their 指 young children；但 a breach of freedom of speech 中被侵犯的是「言论自由」，主语暗指「企业表达自由」，不是「孩子作为准消费者的自由」。
D) ✓ 正确反方第二条论据「损害企业的权利」被选项 D 完整同义改写为「侵犯广告商被赋予的权利」，且两条论据中只有该条被选项明确复述。
  • infringe the rights of corporations to brainwash little children into demanding their products↔violate the rights endowed upon advertisers
  • the rights of corporations to brainwash little children into demanding their products↔the rights endowed upon advertisers
P7

There'll be those who argue that would be a breach of freedom of speech and infringe the rights of corporations to brainwash little children into demanding their products.

会有人辩称（禁止广告）将构成对言论自由的侵犯，并损害企业「向幼儿洗脑、让他们索要产品」的权利。
