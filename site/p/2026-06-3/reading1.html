原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Three artists have brought a lawsuit against Stability AI, DeviantArt, and Midjourney, alleging that the text-to-image AI tools have infringed the rights of thousands of artists.

三位艺术家已对 Stability AI、DeviantArt 和 Midjourney 提起诉讼，指控这些文本生成图像的人工智能工具侵犯了数千名艺术家的权利。

P2

The lawsuit claims that the Stable Diffusion tool used by Stability AI, DeviantArt, and Midjourney was trained on billions of copyrighted images downloaded from the internet and used by the companies "without compensation or consent from the artists."

诉讼称，Stability AI、DeviantArt 和 Midjourney 使用的 Stable Diffusion 工具，是利用从互联网下载的数十亿张受版权保护的图像进行训练的，而这些公司使用这些图像时“没有向艺术家提供补偿，也未获得他们的同意”。

P3

The artists are represented by The Joseph Saveri Law Firm and Matthew Butterick.

这些艺术家由 The Joseph Saveri Law Firm 和 Matthew Butterick 代理。

P4

"If Stable Diffusion and similar products are allowed to continue to operate as they do now, the foreseeable result is they will replace the very artists whose stolen works power these AI products with whom they are competing," the law firm wrote in a press release. "AI image products are not just an infringement of artists' rights; they will eliminate 'artist' as a viable career path. In addition to obtaining compensation for the wrongful conduct, this lawsuit seeks to prevent that outcome and ensure these products follow the same rules as any other new technology that involves the use of massive amounts of intellectual property. If streaming music can be accomplished within the law, so can AI products."

该律师事务所在一份新闻稿中写道：“如果允许 Stable Diffusion 及类似产品继续以目前的方式运营，那么可以预见的结果是，它们将取代那些为这些人工智能产品提供动力、而这些产品正与之竞争的艺术家，而被取代的正是其作品遭到窃取的艺术家。人工智能图像产品不仅侵犯了艺术家的权利，还将使‘艺术家’不再是一条可行的职业道路。除了为不当行为寻求赔偿外，本诉讼还旨在阻止这一结果，并确保这些产品遵守与任何其他涉及使用大量知识产权的新技术相同的规则。如果音乐流媒体能够在法律框架内实现，人工智能产品也同样可以做到。”

P5

Since text-to-image generators have increased in popularity in the past year, many artists have been vocalizing their opposition to AI art after seeing their own distorted signatures appear in AI art and finding out that their work was used to train the tools without their permission.

在过去一年中，随着文本生成图像工具越来越受欢迎，许多艺术家在看到自己扭曲的签名出现在人工智能生成的艺术作品中，并发现自己的作品未经许可就被用于训练这些工具后，纷纷公开表达对人工智能艺术的反对。

P6

Not only are many artists furious at the possible copyright violations of AI art, but they have also been frustrated by how AI tools are able to sidestep the labor and art processes they are required to go through. As a result, many artists have been trying to ban AI art from art-sharing platforms.

许多艺术家不仅对人工智能艺术可能侵犯版权感到愤怒，也因人工智能工具能够绕过他们必须经历的劳动和艺术创作过程而感到沮丧。因此，许多艺术家一直在试图禁止人工智能艺术进入艺术分享平台。

P7

As AI innovation continues at a rapid speed, it seems that artists and lawmakers are trying to put a brake on further developments before it's too late.

随着人工智能创新以极快的速度持续发展，艺术家和立法者似乎正试图在为时已晚之前遏制进一步的发展。

P8

Many tech enthusiasts are still rallying around the promise of AI tools to create detailed images cheaply and quickly—anonymous tech supporters have already published a response to Butterick and Joseph Saveri's lawsuit on a website, using the "fair use" clause as the basis for why copyrighted images could be used by the tool.

许多科技爱好者仍在支持人工智能工具能够低成本、快速创作精细图像这一前景；匿名的科技支持者已经在一个网站上发布了对 Butterick 和 Joseph Saveri 诉讼的回应，以“合理使用”条款为依据，解释为什么这些受版权保护的图像可以被该工具使用。

P9

Fair use, a legal doctrine that allows for a limited usage of copyrighted materials without permission by the original creators for purposes such as teaching and news sharing, is what a lot of generative model creators claim that their training data is covered by. However, since fair use applies differently to each case, it's hard to make a blanket statement about all works in a dataset as being applicable.

合理使用是一项法律原则，允许出于教学和新闻传播等目的，在未经原作者许可的情况下有限使用受版权保护的材料。许多生成模型的创作者声称，他们的训练数据受到这一原则的保护。然而，由于合理使用在不同案件中的适用方式各不相同，因此很难笼统地断言数据集中的所有作品都适用这一原则。

Why have three artists brought a lawsuit against Stability AI, DeviantArt, and Midjourney?

三位艺术家为何起诉 Stability AI、DeviantArt 和 Midjourney？

  • A)They claim that the use of the Stable Diffusion tool is a violation of law.他们声称使用 Stable Diffusion 工具违反法律。
  • B)They claim these companies infringed their intellectual property rights.他们声称这些公司侵犯了自己的知识产权。
  • C)They want to stop the use of all AI tools in the creation of art works.他们想阻止在艺术作品创作中使用一切人工智能工具。
  • D)They want to prevent artists from being compelled to rely on AI tools.他们想防止艺术家被迫依赖人工智能工具。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念They claim that the use of the Stable Diffusion tool is a violation of law.将原文'侵犯权利'过度泛化为'违法'，弱化了'知识产权/版权'这一具体客体。
B) ✓ 正确these companies infringed their intellectual property rights与have infringed the rights of thousands of artists和copyrighted images...without compensation or consent形成同义替换，准确对应起诉原因。
  • have infringed the rights of thousands of artists↔infringed their intellectual property rights
  • copyrighted images downloaded from the internet and used by the companies "without compensation or consent from the artists"↔these companies infringed their intellectual property rights
C) 无中生有They want to stop the use of all AI tools in the creation of art works.中的'all AI tools'在原文无对应，艺术家仅起诉特定公司的特定工具，并非要求禁止所有AI艺术工具。
D) 无中生有They want to prevent artists from being compelled to rely on AI tools.中的'compelled to rely on AI tools'在原文无对应；原文说的是AI会'replace'艺术家、'eliminate artist as a viable career path'，方向是取代而非迫使依赖。

What will happen if Stable Diffusion and similar products are allowed to continue to operate as they do now, according to the law firm?

据该律师事务所称，如果允许 Stable Diffusion 及类似产品继续像现在这样运行，将会发生什么？

  • A)Intellectual property laws will be hard to enforce.知识产权法将难以执行。
  • B)Fewer artists will invest talent in artistic creation.将有更少的艺术家把才华投入艺术创作。
  • C)Artists will lose their means of subsistence.艺术家将失去谋生手段。
  • D)Copied works will flood the art market.复制作品将充斥艺术市场。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 无中生有Intellectual property laws will be hard to enforce.中的'hard to enforce'在定位句及全文中均无对应，属于凭空捏造。
B) 偷换概念Fewer artists will invest talent in artistic creation.把原文'eliminate artist as a viable career path'（当艺术家这条路被堵死）改写为'艺术家投入才华变少'，改变了因果主体与语义重心。
C) ✓ 正确Artists will lose their means of subsistence.对应replace the very artists和eliminate 'artist' as a viable career path，准确概括了AI继续运营对艺术家生计的冲击。
  • they will replace the very artists whose stolen works power these AI products with whom they are competing↔Artists will lose their means of subsistence.
  • they will eliminate 'artist' as a viable career path↔Artists will lose their means of subsistence.
D) 过度推断Copied works will flood the art market.中的'flood the art market'在原文无对应，原文强调的是AI取代艺术家，而非复制品数量多。
P4

"AI image products are not just an infringement of artists' rights; they will eliminate 'artist' as a viable career path."

48

What makes artists furious aside from copyright infringement?

除了侵犯版权之外，什么还令艺术家们感到愤怒？

  • A)Art works produced with AI tools are not banned from art-sharing platforms.利用人工智能工具创作的艺术作品没有被艺术分享平台禁止。
  • B)Text-to-image generators have won such great popularity in the past year.文本生成图像工具在过去一年中获得了如此大的欢迎。
  • C)Their distorted signatures appear more and more frequently in AI art.他们被扭曲的签名越来越频繁地出现在人工智能艺术作品中。
  • D)AI tools can bypass the considerable labor involved in creating art works.人工智能工具可以绕过艺术创作所涉及的大量劳动。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反Art works produced with AI tools are not banned from art-sharing platforms.与原文many artists have been trying to ban AI art from art-sharing platforms方向相反，且不是aside from所问的另一愤怒点。
B) 答非所问Text-to-image generators have won such great popularity in the past year.虽然在原文P5中出现，但它只是背景信息，并非题干所问'aside from copyright infringement'使艺术家愤怒的原因。
C) 答非所问Their distorted signatures appear more and more frequently in AI art.对应seeing their own distorted signatures appear in AI art，这一现象属于版权/未经授权使用的证据，与题干要求排除的copyright infringement属于同一层面，不是aside from所求的另一项。
D) ✓ 正确AI tools can bypass the considerable labor involved in creating art works.与AI tools are able to sidestep the labor and art processes they are required to go through构成精确同义替换，符合题干'aside from copyright infringement'的限定。
  • AI tools are able to sidestep the labor and art processes they are required to go through↔AI tools can bypass the considerable labor involved in creating art works.
P6

Not only are many artists furious at the possible copyright violations of AI art, but they have also been frustrated by how AI tools are able to sidestep the labor and art processes they are required to go through.

许多艺术家不仅对AI艺术可能存在的版权侵权行为感到愤怒，而且还对AI工具如何能够绕开他们必须经历的劳动和艺术创作过程感到沮丧。

What do we learn from the passage about many AI enthusiasts?

  • A)They look forward to AI tools creating good-quality art works.他们期待人工智能工具创作出高质量的艺术作品。
  • B)They claim artists can use copyrighted materials just as teachers do.他们声称艺术家可以像教师一样使用受版权保护的材料。
  • C)They support AI's limited use of copyrighted images without permission.他们支持人工智能未经许可有限度地使用受版权保护的图像。
  • D)They wish the "fair use" clause would be applicable to all works in a dataset.他们希望“合理使用”条款适用于数据集中的所有作品。

C（细节题）
A) 偷换概念They look forward to AI tools creating good-quality art works.把原文create detailed images cheaply and quickly中的'detailed images'改写为'good-quality art works'，改变了对象（图像→艺术作品）和重点（廉价快速→质量）。

C) ✓ 正确They support AI's limited use of copyrighted images without permission.与using the "fair use" clause as the basis for why copyrighted images could be used by the tool形成同义替换，体现了支持者'有限度使用'的核心立场。
  • using the "fair use" clause as the basis for why copyrighted images could be used by the tool↔They support AI's limited use of copyrighted images without permission.
D) 张冠李戴They wish the "fair use" clause would be applicable to all works in a dataset.中的'all works in a dataset'对应all works in a dataset，但这是作者在P9中的判断对象，并非P8中支持者的主张；原文未说支持者希望fair use适用于所有作品。

What does the author think of the "fair use" clause in copyright law?

作者如何看待版权法中的“合理使用”条款？

  • A)It may determine the final verdict of the case.它可能决定案件的最终裁决。
  • B)It may not apply to the present legal case.它可能不适用于当前的法律案件。
  • C)It does not cover the works of all artists.它不涵盖所有艺术家的作品。
  • D)It is subject to different interpretations.它可能受到不同的解读。
解析
D（态度题）
A) 无中生有It may determine the final verdict of the case.中的'final verdict'在原文无对应，作者仅讨论fair use的适用性，未预测判决结果。
B) 过度推断It may not apply to the present legal case.把原文'难以一概而论'过度推断为'可能不适用于本案'，作者并未得出该否定结论。
C) 偷换概念It does not cover the works of all artists.把原文讨论的范围从'数据集里的所有作品能否统一适用fair use'偷换为'是否覆盖所有艺术家'，对象发生转移。
D) ✓ 正确It is subject to different interpretations.与fair use applies differently to each case和it's hard to make a blanket statement形成同义替换，准确表达作者认为fair use需个案判断的立场。
  • fair use applies differently to each case↔It is subject to different interpretations.
  • it's hard to make a blanket statement about all works in a dataset as being applicable↔It is subject to different interpretations.
