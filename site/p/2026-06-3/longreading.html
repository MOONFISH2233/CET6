A

A) The cost of a mortgage or rents, meeting monthly utility bills, paying for a new car—all headaches most people get to worry about from their 20s. But while some may struggle to pay their bills after a few too many nights out at the start of the month, others will have a tightly followed plan for their repayments. So what is the difference between the shrewd saver and the more reckless spender?

抵押贷款或房租的费用、每月缴纳水电等生活账单、支付一辆新车的费用——这些都是大多数人从二十多岁起就要操心的头疼事。不过，有些人可能会因为月初外出玩乐的夜晚太多而难以支付账单，另一些人却会严格遵循还款计划。那么，精明的储蓄者与更为鲁莽的花钱者之间究竟有什么区别呢？

B

B) Experts believe it is all about the age you learn how to spend responsibly. Adult money habits are typically set by the age of seven, according to a large-scale study by the government's Money Advice Service and behaviour experts at Cambridge University. By then, most children have grasped how to recognise the value of money and are capable of complex functions such as planning ahead, delaying a decision until later and understanding that some choices are irreversible.

专家认为，这一切都取决于你在什么年龄学会负责任地消费。政府的金钱咨询服务机构与剑桥大学的行为专家开展的一项大规模研究显示，成年人的金钱习惯通常在七岁时就已经形成。到那时，大多数孩子已经掌握了如何识别金钱价值，并且能够进行一些复杂的思考，例如提前规划、将决定推迟到以后再做，以及理解有些选择是不可逆的。

C

C) Tomorrow marks the start of Global Money Week, a worldwide initiative to help children make smart financial decisions and learn that "money matters matter". But for many young people in the UK, it's a matter of too little, too late. "The habits of mind which influence the ways children approach complex problems and decisions, including financial ones, are largely determined in the first few years of life," says Dr David Whitebread, the co-author of the study. And this core behaviour is very likely to influence their financial decisions when they are adults, he says.

明天将迎来全球金钱周的开始。这是一项旨在帮助儿童作出明智的财务决定，并让他们懂得“金钱问题很重要”的全球性倡议。但对英国的许多年轻人来说，这已经是一个太少、太晚的问题。“影响儿童处理复杂问题和作出决定，包括财务决定的思维习惯，很大程度上是在生命最初几年形成的，”该研究的共同作者戴维·怀特布雷德博士说。他表示，这种核心行为很可能会影响他们成年后的财务决定。

D

D) The study also urges parents not to underestimate their own good and bad money habits. "Since young children have few monetary resources they control independently, it is the basic approaches and skills which are modelled, discussed and demonstrated by parents that are likely to be influential levers, breeding efficient habits and practices," it says.

这项研究还敦促父母不要低估自己良好或不良金钱习惯的影响。“由于幼儿能够独立支配的金钱资源很少，因此，父母所示范、讨论和展示的基本方法与技能，很可能是促成有效习惯和做法的重要影响因素，”研究报告称。

E

E) Parents who choose to help their children learn how to plan ahead, reflect on the past and self-regulate can make a huge difference in promoting good financial behaviour in the future, explains Whitebread. According to a survey by M&G Investments, the vast majority (83%) of parents recognise the value of teaching their children about money. However, this concern does not always carry through to practice—one in six do not feel confident enough to do it. More than one in every four parents think children should take responsibility for understanding money themselves.

怀特布雷德解释说，选择帮助孩子学会提前规划、反思过去并进行自我调节的父母，可以在促进孩子未来良好财务行为方面发挥巨大作用。M&G投资公司的一项调查显示，绝大多数父母（83%）都认识到教孩子了解金钱的价值。然而，这种关注并不总能落实到实践中——六分之一的父母觉得自己没有足够的信心去做这件事。超过四分之一的父母认为，孩子应该自己负责了解金钱。

F

F) "There's joy in the fact that young children are spontaneous and live life in the moment, unrestrained by adult problems. But teaching children about money when they are young is a good way for parents to help them learn to delay satisfaction, so that the child thinks: 'I'd like to do this but I'm not going to'. Hard evidence suggests, if you want your child to do well in life, the earlier you can start trying to teach them inhibition and self-discipline, the better," says Whitebread, pointing to research that has shown people in prison frequently have poor impulse control, a personality trait that can affect relationships and long-term health.

“幼儿 spontanes地生活在当下，不受成年人的烦恼束缚，这其中自有乐趣。但在孩子年幼时教他们认识金钱，是父母帮助他们学会延迟满足的一种好方法，这样孩子就会想：‘我想做这件事，但我不会去做。’确凿的证据表明，如果你希望孩子一生过得好，那么越早开始尝试教他们克制和自律，效果就越好，”怀特布雷德说。他指出，有研究显示，监狱中的人往往缺乏控制冲动的能力，而这种人格特征可能影响人际关系和长期健康。

G

G) But how should a parent try to teach their child to spend responsibly? One simple way is to give them pocket money and then help them to analyse and learn from any foolish spending decisions.

但是，父母应该怎样教孩子负责任地消费呢？一个简单的方法是给孩子零花钱，然后帮助他们分析并从任何不理智的消费决定中吸取教训。

H

H) Another is through board games which can help teach financial responsibility. Pop to the Shops, a shopping board game for five-to-nine-year-olds, has been one of Orchard Toys' top 10 bestselling board games every year for the past 15 years. To win, players must use fake coins to purchase everyday grocery items from various shops around the board, and give the other players who visit their shop the correct change. If they don't have enough money, they must purchase a cheaper item. "It acts as a conversation starter about money—parents can fill in the gaps," says Orchard Toys product manager Rachael Sutcliffe.

另一种方法是通过棋盘游戏来帮助孩子学习财务责任感。面向五至九岁儿童的购物棋盘游戏《去商店吧》在过去15年中每年都跻身Orchard Toys最畅销的十大棋盘游戏之列。为了获胜，玩家必须使用假硬币，在棋盘周围的不同商店购买日常食品，并且要给到访自己商店的其他玩家找对零钱。如果钱不够，他们就必须购买更便宜的商品。Orchard Toys的产品经理蕾切尔·萨克利夫说：“它可以成为一个谈论金钱的开端，父母可以补充其中的不足。”

I

I) There are also some pocket-money-tracking and payment apps, such as RoosterMoney and goHenry, which are designed to be used by under-sevens, and which boast that they can help children develop good money habits. This is dependent on the children receiving enough pocket money to need an app to track their spending, which research suggests is not always the case.

还有一些追踪零花钱和支付的应用程序，例如RoosterMoney和goHenry，它们面向七岁以下儿童设计，并声称能够帮助孩子养成良好的金钱习惯。不过，这取决于孩子是否能获得足够多的零花钱，以至于需要使用应用程序来追踪支出，而研究表明，情况并不总是如此。

J

J) Surprisingly, given the research about habit-forming in young children, there are very few gaming apps that can help under-sevens—Pigby's Fair, developed by NatWest, being a notable exception. In this, children who solve basic puzzles will earn virtual money selling what they have made at different stalls in a fair, then spend or save it. But the need to shift virtual money around, and take into account interest payments and pocket money bonuses, makes it a complex financial education tool.

鉴于有关幼儿养成习惯的研究，令人意外的是，能够帮助七岁以下儿童学习的游戏应用程序非常少，由NatWest开发的Pigby's Fair是一个值得注意的例外。在这款游戏中，解开基础谜题的孩子可以通过在集市的不同摊位出售自己制作的东西来赚取虚拟货币，然后把钱花掉或存起来。不过，转移虚拟货币、同时考虑利息支付和零花钱奖励的需要，使它成为一种复杂的金融教育工具。

K

K) There are plenty of online games which focus on coin recognition and counting with money, such as the Igloo Shopping game or Top Marks' Coins Game. So what if children find such games boring? An exciting (and free) real-world option for parents to use is a visit to Metro Bank. Although, like most banks, it does not allow children to open an account until they turn 11, it specifically tries to offer young children a chance to learn about money inside its branches by inviting kids to use the "magic money machines" to count their coins. Children who accurately guess how much was in their piggy bank(猪形储钱罐) get a prize, and free candies are handed out when the coins are exchanged for banknotes. The bank does not take a commission from the transaction.

有许多在线游戏专门训练儿童识别硬币以及用钱进行计算，例如Igloo Shopping游戏或Top Marks的Coins Game。那么，如果孩子觉得这类游戏很无聊，该怎么办呢？父母可以选择一个令人兴奋且免费的现实体验：去Metro Bank。虽然和大多数银行一样，Metro Bank不允许儿童在年满11岁之前开户，但它专门努力为幼儿提供在银行网点内学习金钱知识的机会，邀请孩子使用“神奇点钞机”清点硬币。准确猜出自己的猪形储钱罐里有多少钱的孩子可以获得奖品；将硬币兑换成纸币时，还会免费发放糖果。银行不会从这笔交易中收取佣金。

L

L) At six years old, my daughter Flora is getting to grips with her finances early. She tested out a number of games aimed at teaching children her age the value of money. She enjoyed them all, but best, in her view, is Money Match Cafe, a board game where she must work out the value of different denominations(面额) of coin in her imaginary restaurant, her "customers" being her stuffed toys. "It was really fun because you get to serve food to your own toys on a little tray with a napkin on it, and then you can hug them," she says.

我六岁的女儿弗洛拉正在很早地学习打理自己的财务。她体验了几款旨在教同龄儿童认识金钱价值的游戏。她喜欢所有这些游戏，但在她看来，最棒的是《Money Match Cafe》，这是一款棋盘游戏。在游戏中，她必须在自己想象中的餐馆里算出不同面额硬币的价值，而她的“顾客”是她的毛绒玩具。她说：“这真的很有趣，因为你可以用一个铺着餐巾的小托盘给自己的玩具上菜，然后还可以拥抱它们。”

M

M) "If you're six and you play this game, you'll learn that you might not have the right amount of money for what you want to buy. You just have to cross your fingers and hope. It's a good challenge, and I enjoyed adding the coins up to see how much they made. But I don't like the name. Money Match Cafe is not a good name for a cafe. I would have liked it more if it had been Flora's Cafe or Fantastically Baked Lunches."

“如果你六岁并且玩这款游戏，你会学到：你可能没有足够的钱买自己想要的东西。你只能祈祷并抱着希望。这是一个很好的挑战，我喜欢把硬币加起来，看看它们总共值多少钱。但我不喜欢这个名字。Money Match Cafe不是一个适合咖啡馆的好名字。如果它叫Flora's Cafe或Fantastically Baked Lunches，我会更喜欢。”

N

N) Seven out of ten teachers think children are now faced with financial decisions earlier. On Wednesday, Lifesavers—a new project which promotes financial education in primary schools—is hosting a conference to encourage teachers to join its programme and get free classroom resources and training. Almost two-thirds of millennials(千禧一代) wish they had received more money advice at a younger age, according to a Santander survey.

十分之七的教师认为，儿童现在更早就要面对财务决定。周三，Lifesavers将举办一场会议。这是一个促进小学金融教育的新项目，旨在鼓励教师加入该项目，并获得免费的课堂资源和培训。桑坦德银行的一项调查显示，近三分之二的千禧一代希望自己在更年轻时就接受过更多的金钱方面的建议。

O

O) Only a third of parents involve their children in discussions about their household finances, even though less than half of young people aged seven to seventeen receive financial education at school, another survey shows.

另一项调查显示，尽管七至十七岁的年轻人中接受过学校金融教育的不到一半，但只有三分之一的父母会让孩子参与讨论家庭财务问题。

P

P) Most adults believe their parents have had the biggest influence on their money behaviour. So when should they start trying to instil(灌输) good habits? As early as possible before the age of seven, research suggests.

大多数成年人认为，父母对他们金钱行为的影响最大。那么，父母应该从什么时候开始努力灌输良好习惯呢？研究表明，应当在七岁之前尽可能早地开始。
