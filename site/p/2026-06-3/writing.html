范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 162 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Faced with growing globalization,students realize that a deep understanding of traditional Chinese culture helps cultivate cultural confidence. In an age when Western brands, movies and festivals flood daily life, many young people begin to look back at their own roots. They find that cultural confidence cannot be borrowed from outside; it must grow from real knowledge of what their ancestors created.

The reason is not hard to understand. Globalization exposes us to different values, yet it also makes us question who we are. Only by learning the ideas behind Confucianism, the beauty of calligraphy, or the spirit of traditional festivals can we answer that question with pride. Take the Spring Festival as an example: those who understand its meaning of reunion and renewal celebrate it not out of habit, but out of identity.

Therefore, college students should actively read classics, visit museums and practice folk arts. The deeper we understand traditional Chinese culture, the more confident we become in facing the world.
