原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

GDP growth is not a good indicator of how well a country is performing, and should not be the primary goal of governments. Unlimited growth is not sustainable, and economic thinking is moving toward the idea that we should aim for sustainability in our economic models. But while a sustainable economy is vital to our future, it is a means to an end, not an end in itself.

国内生产总值的增长并不是衡量一个国家发展状况的良好指标，也不应成为政府的首要目标。无限制的增长是不可持续的，经济学思想正在转向这样一种理念：我们的经济模式应当以可持续性为目标。然而，尽管可持续的经济对我们的未来至关重要，它只是实现目标的手段，而不是目标本身。

P2

The idea that governments should focus on happiness has its critics. There are concerns about how happiness can be measured. Is happiness not a fleeting and subjective psychological state? Don't different people experience different levels of happiness? Even on the broadest interpretation of 'happiness' as prosperity or 'life satisfaction', people want different things.

政府应当把重点放在幸福上这一观点也有批评者。人们担心幸福难以衡量。幸福难道不是一种短暂且主观的心理状态吗？不同的人难道不会体验到不同程度的幸福吗？即使将“幸福”最宽泛地理解为繁荣或“生活满意度”，人们想要的东西也各不相同。

P3

Of course, governments cannot impose life satisfaction on citizens. But our happiness relies on collaborative efforts as a society. A government's obligation lies in creating conditions that promote prosperity. And there is good reason to suppose that such conditions exist, are globally applicable, and are discoverable through research.

当然，政府无法把生活满意度强加给公民。但我们的幸福依赖于整个社会的共同努力。政府的责任在于创造促进繁荣的条件。我们有充分理由认为，这样的条件确实存在，适用于全球，并且可以通过研究加以发现。

P4

In a recently published article, philosopher Julian Baggini suggests we should focus on 'real wealth' for citizens, which does not depend on GDP growth. Access is key: people do not need to own, but rather access things that enable them to live well. Technological advances and changes in social behavior enable us to make more efficient use of the assets that we already have. And focusing on access to the resources people need to live better lives could help reduce inequality.

哲学家朱利安·巴吉尼在最近发表的一篇文章中提出，我们应当关注公民的“真正财富”，这种财富并不依赖于国内生产总值的增长。获取是关键：人们不必拥有各种东西，而只需能够获得那些使他们得以过上美好生活的东西。技术进步和社会行为的变化使我们能够更有效地利用已经拥有的资产。而且，关注人们改善生活所需资源的获取，或许有助于减少不平等。

P5

As far as it goes, this has much in common with proposals tabled by 'happiness' advocates. But it sets the bar far too low for what governments can and should be doing for their citizens.

就其涉及的范围而言，这一观点与“幸福”倡导者提出的建议有许多共同之处。但对于政府能够而且应当为公民做些什么，它所设定的标准实在太低了。

P6

For example, it's not clear how a 'real wealth' economy would remedy the epidemic of mental ill-health that plagues our society. In Western countries, at least—poor mental health is more detrimental to wellbeing than poverty. Over and above a vastly improved provision of therapeutic mental healthcare, there are preventative measures for improving mental health that governments could and should adopt. The WHO recommends establishing institutions that facilitate community participation—educational programs and interventions that provide skills for promoting mental wellbeing. It says a lot, however, that the WHO feels the need to appeal to the economic benefits of improving mental health to persuade governments that the cost of taking proposed measures is justified. As long as the economy is their priority, governments need go no further than ensuring citizens' continued productivity.

例如，目前尚不清楚“真正财富”经济将如何解决困扰我们社会的心理健康问题流行这一现象。至少在西方国家，心理健康状况不佳对福祉的损害大于贫困。除了大幅改善心理健康治疗服务的供给之外，政府还可以并且应当采取一些预防性措施来改善心理健康。世界卫生组织建议建立促进社区参与的机构，并开展能够提供促进心理福祉技能的教育项目和干预措施。然而，世界卫生组织认为有必要诉诸改善心理健康所带来的经济利益，以说服政府采取所建议措施的成本是合理的，这一事实本身就说明了很多问题。只要经济仍是政府的优先事项，政府就不必再进一步，只需确保公民持续保持生产力即可。

P7

To demand that governments set the 'happiness' of citizens as their highest priority is to demand that they view citizens as ends in themselves.

要求政府把公民的“幸福”置于最高优先地位，就是要求政府把公民本身视为目的。

解析逐题详解

51

What does the passage say is the more recent thinking of economic growth?

文章指出，关于经济增长的最新思考是什么?

  • A)It should be made sustainable.应当使其可持续。
  • B)It is vital to the future of humanity.它对人类的未来至关重要。
  • C)It should be governments' chief concern.应当成为政府的首要关注点。
  • D)It is an indicator of government performance.它是政府绩效的指标。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判「应追求可持续」与 It should be made sustainable 字面同向，无需推断，直接锁定。
  • we should aim for sustainability in our economic models↔It should be made sustainable
B) 偷换概念原文说「可持续经济」对未来重要 a sustainable economy is vital to our future，主语是「sustainable economy」，而非 It（指 economic growth）本身。
C) 与原文相反原文明确 GDP growth … should not be the primary goal of governments，作者主张增长不应是政府首要关切，It should be governments' chief concern 正好相反。
D) 与原文相反原文首句即否定 GDP growth is not a good indicator of how well a country is performing，It is an indicator of government performance 正是这个被否定的旧观点。
P1

Unlimited growth is not sustainable, and economic thinking is moving toward the idea that we should aim for sustainability in our economic models.

无限增长不可持续，经济思潮正转向「我们应当在经济模型中以可持续为目标」这一理念。

52

Why are some people opposed to the idea that governments should focus on happiness?

为什么有些人反对政府应当关注幸福的观点?

  • A)Governments cannot impose happiness on citizens.政府无法将幸福强加给公民。
  • B)People's happiness is built upon their own endeavor.人们的幸福建立在自己的努力之上。
  • C)Happiness means different things to different people.幸福对不同的人意味着不同的事物。
  • D)Happiness depends on sustainable economic growth.幸福依赖于可持续的经济增长。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴Governments cannot impose happiness on citizens 是作者本人的让步（governments cannot impose life satisfaction on citizens），而非反对者的反对原因，时空错位。
B) 无中生有原文 P2 从未提到「幸福建立在个人努力上」；endeavor 在全文不存在，是凭空添加的因果。
C) ✓ 正确「幸福因人而异」与 Happiness means different things to different people 同义，三个反问句的共同指向皆收束于此。
  • people want different things↔Happiness means different things to different people
  • Don't different people experience different levels of happiness\?↔Happiness means different things to different people
D) 强加因果P1 把 sustainable 与 happiness 视为两条不同线索，原文从未说 Happiness depends on sustainable economic growth；该选项把两个概念强行嫁接。
P2

Even on the broadest interpretation of 'happiness' as prosperity or 'life satisfaction', people want different things.

即使把「幸福」理解到最宽泛的程度——视为「繁荣」或「生活满意度」——人们想要的东西也各不相同。

53

What does philosopher Julian Baggini suggest governments do in a recently published article?

哲学家Julian Baggini在最近发表的一篇文章中建议政府做什么?

  • A)Try to reduce inequality between the rich and the poor.努力减少贫富之间的不平等。
  • B)Provide people with access to resources for a better life.为人们提供获得更好生活的资源渠道。
  • C)Change people's behaviors to put social wealth to better use.改变人们的行为以更好地利用社会财富。
  • D)Make use of advanced technologies to improve people's lives.利用先进技术改善人们的生活。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 以偏概全could help reduce inequality 只是 Baggini 主张的「附带效益」（could help），不是其建议政府该做的核心；选项 A 把附带效益当成主张主体。
B) ✓ 正确预判「让公民能用上让生活更好的资源」与 Provide people with access to resources for a better life 字面同向。
  • access things that enable them to live well↔Provide people with access to resources for a better life
C) 偷换概念原文说 changes in social behavior enable us to make more efficient use of the assets，是「社会行为已发生的变化」让现有资产用得更高效；选项把它改成「政府去改变人们行为以更好利用社会财富」，主动者与目的都被偷换。
D) 以偏概全Technological advances 在原文同样只是 access 模型成立的「条件」，不是 Baggini 给政府的建议；选项 D 抓了边缘条件而漏掉主张句。
P4

Access is key: people do not need to own, but rather access things that enable them to live well.

「使用权」是关键：人们不必拥有，而是要能用上那些让他们生活得更好的东西。

54

Why does the WHO feel the need to appeal to the economic benefits of improving mental health to justify its recommendations?

为什么WHO认为有必要诉诸改善心理健康的经济效益来证明其建议的合理性?

  • A)Mental health programs cannot be executed without GDP growth.没有GDP增长就无法执行心理健康项目。
  • B)Psychological interventions are conducive to people's wellbeing.心理干预有助于人们的幸福感。
  • C)Poor mental health is detrimental to a nation's economic system.心理健康不良对国家经济体系有害。
  • D)Governments still take economic development as their priority.政府仍然将经济发展作为优先事项。
解析
D（推断题）
A) 无中生有原文从未说「无 GDP 增长就执行不了心理健康项目」，仅说「政府要看到经济收益才肯掏钱」；选项 A 把动机改成了执行前提，凭空捏造。
B) 答非所问Psychological interventions are conducive to people's wellbeing 是事实陈述，原文 improving mental health 也支持这一点，但题干问的是「WHO 为何要诉诸经济收益」，与「干预有益健康」无关。
C) 偷换概念原文强调「政府以经济为先」是导致 WHO 必须用经济话术的「原因」；选项 C 反过来说「心理健康差对经济不利」是 WHO 用经济话术的原因——把工具变成了动机来源。
D) ✓ 正确「政府只听经济话术」的根本原因即「政府仍以经济为先」，与 Governments still take economic development as their priority 同义。
  • As long as the economy is their priority↔Governments still take economic development as their priority
  • It says a lot, however, that the WHO feels the need to appeal to the economic benefits↔Governments still take economic development as their priority
P6

It says a lot, however, that the WHO feels the need to appeal to the economic benefits of improving mental health to persuade governments that the cost of taking proposed measures is justified. As long as the economy is their priority, governments need go no further than ensuring citizens' continued productivity.

然而，WHO 不得不诉诸「改善心理健康带来的经济收益」来说服政府这一举措的成本合理，这一点本身就说明了问题：只要政府仍把经济置于首位，他们就只关心公民能否继续创造产值。

55

What message does the author try to convey at the end of the passage?

作者在文章结尾试图传达什么信息?

  • A)Governments' goal should be prosperity-driven.政府的目标应当以繁荣为导向。
  • B)Governments' goal should be people-oriented.政府的目标应当以人为本。
  • C)Governments should consider citizens' views in decision-making.政府在决策时应当考虑公民的观点。
  • D)Governments should set sustained productivity as their top priority.政府应当将持续生产力设为首要任务。
解析
B（主旨题）
A) 与原文相反全文反对把繁荣/产值当目标（should not be the primary goal / ensuring citizens' continued productivity 被作者批评），Governments' goal should be prosperity-driven 正好是作者反对的立场。
B) ✓ 正确末段「把公民视为目的」是全文落点，与 Governments' goal should be people-oriented 同义。
  • view citizens as ends in themselves↔Governments' goal should be people-oriented
  • set the 'happiness' of citizens as their highest priority↔Governments' goal should be people-oriented
C) 无中生有原文从未谈及「决策时听取公民意见」（views in decision-making）；这是把「以人为本」误解为「问政于民」，添加了原文不存在的程序性内容。
D) 与原文相反作者在 governments need go no further than ensuring citizens' continued productivity 中明显嘲讽了「以产值为先」的政府逻辑；sustained productivity as their top priority 正是作者批评的对象。
