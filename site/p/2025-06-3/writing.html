范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 166 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

With the increasing application of AI technology, there is a growing concern that it may negatively impact human creativity. In my view, this worry is well-grounded, for excessive reliance on AI can gradually dull our capacity to think and create independently.

The reason is straightforward. Creativity is a muscle that strengthens only through active practice. When we ask AI to generate essays, ideas, or solutions at the click of a button, we skip the very struggle that sparks original thinking. Take my own experience as an example. Last semester, I turned to an AI tool whenever an assignment felt difficult. Although the drafts came quickly, I soon realized that my mind had grown lazy: I could no longer brainstorm freely, and my writing lost its personal voice.

Therefore, the concern deserves our serious attention. AI should serve as a helpful assistant rather than a replacement for our own minds. Only by keeping our thinking active can we ensure that technology enriches, rather than erodes, human creativity.
