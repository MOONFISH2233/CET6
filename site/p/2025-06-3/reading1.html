原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Simulators are most often utilized within industries such as nuclear power, aviation and surgery where failure results in disastrous consequences. To maximize the value from a simulation learning experience, participants should immediately and directly apply their learning to a specific intervention within their organization.

模拟器最常用于核电、航空和外科手术等行业，因为在这些行业中，失败会导致灾难性后果。为了最大限度地发挥模拟学习体验的价值，参与者应立即将所学内容直接应用于其组织中的一项具体干预措施。

P2

Most organizations aspire to deploy significant change programs, only to find them nearly impossible to implement. That is largely because successful change requires more than a vision, it requires a workforce that not only doesn't resist change, but embraces it.

大多数组织都希望实施重大的变革计划，却发现这些计划几乎无法落实。这主要是因为成功的变革需要的不仅仅是愿景，还需要一支不仅不抵制变革、反而拥抱变革的员工队伍。

P3

To achieve success, an organization must build a transformation program that will allow change to be rapidly pulled across its departments and throughout its layers. Regardless of the level of senior management commitment, unless key thought leaders at all levels embrace the change, the initiative will wither and die. To create this kind of widespread passion, learning leaders must expose the workforce to what could be, which will enable them to rethink their mental models, enable them to break free from their deep-rooted paradigms and embrace the opportunity to learn.

要取得成功，组织必须建立一项转型计划，使变革能够迅速在各部门之间及组织的各个层级中推进。无论高级管理层的支持程度如何，除非各个层级的关键思想领袖都接受变革，否则这一倡议终将衰败并消亡。为了激发这种广泛的热情，学习负责人必须让员工接触可能实现的情形，从而使他们能够重新思考自己的思维模式，摆脱根深蒂固的范式，并拥抱学习的机会。

P4

Allowing participants to enter a simulated environment provides them with the opportunity to experience alternative realities which can prompt them to rethink their current beliefs.

允许参与者进入模拟环境，可以为他们提供体验其他现实的机会，从而促使他们重新思考当前的信念。

P5

Behavioral change is not easy for most adults. Lectures, training programs and workshops can explain the intellectual elements of transformation, but they are seldom effective at getting to the behavioral aspects that lie at the heart of a significant change initiative. Further, under normal working conditions, managers rarely see the full effect of their employee development efforts. As such, an intervention like a simulation can provide the stimulus for change.

对大多数成年人来说，行为改变并不容易。讲座、培训项目和研讨会可以解释转型的理性要素，但它们很少能有效触及重大变革倡议核心的行为层面。此外，在正常的工作条件下，管理者很少能看到员工发展努力所产生的全部效果。因此，像模拟这样的干预措施可以为变革提供推动力。

P6

An effective simulation can be better than experience as a learning tool because it accelerates time, compresses space, and unlike reality, is specifically designed to maximize participant learning. Simulations provide an immersive learning experience where skills, processes and knowledge all can be highlighted in a way reality cannot. The ability to explore, experiment and repeatedly apply new knowledge in unlimited, risk-free models is what makes simulation one of the most productive forms of learning.

作为一种学习工具，有效的模拟可能比实际经验更好，因为它能够加速时间、压缩空间，而且不同于现实，它是专门设计来最大限度地促进参与者学习的。模拟提供了一种沉浸式学习体验，使技能、流程和知识都能够以现实无法实现的方式得到突出呈现。在无限的、无风险的模型中探索、试验并反复应用新知识的能力，正是模拟成为最具成效的学习形式之一的原因。

P7

Well-designed simulations can enable individuals and groups to develop a deep level of understanding about how their decisions and intuitive responses to business stimuli affect their fellow participants and the organization as a whole. To reap the benefits, however, simulations must feel like reality. At the end of the successful simulation, participants must declare "this is us." If they don't, they will view the experience as a game, which can be difficult to apply on the job, or worse, irrelevant to everyday work tasks. To maximize benefits from simulation, participants should immediately apply the learning from the experience to forge a smooth link between learning and doing.

设计良好的模拟能够使个人和群体深入理解：他们针对业务刺激所作出的决策和直觉反应，如何影响其他参与者以及整个组织。然而，要获得这些益处，模拟必须让人感觉像现实一样。成功的模拟结束时，参与者必须说：“这就是我们。”如果做不到这一点，他们就会把这种体验视为游戏，而这可能很难应用到工作中，或者更糟糕的是，认为它与日常工作任务无关。为了最大限度地发挥模拟的益处，参与者应立即将体验中学到的内容付诸实践，在学习与行动之间建立顺畅的联系。

解析逐题详解

46

What do we learn about successful changes in organizations?

关于组织中的成功变革,我们了解到什么?

  • A)They can be immediately implemented with great ease.它们可以非常容易地立即实施。
  • B)They are usually led by organization leaders of vision.它们通常由有远见的组织领导者引领。
  • C)They call for enthusiastic support from the workforce.它们需要员工队伍的热情支持。
  • D)They often result from simulation learning experiences.它们往往源于模拟学习体验。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反They can be immediately implemented with great ease. 称变革「轻易立刻落地」，但 Most organizations aspire to deploy significant change programs, only to find them nearly impossible to implement 明说大多数变革极难落地，两者完全相反。
B) 偷换概念They are usually led by organization leaders of vision. 把成功变革的关键归到「有远见的领导」上，但定位句 successful change requires more than a vision 恰恰说仅有 vision 不够，关键在 workforce；把强调点偷换了。
C) ✓ 正确They call for enthusiastic support from the workforce. 的「require + 全员热情支持」与定位句 requires a workforce that ... embraces it 精准对位，且对位完整、无外加成分。
  • it requires a workforce that not only doesn't resist change, but embraces it↔They call for enthusiastic support from the workforce.
  • a workforce↔the workforce
D) 强加因果They often result from simulation learning experiences. 说成功变革「源于」模拟学习，但全文逻辑是 simulation 服务于 change，并非 change 的来源；定位句也未涉及 simulation。
P2

That is largely because successful change requires more than a vision, it requires a workforce that not only doesn't resist change, but embraces it.

这在很大程度上是因为成功的变革不只需要愿景，它还需要一支不但不抵触变革、还会拥抱变革的员工队伍。

47

What should learning leaders do to arouse learners' passion for change?

学习领导者应该做什么来激发学习者对变革的热情?

  • A)Allow them to see what could possibly be achieved.让他们看到可能实现的目标。
  • B)Help them break free from their old paradigms.帮助他们摆脱旧有的范式。
  • C)Encourage them to rethink their thought models.鼓励他们重新思考自己的思维模式。
  • D)Stimulate them to embrace fresh opportunities.激励他们拥抱新的机会。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判落在主句动作 expose…to what could be 上，A 用 allow them to see what could possibly be achieved 与之一一对应。
  • expose the workforce to what could be↔Allow them to see what could possibly be achieved.
  • the workforce↔them
B) 答非所问Help them break free from their old paradigms. 取自定位句后面的 which 从句中的并列动作 enable them to break free from their deep-rooted paradigms，那是 expose 之后的结果，而非 learning leaders 直接「应做」的动作。
C) 答非所问Encourage them to rethink their thought models. 取自 which 从句中的 rethink their mental models，依然属于 expose 之后的结果，不是题干所问的直接动作。
D) 答非所问Stimulate them to embrace fresh opportunities. 改写自 embrace the opportunity to learn，仍是 which 结果从句尾部的并列结果之一，不是题干问的 learning leaders 的直接动作。
P3

To create this kind of widespread passion, learning leaders must expose the workforce to what could be, which will enable them to rethink their mental models, enable them to break free from their deep-rooted paradigms and embrace the opportunity to learn.

为了激发这种广泛的热情，learning leaders 必须把员工带去看「事情本可以是什么样」，这将使他们重新审视心智模型、挣脱根深蒂固的旧范式，并拥抱学习机会。

48

What does the passage say about lectures, training programs and workshops?

文章关于讲座、培训项目和研讨会说了什么?

  • A)They are generally incapable of changing workers' behaviors on the job.它们通常无法改变员工在工作中的行为。
  • B)They are interventions different from simulations in creating stimuli for change.它们是不同于模拟的干预措施,用于创造变革的刺激。
  • C)They aim at transforming the behaviors of the workers in an organization.它们旨在改变组织中员工的行为。
  • D)They help managers achieve the full effect of employee development efforts.它们帮助管理者充分实现员工发展工作的效果。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判「改不了行为」直接对应 generally incapable of changing workers' behaviors，两端的否定强度通过 seldom↔generally not 桥接成立。
  • they are seldom effective at getting to the behavioral aspects↔They are generally incapable of changing workers' behaviors on the job.
  • the behavioral aspects that lie at the heart of a significant change initiative↔workers' behaviors on the job
B) 无中生有They are interventions different from simulations in creating stimuli for change. 把三者说成「在制造变革刺激上不同于 simulation 的干预」，原文只把它们与 simulation 在「能不能触及行为」上作对照，未把它们也定性为 intervention，更未谈它们「创造刺激」。
C) 与原文相反They aim at transforming the behaviors of the workers in an organization. 称它们「以改变行为为目标」，与原文「在行为层面 seldom effective」的判定方向相反——原文恰是说它们做不到这件事。
D) 张冠李戴They help managers achieve the full effect of employee development efforts. 让三者「帮助管理者获得员工发展投入的全部成效」，但原文 managers rarely see the full effect of their employee development efforts 是说在常规工作里 managers 几乎看不到全部成效，且这一判断是关于「常规工作条件」整体，不是三者帮助下的状况。
P5

Lectures, training programs and workshops can explain the intellectual elements of transformation, but they are seldom effective at getting to the behavioral aspects that lie at the heart of a significant change initiative.

讲座、培训项目和工作坊能讲清变革的认知层面，但它们在触及行为层面（而行为层面恰是重大变革的核心）这件事上几乎无效。

49

What makes simulation one of the most fruitful forms of learning?

是什么使模拟成为最富有成效的学习形式之一?

  • A)Its capability of saving time by accelerating the immersive learning experience.它能够通过加速沉浸式学习体验来节省时间。
  • B)Its potential for learners to examine their skills, knowledge and learning process.它让学习者有可能审视自己的技能、知识和学习过程。
  • C)Its capability of providing all participants with a practical learning experience.它能够为所有参与者提供实践性的学习体验。
  • D)Its potential for learners to explore, experiment and practice without any risk.它让学习者有可能在没有任何风险的情况下探索、试验和实践。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 偷换概念Its capability of saving time by accelerating the immersive learning experience. 把 accelerates time 拎出来当主因，但原文里它只是 simulation 优点的描述之一，不是 is what makes 强调结构所归的原因。
B) 偷换概念Its potential for learners to examine their skills, knowledge and learning process. 取自 skills, processes and knowledge all can be highlighted，但原文是 processes（业务流程/作业过程），选项改成了 learning process（学习过程），主体偷换；且原文是 simulation 来 highlight，不是 learners 来 examine。
C) 以偏概全Its capability of providing all participants with a practical learning experience. 用 practical learning experience 模糊概括 simulation，未含 risk-free、reflect、experiment 等核心元素；原文 is what makes 主语处也并未用 practical 来归因。
D) ✓ 正确D 保留了原文的三动词并列与 risk-free 条件，是唯一同时满足两项核心的选项。
  • The ability to explore, experiment and repeatedly apply new knowledge↔Its potential for learners to explore, experiment and practice without any risk.
  • in unlimited, risk-free models↔without any risk
  • The ability↔Its potential
P6

The ability to explore, experiment and repeatedly apply new knowledge in unlimited, risk-free models is what makes simulation one of the most productive forms of learning.

在数量无限、零风险的模型里探索、实验并反复运用新知的能力，正是让 simulation 成为最高效学习方式之一的原因。

50

What should participants do in a simulation to reap the greatest benefits possible?

参与者在模拟中应该做什么才能获得最大可能的收益?

  • A)Take the experience as a mere game.将这种体验仅仅当作游戏。
  • B)Apply promptly what they learn to their jobs.将所学内容迅速应用到工作中。
  • C)Develop a deep level of understanding.培养深层次的理解。
  • D)Strive to connect closely with their leaders.努力与领导者建立紧密联系。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反Take the experience as a mere game. 让参与者「把体验当作单纯的游戏」，但 If they don't, they will view the experience as a game, which can be difficult to apply on the job 明确把「视为游戏」当作负面后果。
B) ✓ 正确B 在副词（promptly ↔ immediately）、核心动作（apply what they learn ↔ apply the learning）与落点（to their jobs ↔ between learning and doing）三处与定位句完全对位。
  • participants should immediately apply the learning from the experience↔Apply promptly what they learn to their jobs.
  • to forge a smooth link between learning and doing↔to their jobs
C) 答非所问Develop a deep level of understanding. 取自 enable individuals and groups to develop a deep level of understanding，那是 simulation 的功效（果），不是参与者「应当做什么以最大化收益」（因）。
D) 无中生有Strive to connect closely with their leaders. 让参与者「努力与领导紧密联系」，但全文从未要求 participants 去与 leaders 建立紧密联系，原文 forge a link 的对象是 learning 与 doing，不是 participants 与 leaders。
P7

To maximize benefits from simulation, participants should immediately apply the learning from the experience to forge a smooth link between learning and doing.

为最大化从模拟中获得的收益，参与者应立刻把从体验中所学应用到工作中，以打通「学」与「做」之间的桥梁。
