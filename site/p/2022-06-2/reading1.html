原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Since American idol star Taryn Southern started composing music with AI in 2017, musicians all over the world have begun wondering about the implications of AI and modern technology where music production is concerned. Using AI in the creation of music is perceived by some as a helpful tool and by others as almost "the beginning of the end".

自2017年美国偶像明星Taryn Southern开始使用人工智能创作音乐以来，世界各地的音乐家都开始思考人工智能和现代技术对音乐制作的影响。有人认为，利用人工智能创作音乐是一种有用的工具；而另一些人则认为，这几乎是“末日的开始”。

P2

In Taryn's case, AI software enabled her to communicate melodies and chords that she didn't know how to put together herself. The end product was therefore a collaborative effort, rather than a piece entirely produced by technology. Taryn's story has a distinctly positive feel that highlights the advantages of using AI in music production. It can serve as a source of inspiration, and as an ideal jumping-off point should a musician be hit with writer's block(文思枯竭).

就Taryn而言，人工智能软件使她能够表达出旋律和和弦，而她自己并不知道如何将它们组合起来。因此，最终作品是合作完成的成果，而不是完全由技术制作的作品。Taryn的故事明显带有积极色彩，突出了在音乐制作中使用人工智能的优势。它可以成为灵感的来源，也可以在音乐家陷入文思枯竭时，作为一个理想的起点。

P3

Contrary to seeing AI as a tool, some musicians consider it to be hugely detrimental to the music scene. At the moment, because such technology is still so young, the music it's producing is not necessarily what we want to hear. In short, it's not of great quality. Those who have produced their own music, or even fans of authentic, artistic music, will also argue that a computer could never emulate the work (and human touch) of a true musician.

与将人工智能视为工具的看法相反，一些音乐家认为它会对音乐界造成极大的损害。目前，由于这项技术还很年轻，它创作出的音乐未必是我们想听的。简而言之，其质量并不高。那些创作过自己的音乐的人，甚至是真正喜爱艺术性音乐的乐迷，也会认为计算机永远无法模仿真正音乐家的作品以及其中的人性触感。

P4

Music has been an integral part of the story of humans for ages; in fact, the first known piece of music is believed to be around 3,400 years old. Songs have long been used as a means of communicating messages and folk stories, covering everything from societal ethics to world history. Since many people see music as such an inherently human expression, it is often considered as too precious to impart to technology. The thought of a computer generating a "random" piece of music that hasn't been painstakingly created by an artist is almost seen as sacrilegious(亵渎神圣的).

数千年来，音乐一直是人类发展历程中不可或缺的一部分；事实上，已知最早的音乐作品据认为已有约3400年的历史。长期以来，歌曲一直被用来传达信息和民间故事，内容涵盖社会伦理到世界历史的方方面面。由于许多人认为音乐是一种与生俱来的人类表达方式，因此常常觉得它过于珍贵，不应交给技术。人们几乎认为，让计算机生成一段未经艺术家精心创作的“随机”音乐，是一种亵渎神圣的行为。

P5

Regardless of which side of the argument you fall on, it seems likely that the use of AI in music production will only become more frequent. Our modern world is preoccupied with technological advancements. Instead of shying away from the idea of this bleak future, the best approach to take is one of optimism and curiosity. While there are always bound to be stubborn old-school musicians who refuse to use tech, music producers should consider AI as something to be embraced. AI music software is still very much in its infancy, but with more investors interested in the development and outcomes of such technology, and considering the rapid growth rate of other tech advances in recent years, it's only a matter of time before AI-produced music is seen as the new norm.

无论你支持哪一方的观点，人工智能在音乐制作中的使用似乎都只会越来越频繁。我们这个现代世界十分关注技术进步。与其回避这个黯淡未来的想法，不如采取乐观且充满好奇心的态度。尽管总会有一些顽固的老派音乐家拒绝使用科技，但音乐制作人应当考虑接受人工智能。人工智能音乐软件仍处于非常初级的阶段，但随着越来越多的投资者对这项技术的发展及其成果感兴趣，再考虑到近年来其他技术进步的快速发展速度，人工智能创作的音乐被视为新常态只是时间问题。

46

How do some musicians perceive using AI in creating music?

一些音乐人如何看待在音乐创作中使用人工智能?

  • A)It would help to produce more music idols.这将有助于产生更多音乐偶像。
  • B)It would be detrimental to music production.这将不利于音乐制作。
  • C)It would hinder the understanding of authentic music.这将妨碍对真正音乐的理解。
  • D)It would be the beginning of a new era in music creation.这将是音乐创作新时代的开端。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 无中生有原文首段提到 American idol star Taryn Southern 是引子人名，与音乐人对 AI 的看法无关；produce more music idols 拿这一无关词造句，属无中生有。
B) ✓ 正确预判得到"对音乐创作有害"，It would be detrimental to music production 是其逐词同义改写，最直接对应，故选 B。
  • hugely detrimental to the music scene↔detrimental to music production
C) 偷换概念反对派担心的是 AI not necessarily what we want to hear"产出质量"和never emulate the work"复刻原创"，从未说过 AI 会妨碍"听众理解"真正的音乐；hinder the understanding of authentic music 把动作对象从"产出"偷换为"理解"。
D) 与原文相反the beginning of a new era"新时代的开端"是正向积极评价，而原文反对派恰恰说的是 the beginning of the end"终结的开端"——选项把 end 偷换成 new era，方向完全相反。
P3

Contrary to seeing AI as a tool, some musicians consider it to be hugely detrimental to the music scene.

与把 AI 视作工具的看法相反，一些音乐人认为它对音乐界极为有害。

47

What does Taryn Southern's story illustrate?

Taryn Southern的故事说明了什么?

  • A)AI technology is conducive to music composition.人工智能技术有利于音乐创作。
  • B)Musicians will be unable to create music without high tech.音乐人如果没有高科技将无法创作音乐。
  • C)Musicians are often at their wits' end in their creative effort.音乐人在创作过程中常常束手无策。
  • D)AI technology is indispensable to creating melodies and chords.人工智能技术对于创作旋律和和弦是必不可少的。
解析
A（例证目的题）
A) ✓ 正确预判得到"AI 有助于音乐创作"，AI technology is conducive to music composition 精确对应，故选 A。
  • highlights the advantages of using AI in music production↔conducive to music composition
B) 过度推断原文只说 AI 是"协作工具""灵感来源"，从未说"没有高科技就无法创作"；unable to create music without high tech 是把"有帮助"夸大成"不可或缺"。
C) 以偏概全原文只说"如果"音乐人遇到 writer's block，AI 是一个 jumping-off point；选项 often at their wits' end"经常技穷"把假设条件偷换为普遍现象，且偏离例证目的。
D) 过度推断原文称 AI 是 source of inspiration 与 jumping-off point——是"加分项"，而 indispensable to creating melodies and chords"不可或缺"把作用拔高到"必需"，与原文协作性定位不符。
P2

Taryn's story has a distinctly positive feel that highlights the advantages of using AI in music production. It can serve as a source of inspiration, and as an ideal jumping-off point should a musician be hit with writer's block.

Taryn 的故事带有明显的积极意味，凸显出在音乐创作中使用 AI 的种种好处。它可以作为灵感的源泉，并且在音乐人遭遇文思枯竭时，是一个理想的起点。

48

Why are some musicians opposed to the use of AI in creating music?

为什么一些音乐人反对在音乐创作中使用人工智能?

  • A)Music produced with AI technology lacks humanness.用人工智能技术制作的音乐缺乏人性。
  • B)Music created with AI technology is easily emulated.用人工智能技术创作的音乐很容易被模仿。
  • C)It will depreciate humans' role in music composition.这将贬低人类在音乐创作中的作用。
  • D)It will deplete young musicians, creative inspiration.这将耗尽年轻音乐人的创作灵感。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判"缺人性"与 Music produced with AI technology lacks humanness 完全等价，故选 A。
  • a computer could never emulate the work (and human touch) of a true musician↔lacks humanness
B) 与原文相反原文说"电脑无法 emulate 真正音乐人"——是 AI 模仿不来人；选项 easily emulated"AI 音乐容易被模仿"把模仿的主客关系颠倒，且 easily 与原文 never 直接相反。
C) 答非所问depreciate humans' role"贬低人类在音乐创作中的角色"虽然在情感上和反对派接近，但原文给出的反对理由是"复刻不出人性"而非"会贬低人类角色"——本题问的是 Why 反对，必须是原文实际给出的理由。
D) 无中生有原文从未提到 AI 会"耗尽年轻音乐人的创作灵感"，deplete young musicians, creative inspiration 是凭空造话。注意 P2 提到 AI 是"灵感来源"——正好相反。
P3

Those who have produced their own music, or even fans of authentic, artistic music, will also argue that a computer could never emulate the work (and human touch) of a true musician.

那些自己创作过音乐的人，甚至是真正的艺术音乐爱好者也会争辩说，电脑永远无法复刻一位真正音乐人的作品（以及那种人性温度）。

49

Why do many people think music is too precious to impart to AI technology?

为什么许多人认为音乐太宝贵而不能传授给人工智能技术?

  • A)It cannot be created without pains.音乐不能不经过辛苦就创作出来。
  • B)It cannot be produced at random.音乐不能随意产生。
  • C)It is part of human life.音乐是人类生活的一部分。
  • D)It is human specific.音乐是人类特有的。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 答非所问原文用 painstakingly"煞费苦心"形容真正音乐人的创作，强调过程不易；cannot be created without pains"没有痛苦就无法创作"虽与文本有触点，但本题问的是"为何太珍贵"，答案应是"音乐属于人类"，而不是"创作过程辛苦"。
B) 偷换概念原文说"电脑生成 random 的音乐被视为亵渎"，并非"音乐不能随机产生"；cannot be produced at random 把原文的 random（贬义形容词修饰AI产品）偷换为"产生方式"的逻辑命题，且并非太珍贵的理由。
C) 以偏概全part of human life"是人类生活的一部分"虽然符合原文"陪伴人类多年"的事实（integral part of the story of humans for ages），但本题问的是"为何太珍贵"，关键论点是"人类专属"而非"陪伴时间长"。Part of human life 比 human specific 弱：很多东西都是"人类生活的一部分"（如食物、衣物），未必只属人类——这恰恰落不到"专属"的关键力度。
D) ✓ 正确预判"音乐是人类专属"与 It is human specific 完全等价，故选 D。
  • an inherently human expression↔human specific
P4

Since many people see music as such an inherently human expression, it is often considered as too precious to impart to technology.

因为许多人把音乐视为人类固有的一种表达方式，所以它常被认为太珍贵了，不应交付给技术。

50

What does the author think of the future of AI music?

作者如何看待人工智能音乐的未来?

  • A)It will continue to arouse the interest of music investors.它将继续引起音乐投资者的兴趣。
  • B)It has the prospect of becoming the norm in the future.它有望在未来成为常态。
  • C)It will be gradually accepted by old-school musicians.它将逐渐被传统派音乐人接受。
  • D)It may eventually lose its freshness and appeal.它最终可能会失去新鲜感和吸引力。
解析
B（态度题）
A) 以偏概全continue to arouse the interest of music investors 投资者的兴趣，在原文 with more investors interested in the development 中只是作者用来支持"AI 音乐必成常态"的论据，本身不是作者对"未来"的核心判断；本题问 What does the author think of the future，答案要选最终判断而不是中间论据。
B) ✓ 正确预判"AI 音乐终将成为常态"与 It has the prospect of becoming the norm in the future 同义，故选 B。
  • it's only a matter of time before AI-produced music is seen as the new norm↔has the prospect of becoming the norm in the future
C) 与原文相反原文 there are always bound to be stubborn old-school musicians who refuse to use tech 明确指出守旧派始终会拒绝技术；gradually accepted by old-school musicians 反过来说他们会"逐渐接受"，与原文 refuse 直接相反。
D) 无中生有may eventually lose its freshness and appeal"最终失去新鲜感和吸引力"——原文从未提到 AI 音乐会失去吸引力，反而判断它会成为新常态。属凭空构造。
