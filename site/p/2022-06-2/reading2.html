原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

A few weeks ago, a well-meaning professor tried to explain the physiological process behind viruses and the human body in a tweet and was immediately criticized for a mistake in his information. He then issued an apology and deleted his erroneous tweet.

几周前，一位善意的教授试图在一条推文中解释病毒与人体背后的生理过程，却因信息中的一处错误立即受到批评。随后，他发表道歉声明，并删除了那条错误的推文。

P2

Communicating science beyond the academic bubble is necessary to augmenting public understanding of health and environmental issues and helping individuals make well-informed personal decisions.

将科学传播到学术圈之外，对于增进公众对健康和环境问题的理解，以及帮助个人作出充分知情的决定，都是必要的。

P3

However, scientists who engage in science communication must acknowledge that even in their area, their expertise is deep but narrow. They need to recognize the constraints in their own knowledge. That is not to suggest that they only write or present on their own research, but rather, that they consult with an expert if the topic is outside of their discipline. Fact-checking with a scientist who works in the specialty will prevent the unintentional spread of misinformation, and the process of doing so may yield tiny pieces of interesting new information that can be incorporated.

然而，参与科学传播的科学家必须承认，即使在自己的领域内，他们的专业知识也是深而窄的。他们需要认识到自身知识的局限。这并不是说他们只能围绕自己的研究进行写作或演讲，而是说，如果主题超出自己的学科范围，就应当向相关专家咨询。请该专业领域的科学家进行事实核查，可以防止无意中传播错误信息，而这一过程也可能产生一些有趣的新信息片段，并将其纳入传播内容。

P4

Some have argued that the public is not educated enough to understand scientific information, especially for any complex phenomena, but this is absurd. Science instruction can be found at all levels of public education with most secondary schools offering classes on biology, physics, and chemistry. If anything, social media has shown that the public craves knowledge based on a solid scientific foundation. Even the public discourse that follows most scientific articles shows that online readers can understand even the most baffling of scientific principles.

有人认为，公众没有受过足够的教育，无法理解科学信息，尤其是那些复杂的科学现象，但这种说法十分荒谬。公共教育的各个阶段都设有科学教学，大多数中学还开设生物学、物理学和化学课程。恰恰相反，社交媒体表明，公众渴望获得建立在坚实科学基础上的知识。大多数科学文章发表后引发的公众讨论甚至表明，网络读者能够理解最令人困惑的科学原理。

P5

It is equally imperative to emphasize that being an expert on a topic does not automatically make a scholar qualified to communicate it to a nonscientific audience. A number of scientists recently have been offering public-aimed explanations of scientific phenomena. Even though they have appropriate credentials, they often do very little in the way of explaining. One biologist shared an intricate analogy involving a library, books, paper, a recipe, ingredients, and a cake to explain the process behind vaccines. Any explanation that requires a written key to keep track of what each item represents is not a clear example for public consumption.

同样必须强调的是，成为某一主题的专家，并不会自动使学者具备向非科学受众进行讲解的资格。最近，不少科学家一直在面向公众解释科学现象。尽管他们拥有相应的资历，但在解释方面往往做得很少。一位生物学家曾用一个涉及图书馆、书籍、纸张、食谱、配料和蛋糕的复杂类比，来解释疫苗背后的原理。任何需要借助一份书面说明来记住每件物品分别代表什么的解释，都不是一个适合公众理解的清晰范例。

P6

Science communication is a science in and of itself. It requires rigorous training and instruction. A scientist should take communication courses that can teach a person how to identify and eliminate jargon and how to develop effective analogies to explain complex concepts. One cannot assume communication expertise—imagine if someone just decided that they were a physicist and started trying to contribute to the field without the necessary background. Doing a poor job communicating science to the public will only create confusion and widen the gap between science and society, a gap that scientists are trying to close.

科学传播本身就是一门科学。它需要严格的培训和指导。科学家应当参加传播课程，学习如何识别并消除术语，以及如何构建有效的类比来解释复杂概念。不能想当然地认为自己具备传播方面的专业能力——试想，如果有人只是决定自己是一名物理学家，然后在没有必要背景知识的情况下开始试图为这一领域作出贡献，会是怎样的情形。向公众进行糟糕的科学传播只会造成混乱，扩大科学与社会之间的鸿沟，而科学家正努力弥合这一鸿沟。

解析逐题详解

51

What does the author say about communicating science to the general public?

作者对向公众传播科学有何看法?

  • A)It will help them to keep abreast of the latest scientific developments.这将帮助他们跟上最新的科学发展。
  • B)It is a necessary means to improve their understanding of scientific issues.这是提高他们对科学问题理解的必要手段。
  • C)It will get them more involved in academic debates on environmental problems.这将使他们更多地参与关于环境问题的学术辩论。
  • D)It is an effective way to augment scientists' influence beyond the academic circle.这是增强科学家在学术圈之外影响力的有效方式。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 无中生有keep abreast of the latest scientific developments 把「最新科学进展」塞进选项，而 P2 只说提升理解、辅助决策，从未提及 latest developments。
B) ✓ 正确a necessary means to improve their understanding of scientific issues 同时命中预判的「必要性」与「提升理解」两条语义槽，无多余成分，是定位句的标准同义改写。
  • necessary to augmenting public understanding of health and environmental issues↔a necessary means to improve their understanding of scientific issues
C) 偷换概念get them more involved in academic debates on environmental problems 把原文「帮助个人做明智决策」偷换为「让公众参与学术辩论」，方向完全相反——原文恰恰是要把科学带到学术圈外，而非把公众拉进学术辩论。
D) 张冠李戴augment scientists' influence beyond the academic circle 把「augment 的对象」从原文的 public understanding 偷换为 scientists' influence——augment 这个词留下了，受益人却换了，是典型「同词不同对象」陷阱。
P2

Communicating science beyond the academic bubble is necessary to augmenting public understanding of health and environmental issues and helping individuals make well-informed personal decisions.

把科学传播到学术圈外，是增进公众对健康与环境议题理解、帮助个人做出明智决策的必要途径。

52

What does the author advise scientists to do to deal with topics outside of their specialty?

作者建议科学家如何处理其专业领域之外的话题?

  • A)Write or present on them from new angles.从新的角度撰写或阐述这些话题。
  • B)Utilize information from diverse sources.利用来自不同来源的信息。
  • C)Turn to a specialist for professional help.向专业人士寻求专业帮助。
  • D)Fact-check with colleagues in their field.与其领域的同事进行事实核查。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 无中生有Write or present on them from new angles 凭空加入 new angles 这一限定，原文 they only write or present on their own research 是作者要排除的误读，且无任何「新角度」之意。
B) 偷换概念Utilize information from diverse sources 把作者明确指出的「咨询专家 + 事实核查」泛化为「使用多元来源信息」，方向被悄悄稀释——作者强调的是「找对人」，不是「找够多源头」。
C) ✓ 正确Turn to a specialist for professional help 把预判的「请教 + 该领域专家」两要素逐一对上，且 specialist 与 specialty 同根，符合作者「领域内行家」的限定。
  • consult with an expert if the topic is outside of their discipline↔Turn to a specialist for professional help
D) 张冠李戴Fact-check with colleagues in their field 保留 Fact-check 这一动词（直接借自原文 Fact-checking with a scientist who works in the specialty），但把对象偷换为 colleagues in their field——原文恰恰强调因为话题在 their field 之外，才要找该话题所在领域（specialty）的专家。
P3

That is not to suggest that they only write or present on their own research, but rather, that they consult with an expert if the topic is outside of their discipline.

这并不是说他们只能写或讲自己的研究，而是说，如果话题超出本人学科范围，他们应当请教该领域专家。

53

What does the author say we can learn from social media?

作者说我们能从社交媒体中学到什么?

  • A)A solid academic foundation is essential to understanding baffling scientific principles.扎实的学术基础对于理解令人困惑的科学原理至关重要。
  • B)Modern technology has facilitated communication between scientists and the public.现代技术促进了科学家与公众之间的交流。
  • C)Scientific articles have gained increasing popularity among the general public.科学文章在公众中越来越受欢迎。
  • D)The public's understanding of science is much better than some have claimed.公众对科学的理解比一些人声称的要好得多。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反A solid academic foundation is essential to understanding baffling scientific principles 把原文「公众渴望基于科学的知识」误读为「必须具备学术根基才能理解科学」——原文是「公众主动 craves 知识」，不是「没有 foundation 就不行」。
B) 偷换概念Modern technology has facilitated communication between scientists and the public 把 social media 上位概括为 modern technology，又把「公众理解力」偷换为「双向沟通」，原文从未谈及 facilitated communication。
C) 以偏概全Scientific articles have gained increasing popularity among the general public 把原文用作佐证的 the public discourse that follows most scientific articles 偷换为「科学文章越来越受欢迎」——原文谈的是文章后面的「公众讨论显示理解力」，不是文章本身受欢迎程度。
D) ✓ 正确The public's understanding of science is much better than some have claimed 把作者用 social media 反驳「公众不够聪明」的结论一一对应——选项里 some have claimed 与原文 Some have argued 同源指代，结论同向。
  • online readers can understand even the most baffling of scientific principles↔The public's understanding of science is much better than some have claimed
  • Some have argued that the public is not educated enough to understand scientific information…but this is absurd↔much better than some have claimed
P4

If anything, social media has shown that the public craves knowledge based on a solid scientific foundation. Even the public discourse that follows most scientific articles shows that online readers can understand even the most baffling of scientific principles.

如果说有什么的话，社交媒体反而表明，公众渴望以扎实科学基础为支撑的知识。甚至大多数科学文章后面的公众讨论都显示，在线读者能够理解最难懂的科学原理。

54

What does the example of the biologist who shared an intricate analogy show?

分享了一个复杂类比的生物学家的例子说明了什么?

  • A)It is helpful to use illustrations in explaining scientific phenomena.使用插图来解释科学现象是有帮助的。
  • B)It is imperative to have appropriate titles to explain scientific issues.用适当的标题来解释科学问题是必要的。
  • C)A learned scholar is not necessarily a qualified science communicator.博学的学者不一定是合格的科学传播者。
  • D)A nonscientific audience cannot duly understand principles of science.非科学受众无法充分理解科学原理。
解析
C（例证目的题）
A) 与原文相反It is helpful to use illustrations in explaining scientific phenomena 把「复杂类比」当成正面建议，与作者用此例所要表达的「这种类比反而不清晰」相反——作者明确说 not a clear example for public consumption。
B) 偷换概念It is imperative to have appropriate titles to explain scientific issues 把原文 appropriate credentials 偷换为 appropriate titles，且作者强调的恰恰是「光有 credentials 不够」，本选项反而说「必须有合适头衔」，方向与作者完全相反。
C) ✓ 正确A learned scholar is not necessarily a qualified science communicator 把作者「专家≠科普者」的论点压缩成一句格言式陈述，与论点句一对一。
  • being an expert on a topic does not automatically make a scholar qualified to communicate it to a nonscientific audience↔A learned scholar is not necessarily a qualified science communicator
  • Even though they have appropriate credentials, they often do very little in the way of explaining.↔A learned scholar is not necessarily a qualified science communicator
D) 张冠李戴A nonscientific audience cannot duly understand principles of science 把矛头指向受众理解力不足，与本段乃至 P4 的论调（公众有理解力，问题在科学家不会讲）相反；本题考点是「专家≠科普者」，与受众能力无关。
P5

It is equally imperative to emphasize that being an expert on a topic does not automatically make a scholar qualified to communicate it to a nonscientific audience.

同样必须强调的是，成为某话题的专家并不自动使学者具备向非科学受众传播该话题的资格。

55

What does the author suggest scientists do to close the gap between science and society?

作者建议科学家如何缩小科学与社会之间的差距?

  • A)Explain complex concepts scientifically.科学地解释复杂概念。
  • B)Make appropriate use of scientific terms.恰当地使用科学术语。
  • C)Take courses in public speaking.参加公开演讲课程。
  • D)Develop communication skills.培养沟通技能。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反Explain complex concepts scientifically 把建议对象偷换为「科学地解释复杂概念」——原文要做的恰好相反，是用 effective analogies 把复杂概念变得通俗，而不是「更科学地」讲。
B) 与原文相反Make appropriate use of scientific terms 与作者建议背道而驰——原文明确说 identify and eliminate jargon，选项却让科学家「合理使用术语」，方向反置。
C) 以偏概全Take courses in public speaking 把原文 communication courses 偷换为 public speaking——「沟通课」覆盖书面/口头/类比/受众分析等多个维度，而 public speaking 只是其中一种，且方向更窄。
D) ✓ 正确Develop communication skills 用最高一级的概括把原文末段全部建议吃下，且与段首主张 Science communication is a science in and of itself 完全同向，是稳妥的正确答案。
  • take communication courses that can teach a person how to identify and eliminate jargon and how to develop effective analogies↔Develop communication skills
  • Science communication is a science in and of itself. It requires rigorous training and instruction.↔Develop communication skills
P6

A scientist should take communication courses that can teach a person how to identify and eliminate jargon and how to develop effective analogies to explain complex concepts.

科学家应当修习传播课程，这些课程能教人如何识别并消除行话、如何构造有效的类比以解释复杂概念。
