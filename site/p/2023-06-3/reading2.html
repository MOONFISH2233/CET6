原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Chimpanzees(黑猩猩), human beings' closest animal relatives, share up to 98% of our genes. Yet humans and chimpanzees lead very different lives. Fewer than 300,000 wild chimpanzees live in a few forested corners of Africa today, while humans have colonized every corner of the globe. At more than 7 billion, human population dwarfs that of nearly all other mammals—despite our physical weaknesses.

黑猩猩作为人类最亲近的动物近亲，与人类共享高达98%的基因。然而，人类和黑猩猩过着截然不同的生活。如今，只有不到30万只野生黑猩猩生活在非洲少数覆盖森林的角落，而人类则拓展到了地球的每一个角落。尽管我们在体质上存在弱点，但超过70亿的人口数量使几乎所有其他哺乳动物都相形见绌。

P2

What could account for our species' incredible evolutionary successes?

究竟是什么能够解释我们这一物种令人难以置信的演化成功？

P3

One obvious answer is our big brains. It could be that our raw intelligence gave us an unprecedented ability to think outside the box, innovating solutions to thorny problems as people migrated across the globe.

一个显而易见的答案是我们庞大的大脑。可能是我们的原始智力赋予了我们前所未有的打破常规思考的能力，使人们在向全球迁移的过程中能够创新出解决棘手问题的方案。

P4

But a growing number of cognitive scientists and anthropologists(人类学家) are rejecting that explanation. They think that, rather than making our living as innovators, we survive and thrive precisely because we don't think for ourselves. Instead, people cope with challenging climates and ecological contexts by carefully copying others.

但越来越多的认知科学家和人类学家正在否定这一解释。他们认为，我们之所以能够生存和繁衍，恰恰是因为我们没有独立思考，而不是依靠成为创新者来谋生。相反，人们通过仔细模仿他人来应对具有挑战性的气候和生态环境。

P5

In a famous study, psychologists Victoria Horner and Andrew Whiten showed two groups of test subjects—children and chimpanzees—a mechanical box with a treat inside. In one condition, the box was opaque, while in the other it was transparent. The experimenters demonstrated how to open the box to retrieve the treat, but they also included the irrelevant step of tapping on the box with a stick.

在一项著名的研究中，心理学家维多利亚·霍纳和安德鲁·怀腾向两组受试者——儿童和黑猩猩——展示了一个里面装有奖励的机械盒。在一种情况下，盒子是不透明的，而在另一种情况下，盒子则是透明的。实验人员演示了如何打开盒子取回奖励，但他们也包含了一个无关紧要的步骤：用棍子敲击盒子。

P6

Oddly, the children carefully copied all the steps to open the box, even when they could see that the stick had no practical effect. That is, they copied irrationally: Instead of doing only what was necessary to get their reward, children slavishly imitated every action they'd witnessed.

奇怪的是，孩子们仔细地复制了打开盒子的所有步骤，即使他们能看到棍子没有任何实际作用。也就是说，他们进行了非理性的模仿：孩子们盲目地模仿了他们所目睹的每一个动作，而不是只做获得奖励所必需的事情。

P7

Of course, that study only included three—and four-year-olds. But additional research has shown that older children and adults are even more likely to mindlessly copy others' actions, and infants are less likely to over-imitate—that is, to precisely copy even impractical actions.

当然，该研究仅包含三岁和四岁的儿童。但进一步的研究表明，大龄儿童和成年人更有可能盲目地模仿他人的行为，而婴儿则不太可能过度模仿——即精确复制哪怕是不切实际的动作。

P8

By contrast, chimpanzees in the study only over-imitated in the opaque condition. In the transparent condition—where they saw that the stick was mechanically useless—they ignored that step entirely. Other research has since supported these findings.

相比之下，研究中的黑猩猩仅在不透明的情况下才会过度模仿。在透明的情况下——当它们看到棍子在机械上毫无用处时——它们完全忽略了那个步骤。此后的其他研究也支持了这些发现。

P9

When it comes to copying, chimpanzees are more rational than human children or adults.

在模仿方面，黑猩猩比人类儿童或成年人更理性。

P10

Where does the seemingly irrational human preference for over-imitation come from? Anthropologist Joseph Henrich points out that people around the world rely on technologies that are often so complex that no one can learn them rationally. Instead, people must learn them step by step, trusting in the wisdom of more experienced elders and peers.

人类对过度模仿的这种看似非理性的偏好究竟来自何处？人类学家约瑟夫·亨里奇指出，世界各地的人们所依赖的技术往往非常复杂，以至于没有任何人能够理性地学会它们。相反，人们必须一步一步地学习它们，信任更有经验的长者和平辈的智慧。

P11

So the next time you hear someone arguing passionately that everyone should embrace nonconformity and avoid imitating others, you might laugh a little bit. We're not chimpanzees, after all.

因此，下次当你听到有人慷慨激昂地主张每个人都应该追求特立独行、避免模仿他人时，你可能会微微一笑。毕竟，我们不是黑猩猩。

解析逐题详解

51

What might explain humans' having the largest population of almost all mammals?

什么可能解释人类拥有几乎所有哺乳动物中最大的种群数量?

  • A)They are equipped with raw strength for solving the most challenging problems.他们具备解决最具挑战性问题的原始力量。
  • B)They cope with the outside world more effectively than their animal relatives.他们比动物亲缘物种更有效地应对外部世界。
  • C)They possess the most outstanding ability to think.他们拥有最杰出的思考能力。
  • D)They know how to survive everywhere on earth.他们知道如何在地球上任何地方生存。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 偷换概念选项 equipped with raw strength for solving the most challenging problems 用 raw strength 替换原文 raw intelligence，正话反说——文章首段恰恰强调 despite our physical weaknesses，人类身体反而是弱的。
B) 答非所问They cope with the outside world more effectively than their animal relatives 用 animal relatives 把题点引向 Chimpanzees, human beings' closest animal relatives——黑猩猩，但 51 题问的是 largest population of almost all mammals 的原因，原因句在 P3 而非 P1。
C) ✓ 正确预判已锁定卓越的思考能力，C 选项 possess the most outstanding ability to think 在词面与语义上都与定位句一一对应，是定位句的标准同义替换。
  • our raw intelligence gave us an unprecedented ability to think outside the box↔possess the most outstanding ability to think
  • our big brains↔possess
D) 答非所问They know how to survive everywhere on earth 是 humans have colonized every corner of the globe 的同义改写，但这是现象本身，不是题目所问的解释；题干 What might explain 要的是 原因。
P3

One obvious answer is our big brains. It could be that our raw intelligence gave us an unprecedented ability to think outside the box, innovating solutions to thorny problems as people migrated across the globe.

一个显而易见的答案是我们的大脑。可能是我们的纯粹智力赋予了我们一种前所未有的跳出思维定式的能力，让人们在迁徙到全球各处时能够创新性地解决棘手难题。

52

What accounts for humans' evolutionary successes according to a growing number of cognitive scientists and anthropologists?

根据越来越多认知科学家和人类学家的观点,什么解释了人类进化的成功?

  • A)They are better at innovating solutions.他们更擅长创新解决方案。
  • B)They thrive through creative strategies.他们通过创造性策略繁荣发展。
  • C)They are naturally adaptive to ecological contexts.他们天生适应生态环境。
  • D)They meet challenges by imitating others carefully.他们通过仔细模仿他人来应对挑战。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴They are better at innovating solutions 来自 innovating solutions to thorny problems——但这是 P4 But 转折之前被驳的旧解释，不是 a growing number of 科学家的观点；题干限定了观点出处，必须取转折之后。
B) 与原文相反They thrive through creative strategies 用 creative——这与 we don't think for ourselves 直接相反，文章明确说人类成功不靠自己思考创新。
C) 无中生有They are naturally adaptive to ecological contexts 把 ecological contexts 抄过来制造熟悉感，但 naturally adaptive（与生俱来的适应性）这种说法在原文没有任何出处——文章强调的是后天模仿。
D) ✓ 正确预判已锁定靠仔细模仿来应对挑战，They meet challenges by imitating others carefully 在结构（meet challenges by + 动名词短语）和措辞上与定位句完全同义。
  • by carefully copying others↔imitating others carefully
  • cope with challenging climates and ecological contexts↔meet challenges
P4

They think that, rather than making our living as innovators, we survive and thrive precisely because we don't think for ourselves. Instead, people cope with challenging climates and ecological contexts by carefully copying others.

他们认为：我们之所以存活下来并蓬勃发展，恰恰不是因为我们以创新者的身份谋生，而恰恰是因为我们不自己思考。相反，人类是通过仔细地模仿他人来应对极具挑战的气候与生态环境的。

53

What does the author think is odd about the findings of the study by Victoria Horner and Andrew Whiten?

作者认为Victoria Horner和Andrew Whiten的研究发现有什么奇怪之处?

  • A)Children irrationally imitated every action of the experimenters.儿童非理性地模仿了实验者的每一个动作。
  • B)Chimpanzees could tell the transparent box from the opaque one.黑猩猩能够区分透明盒子和不透明盒子。
  • C)Chimpanzees could retrieve the treat more quickly than children did.黑猩猩取得奖励的速度比儿童更快。
  • D)Children omitted the step of tapping on the box with a stick to open it.儿童省略了用棍子敲击盒子以打开它的步骤。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确A 选项 Children irrationally imitated every action of the experimenters 完整对应定位句的两个关键点：irrationally 对应 slavishly/irrationally，every action of the experimenters 对应 every action they'd witnessed。
  • children slavishly imitated every action they'd witnessed↔Children irrationally imitated every action of the experimenters
  • every action they'd witnessed↔every action of the experimenters
B) 答非所问Chimpanzees could tell the transparent box from the opaque one 描述黑猩猩能区分透明与不透明盒子——这是 P8 黑猩猩对比段的隐含信息，但题干问的是作者认为 odd 的发现，奇怪在儿童这边，不在黑猩猩能不能区分上。
C) 无中生有Chimpanzees could retrieve the treat more quickly than children did 提到黑猩猩比儿童更快取得食物——这种速度比较在文中没有任何描写。
D) 与原文相反Children omitted the step of tapping on the box with a stick to open it 说儿童跳过了敲打这一步——与 children carefully copied all the steps 完全相反，且 P8 显示是黑猩猩在透明条件下才跳过这一步。
P6

Oddly, the children carefully copied all the steps to open the box, even when they could see that the stick had no practical effect. That is, they copied irrationally: Instead of doing only what was necessary to get their reward, children slavishly imitated every action they'd witnessed.

奇怪的是，儿童仔细地模仿了开盒子的每一步动作，哪怕他们能看出那根木棒并没有任何实际作用。也就是说，他们的模仿是非理性的：他们没有只做获得奖励所必需的动作，而是奴隶般地模仿了所看到的每一个动作。

54

What is anthropologist Joseph Henrich's explanation for the human preference for copying?

人类学家Joseph Henrich对人类偏好模仿的解释是什么?

  • A)It originates in the rationality of people around the world.它源于世界各地人们的理性。
  • B)It stems from the way people learn complex technologies.它源于人们学习复杂技术的方式。
  • C)It results from people distrusting their own wisdom.它源于人们不信任自己的智慧。
  • D)It derives from the desire to acquire knowledge step by step.它源于逐步获取知识的愿望。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反It originates in the rationality of people around the world 说模仿源于人们的理性——可文中明确说 no one can learn them rationally，恰恰是 没法理性学 才需要模仿；选项把因果反过来了。
B) ✓ 正确B It stems from the way people learn complex technologies 把 Henrich 的两步因果（技术复杂 → 必须一步步模仿）压缩成 模仿源于学习复杂技术的方式，与预判完全吻合。
  • people must learn them step by step, trusting in the wisdom of more experienced elders and peers↔It stems from the way people learn complex technologies
  • technologies that are often so complex↔complex technologies
  • rely on technologies↔stems from
C) 过度推断It results from people distrusting their own wisdom——文章里只有 trusting in the wisdom of more experienced elders and peers，即信任 前辈与同伴 的智慧，并未说 不信任自己的智慧；distrust their own 是过度推断 + 与原文相反的合成。
D) 偷换概念It derives from the desire to acquire knowledge step by step 把 step by step 抄过来，但说成 是因为人有一步步获取知识的欲望——文章是说 因为技术太复杂、不得不 一步步学习，并不是出于 欲望。手段被替换成动机。
P10

Anthropologist Joseph Henrich points out that people around the world rely on technologies that are often so complex that no one can learn them rationally. Instead, people must learn them step by step, trusting in the wisdom of more experienced elders and peers.

人类学家 Joseph Henrich 指出：世界各地的人都依赖于一些非常复杂的技术，复杂到没有人能够完全理性地学会它们。因此，人们必须一步一步地学习这些技术，并信任那些更有经验的前辈与同伴的智慧。

55

What point does the author want to emphasize when he says "We're not chimpanzees"?

当作者说"我们不是黑猩猩"时,他想强调什么观点?

  • A)It is arguable whether everyone should avoid imitation.每个人是否都应该避免模仿是有争议的。
  • B)It is characteristic of human beings to copy others.模仿他人是人类的特征。
  • C)It is desirable to trust in more knowledgeable peers.信任更有知识的同伴是可取的。
  • D)It is naive to laugh at someone embracing nonconformity.嘲笑拥抱不从众的人是幼稚的。
解析
B（推断题）
A) 偷换概念It is arguable whether everyone should avoid imitation 把作者的话理解为 模仿是否该避免是个值得讨论的问题——但作者并没有在讨论 是否该避免，而是直接调侃那些反模仿者；arguable（有争议的）是无中生有的态度。
B) ✓ 正确B It is characteristic of human beings to copy others 恰好把反语还原为正面陈述：模仿是人类的特征；其余三项要么没抓到反语指向、要么停留在字面。
  • We're not chimpanzees, after all↔It is characteristic of human beings to copy others
  • carefully copying others↔to copy others
C) 以偏概全It is desirable to trust in more knowledgeable peers 来自 trusting in the wisdom of more experienced elders and peers——这只是 Henrich 一段的局部解释，不是末段反语 We're not chimpanzees 想强调的点；末段强调的是 人类天性模仿，不是 信任前辈这件事好不好。
D) 与原文相反It is naive to laugh at someone embracing nonconformity 说嘲笑追求不从众的人是天真的——可文中作者恰恰说 you might laugh a little bit，即可以笑一笑，作者站的是支持嘲笑那一方；naive（天真的）正话反说。
