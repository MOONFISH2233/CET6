原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

If you're someone who has turned to snacking on junk food more in the pandemic(大流行病), you're not alone. Investigative reporter Michael Moss says processed food is engineered to hook you, not unlike alcohol, cigarettes, or other harmful substances. His 2013 book, Salt Sugar Fat, explored food companies' aggressive marketing of those products and their impact on our health. In his new book, Hooked, Moss updates the food giants' efforts to keep us eating what they serve, and how they're responding to complaints from consumers and health advocates.

如果你是在这场大流行病期间转而更多地吃垃圾食品的人，那么你并不孤单。调查记者迈克尔·莫斯说，加工食品经过精心设计，会让你上瘾，这与酒精、香烟或其他有害物质并无二致。他2013年出版的《盐、糖、脂肪》一书探讨了食品公司对这些产品的激进营销及其对我们健康的影响。在他的新书《上瘾》中，莫斯进一步介绍了食品巨头为让我们继续食用它们所提供的食品而采取的措施，以及它们如何回应消费者和健康倡导者的投诉。

P2

Processed food is inexpensive, it's legal, and it's everywhere. Companies' advertising is cueing us to remember those products and we want those products constantly. So the food environment is one of those key things that makes food even more problematic for so many people. Memory, nostalgia(怀旧) in particular, plays a big role in the foods we crave. Soda companies discovered that if they put a soda in the hands of a child when they're at a ball game with their parents, that soda will forever be associated with that joyous moment. Later in life, when that child wants to experience a joyous moment, they're going to think of soda. Many people seek comfort in the snacks they remember from childhood.

加工食品价格低廉、合法，而且无处不在。公司的广告不断给我们提示，让我们记住这些产品，而我们也总是想要这些产品。因此，对许多人来说，食品环境是使食物问题变得更加严重的关键因素之一。记忆，尤其是怀旧，在我们渴望的食物中发挥着重要作用。汽水公司发现，如果他们在孩子和父母一起观看球赛时把一瓶汽水递到孩子手中，那么这瓶汽水将永远与那个欢乐时刻联系在一起。这个孩子长大后，当他想体验欢乐时刻时，就会想到汽水。许多人会从儿时记忆中的零食中寻求慰藉。

P3

Moss examines the way companies capitalize on our memories, cravings and brain chemistry to keep us snacking.

莫斯考察了食品公司如何利用我们的记忆、渴望和大脑化学机制，让我们不停地吃零食。

P4

One of the reasons I came to think that some of these food products are even more powerful, more troublesome than drugs can be is memory. What we eat is all about memory. And we begin forming memories for food at a really early age. And we keep those memories for a lifetime. Knowing this, the food industry spends lots of time trying to shape the memories that we have for their products. One of the features of addiction that scientists studying drug addiction discovered back in the 1990s was that the faster a substance hits the brain, the more apt we are as a result to act impulsively. There's nothing faster than food in its ability to hit the brain. For Moss, this puts the notion of "fast food" in an entirely new light as this isn't limited to fast food chains-almost 90% of food products in grocery stores are processed foods. Everything in the industry is about speed, from manufacturing to packaging.

我之所以开始认为其中一些食品甚至比毒品更有力量、更令人苦恼，原因之一就是记忆。我们吃什么，全都与记忆有关。而且，我们在很小的时候就开始形成关于食物的记忆，并会将这些记忆保留一生。食品行业深知这一点，因此花费大量时间试图塑造我们对其产品的记忆。20世纪90年代，研究毒品成瘾的科学家发现，成瘾的一个特征是，一种物质越快作用于大脑，我们随后就越容易冲动行事。没有什么比食物作用于大脑的速度更快。对莫斯来说，这让“快餐”这一概念有了全新的含义，因为这并不局限于快餐连锁店——杂货店中近90%的食品都是加工食品。从生产到包装，这个行业的一切都与速度有关。

P5

Overall, Moss outlines the industry's dependence on making their products inexpensive, super delicious, and incredibly convenient for consumers. Now that more and more people care about what they put in their bodies and are wanting to eat healthier, these companies are finding it really difficult to meet that new demand because of their own addiction to making these convenience foods.

总的来说，莫斯概述了这个行业对以下做法的依赖：让产品价格低廉、极其美味，并为消费者提供难以置信的便利。如今，越来越多的人开始关注自己摄入的东西，并希望吃得更健康，而由于这些公司自身沉迷于生产这类便利食品，它们发现要满足这种新的需求非常困难。

解析逐题详解

46

In what way does Michael Moss think processed food is comparable to alcohol and cigarettes?

Michael Moss 认为加工食品在哪方面可与酒精和香烟相提并论?

  • A)They are all addictive.它们都会使人上瘾。
  • B)They are all necessary evils.它们都是必要之恶。
  • C)They are all engineered to be enjoyed.它们都经过设计以让人享受。
  • D)They are all in increasingly great demand.它们的需求都在日益增长。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判"勾住人"对应 addictive，A 即为对定位句双重否定+动词短语的整体重述。
  • engineered to hook you↔addictive
  • not unlike alcohol, cigarettes, or other harmful substances↔They are all addictive.
B) 无中生有necessary evils 原文未提及；Moss 全文批评加工食品，没有任何"必要"评价。
C) 偷换概念engineered to be enjoyed 把原文 engineered to hook you 偷换为"为享受而设计"——hook 是"勾住、使上瘾"的强迫语义，与"被享受"的中性愉悦语义不等。
D) 偷换概念increasingly great demand 原文未提及"需求日益增大"；Moss 说的是公司在"积极推销"（aggressive marketing），不是消费者需求暴涨。
P1

Investigative reporter Michael Moss says processed food is engineered to hook you, not unlike alcohol, cigarettes, or other harmful substances.

调查记者 Michael Moss 说，加工食品被精心设计来"勾住"你，这一点与酒精、香烟或其他有害物质并无不同。

47

What does the author say plays a key role in the foods we crave?

作者认为什么在我们渴望的食物中起着关键作用?

  • A)The food environment.食物环境。
  • B)Aggressive marketing.激进的营销。
  • C)Convenience.便利性。
  • D)Memory.记忆。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 答非所问The food environment. 出现在 the food environment is one of those key things that makes food even more problematic，原文说的是"让食物对人更具危害"，不是"在我们渴求的食物中起关键作用"。
B) 张冠李戴Aggressive marketing. 来自首段 food companies' aggressive marketing，是 Moss 上一本书的话题，不在 Q47 定位句中。
C) 张冠李戴Convenience. 是末段 incredibly convenient for consumers 提到的食品属性之一，与 Q47 的"渴求"无直接因果关系。
D) ✓ 正确预判的主语 Memory 与 Memory. 原词一致，是最直接的细节复现型答案。
  • Memory, nostalgia(怀旧) in particular↔Memory.
P2

Memory, nostalgia(怀旧) in particular, plays a big role in the foods we crave.

记忆，尤其是怀旧情感，在我们渴求的食物中起着举足轻重的作用。

48

What do food companies do to capitalize on consumers' association with their food products?

食品公司如何利用消费者对其食品产品的联想?

  • A)They strive to influence how consumers remember their products.它们努力影响消费者对其产品的记忆方式。
  • B)They attempt to use consumers' long-term memories to promote addiction.它们试图利用消费者的长期记忆来促进成瘾。
  • C)They try to exploit consumers' memories for their products as early as possible.它们试图尽早利用消费者对其产品的记忆。
  • D)They endeavor to find what consumers remember about their products.它们努力找出消费者对其产品的记忆内容。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判的"塑造记忆"与 influence how consumers remember 在动作（塑造/影响）和对象（记忆/如何记住）上双重对应。
  • trying to shape the memories that we have for their products↔strive to influence how consumers remember their products
B) 过度推断use consumers' long-term memories to promote addiction 把"塑造记忆"曲解为"利用长期记忆推动成瘾"——原文虽然提到 features of addiction，但其语境是说"食物入脑速度快"，并不是把记忆当成瘾工具。
C) 偷换概念exploit consumers' memories for their products as early as possible 把动作偷换为"尽早利用记忆"，并嫁接首段 we begin forming memories for food at a really early age 的"早期"细节——但原文说的是"消费者从小开始形成记忆"，不是"公司尽早去利用"。
D) 偷换概念endeavor to find what consumers remember about their products 把"塑造（shape）"偷换为"查明（find）"——前者是主动改变记忆，后者是被动调查记忆，方向完全相反。
P4

Knowing this, the food industry spends lots of time trying to shape the memories that we have for their products.

明白这一点之后，食品行业花费大量时间，努力塑造我们对其产品所留存的记忆。

49

How does the food industry operate from manufacturing to packaging, according to Moss?

根据 Moss 的说法,食品行业从生产到包装是如何运作的?

  • A)Placing the idea of fast food in an entirely new light.将快餐的概念置于全新的视角。
  • B)Setting no limit to the number of fast food chains.对快餐连锁店的数量不设限制。
  • C)Focusing on how quickly the work is done.专注于工作完成的速度。
  • D)Prioritizing the quality of their products.优先考虑其产品的质量。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 答非所问Placing the idea of fast food in an entirely new light 是 Moss 对"fast food"概念的重新解读结果，对应 this puts the notion of "fast food" in an entirely new light，但 Q49 问的是行业"如何运作"，不是 Moss"如何看待 fast food 概念"。
B) 偷换概念Setting no limit to the number of fast food chains 来自对 isn't limited to fast food chains 的曲解——原文是说"fast food 不限于连锁快餐店（含义=加工食品也算 fast food）"，不是说"快餐连锁店数量没有上限"。
C) ✓ 正确预判的"以速度为核心"与 Focusing on how quickly the work is done 在核心概念上完全等值。
  • Everything in the industry is about speed↔Focusing on how quickly the work is done
D) 与原文相反Prioritizing the quality of their products 说"优先考虑质量"，但原文明确说 Everything in the industry is about speed——是"速度"优先，不是"质量"优先。
P4

Everything in the industry is about speed, from manufacturing to packaging.

整个行业的一切都围绕"速度"展开，从生产到包装无一例外。

50

Why are companies finding it difficult to satisfy consumers' demand for healthier food products?

为什么公司发现难以满足消费者对更健康食品的需求?

  • A)They think speed of production outweighs consumers' health.它们认为生产速度比消费者健康更重要。
  • B)They believe their industry would perish without fast foods.它们相信没有快餐其行业将会消亡。
  • C)They have to strike a balance between taste and nutrition.它们必须在口味和营养之间取得平衡。
  • D)They are hooked on manufacturing convenience foods.它们沉迷于生产方便食品。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 过度推断speed of production outweighs consumers' health 把第四段"速度优先"与末段"难满足健康需求"硬串成因果——原文确实说过 about speed，但并未说"速度压倒健康"是公司难以满足新需求的原因。
B) 无中生有their industry would perish without fast foods 完全是凭空想象，原文未涉及行业"消亡"或"存亡"。
C) 过度推断strike a balance between taste and nutrition 把末段 super delicious 和 eat healthier 硬凑成"口味与营养的平衡"——但原文没有任何"平衡"的表述，给出的真正原因是公司自身"上瘾"。
D) ✓ 正确预判的"对制造便利食品上瘾"与 hooked on manufacturing convenience foods 完全等值，且 hooked on 回扣首段 hook 一词，是作者刻意的呼应。
  • their own addiction to making these convenience foods↔hooked on manufacturing convenience foods
  • making↔manufacturing
P5

Now that more and more people care about what they put in their bodies and are wanting to eat healthier, these companies are finding it really difficult to meet that new demand because of their own addiction to making these convenience foods.

如今越来越多的人在意吃进体内的东西、想吃得更健康，这些公司发现自己很难满足这一新需求，原因在于它们自身已经"上瘾"于生产这些便利食品。
