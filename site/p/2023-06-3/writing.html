范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 169 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

It is widely accepted that an important goal of education is to help students learn how to learn. In my view, teaching learners to acquire knowledge on their own matters far more than simply filling their heads with facts, because the ability to learn lasts a lifetime.

The reason is that knowledge itself keeps changing, while the skill of learning does not. Consider two students: one memorizes every formula the teacher offers, and the other masters how to find, question and apply new information. When they leave campus and face problems no textbook has covered, the first often feels lost, whereas the second keeps growing. My own experience confirms this. In college I taught myself programming through online courses; what my teachers really gave me was not the code, but the confidence and method to explore an unfamiliar field by myself.

For these reasons, education should do more than deliver content. Once students truly learn how to learn, they hold a key that opens every door throughout their lives.
