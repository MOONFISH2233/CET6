原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

The suggestion that people should aim for dietary diversity by trying to eat a variety of foods has been a basic public health recommendation for decades in the United States and elsewhere. Now, however, experts are warning that aiming for a diverse diet may actually lead to just eating more calories, and, thus, to obesity. One issue is that people may not interpret "variety" the way nutritionists intend. This problem is highlighted by new research conducted by the American Heart Association. Researchers reviewed all the evidence published related to dietary diversity and saw a correlation between dietary diversity and a greater intake of both healthy and unhealthy foods. This had implications for obesity, as researchers found a greater prevalence of obesity amongst people with a greater dietary diversity.

几十年来，在美国及其他地区，公共卫生领域一直基本建议人们通过尝试食用多种食物来实现饮食多样化。然而，如今专家警告说，以饮食多样化为目标实际上可能会导致人们摄入更多热量，进而造成肥胖。问题之一在于，人们对“多样性”的理解可能与营养学家所指的含义不同。美国心脏协会开展的一项新研究凸显了这一问题。研究人员审查了所有与饮食多样性相关的已发表证据，发现饮食多样性与健康食品和不健康食品摄入量的增加均存在相关性。这对肥胖问题有一定影响，因为研究人员发现，饮食多样性较高的人群中肥胖的比例也较高。

P2

One author of the new study explained that their findings contradict standard dietary advice, as most dietary guidelines around the world include a statement of eating a variety of foods. But this advice does not seem to be supported by science, possibly because there is little agreement about the meaning of "dietary diversity," which is not clearly and consistently defined. Some experts measure dietary diversity by counting the number of food groups eaten, while others look at the distribution of calories across individual foods, and still others measure how different the foods eaten are from each other.

这项新研究的一位作者解释说，他们的研究结果与标准饮食建议相矛盾，因为世界各地的大多数膳食指南都包含“食用多种食物”的表述。但这一建议似乎没有得到科学依据的支持，可能是因为人们对“饮食多样性”的含义缺乏共识，而这一概念也没有得到清晰且一致的定义。一些专家通过统计所食用食物组的数量来衡量饮食多样性，另一些专家则考察各种单独食物之间的热量分布，还有一些专家则衡量所食用食物彼此之间的差异程度。

P3

Although the findings of this new study contradict standard dietary advice, they do not come as a surprise to all of the researchers involved. Dr. Rao, one of the study authors, noted that, after 20 years of experience in the field of obesity, he has observed that people who have a regimented lifestyle and diet tend to be thinner and healthier than people with a wide variety of consumption. This anecdotal evidence matches the conclusions of the study, which found no evidence that dietary diversity promotes healthy body weight or optimal eating patterns, and limited evidence that eating a variety of foods is actually associated with consuming more calories, poor eating patterns and weight gain. Further, there is some evidence that a greater variety of food options in a single meal may delay people's feeling of fullness and actually increase how much they eat.

尽管这项新研究的结果与标准饮食建议相矛盾，但并非所有参与研究的人员都对此感到意外。研究作者之一 Rao 医生指出，凭借20年的肥胖研究经验，他观察到，生活方式和饮食较为规律的人往往比饮食种类多样的人更苗条、更健康。这一轶闻式证据与该研究的结论相吻合：研究没有发现饮食多样性能够促进健康的体重或最佳的饮食模式，而有限的证据表明，食用多种食物实际上与摄入更多热量、不良饮食模式以及体重增加有关。此外，有证据表明，一顿饭中食物选择的多样性增加，可能会延迟人们产生饱腹感的时间，实际上使他们吃得更多。

P4

Based on their findings, the researchers endorse a diet consisting of a limited number of healthy foods such as vegetables, fruits, grains, and poultry. They also recommend that people simultaneously endeavor to restrict consumption of sweets, sugar and red meat. The researchers stress, however, that their dietary recommendations do not imply dietary diversity is never positive, and that, in the past, diversity in diets of whole, unprocessed food may have actually been very beneficial.

基于研究结果，研究人员支持由数量有限的健康食品组成的饮食，例如蔬菜、水果、谷物和禽肉。他们还建议人们同时努力限制甜食、糖和红肉的摄入。不过，研究人员强调，他们的饮食建议并不意味着饮食多样性从来没有积极作用；过去，完整且未经加工的食物构成的多样化饮食实际上可能非常有益。

解析逐题详解

51

What has been a standard piece of dietary advice for decades?

几十年来的标准饮食建议是什么?

  • A)People should cultivate a healthy eating habit.人们应该培养健康的饮食习惯。
  • B)People should have a well-balanced diet.人们应该均衡饮食。
  • C)People should diversify what they eat.人们应该多样化饮食。
  • D)People should limit calorie intake to avoid obesity.人们应该限制热量摄入以避免肥胖。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 无中生有cultivate a healthy eating habit 是合理的常识建议，但原文标准建议特指"多样化"而非泛泛的"健康习惯"——属于范围模糊的偷换。
B) 偷换概念well-balanced diet 在中文里和"多样化"语感相近，但原文用的是 dietary diversity，强调"种类多"而非"营养均衡"，两者不等值。
C) ✓ 正确预判要的是"使饮食多样化"，C 项 diversify what they eat 就是这层意思的标准同义改写，锁定 C。
  • aim for dietary diversity by trying to eat a variety of foods↔diversify what they eat
D) 张冠李戴limit calorie intake to avoid obesity 是文章后文 obesity 话题的延伸，但题干问的是"几十年来的标准建议"，原文标准建议是 diversity 而非"限制热量"。
P1

The suggestion that people should aim for dietary diversity by trying to eat a variety of foods has been a basic public health recommendation for decades in the United States and elsewhere.

几十年来，在美国及其他地方，建议人们通过尝试多种食物来追求饮食多样化，一直是一条基本的公共健康建议。

52

What did the new research by the American Heart Association find?

美国心脏协会的新研究发现了什么?

  • A)People seeking dietary diversity tend to eat more.追求饮食多样性的人往往吃得更多。
  • B)Big eaters are more likely to become overweight.食量大的人更容易超重。
  • C)Dietary diversity is positively related to good health.饮食多样性与良好健康呈正相关。
  • D)Unhealthy food makes people gain weight more easily.不健康的食物使人更容易增重。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判是"多样化与吃得更多相关"，A 项 People seeking dietary diversity tend to eat more 即此意，且与原文 correlation 关系无矛盾，锁定 A。
  • a correlation between dietary diversity and a greater intake of both healthy and unhealthy foods↔People seeking dietary diversity tend to eat more
B) 偷换概念Big eaters are more likely to become overweight 的主语是"吃得多的人"，而原文论述对象是"追求多样化的人"；"吃得多→变胖"是常识，但不是本研究发现的关系。
C) 与原文相反原文说多样化与"更多肥胖患病率"相关，是负面相关；positively related to good health 却说与"健康"正相关——意思完全相反。
D) 无中生有Unhealthy food makes people gain weight more easily 是常识，但原文研究发现的是"多样化与吃得更多/肥胖率高"相关，并非"不健康食物本身致胖"。
P1

Researchers reviewed all the evidence published related to dietary diversity and saw a correlation between dietary diversity and a greater intake of both healthy and unhealthy foods. This had implications for obesity, as researchers found a greater prevalence of obesity amongst people with a greater dietary diversity.

研究者回顾了所有已发表的与饮食多样化相关的证据，发现饮食多样化与摄入更多健康和不健康食物之间存在相关性。这对肥胖问题有重要含义，因为研究者发现，饮食多样化程度更高的人群中肥胖患病率也更高。

53

What could help to explain the contradiction between the new findings and the common public health recommendation?

什么可以帮助解释新发现与常见公共卫生建议之间的矛盾?

  • A)Most dietary guidelines around the world contradict one another.世界各地的大多数饮食指南相互矛盾。
  • B)Conventional wisdom about diet is seldom supported by science.关于饮食的传统观念很少得到科学支持。
  • C)The methods researchers use to measure nutrition vary greatly.研究人员测量营养的方法差异很大。
  • D)There is little consensus on the definition of dietary diversity.对饮食多样性的定义几乎没有共识。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 偷换概念Most dietary guidelines around the world contradict one another 把"指南间彼此矛盾"安给原文，原文实际说的是"新研究与指南矛盾"，而不是"指南之间矛盾"。
B) 过度推断Conventional wisdom about diet is seldom supported by science 把原文"这条建议似乎未被科学支持"扩大为"所有饮食常识都很少被科学支持"，从个例跳到普遍规律。
C) 偷换概念The methods researchers use to measure nutrition vary greatly 把"测量饮食多样化的方法不同"偷换为"测量营养的方法不同"——nutrition ≠ dietary diversity，主体偏离。
D) ✓ 正确预判"定义没共识"，D 项 little consensus on the definition of dietary diversity 是其标准同义改写，锁定 D。
  • there is little agreement about the meaning of "dietary diversity"↔There is little consensus on the definition of dietary diversity.
P2

But this advice does not seem to be supported by science, possibly because there is little agreement about the meaning of "dietary diversity," which is not clearly and consistently defined.

但这一建议似乎并未得到科学的支持，可能是因为对于"饮食多样化"的含义，学界几乎没有共识——它本身就没有清晰一致的定义。

54

What did Dr. Rao find after 20 years of research on obesity?

Rao博士在20年的肥胖研究后发现了什么?

  • A)Dietary diversity promotes healthy body weight.饮食多样性促进健康体重。
  • B)Eating patterns and weight gain go hand in hand.饮食模式与体重增加密切相关。
  • C)Diversified food intake may not contribute to health.多样化的食物摄入可能无助于健康。
  • D)There is no clear definition of optimal eating patterns.最佳饮食模式没有明确定义。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反Dietary diversity promotes healthy body weight 说"多样化促进健康体重"，与原文 Rao 的观察（多样化的人不如规律饮食的人瘦）方向完全相反。
B) 答非所问Eating patterns and weight gain go hand in hand 谈"饮食模式与体重增加同步"，与原文 Rao 的观察焦点（多样化 vs 规律化的比较）不对位，且 Rao 句中并未直接给出这一结论。
C) ✓ 正确预判是"多样化未必有利于健康/体重"，C 项 Diversified food intake may not contribute to health 用否定形式表达同义，锁定 C。
  • people who have a regimented lifestyle and diet tend to be thinner and healthier than people with a wide variety of consumption↔Diversified food intake may not contribute to health.
D) 张冠李戴no clear definition of optimal eating patterns 把 53 题里"dietary diversity 没有清晰定义"换成"optimal eating patterns 没有清晰定义"，并把这个内容安到 Rao 的发现头上——既偷换概念又移花接木。
P3

Dr. Rao, one of the study authors, noted that, after 20 years of experience in the field of obesity, he has observed that people who have a regimented lifestyle and diet tend to be thinner and healthier than people with a wide variety of consumption.

研究作者之一 Rao 博士指出，凭借 20 年肥胖研究领域的经验，他观察到饮食和生活方式有规律的人，往往比饮食种类繁多的人更瘦、也更健康。

55

What does the passage say about people who eat a great variety of food?

文章对吃各种各样食物的人有什么说法?

  • A)They tend to consume more sweets, sugar and red meat.他们往往摄入更多甜食、糖和红肉。
  • B)They don't feel they have had enough until they overeat.他们直到吃过量才觉得吃够了。
  • C)They don't have any problems getting sufficient nutrition.他们在获取充足营养方面没有任何问题。
  • D)They are more likely to eat foods beneficial to their health.他们更有可能吃对健康有益的食物。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴tend to consume more sweets, sugar and red meat 出自末段研究者建议的"少吃这些"，并不是"饮食多样的人实际吃得更多"的内容——把第四段研究者的建议张冠到第三段对群体的描述上。
B) ✓ 正确预判是"饱腹感推迟、吃过头"，B 项 don't feel they have had enough until they overeat 是这一因果链的同义改写，锁定 B。
  • may delay people's feeling of fullness and actually increase how much they eat↔They don't feel they have had enough until they overeat.
C) 无中生有don't have any problems getting sufficient nutrition 谈"获取营养无障碍"，原文从未涉及"营养是否充足"这个话题——属凭空捏造。
D) 与原文相反more likely to eat foods beneficial to their health 说"更可能吃有益健康食物"，与原文 Rao 的观察（多样化者更胖更不健康）方向相反。
P3

Further, there is some evidence that a greater variety of food options in a single meal may delay people's feeling of fullness and actually increase how much they eat.

此外，还有一些证据表明，一餐中食物种类越多，可能会推迟人们的饱腹感，反而让他们吃得更多。
