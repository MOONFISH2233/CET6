范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 169 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

People are now increasingly aware of the danger of ‘appearance anxiety’ or being obsessed with one’s looks. This uneasiness rarely appears out of nowhere. Bombarded daily by filtered images and narrow aesthetic standards on social media, many young people measure themselves against unreachable models and feel that they never look good enough. The constant comparison quietly drains their confidence and even distracts them from things that truly matter.

Yet a healthier attitude is well within our reach. The key is to shift attention from the mirror to inner value — our knowledge, kindness and abilities. I once spent hours worrying about a small scar before a presentation, until a friend reminded me that the audience remembered my ideas, not my face. That moment taught me that self-acceptance, rather than a flawless appearance, is what sets us free.

In short, looks are only a thin surface of who we are. Once we learn to value character over appearance, ‘appearance anxiety’ loses its grip, and we can live with genuine confidence.
