原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

The ability to make inferences from same and different, once thought to be unique to humans, is viewed as a cornerstone of abstract intelligent thought. A new study, however, has shown that what psychologists call same-different discrimination is present in creatures generally seen as unintelligent: newborn ducklings(小鸭).

从相同与不同中进行推断的能力曾被认为是人类独有的能力，被视为抽象智能思维的基石。然而，一项新研究表明，心理学家所称的相同—不同辨别能力也存在于通常被认为并不聪明的生物身上：刚出生的小鸭。

P2

The study, published Thursday in Science, challenges our idea of what it means to have a birdbrain, said Edward Wasserman, an experimental psychologist at the University of Iowa who wrote an independent review of the study.

爱荷华大学实验心理学家Edward Wasserman为这项研究撰写了独立评论。他说，这项于周四发表在《科学》杂志上的研究，挑战了我们对拥有“鸟脑”意味着什么的认识。

P3

"In fact, birds are extremely intelligent and our problem pretty much lies in figuring out how to get them to 'talk' to us, or tell us how smart they really are," he said.

他说：“事实上，鸟类极其聪明，而我们的问题基本在于如何让它们‘与我们交谈’，或者告诉我们它们究竟有多聪明。”

P4

Antone Martinho and Alex Kacelnik, co-authors of the new paper, devised a clever experiment to better test bird intelligence.

新论文的共同作者Antone Martinho和Alex Kacelnik设计了一项巧妙的实验，以更好地检验鸟类的智力。

P5

First, they took 1-day-old ducklings and exposed them to a pair of moving objects. The two objects were either the same or different in shape or color. Then they exposed each duckling to two entirely new pairs of moving objects.

首先，他们将一日龄的小鸭置于一对运动物体前。这两个物体的形状或颜色要么相同，要么不同。随后，他们又让每只小鸭观察两对全新的运动物体。

P6

The researchers found that about 70% of the ducklings preferred to move toward the pair of objects that had the same shape or color relationship as the first objects they saw. A duckling that was first shown two green spheres, in other words, was more likely to move toward a pair of blue spheres than a mismatched pair of orange and purple spheres.

研究人员发现，大约70%的小鸭更喜欢朝着这样一对物体移动：这对物体与它们最初看到的物体之间具有相同的形状或颜色关系。换句话说，一只最初看到两个绿色球体的小鸭，更有可能朝着一对蓝色球体移动，而不是朝着一对由橙色和紫色球体组成的不匹配物体移动。

P7

Ducklings go through a rapid learning process called imprinting shortly after birth—it's what allows them to identify and follow their mothers.

小鸭出生后不久会经历一个称为印刻的快速学习过程，这一过程使它们能够辨认并跟随自己的母亲。

P8

These findings suggest that ducklings use abstract relationships between sensory inputs like color, shape, sounds and odor to recognize their mothers, said Dr. Kacelnik.

Kacelnik博士说，这些发现表明，小鸭利用颜色、形状、声音和气味等感官输入之间的抽象关系来辨认自己的母亲。

P9

By studying imprinting, the authors of this study have shown for the first time that an animal can learn relationships between concepts without training, said Jeffrey Katz, an experimental psychologist at Auburn University who was not involved in the study.

奥本大学实验心理学家Jeffrey Katz没有参与这项研究。他说，通过研究印刻，这项研究的作者首次证明，动物能够在没有训练的情况下学习概念之间的关系。

P10

Previous studies have suggested that other animals, including pigeons, dolphins, honeybees and some primates(灵长类动物), can discern same from different, but only after extensive training.

此前的研究表明，包括鸽子、海豚、蜜蜂和某些灵长类动物在内的其他动物也能辨别相同与不同，但这只有在经过大量训练之后才能做到。

P11

Adding ducklings to the list—particularly untrained newborn ducklings—suggests that the ability to compare abstract concepts "is far more necessary to a wider variety of animals' survival than we previously thought," Dr. Martinho said. He believes the ability is so crucial because it helps animals consider context when identifying objects in their environment.

Martinho博士说，将小鸭，尤其是未经训练的初生小鸭，加入这一名单表明，比较抽象概念的能力“对于更多种类动物的生存而言，比我们此前认为的更加必要”。他认为，这种能力至关重要，因为它能帮助动物在辨认周围环境中的物体时考虑情境。

P12

It's clear from this study and others like it that "animals process and appreciate far more of the intricacies in their world than we've ever understood," Dr. Wasserman said. "We are in a revolutionary phase in terms of our ability to understand the minds of other animals."

Wasserman博士说，从这项研究以及其他类似研究中可以清楚看出，“动物处理和理解其世界复杂性的程度，远远超过我们以往的认识。”“就我们理解其他动物心智的能力而言，我们正处于一个革命性阶段。”

解析逐题详解

46

In what way were humans thought to be unique?

人类曾被认为在哪方面具有独特性?

  • A)Being a major source of animal intelligence.作为动物智能的主要来源。
  • B)Being the cornerstone of the creative world.作为创造性世界的基石。
  • C)Being capable of same-different discrimination.能够进行相同-不同辨别。
  • D)Being able to distinguish abstract from concrete.能够区分抽象与具体。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 无中生有选项 Being a major source of animal intelligence 说人类是 "动物智力的主要来源"，原文从未提出这一观点；文章只对比人与动物的智力，没说人是动物智力的源头。
B) 偷换概念cornerstone of abstract intelligent thought 修饰的是 "同/异推断能力"，是 "抽象智力的基石"，不是 "创造性世界的基石"；选项 Being the cornerstone of the creative world 把对象偷换成 the creative world。
C) ✓ 正确预判 "人类独有的是 same-different 推断能力"，C 选项 Being capable of same-different discrimination 把这一信息完整还原，且来源于原文同一句话，是定位句的标准同义改写。
  • make inferences from same and different↔same-different discrimination
  • once thought to be unique to humans↔Being capable of same-different discrimination
D) 无中生有原文只提及 abstract intelligent thought，从未提及 concrete 这一概念；选项 Being able to distinguish abstract from concrete 把抽象与具体编造成可区分的对象。
P1

The ability to make inferences from same and different, once thought to be unique to humans, is viewed as a cornerstone of abstract intelligent thought.

曾被认为是人类独有的、对 "同" 与 "异" 进行推断的能力，被视为抽象智力思维的基石。

47

What do we learn from the study published in Science?

我们从发表在《科学》杂志上的研究中了解到什么?

  • A)Our understanding of the bird world was biased.我们对鸟类世界的理解存在偏见。
  • B)Our conception of birds' intelligence was wrong.我们对鸟类智能的概念是错误的。
  • C)Our communication with birds was far from adequate.我们与鸟类的交流远远不够充分。
  • D)Our knowledge about bird psychology needs updating.我们关于鸟类心理学的知识需要更新。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 以偏概全选项 Our understanding of the bird world was biased 把对象从 "鸟的智力" 扩大成 "鸟的世界"——原文只挑战对鸟智力的看法，没说我们对整个鸟类世界的理解有偏见。
B) ✓ 正确预判 "鸟脑认知被推翻"，Our conception of birds' intelligence was wrong 与之严丝合缝；B 是定位句的标准同义改写。
  • challenges our idea of what it means to have a birdbrain↔Our conception of birds' intelligence was wrong
  • birds are extremely intelligent and our problem pretty much lies in figuring out how to get them to 'talk' to us↔Our conception of birds' intelligence was wrong
C) 答非所问our problem pretty much lies in figuring out how to get them to 'talk' to us 是 Wasserman 的延伸说明，不是该研究本身告诉我们的核心结论；选项 Our communication with birds was far from adequate 误把延伸内容当作主结论。
D) 偷换概念选项 Our knowledge about bird psychology needs updating 用 bird psychology 替换 birds' intelligence，鸟类心理学是更广的学科范畴，且 needs updating 改变了原文 "挑战"（即推翻）的强度。
P2

The study, published Thursday in Science, challenges our idea of what it means to have a birdbrain, said Edward Wasserman, an experimental psychologist at the University of Iowa who wrote an independent review of the study.

Wasserman 表示，这项发表于周四《科学》杂志上的研究挑战了我们对 "birdbrain" 一词含义的认知。

48

What did the researchers discover about most ducklings from their experiment?

研究人员从实验中发现大多数雏鸭有什么特点?

  • A)They could associate shape with color.它们能够将形状与颜色联系起来。
  • B)They reacted quickly to moving objects.它们对移动物体反应迅速。
  • C)They preferred colored objects to colorless ones.它们更喜欢有色物体而不是无色物体。
  • D)They could tell whether the objects were the same.它们能够判断物体是否相同。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 偷换概念原文是 the same shape or color relationship——这里 or 表示 "形状或颜色" 二者之一的关系，不是把形状和颜色 "关联起来"；选项 They could associate shape with color 是错读 or 的产物。
B) 无中生有原文从未谈及小鸭 "反应速度"；选项 They reacted quickly to moving objects 是利用 moving objects 这一原词，凭空加上 quickly 这一速度属性。
C) 无中生有原文只提到不同颜色（green、blue、orange、purple）之间的对照，从未涉及 "colorless 无色" 物体；选项 They preferred colored objects to colorless ones 凭空造出有色与无色的对比。
D) ✓ 正确预判 "小鸭能识别同异关系"，They could tell whether the objects were the same 与之精确对应；tell whether X were the same 正是 same-different discrimination 的口语化表述。
  • the same shape or color relationship as the first objects they saw↔They could tell whether the objects were the same
  • make inferences from same and different↔They could tell whether the objects were the same
P6

The researchers found that about 70% of the ducklings preferred to move toward the pair of objects that had the same shape or color relationship as the first objects they saw.

研究者发现，约 70% 的小鸭更倾向于走向与它们第一次看到的物体保持同种形状或颜色关系的那一对物体。

49

What was novel about the experiment in the study reported in Science?

《科学》杂志报道的研究中,该实验的新颖之处是什么?

  • A)The animals used received no training.所使用的动物未接受任何训练。
  • B)It used a number of colors and shapes.实验使用了多种颜色和形状。
  • C)It was conducted by experimental psychologists.实验由实验心理学家进行。
  • D)The ducklings were compared with other animals.雏鸭与其他动物进行了比较。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判 "未受训练即首次"，The animals used received no training 与之严丝合缝；A 是 without training 与 untrained 的标准动宾化改写。
  • an animal can learn relationships between concepts without training↔The animals used received no training
  • untrained newborn ducklings↔The animals used received no training
B) 答非所问原文 two objects ... in shape or color 提到使用了不同颜色和形状，但这只是实验材料的描述，不是 "新颖之处"——只是惯常做法；选项 It used a number of colors and shapes 把背景信息冒充为新颖之处。
C) 答非所问experimental psychologist 与 experimental psychologist 只是介绍研究人员身份，从未将 "实验心理学家" 当作本研究的新颖之处；选项 It was conducted by experimental psychologists 把背景信息错认为答案。
D) 与原文相反原文 Previous studies have suggested that other animals ... can discern same from different 是用来反衬本研究新颖性的，而不是说本实验 "把小鸭与其他动物作了比较"；选项 The ducklings were compared with other animals 误把语段功能当成实验设计。
P9

By studying imprinting, the authors of this study have shown for the first time that an animal can learn relationships between concepts without training, said Jeffrey Katz, an experimental psychologist at Auburn University who was not involved in the study.

Auburn 大学的实验心理学家 Jeffrey Katz（未参与本研究）表示，通过研究印随现象，本研究作者首次证明动物可以在 "无需训练" 的情况下学会概念之间的关系。

50

What do we learn from Dr. Wasserman's comment on the study of animal minds at the end of the passage?

从文章结尾Wasserman博士对动物思维研究的评论中我们了解到什么?

  • A)It is getting more and more intricate.研究正变得越来越复杂。
  • B)Research methods are being updated.研究方法正在更新。
  • C)It is attracting more public attention.研究正吸引更多公众关注。
  • D)Remarkable progress is being made.研究正取得显著进展。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 偷换概念原文 far more of the intricacies in their world 说的是 "动物世界的复杂性"，不是 "研究本身越来越复杂"；选项 It is getting more and more intricate 把 intricacies 这一原词扎根，但用错了对象。
B) 无中生有Wasserman 谈的是认知层面的革命，不是方法层面的革新；原文未提 research methods are being updated。
C) 无中生有Wasserman 谈的是学界对动物心智认知的进展，没有提及 "公众关注" 这一话题；选项 It is attracting more public attention 完全偏离。
D) ✓ 正确预判 "动物心智研究取得突破"，Remarkable progress is being made 把 revolutionary phase 这一比喻翻译为 "显著进展"，是定位句的标准还原。
  • revolutionary phase in terms of our ability to understand the minds of other animals↔Remarkable progress is being made
  • animals process and appreciate far more of the intricacies in their world than we've ever understood↔Remarkable progress is being made
P12

We are in a revolutionary phase in terms of our ability to understand the minds of other animals.

在理解其他动物心智的能力方面，我们正处于一个 "革命性的阶段"。
