原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Technology is never a neutral tool for achieving human ends. Technological innovations reshape people as they use these innovations to control their environment. Artificial intelligence, for example, is altering humanity.

技术从来都不是实现人类目的的中性工具。人们利用技术创新来控制环境的同时，这些创新也在重塑人类。例如，人工智能正在改变人类。

P2

While the term AI conjures up anxieties about killer robots or catastrophic levels of unemployment, there are other, deeper implications. As AI increasingly shapes the human experience, how does this change what it means to be human? Central to the problem is a person's capacity to make choices, particularly judgments that have moral implications.

虽然“人工智能”这个词会让人联想到杀手机器人或灾难性程度的失业，但它还带来了其他更深层的影响。随着人工智能日益塑造人类的体验，这会如何改变“成为人类”的含义？问题的核心在于人的选择能力，尤其是作出具有道德影响的判断的能力。

P3

Aristotle argued that the capacity for making practical judgments depends on regularly making them—on habit and practice. We see the emergence of machines as substitute judges in a variety of everyday contexts as a potential threat to people learning how to effectively exercise judgment themselves.

亚里士多德认为，作出实践判断的能力取决于经常作出这类判断，也就是说，取决于习惯和实践。我们认为，在各种日常情境中，机器作为替代性裁判的出现，可能会威胁到人们学习如何有效地自行行使判断力。

P4

In the workplace, managers routinely make decisions about who to hire or fire and which loan to approve, to name a few. These are areas where algorithmic(算法的) prescription is replacing human judgment, and so people who might have had the chance to develop practical judgment in these areas no longer will.

在工作场所，管理者经常要决定雇用或解雇谁，以及批准哪笔贷款，等等。在这些领域，算法处方正在取代人的判断，因此，那些原本可能有机会在这些领域培养实践判断力的人，将不再拥有这样的机会。

P5

Recommendation engines, which are increasingly prevalent intermediaries in people's consumption of culture, may serve to constrain choice and minimize luck. By presenting consumers with algorithmically selected choices of what to watch, read, stream and visit next, companies are replacing human taste with machine taste. In one sense, this is helpful. After all, machines can survey a wider range of choices than any individual is likely to have the time or energy to do on their own.

推荐引擎日益成为人们文化消费中普遍存在的中介，它们可能会限制选择并减少偶然性。通过向消费者展示由算法选择的下一部要观看、阅读、通过流媒体收听或访问的内容，公司正在用机器品味取代人的品味。从某种意义上说，这很有帮助。毕竟，机器能够考察比任何个人都更广泛的选择，而个人很可能没有时间或精力独自完成这件事。

P6

At the same time, though, this selection is optimizing for what people are likely to prefer based on what they've preferred in the past. We think there is some risk that people's options will be constrained by their past in a new and unanticipated way.

不过与此同时，这种选择是在根据人们过去的偏好，优化他们可能会喜欢的内容。我们认为，人们的选择可能会以一种全新且出乎意料的方式受到其过去经历的限制，这其中存在一定风险。

P7

As machine learning algorithms improve and as they train on more extensive data sets, larger parts of everyday life are likely to become utterly predictable. The predictions are going to get better and better, and they will ultimately make common experiences more efficient and pleasant.

随着机器学习算法不断改进，并在更大规模的数据集上进行训练，日常生活中更大范围的部分很可能会变得完全可预测。预测将变得越来越准确，并最终使常见体验更加高效、愉快。

P8

Algorithms could soon—if they don't already—have a better idea about which show you'd like to watch next and which job candidate you should hire than you do. One day, humans may even find a way for machines to make these decisions without some of the biases that humans typically display.

算法很快就可能，或者说如果现在还没有的话，能够比你自己更清楚地判断你下一步想看哪部节目，以及你应该录用哪位求职者。有一天，人类甚至可能找到一种方法，让机器在作出这些决定时避免人类通常表现出的某些偏见。

P9

But to the extent that unpredictability is part of how people understand themselves and part of what people like about themselves, humanity is in the process of losing something significant. As they become more and more predictable, the creatures inhabiting the increasingly AI-mediated world will become less and less like us.

但是，如果不可预测性是人们认识自我的方式之一，也是人们喜欢自身特点的一部分，那么人类正在逐渐失去某种重要的东西。随着这些生物变得越来越可预测，生活在这个日益由人工智能中介的世界中的生物，将越来越不像我们。

解析逐题详解

46

What do we learn about the deeper implications of AI?

关于人工智能更深层次的影响,我们了解到什么?

  • A)It is causing catastrophic levels of unemployment.它正在造成灾难性的失业水平。
  • B)It is doing physical harm to human operators.它正在对人类操作者造成身体伤害。
  • C)It is altering moral judgments.它正在改变道德判断。
  • D)It is reshaping humanity.它正在重塑人类。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反原文恰恰把catastrophic levels of unemployment列为人们对AI的浅层焦虑，作者要说的"更深层影响"是与之对立的——A把作者明示否定的浅层焦虑拿来当深层影响，方向反了。
B) 无中生有全文从未提及AI对操作者造成身体伤害，physical harm属凭空虚构。
C) 以偏概全道德判断（moral implications）只是judgments that have moral implications里举的一个具体例子，是"改变人之为人"的下属支撑，不是"deeper implications"本身。
D) ✓ 正确预判"改变人本身"，四个选项里只有It is reshaping humanity.在说人本身被改写，正面命中。
  • Technological innovations reshape people as they use these innovations↔It is reshaping humanity.
  • how does this change what it means to be human↔It is reshaping humanity.
P2

While the term AI conjures up anxieties about killer robots or catastrophic levels of unemployment, there are other, deeper implications. As AI increasingly shapes the human experience, how does this change what it means to be human?

虽然"AI"一词让人联想到杀人机器人或灾难性的失业，但还有其他更深层的影响。随着AI日益塑造人类经验，这将如何改变"作为人"的含义？

47

What is the consequence of algorithmic prescription replacing human judgment?

算法规定取代人类判断的后果是什么?

  • A)People lose the chance to cultivate the ability to make practical judgments.人们失去了培养实践判断能力的机会。
  • B)People are prevented from participating in making major decisions in the workplace.人们被阻止参与工作场所的重大决策。
  • C)Managers no longer have the chance to decide which loan to approve.管理者不再有机会决定批准哪笔贷款。
  • D)Managers do not need to take the trouble to determine who to hire or fire.管理者不需要费心确定雇用或解雇谁。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判正是"失去培养判断力的机会"，People lose the chance to cultivate the ability to make practical judgments.逐字对应。
  • people who might have had the chance to develop practical judgment in these areas no longer will↔People lose the chance to cultivate the ability to make practical judgments.
B) 偷换概念原文是"失去培养判断力的练习机会"，B把它偷换成"被阻止参与重大决策"——重大决策的参与权并未被剥夺，被剥夺的是"练手"机会。
C) 张冠李戴原文说被剥夺机会的是普通人（people），不是经理（managers）；经理本是行使判断的一方，正被算法替代。
D) 与原文相反原文是中性陈述"经理们日常会做这些决定"，并未说他们觉得麻烦或希望被替代；D把负面后果"失去机会"扭曲成正面"省事"。
P4

These are areas where algorithmic prescription is replacing human judgment, and so people who might have had the chance to develop practical judgment in these areas no longer will.

这些就是算法处方正在取代人类判断的领域，因此本来可能有机会在这些领域培养实践判断力的人，不再有此机会。

48

What may result from increasing application of recommendation engines in our consumption of culture?

推荐引擎在我们的文化消费中日益广泛的应用可能会导致什么结果?

  • A)Consumers will have much limited choice.消费者的选择将大大受限。
  • B)Consumers will actually enjoy better luck.消费者实际上会享受到更好的运气。
  • C)It will be easier to decide on what to enjoy.决定享受什么将变得更容易。
  • D)Humans will develop tastes similar to machines'.人类将发展出与机器相似的品味。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判"限制选择"，Consumers will have much limited choice.恰是其形容词化版本，正面对应。
  • may serve to constrain choice and minimize luck↔Consumers will have much limited choice.
B) 与原文相反原文是minimize luck（削减偶然/运气），B却说"享受更好的运气"——意思完全反向。
C) 以偏概全原文中"机器能扫描更广范围"只是让步性铺垫（"In one sense, this is helpful"），紧接P6"At the same time, though"明确转折，作者的核心结论是限制选择，而不是"决定更容易"。
D) 偷换概念原文是"用机器口味替代人的口味"（machine taste replaces human taste），即人的口味被压制；D却说"人将培养出与机器相似的口味"，主客体反了。
P5

Recommendation engines, which are increasingly prevalent intermediaries in people's consumption of culture, may serve to constrain choice and minimize luck.

推荐引擎在人们的文化消费中日益普遍地充当中介，可能会限制选择、削减偶然。

49

What is likely to happen to larger parts of our daily life as machine learning algorithms improve?

随着机器学习算法的改进,我们日常生活的更大部分可能会发生什么?

  • A)They will turn out to be more pleasant.它们将变得更加愉快。
  • B)They will repeat our past experience.它们将重复我们过去的经历。
  • C)They can be completely anticipated.它们可以被完全预测。
  • D)They may become better and better.它们可能会变得越来越好。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 答非所问P7主句的核心结论是utterly predictable；more efficient and pleasant是其后的次级描述。题干问的是larger parts of daily life本身会如何，而非体验如何——A答非所问。
B) 偷换概念constrained by their past说的是推荐引擎按过去偏好限制选择，与P7"算法越准、生活越可预测"是两段不同论点；B把前一段的素材搬来回答后一段的问题，错位。
C) ✓ 正确预判核心是"完全可预测"，They can be completely anticipated.中completely+anticipated双重对应，是唯一保留"utterly"程度的选项。
  • larger parts of everyday life are likely to become utterly predictable↔They can be completely anticipated.
D) 张冠李戴原文是"预测会越来越好"（The predictions are going to get better and better），即"算法预测"这个东西本身越来越好；D却把better and better的主语挂在larger parts of daily life上，主语错位。
P7

As machine learning algorithms improve and as they train on more extensive data sets, larger parts of everyday life are likely to become utterly predictable.

随着机器学习算法的改进、以及在更大数据集上的训练，日常生活越来越大的部分将变得完全可被预测。

50

Why does the author say the creatures living in the more and more AI-mediated world will become increasingly unlike us?

为什么作者说生活在越来越多由人工智能调节的世界中的生物将变得越来越不像我们?

  • A)They will have lost the most significant human element of being intelligent.他们将失去智能存在的最重要的人类元素。
  • B)They will no longer possess the human characteristic of being unpredictable.他们将不再拥有不可预测这一人类特征。
  • C)They will not be able to understand themselves as we can do today.他们将无法像我们今天这样理解自己。
  • D)They will be deprived of what their predecessors were proud of about themselves.他们将被剥夺其前辈引以为豪的东西。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 无中生有全文从未把intelligent列为人类最重要特征；作者强调的是unpredictability。A自行虚构了一种"智能"的核心人性。
B) ✓ 正确预判核心词是unpredictability，四个选项中只有They will no longer possess the human characteristic of being unpredictable.点出unpredictable，正面命中。
  • unpredictability is part of how people understand themselves and part of what people like about themselves↔They will no longer possess the human characteristic of being unpredictable.
  • As they become more and more predictable↔They will no longer possess the human characteristic of being unpredictable.
C) 过度推断原文说unpredictability是"how people understand themselves"的一部分，并没有说"未来人将无法理解自己"——C把作为成分的unpredictability当成了理解能力本身。
D) 以偏概全D只说"被剥夺了前人曾为之自豪的东西"——但"前人曾为之自豪的东西"具体是什么，D避而不答；正确答案B则点明那就是unpredictability。Why题要的是具体原因，不是含糊代称。
P9

But to the extent that unpredictability is part of how people understand themselves and part of what people like about themselves, humanity is in the process of losing something significant. As they become more and more predictable, the creatures inhabiting the increasingly AI-mediated world will become less and less like us.

但是，由于不可预测性本身就是人类自我认知与自我喜爱的一部分，人类正在失去某种重要的东西。随着他们变得越来越可预测，居住在愈发AI化的世界里的这些"生物"，将越来越不像我们。
