原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Phonics, which involves sounding out words syllable(音节) by syllable, is the best way to teach children to read. But in many classrooms, this can be a dirty word. So much so that some teachers have had to sneak phonics teaching materials into the classroom. Most American children are taught to read in a way that study after study has found to be wrong.

自然拼读法，即逐音节拼读单词，是教孩子阅读的最佳方法。但在许多课堂上，这个词却成了忌讳，甚至有些教师不得不偷偷把自然拼读教学材料带进课堂。大多数美国儿童接受的阅读教学方式，已经被一项又一项研究证明是错误的。

P2

The consequences of this are striking. Less than half of all American adults were proficient readers in 2017. American fourth graders rank 15th on the Progress in International Literacy Study, an international exam.

由此造成的后果十分惊人。2017年，所有美国成年人中具备熟练阅读能力的还不到一半。在一项国际考试“国际阅读素养研究”中，美国四年级学生排名第15位。

P3

America is stuck in a debate about teaching children to read that has been going on for decades. Some advocate teaching symbol-sound relationships (the sound k can be spelled as c, k, ck, or ch), known as phonics. Others support an immersive approach (using pictures of a cat to learn the word cat), known as "whole language". Most teachers today, almost three out of four according to a survey by the Ed Week Research Centre in 2019, use a mix called "balanced literacy". This combination of methods is ineffective. "You can't sprinkle in a little phonics," says Tenette Smith, executive director of elementary education and reading at Mississippi's education department. "It has to be systematic and explicitly taught."

美国陷入了一场关于如何教孩子阅读的争论之中，而这场争论已经持续了数十年。一些人主张教授符号与声音之间的关系（声音 k 可以拼写为 c、k、ck 或 ch），这种方法被称为“自然拼读法”。另一些人支持沉浸式方法（用猫的图片来学习单词 cat），这种方法被称为“整语言法”。如今，大多数教师采用一种称为“平衡读写法”的综合方法；根据 Ed Week Research Centre 2019年的一项调查，近四分之三的教师使用这种方法。这种方法的组合并不起作用。密西西比州教育部门小学教育与阅读执行主任 Tenette Smith 说：“你不能只掺进去一点自然拼读法。它必须是系统性的，并且要进行明确的教学。”

P4

Mississippi, often behind in social policy, has set an example here. In a state once notorious for its low reading scores, the Mississippi state legislature passed new literacy standards in 2013. Since then Mississippi has seen remarkable gains. Its fourth graders have moved from 49th (out of 50 states) to 29th on the National Assessment of Educational Progress, a nationwide exam. In 2019 it was the only state to improve its scores. For the first time since measurement began, Mississippi's pupils are now average readers, a remarkable achievement in such a poor state.

密西西比州在社会政策方面往往落后，但在这里却树立了榜样。在这个曾因阅读成绩低下而臭名昭著的州，密西西比州议会于2013年通过了新的读写能力标准。自那以后，密西西比州取得了显著进步。在一项全国性考试“全国教育进展评估”中，该州四年级学生的排名从第49位（50个州中的倒数第二位）上升到第29位。2019年，它是唯一一个成绩有所提高的州。自开始测量以来，密西西比州的学生首次成为平均水平的阅读者，这对于这样一个贫困州来说是一项非凡的成就。

P5

Mississippi's success is attributed to implementing reading methods supported by a body of research known as the science of reading. In 1997 Congress requested the National Institute of Child Health and Human Development and the Department of Education to convene a National Reading Panel to end the "reading wars" and synthesize the evidence. The panel found that phonics, along with explicit instruction in phonemic(音位的) awareness, fluency and comprehension, worked best.

密西西比州的成功归因于实施了由一系列研究支持的阅读教学方法，这些研究被称为“阅读科学”。1997年，美国国会要求国家儿童健康与人类发展研究所和教育部召集全国阅读小组，以结束“阅读战争”并综合各种证据。该小组发现，自然拼读法与音位意识、流利度和理解力的明确教学相结合，效果最佳。

P6

Yet over two decades on, "balanced literacy" is still being taught in classrooms. But advances in statistics and brain imaging have disproved the whole-language method. To the teacher who is a proficient reader, literacy seems like a natural process that requires educated guessing, rather than the deliberate process emphasized by phonics. Teachers can imagine that they learned to read through osmosis(潜移默化) when they were children. Without proper training, they bring this to classrooms.

然而，二十多年过去了，“平衡读写法”仍在课堂上教授。但统计学和脑成像技术的进步已经证伪了整语言法。对于一名具备熟练阅读能力的教师来说，读写能力似乎是一种需要凭借有根据的猜测的自然过程，而不是自然拼读法所强调的有意识的过程。教师们可能会想当然地认为，自己小时候是通过潜移默化学会阅读的。在没有接受适当培训的情况下，他们把这种观念带进了课堂。

解析逐题详解

51

What do we learn about phonics in many American classrooms?

关于自然拼读法在许多美国课堂中的情况，我们了解到什么？

  • A)It is ill reputed.它声誉不佳。
  • B)It is mostly misapplied.它大多被误用。
  • C)It is arbitrarily excluded.它被武断地排除。
  • D)It is misrepresented.它被曲解。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判得到 phonics 在课堂里名声不好；四个选项里只有 It is ill reputed. 把 dirty word 的核心语义 "被嫌弃/名声差" 直接压缩出来，与预判同义。
  • this can be a dirty word↔It is ill reputed.
  • some teachers have had to sneak phonics teaching materials into the classroom↔ill reputed
B) 无中生有It is mostly misapplied. 指 phonics 被错误地使用，但定位句只说它名声差、被偷偷带进教室，并未说它被 "误用"。
C) 过度推断It is arbitrarily excluded. 指被武断地排除在课堂之外，但 Most teachers today, almost three out of four ... use a mix called "balanced literacy" 表明 phonics 至少被以混合形式部分使用，并未被武断排除；且 Q51 问的是 in many classrooms 的状况，不是普遍排除。
D) 无中生有It is misrepresented. 指被歪曲/错误地呈现，但定位句谈论的是它在课堂氛围中受冷遇，并不涉及 "歪曲"。
P1

But in many classrooms, this can be a dirty word. So much so that some teachers have had to sneak phonics teaching materials into the classroom.

但在许多课堂里，它（拼读法）几乎成了一个令人嫌弃的词。以至于一些老师不得不偷偷把拼读法教材带进教室。

52

What has America been witnessing for decades?

几十年来美国一直在目睹什么？

  • A)An obsession with innovating teaching methodologies of reading.对创新阅读教学方法的痴迷。
  • B)An enduring debate over the approach to teaching children to read.关于儿童阅读教学方法的持久争论。
  • C)An increasing concern with many children's inadequacy in literacy.对许多儿童读写能力不足的日益关注。
  • D)An ever-forceful advocacy of a combined method for teaching reading.对综合阅读教学法的日益强有力的倡导。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念An obsession with innovating teaching methodologies of reading. 把核心从 "争论" 偷换成 "对创新阅读教学法的痴迷"，原文谈的是两派持续对立而非痴迷创新。
B) ✓ 正确B 项把定位句里 "持续数十年" + "关于教孩子阅读的争论" 两个核心元素分别压缩为 enduring + debate over the approach to teaching children to read，与预判精准对应。
  • a debate about teaching children to read↔An enduring debate over the approach to teaching children to read.
  • has been going on for decades↔enduring
C) 偷换概念An increasing concern with many children's inadequacy in literacy. 把对象换成 "对许多孩子识字不足的担忧"，原文 P2 提到识字率低只是后果数据，并不是 "美国数十年来一直在见证" 的主体。
D) 与原文相反An ever-forceful advocacy of a combined method for teaching reading. 说 "对组合法越来越有力的倡导"，但原文明确通过 Tenette Smith 说 This combination of methods is ineffective，与选项方向相反。
P3

America is stuck in a debate about teaching children to read that has been going on for decades.

美国陷入了一场关于如何教孩子阅读的争论，这场争论已经持续了几十年。

53

Why does Tenette Smith think a combination of teaching methods is ineffective?

为什么 Tenette Smith 认为综合教学法无效？

  • A)Elementary schoolchildren will be frustrated when taught with several methods combined.小学生在接受多种方法结合的教学时会感到沮丧。
  • B)Phonics has to be systematically applied and clearly taught to achieve the desired effect.自然拼读法必须系统地应用并清晰地教授才能达到预期效果。
  • C)Sprinkling in a little phonics deters the progress of even adequately motivated children.零星地加入一点自然拼读法会阻碍即使是有充分动力的儿童的进步。
  • D)Balanced literacy fails to sustain children's interest in developing a good reading habit.平衡读写教学法无法维持儿童培养良好阅读习惯的兴趣。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 无中生有Elementary schoolchildren will be frustrated when taught with several methods combined. 把 ineffective 的原因换成 "孩子受挫"，原文未提及任何 frustration。
B) ✓ 正确选项 Phonics has to be systematically applied and clearly taught to achieve the desired effect. 把 Tenette Smith 引号里的两条要求 systematic + explicitly taught 完整保留，与预判一致。
  • It has to be systematic and explicitly taught.↔Phonics has to be systematically applied and clearly taught to achieve the desired effect.
  • You can't sprinkle in a little phonics↔Phonics has to be systematically applied
C) 过度推断Sprinkling in a little phonics deters the progress of even adequately motivated children. 把原文的 "不能零星撒拼读法" 错误延伸为 "零星撒拼读法会阻碍连足够有动力的孩子的进步"，文中并未谈到 "motivated children" 或被 "deter" 这一具体后果。
D) 偷换概念Balanced literacy fails to sustain children's interest in developing a good reading habit. 把无效的原因换成 "无法维持孩子对培养良好阅读习惯的兴趣"，原文 Tenette Smith 完全没谈兴趣/习惯。
P3

"You can't sprinkle in a little phonics," says Tenette Smith, executive director of elementary education and reading at Mississippi's education department. "It has to be systematic and explicitly taught."

"你不能只是零星地撒点拼读法进去"，密西西比州教育部基础教育与阅读执行主任 Tenette Smith 说，"它必须被系统、明确地教授。"

54

What does the author say Mississippi's success is attributed to?

作者说 Mississippi 州的成功归因于什么？

  • A)Convening a National Reading Panel to synthesize research evidence.召集全国阅读委员会来综合研究证据。
  • B)Placing sufficient emphasis upon both fluency and comprehension.对流畅性和理解力都给予充分重视。
  • C)Adopting scientifically grounded approaches to teaching reading.采用有科学依据的阅读教学方法。
  • D)Obtaining support from Congress to upgrade teaching methods.获得国会支持以升级教学方法。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴Convening a National Reading Panel to synthesize research evidence. 把 1997 年国会召集小组这一历史背景错当成密西西比成功的直接归因，但原文归因句的对象是 implementing reading methods，不是 convening a panel。
B) 以偏概全Placing sufficient emphasis upon both fluency and comprehension. 取的是 panel 结论 phonics, along with explicit instruction in phonemic awareness, fluency and comprehension 中的两个并列项，但归因对象是整体阅读科学，不是 fluency + comprehension 这两点。
C) ✓ 正确选项 C 把 "实施" "由研究支持的" "阅读教学法" 三要素分别压缩为 Adopting、scientifically grounded、approaches to teaching reading，与预判精确对应。
  • reading methods supported by a body of research known as the science of reading↔Adopting scientifically grounded approaches to teaching reading.
  • implementing reading methods↔Adopting ... approaches to teaching reading
D) 无中生有Obtaining support from Congress to upgrade teaching methods. 误把 1997 年国会的角色解读为对密西西比的支持，但原文中国会只是请专家组综述证据，并未对密西西比直接 upgrade 教学法。
P5

Mississippi's success is attributed to implementing reading methods supported by a body of research known as the science of reading.

密西西比州的成功要归因于实施了由一整套被称为 "阅读科学" 的研究所支持的阅读教学法。

55

What have advances in statistics and brain imaging proved ineffective?

统计学和脑成像技术的进步证明了什么是无效的？

  • A)The teaching of symbol-sound relationships.符号与音的对应关系的教学。
  • B)Explicit instruction in phonemic awareness.音位意识的明确指导。
  • C)Efforts to end the reading wars.结束阅读之争的努力。
  • D)The immersive approach.沉浸式教学法。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反The teaching of symbol-sound relationships. 取自 Some advocate teaching symbol-sound relationships ... known as phonics，但被证伪的对象是 whole-language，而 phonics 在文中被反复认定为 the best way to teach children to read。
B) 与原文相反Explicit instruction in phonemic awareness. 取自 phonics, along with explicit instruction in phonemic awareness, fluency and comprehension, worked best，是 panel 推荐有效的做法，并非被证伪的对象。
C) 答非所问Efforts to end the reading wars. 来自 end the "reading wars"，但原文并未说这种努力被证伪；被证伪的是具体的 whole-language method。
D) ✓ 正确本题难度在于：题干用 proved ineffective、原文用 disproved、选项用 The immersive approach. 而非 the whole-language method——只有读出 P3 里 immersive approach = whole language 的等值，才能锁定 D。
  • the whole-language method↔The immersive approach.
  • disproved↔proved ineffective
P6

But advances in statistics and brain imaging have disproved the whole-language method.

但统计学和脑成像方面的进展已经证伪了整体语言法。
