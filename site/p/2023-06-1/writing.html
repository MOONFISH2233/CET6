范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 184 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Today there is a growing awareness that mental well-being needs to be given as much attention as physical health. For too long, we have measured a person's health only by the body, ignoring the mind that quietly suffers behind a healthy-looking face. Yet a troubled mind can be just as disabling as a broken leg, which is why the two deserve equal care.

My own experience convinces me of this. During my sophomore year, a close friend kept a perfect exercise routine but slid into deep anxiety before exams. He looked strong, so no one noticed until his grades and sleep collapsed. Only after he sought counseling did he truly recover. His story shows that neglecting the mind can undo all the benefits of a fit body.

Therefore, we should treat mental and physical health as two wheels of the same cart. Schools and workplaces ought to offer counseling as readily as gyms, and each of us should learn to talk about our feelings without shame. When the mind is cared for as much as the body, a person can be genuinely, fully healthy.
