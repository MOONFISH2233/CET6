2

What's the solution? Perhaps the most obvious is curtailing screen time. Commercial content is there for a reason: to elicit purchasing behavior. So parents might want to shut off the TV. Researchers concede that this is easier said than done. So they suggest another option. Parents can change how they talk to their kids about purchases.

解决办法是什么？也许最明显的办法是限制屏幕使用时间。商业内容之所以存在，是有其原因的：诱导人们进行购买。因此，家长可能会想关掉电视。研究人员承认，这说起来容易做起来难。因此，他们提出了另一种选择。家长可以改变与孩子谈论购买行为的方式。

3

The researchers suggest that parents seek input from their children on family purchasing decisions. They shouldn't try to control all purchases. Instead, parents might tell their children things like "I will listen to your advice on certain products or brands." This type of communication, the researchers assert, can lead to children making fewer purchasing demands. That means less parents' stress.

研究人员建议，家长在家庭购买决策中征求孩子的意见。他们不应试图控制所有购买行为。相反，家长可以对孩子说：“我会听取你对某些产品或品牌的建议。”研究人员断言，这种沟通方式可以减少孩子提出购买要求的次数。这意味着父母的压力会减轻。

Background speech had the biggest negative effect; vocal music was slightly more disruptive than instrumental; and general background noise and silence were least disruptive. It seems the degree of interference from background noise depends on the overlap between the processing required on the task and the processing required to screen out the background noise.

英国研究人员比较了背景语音、声乐、器乐、一般背景噪声和安静环境对短时记忆的影响。背景语音的负面影响最大；声乐的干扰略大于器乐；一般背景噪声和安静环境的干扰最小。背景噪声造成的干扰程度似乎取决于任务所需的加工过程与排除背景噪声所需的加工过程之间的重叠程度。

4

The studies suggest that when people read and when they try to remember any verbal material, background speech will inhibit their ability. Instrumental music will have at worst a slight effect. When students write essays, however, other research has found it is best to reduce all background noise as much as possible.

这些研究表明，当人们阅读或试图记忆任何文字材料时，背景语音会抑制他们的能力。器乐充其量只会产生轻微影响。然而，其他研究发现，学生写文章时，最好尽可能减少所有背景噪声。

5

Not everyone reacts in the same way to distractions. Other studies suggest some aspects of personality may make a difference. The researchers subjected shy quiet people and confident outgoing ones to high arousal or low arousal background music, general noise or silence while asking them to remember words. Everyone performed best in the silent condition, but less sociable people were more negatively affected by each of the distractions. So when children are reading and trying to incorporate new material, parents could consider allowing some background music, particularly if it's instrumental and their child is the outgoing type.

并非每个人对干扰的反应都相同。其他研究表明，个性中的某些方面可能会产生影响。研究人员让害羞安静的人和自信外向的人在高唤醒或低唤醒的背景音乐、一般噪声或安静环境中记忆词语。每个人在安静环境中的表现都最好，但不太合群的人受到每种干扰的负面影响都更大。因此，当孩子阅读并试图吸收新材料时，父母可以考虑允许播放一些背景音乐，尤其是在音乐为器乐且孩子性格外向的情况下。

【题目】 What is best for students to do when writing essays according to some research?

  • A)Keep everything as quiet as possible.尽可能保持一切安静。
  • B)Play nothing but instrumental music.只播放器乐。
  • C)Use vocal material as little as possible.尽可能少使用声乐材料。
  • D)Wear a pair of earphones or headphones.戴一副耳塞或耳机。

When students write essays, however, other research has found it is best to reduce all background noise as much as possible.
