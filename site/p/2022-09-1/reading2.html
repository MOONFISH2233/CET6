原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Born from the accessibility of mass air travel, modern international tourism has been popularized as "holiday-making" in regions that offer comparative advantages of sand, sun and sea. Travel is often portrayed as a tool for personal growth and tourism as an economic motor for destination countries and cities. There is a tendency to assume that tourism is good for everyone involved.

现代国际旅游源于大众航空旅行的普及，并在那些拥有沙滩、阳光和海洋等比较优势的地区以“度假旅游”的形式得到推广。旅行常被描绘成促进个人成长的工具，而旅游则被视为目的地国家和城市的经济引擎。人们往往想当然地认为，旅游对所有相关方都有益处。

P2

Today the big bang of tourism drives over 1.2 billion tourists across international borders annually. Many popular places are literally being loved to death. Recent protests in ports of call like Venice and Barcelona against disturbances created by cruise ships show the unfortunate consequences of emphasizing quantity over quality in tourism.

如今，旅游业的蓬勃发展每年推动超过12亿名游客跨越国境。许多热门景点实际上正被人们“爱”到毁灭。近期，威尼斯和巴塞罗那等停靠港针对邮轮造成的扰乱而举行的抗议，显示出旅游业一味重数量、轻质量所带来的不幸后果。

P3

Uncontrolled tourism development has become a primary driver of social and environmental disruption. Tourism studies provide much documentation of the many negative social impacts of tourism and resulting resentment that local populations direct toward visitors.

失控的旅游业发展已成为社会和环境遭受破坏的主要驱动力。旅游研究为旅游业造成的诸多负面社会影响，以及由此产生的当地居民对游客的怨恨，提供了大量记录。

P4

Antagonism toward tourists typically develops in mature, heavily visited destinations. Protests in heavily visited destinations suggest that traditional tourism has overstayed its welcome.

对游客的敌意通常出现在已经成熟且游客云集的目的地。游客高度集中的目的地爆发的抗议表明，传统旅游已经逾期滞留，失去了东道主的欢迎。

P5

Residents often become frustrated when the benefits of tourism are not felt locally. Although it can generate foreign exchange, income and employment, there's no guarantee that multinational hotel chains will allocate these benefits equitably among local communities.

当旅游业带来的利益没有惠及当地时，居民往往会感到沮丧。尽管旅游业能够创造外汇、收入和就业机会，但跨国连锁酒店并不能保证会在当地社区之间公平分配这些利益。

P6

On the contrary, when people stay at large resorts or on cruise ships, they make most of their purchases there, leaving local communities little opportunity to benefit from tourist spending. These forms of tourism widen economic and political gaps between haves and have-nots at local destinations.

恰恰相反，当人们住在大型度假村或乘坐邮轮时，他们的大部分消费都发生在那里，使当地社区几乎没有机会从游客消费中获益。这些旅游形式扩大了当地目的地有产者与无产者之间的经济和政治差距。

P7

In recent decades, local residents in destination communities also have found themselves negotiating new cultural boundaries, class dynamics, service industry roles and lifestyle transformations. For example, data show that tourism activity corresponds to increased social problems as local residents adopt the behaviors of tourists.

近几十年来，旅游目的地社区的当地居民还发现自己不得不应对新的文化边界、阶级关系、服务业角色以及生活方式转变。例如，数据显示，随着当地居民采用游客的行为方式，旅游活动与社会问题的增加相伴而生。

P8

What does all this mean for the everyday traveler?

这一切对日常旅行者意味着什么？

P9

First, all tourists should make every effort to honor their hosts and respect local conditions. This means being prepared to adapt to local customs and norms, rather than expecting local conditions to adapt to travelers.

首先，所有游客都应尽力尊重东道主和当地情况。这意味着要做好适应当地习俗和规范的准备，而不是期待当地情况去适应旅行者。

P10

Second, tourism is a market-based activity and works best when consumers reward better performers. In the information age, there's little excuse for travelers being uninformed about where their vacation money goes and who it enriches.

其次，旅游是一种以市场为基础的活动，只有当消费者奖励表现更好的经营者时，它才能发挥最佳作用。在信息时代，旅行者没有什么借口不了解自己的度假消费流向何处，以及这些钱让谁获益。

P11

Informed travelers also are better able to distinguish between multinational companies and local entrepreneurs whose businesses provide direct social, environmental, and economic benefits for local residents. Such businesses are in love with the destination and are therefore deserving of market reward. In the long run, being a responsible traveler means ensuring net positive impacts for local people and environments. With the information available at our fingertips, there has never been more opportunity to do so.

知情的旅行者也更有能力区分跨国公司和当地企业家，后者的企业能够直接为当地居民带来社会、环境和经济方面的益处。这类企业热爱自己的目的地，因此值得得到市场的奖励。从长远来看，成为负责任的旅行者意味着确保当地居民和环境获得净正面影响。借助触手可及的信息，我们从未有过如此多的机会做到这一点。

解析逐题详解

51

What is the popular assumption about international tourism?

关于国际旅游业，人们普遍的假设是什么？

  • A)Its benefits may compensate for the adverse environmental consequences.其益处可能会补偿不利的环境后果。
  • B)Its rapid development is attributed to people's improved living standard.其快速发展归因于人们生活水平的提高。
  • C)It appeals to people in places with favorable geographical conditions.它对地理条件优越的地方的人们具有吸引力。
  • D)It contributes to the economy of destination countries and regions.它有助于目的地国家和地区的经济发展。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 无中生有选项 A 谈"收益可以弥补不利环境后果"，但 P1 只笼统说"旅游对各方都好"，从未提出"补偿环境代价"的对比框架。
B) 偷换概念选项 B 说"旅游业的快速发展归因于生活水平提高"，原文虽提到 Born from the accessibility of mass air travel——"源于大众航空旅行的可及性"——但归因对象是"航空可及"而非"生活水平"。
C) 张冠李戴选项 C 说旅游业"吸引地理条件优越地区的人"，但原文 has been popularized as "holiday-making" in regions that offer comparative advantages of sand, sun and sea 讲的是旅游业"在"那些地区被普及，并不是这些地区的人"被"旅游业吸引。
D) ✓ 正确预判中"推动目的地经济"在选项 contributes to the economy of destination countries and regions 中找到对应，且 destination 一词原词重合，锁定 D。
  • tourism as an economic motor for destination countries and cities↔It contributes to the economy of destination countries and regions
P1

Travel is often portrayed as a tool for personal growth and tourism as an economic motor for destination countries and cities. There is a tendency to assume that tourism is good for everyone involved.

旅行常被描绘为个人成长的工具，旅游业则被描绘为目的地国家和城市的经济发动机；人们倾向于假设旅游业对所有相关方都是好事。

52

What do we learn from some studies about uncontrolled tourism development?

关于不受控制的旅游业发展，我们从一些研究中了解到什么？

  • A)It gives rise to an increase in mass confrontations.它导致大规模冲突增加。
  • B)It incurs local residents' antagonism to tourists.它招致当地居民对游客的敌意。
  • C)It inhibits the steady growth of local economy.它抑制当地经济的稳定增长。
  • D)It brings in a large chunk of mobile population.它带来大量流动人口。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 以偏概全选项 A 说"导致大众对抗增加"，原文虽提到 Recent protests in ports of call like Venice and Barcelona，但只是举例两地抗议，且这是 uncontrolled tourism development 的背景，不是"研究告诉我们的内容"。
B) ✓ 正确预判中"研究记录居民对游客的怨恨"在选项 It incurs local residents' antagonism to tourists 中找到几乎一一对应的同义替换，锁定 B。
  • resulting resentment that local populations direct toward visitors↔It incurs local residents' antagonism to tourists
C) 无中生有选项 C 说"它抑制本地经济稳定增长"，原文仅说收益分配不均（no guarantee that multinational hotel chains will allocate these benefits equitably），并未说旅游会让本地经济"不增长"。
D) 答非所问选项 D 说"它带来大量流动人口"，原文虽提到 over 1.2 billion tourists across international borders annually，但这是"旅游业体量"的描述，不是"失控发展"研究的结论。
P3

Uncontrolled tourism development has become a primary driver of social and environmental disruption. Tourism studies provide much documentation of the many negative social impacts of tourism and resulting resentment that local populations direct toward visitors.

失控的旅游发展已成为社会和环境破坏的首要推手。旅游研究大量记录了旅游业的种种负面社会影响，以及本地居民由此对游客产生的怨恨。

53

Why does the author say local residents of popular destinations often feel frustrated?

作者为什么说热门目的地的当地居民常常感到沮丧？

  • A)They fall victim to social conflicts and environmental disturbances.他们成为社会冲突和环境干扰的受害者。
  • B)They have little opportunity to enjoy themselves on cruise ships.他们几乎没有机会在游轮上享受自己。
  • C)They cannot find employment in multinational hotel chains.他们无法在跨国连锁酒店找到工作。
  • D)They do not think they benefit as much as they deserve.他们认为自己没有获得应得的利益。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴选项 A 说"他们沦为社会冲突和环境干扰的受害者"，原文 P3 谈过失控的社会环境破坏，但 P5 解释 frustrated 的原因是经济收益分配不公，而不是"自己成了社会/环境的受害者"。
B) 张冠李戴选项 B 说"他们没机会在邮轮上玩乐"，但原文 when people stay at large resorts or on cruise ships 谈的是"游客"在邮轮消费，而不是"本地居民"没机会享乐。
C) 与原文相反选项 C 说"他们在跨国酒店连锁找不到工作"，原文 Although it can generate foreign exchange, income and employment 明确说旅游业"能"带来就业，问题在于"分配不公"而非"无工作"。
D) ✓ 正确预判中"没拿到应得份额"在 They do not think they benefit as much as they deserve 落地，锁定 D。
  • the benefits of tourism are not felt locally↔They do not think they benefit as much as they deserve
P5

Residents often become frustrated when the benefits of tourism are not felt locally. Although it can generate foreign exchange, income and employment, there's no guarantee that multinational hotel chains will allocate these benefits equitably among local communities.

当旅游业的好处在本地感受不到时，居民们常常感到失望。尽管旅游业可以带来外汇、收入和就业，但跨国酒店集团并不一定会把这些好处公平地分配给本地社区。

54

How does the author say local residents in destination communities respond to tourism activity?

作者说目的地社区的当地居民如何应对旅游活动？

  • A)They endeavor to adapt to it.他们努力适应它。
  • B)They readily adopt new lifestyles.他们欣然接受新的生活方式。
  • C)They immerse tourists in their culture.他们让游客沉浸在他们的文化中。
  • D)They try to upgrade their business models.他们试图升级自己的商业模式。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判"居民在主动调适"在 They endeavor to adapt to it 落地：endeavor 抓住主动努力的语气，adapt 抓住调适的方向，锁定 A。
  • negotiating new cultural boundaries, class dynamics, service industry roles and lifestyle transformations↔They endeavor to adapt to it
B) 过度推断选项 B 说"他们乐于采纳新生活方式"，但原文 as local residents adopt the behaviors of tourists 与 tourism activity corresponds to increased social problems 并列，说明这种采纳被作者视为社会问题，绝非"乐于"。
C) 答非所问选项 C 说"他们让游客沉浸在本地文化里"，方向完全相反；原文是 being prepared to adapt to local customs and norms, rather than expecting local conditions to adapt to travelers，恰是"游客适应当地"，但本题问的是本地居民的反应，不是游客的反应。
D) 无中生有选项 D 说"他们尝试升级商业模式"，原文虽提到 local entrepreneurs whose businesses provide direct social, environmental, and economic benefits，但这是作者对游客的建议"应该奖励哪类商家"，与居民是否"升级商业模式"无关。
P7

In recent decades, local residents in destination communities also have found themselves negotiating new cultural boundaries, class dynamics, service industry roles and lifestyle transformations.

近几十年来，目的地社区的本地居民发现自己正在为新的文化边界、阶层动态、服务业角色和生活方式转变进行磋商和调适。

55

What can tourists do to exert more positive impacts on the tourist destinations?

游客可以做什么来对旅游目的地产生更积极的影响？

  • A)Show interest in local customs and lifestyles.对当地习俗和生活方式表现出兴趣。
  • B)Seek possibilities to invest in local companies.寻求投资当地企业的可能性。
  • C)Use the services provided by local businesses.使用当地企业提供的服务。
  • D)Give favorable comments about their services.对他们的服务给予好评。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 以偏概全选项 A 说"对当地风俗和生活方式表示兴趣"，原文 being prepared to adapt to local customs and norms 谈的是"准备好去适应"，且该建议是"第一条"，而题干问的是"对目的地施加更多正面影响"，需的是更具体的对策——本地消费。
B) 过度推断选项 B 说"寻找投资本地公司的可能性"，原文谈的是 deserving of market reward 即消费层面的市场奖励，从未要求游客投资本地企业。
C) ✓ 正确预判中两个要素"消费"与"本地企业"在 Use the services provided by local businesses 一一落地，锁定 C。
  • local entrepreneurs whose businesses provide direct social, environmental, and economic benefits for local residents↔Use the services provided by local businesses
  • tourism is a market-based activity and works best when consumers reward better performers↔Use the services provided by local businesses
D) 无中生有选项 D 说"对其服务给好评"，原文 deserving of market reward 的市场奖励是消费行为，没有任何关于点评/留言的字样。
P11

Informed travelers also are better able to distinguish between multinational companies and local entrepreneurs whose businesses provide direct social, environmental, and economic benefits for local residents. Such businesses are in love with the destination and are therefore deserving of market reward.

见多识广的游客也更能区分跨国公司与本地企业家——后者的生意能为本地居民带来直接的社会、环境与经济效益。这类企业对目的地怀有情感，因而值得获得市场回报。
