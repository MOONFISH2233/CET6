范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 176 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Nowadays more and more students are becoming increasingly aware of the importance of developing digital skills. This awareness is well founded, for in an age reshaped by technology, the ability to handle digital tools has become as basic as reading and writing.

There are solid reasons behind this trend. For one thing, digital competence directly boosts academic efficiency. In my own case, learning to use spreadsheets and reference software turned a painful week of manual data sorting into an afternoon's work. Moreover, such skills open doors in the job market. For instance, a classmate who taught herself basic programming stood out among hundreds of applicants and landed an internship at a tech firm. As a result, students who master these tools gain a clear edge both in study and in career.

In conclusion, the growing attention to digital skills reflects a genuine need rather than a passing fashion. Students would be wise to invest time in mastering practical tools instead of waiting until graduation, so that they can thrive in a world that runs on technology.
