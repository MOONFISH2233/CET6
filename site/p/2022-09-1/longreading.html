信息匹配题：先读下面的陈述句，再回到这里找它出自哪一段。每段标了字母（A、B、C…），就是答案要填的；下拉框里也带了每段首句作提示。

A

A) As computer coding has become an increasingly sought-after skill, more K-12 schools are working it into their curriculums. Some states have considered allowing students to forgo(放弃) foreign language for coding classes, despite opposition from educators.

随着计算机编程日益成为备受追捧的技能，越来越多的幼儿园至高中学校将其纳入课程体系。尽管教育工作者表示反对，一些州仍考虑允许学生以编程课替代外语课。

B

B) There's a debate over whether it's appropriate to teach coding in elementary schools, with fierce opinions on each side. When it comes to allowing coding to fill foreign language requirements, though, most educators agree: Coding should be added to curriculums, but not at the expense of foreign language classes.

小学是否适合教授编程一直存在争议，双方的意见都十分强烈。不过，就允许编程课满足外语学习要求而言，大多数教育工作者都认同一点：编程应当被纳入课程体系，但不应以牺牲外语课为代价。

C

C) The idea is that computer programming is a language, allowing people to communicate with machines and programs. It's the language of the 21st century and more valuable than a natural language, some advocates argue. The computer science field is growing faster than schools can keep up because of budget constraints and a lack of skills training for teachers.

这种观点认为，计算机编程是一种语言，使人们能够与机器和程序交流。一些倡导者认为，这是21世纪的语言，比自然语言更有价值。由于预算限制以及教师缺乏技能培训，计算机科学领域的发展速度快于学校所能跟上的速度。

D

D) According to the 2016 U.S. News/Raytheon STEM Index, computer science jobs have helped boost wages in the U.S., and computer-related jobs hold the top seven positions in STEM fields for highest number of workers. Foreign language interest, on the other hand, is declining for the first time since 1995. The number of higher education language enrollments declined between 2009 and 2013 by more than 111,000 spots, according to the Modern Language Association of America.

根据《美国新闻与世界报道》/雷神公司的《2016年美国STEM指数》，计算机科学相关工作帮助推动了美国工资水平的提高，而在STEM领域中，按从业人员数量计算，计算机相关工作占据了排名最高的七个职位。另一方面，自1995年以来，人们对外语的兴趣首次出现下降。据美国现代语言协会统计，2009年至2013年间，高等教育阶段的语言课程注册人数减少了11.1万多人。

E

E) "I think the opportunity to give people a choice is important," says Florida state Senator Jeremy Ring, who introduced a bill last year that would allow Florida students to choose between foreign language and coding classes for the purpose of university admissions requirements, "I think if you're going to give two years of language in high school, you might as well do computer coding."

佛罗里达州参议员杰里米·林说：“我认为，给人们提供选择的机会很重要。”他去年提出一项法案，允许佛罗里达州学生在外语课和编程课之间进行选择，以满足大学招生要求。“我认为，如果你打算要求学生在高中学习两年语言，那还不如学习计算机编程。”

F

F) The Florida bill died this year after passing 35:5 in the state Senate when the full Legislature failed to take action. It would have been the first state to try this initiative. Ring says that although he will be out of office, an identical bill will be reintroduced within the next year by others on his behalf. "In the speech I gave on the Senate floor, I said, 'We can be the first state to do this, or we can be the 50th state to do it. It's our choice. It's going to happen,'" Ring says.

佛罗里达州的法案在州参议院以35比5的结果获得通过后，因全体州议会未采取行动而于今年夭折。它本来会成为首个尝试这一举措的州。林说，尽管他将离任，但其他人会代表他在未来一年内重新提出一项内容相同的法案。林说：“在我于参议院发表的演讲中，我说：‘我们可以成为这样做的第一个州，也可以成为第五十个州。这是我们的选择。这件事终将发生。’”

G

G) A Kentucky bill similar to the one in Florida was met with complaints from educators, and was then amended to promote computer science education initiatives with no mention of foreign language requirements. Instead, the state will provide support for higher quality certified teachers for programming classes. Under the Washington bill, public universities would accept two years of computer science classes in place of two years of foreign language for admission purposes. A report detailing the opinions of state university officials is due to the Legislature by November 2017.

一项与佛罗里达州法案类似的肯塔基州法案遭到教育工作者的投诉，随后被修改为推动计算机科学教育举措，不再提及外语要求。相反，该州将为编程课程提供支持，以聘用质量更高、经过认证的教师。根据华盛顿州的法案，公立大学在招生时将接受两年计算机科学课程，以替代两年外语课程。州立大学官员意见的详细报告须在2017年11月前提交给州议会。

H

H) Texas passed a bill in 2013 that allows students to substitute computer coding only after they have attempted and performed poorly in a foreign language class. Srini Mandyam, CTO and co-founder of kid-friendly instructional coding company Tynker, believes allowing students to forgo foreign language because they struggle with it is unproductive because every subject, whether art, math or language, is a significant contribution to a well-rounded existence. "Many students don't fare well with algebra but we never discuss eliminating it or … say chemistry is now counted as an algebra class," he said via email. "We teach algebra because it's important and we should teach foreign language and coding for the same reason. Exposure to a wide breadth of subjects and material results in well-rounded students who are able to make informed decisions … about what they want to pursue."

得克萨斯州于2013年通过一项法案，允许学生只有在尝试学习外语课程且成绩不佳之后，才能用计算机编程替代外语课程。面向儿童的编程教学公司Tynker的首席技术官兼联合创始人斯里尼·曼迪亚姆认为，允许学生因为外语学习困难而放弃外语是没有成效的，因为无论是艺术、数学还是语言，每一门学科都对全面发展作出了重要贡献。他在电子邮件中说：“许多学生代数成绩不好，但我们从未讨论过取消代数，也不会说化学课现在可以算作代数课。我们教授代数，是因为它很重要；出于同样的原因，我们也应当教授外语和编程。接触广泛的学科和材料，能够培养全面发展的学生，使他们能够就自己想要学习的方向作出明智的决定。”

I

I) Computer science courses already fulfill a math or science high school graduation requirement in 28 states and the District of Columbia, up from only 12 states in 2013. And while advocates of the bills say they should count as foreign language instead, opponents stress the importance of balancing computer and foreign language skills.

在28个州和哥伦比亚特区，计算机科学课程已经可以满足高中毕业所需的数学或科学课程要求，而2013年时这一数字还只有12个州。法案的倡导者认为，计算机科学课程应当改为计入外语要求，但反对者强调，平衡计算机技能与外语技能十分重要。

J

J) Studies show that bilingualism(双语) correlates with cognitive development, intelligence, memory and problem solving abilities, according to the American Council on the Teaching of Foreign Languages. A 2007 study showed that foreign language students outperformed their non-foreign language peers on standardized tests after only two to three years of study. And while a 2014 report from German and American universities suggests that programmers are using language (but not mathematical) regions of the brain when understanding code, critics remain wary. They say that regardless of cognitive functions, being monolingual is a disadvantage in the increasingly international economy, even if English has become the de facto(事实上的) language of business.

根据美国外语教学委员会的说法，研究表明，双语能力与认知发展、智力、记忆力和解决问题的能力相关。一项2007年的研究显示，外语学习者只经过两到三年的学习，在标准化考试中的表现就超过了未学习外语的同龄人。尽管德国和美国高校于2014年发布的一份报告表明，程序员在理解代码时使用的是大脑中与语言有关的区域，而不是与数学有关的区域，但批评者仍持谨慎态度。他们认为，无论认知功能如何，在日益国际化的经济环境中，只会一种语言都是一种劣势，即使英语已经成为事实上的商业语言。

K

K) "Our world is shrinking but its problems are really growing," says ACTFL National Language Teacher of the Year Ted Zarrow, who teaches high school Latin in Westwood, Massachusetts, and has also studied Spanish, French, German, Italian and Greek. "We need to find a way to put ourselves at the global table and to treat each other with mutual respect. And learning languages allows us to do that because language is not part of culture, language is culture."

在马萨诸塞州韦斯特伍德教授高中拉丁语、同时还学习过西班牙语、法语、德语、意大利语和希腊语的美国外语教学委员会年度语言教师特德·扎罗说：“我们的世界正在缩小，但其中的问题却在不断增加。我们需要找到一种方式，让自己参与全球事务，并以相互尊重的态度对待彼此。学习语言能够帮助我们做到这一点，因为语言不是文化的一部分，语言就是文化。”

L

L) Even with the benefits and skill sets languages provide, recruiters and employers value computer skills more. According to the National Association of Colleges and Employers' 2016 report, study abroad and foreign language fluency were not very influential in the employee hiring processes, but 55 percent of employers looked for computer skills on applicants' resumes. However, although 2016 computer science graduates can expect to make the second highest starting salary compared with other jobs this year, the Bureau of Labor predicts the demand for computer programmers will decrease 8 percent or by 26,500 jobs by 2024.

尽管语言能够带来诸多益处和技能组合，但招聘人员和雇主更加看重计算机技能。根据美国大学与雇主协会的《2016年报告》，留学经历和外语流利程度在员工招聘过程中并没有产生很大影响，但55%的雇主会在求职者的简历中寻找计算机技能。然而，尽管2016年的计算机科学毕业生预计将获得本年度各类工作中第二高的起薪，美国劳工统计局预测，到2024年，对计算机程序员的需求将下降8%，即减少26,500个职位。

M

M) Ring says foreign language skills are important, but expresses doubt that school districts could work both coding and language into their curriculum in a significant way because they lack the time in the school day. "Nothing against language," he says. "I just think it's something you have to start early and not just have something that you do for a couple of years in high school," he says.

林说，外语技能很重要，但他怀疑学区能否在课程中以充分的方式同时安排编程和语言学习，因为学校每天的教学时间有限。他说：“我并不是反对语言。我只是认为，语言学习必须尽早开始，而不应只是在高中学习几年。”

N

N) Zarrow agrees that foreign language education should begin earlier, but says it is possible to work both computer programming and foreign language learning into schools evenly. He suggests an immersive, dual language program where students spend half the day in English and half the day in another language, as several schools around the country have successfully implemented. "The study of language fosters a respect for diversity, a respect for ethnicity and really a respect for language," Zarrow says.

扎罗同意外语教育应当更早开始，但他说，学校完全可以均衡地安排计算机编程和外语学习。他建议采用沉浸式双语项目，让学生半天使用英语学习，半天使用另一种语言学习；美国各地已有几所学校成功实施了这种项目。扎罗说：“语言学习培养了对多样性的尊重、对族群的尊重，以及真正对语言的尊重。”

O

O) Though the benefits of computer programming skills are vast, foreign language and coding experts agree that computer science should be negotiated into curriculums rather than replacing foreign language outright. Mandyam says the two skill sets are essential but unrelated. "Coding is an incredibly important 21st century skill for our kids to learn, and that's why we spend so much time trying to teach it," Mandyam says via email. "But I believe it is the same as or even really comparable to learning a foreign language. It would be a shame to lose something so important for the sake of adding something else, even something as important as coding. Clearly, education leaders must figure out a way to teach both."

尽管计算机编程技能的益处十分广泛，外语和编程领域的专家都同意，计算机科学应当被纳入课程体系，而不是彻底取代外语。曼迪亚姆说，这两种技能组合都不可或缺，但彼此并无关联。他在电子邮件中说：“编程是孩子们在21世纪必须学习的一项极其重要的技能，这就是我们花费大量时间努力教授编程的原因。但我认为，编程与学习外语同等重要，或者说二者确实具有可比性。为了增加某种东西，即使这种东西像编程一样重要，而失去另一种同样重要的东西，将是一件令人遗憾的事。显然，教育领导者必须找到同时教授这两种技能的方法。”

One study showed that foreign language learning improved students' academic performance.

一项研究表明,外语学习提高了学生的学业成绩。

答案：J 段
锚点一项研究表明外语学习提升学业表现
定位J 段 A 2007 study showed that foreign language students outperformed their non-foreign language peers on standardized tests after only two to three years of study. 2007 年的一项研究表明，外语学生仅在两到三年的学习后，就在标准化考试中胜过非外语学生。
改写One study showed↔A 2007 study showed 通称研究→具体年份研究 foreign language learning improved students' academic performance↔foreign language students outperformed their non-foreign language peers on standardized tests after only two to three years of study “提升学业表现” = “在标准化考试中胜出”
辨邻L L 段的 National Association of Colleges and Employers' 2016 report 讨论的是招聘中雇主对计算机技能的偏好，并非外语学习对学业表现的影响。 study abroad and foreign language fluency were not very influential in the employee hiring processes

Being short of funding and qualified teachers, schools lag behind the fast developing computer science field.

由于缺乏资金和合格的教师,学校落后于快速发展的计算机科学领域。

答案：C 段
锚点学校因缺钱缺合格教师而落后于计算机科学
定位C 段 The computer science field is growing faster than schools can keep up because of budget constraints and a lack of skills training for teachers. 由于预算紧张和教师技能培训不足，计算机科学领域的发展速度超过了学校所能跟上的速度。
改写Being short of funding and qualified teachers↔budget constraints and a lack of skills training for teachers “缺资金 + 缺合格教师”同义替换 schools lag behind the fast developing computer science field↔The computer science field is growing faster than schools can keep up “落后于” = “跟不上”的句式重构
辨邻G G 段 higher quality certified teachers for programming classes 讲的是某州出面保障教师，对应 39 题，而不是 44 题“因缺资金缺教师而落后”的现状陈述。 the state will provide support for higher quality certified teachers for programming classes

A distinguished high school language teacher also believes it is advisable to start learning a foreign language at an earlier age.

一位杰出的高中语言教师也认为更早开始学习外语是明智的。

答案：N 段
锚点一位知名高中语言教师认同外语应更早学
定位N 段 Zarrow agrees that foreign language education should begin earlier, but says it is possible to work both computer programming and foreign language learning into schools evenly. Zarrow 同意外语教育应更早开始，但表示可以把编程与外语学习都均衡地纳入学校课程。
改写A distinguished high school language teacher↔Zarrow agrees that foreign language education should begin earlier “知名高中语言教师” = Zarrow（ACTFL 年度教师） also believes it is advisable to start learning a foreign language at an earlier age↔Zarrow agrees that foreign language education should begin earlier “also believes” = “agrees” + “earlier age” = “begin earlier”
