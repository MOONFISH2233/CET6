原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Many see friendships as a comfort blanket: a shoulder to cry on, a reliable soul to confide in. However, more often than not, in our eagerness to seek individual success, we lose friends along the way and that has become part of the process.

许多人把友谊视为一条安慰毯：在你哭泣时可以依靠的肩膀，以及一个值得信赖、可以倾诉心事的人。然而，更多时候，我们急于追求个人成功，在前进的路上失去了朋友，而这已经成为这一过程的一部分。

P2

The chaos and never-ending turmoil that accompanies everyday life is an aggressive obstacle in the pursuit of stable friendships. As students, we use this excuse to shrug off losing people who we once thought to be loved ones.

伴随日常生活而来的混乱与永无止境的动荡，是追求稳定友谊的巨大障碍。作为学生，我们用这个借口来淡然接受那些我们曾经以为是亲爱的人的离去。

P3

Instead of viewing our lives as a race, where everyone is on their own path to some form of success, we must relearn to value the connections we make along the track. We must reevaluate the harm of radical, individual freedom.

我们不应把生活看作一场赛跑，仿佛每个人都在各自的道路上奔向某种形式的成功；相反，我们必须重新学会珍视自己在这条道路上建立的联系。我们必须重新评估激进的个人自由所带来的伤害。

P4

Radical individualism elevates the freedom of the individual over the collective. It argues, for instance, that universal healthcare, an example of collectivism, threatened the dream of many Americans.

激进的个人主义将个人的自由置于集体之上。例如，它认为全民医疗保健这一集体主义的体现威胁到了许多美国人的梦想。

P5

Many of us have fallen victim to an individualistic culture. Society tends to celebrate personal achievement and commend it based on the assumption that winning or succeeding is a consequence of working harder than the next person.

我们中的许多人都成了个人主义文化的受害者。社会往往赞美个人成就，并基于这样一种假设对其表示肯定：获胜或取得成功，是因为比下一个人更加努力。

P6

As a society, we idolize the concept of "losing friends on the road to success". I remember hearing in high school, "Don't worry about losing friendships, just focus on study and getting into college. That's all that will matter in the long run," a sentiment that is not unique to my experience. It's preached in many success stories, and it's embedded in the way we see growth. Motivational speakers will push their audience to be complacent to the inevitable end of losing friends. Spend too much time with friends? You're losing time better utilized on your achievements. Invest time in your relationships? Invest time in your work instead.

作为一个社会，我们把“在通往成功的路上失去朋友”这一观念奉为圭臬。我记得高中时听人说过：“别担心失去友谊，只管专心学习并考上大学。从长远来看，真正重要的就只有这些。”这种看法并非我个人经历所独有。许多成功故事都在宣扬这一点，而它也根植于我们看待成长的方式之中。励志演讲者会鼓动听众坦然接受失去朋友这一不可避免的结局。花太多时间和朋友相处？那你就是在浪费本可以更好地用于成就自己的时间。把时间投入到人际关系中？不如把时间投入到工作中。

P7

This constant need to succeed has blinded us from seeing through the whirlwind of change that accompanies our everyday lives. We are expected to tangibly succeed, earn awards, get higher positions, earn better grades. In order to succeed, we lose friends, and that has sadly become normalized.

这种持续不断的成功需求使我们无法看清伴随日常生活而来的变动风暴。人们期待我们取得看得见的成功、赢得奖项、获得更高的职位、取得更好的成绩。为了成功，我们失去了朋友，而这令人遗憾地已经被正常化了。

P8

We cannot be complacent with losing friendships because they are our anchors. Yes, pursue success. Challenge yourself. Push limits. But contrary to all the self-proclaimed success experts, doing it alone does not make you more successful or any better than someone who achieved it with the help of others.

我们不能甘于接受友谊的失去，因为友谊是我们的锚。是的，去追求成功。挑战自己。突破极限。但与那些自称成功专家的人所说的相反，独自完成并不会让你更加成功，也不会让你比那些在他人帮助下取得成功的人更优秀。

P9

Put effort into fostering healthy relationships, and maintaining them through your busy schedule that seems like it leaves no time for anyone else. Although we may be living in a society that treasures the independent pursuit of achievement, having people to support and love you on the way is far more important than any end result.

努力培养健康的人际关系，并在那种似乎没有时间留给任何其他人的忙碌日程中维持这些关系。尽管我们可能生活在一个重视独立追求成就的社会中，但在前进的路上有人支持你、爱你，远比任何最终结果都重要。

解析逐题详解

46

What often happens when people are eager to pursue individual success?

当人们急于追求个人成功时,常常会发生什么?

  • A)They lack a shoulder to cry on.他们缺少一个可以倾诉的肩膀。
  • B)They have no soul to confide in.他们没有可以吐露心声的知己。
  • C)They cannot find reliable friends.他们找不到可靠的朋友。
  • D)They ignore their ties with friends.他们忽视了与朋友的联系。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 答非所问They lack a shoulder to cry on. 把 a shoulder to cry on 这一对友谊功能的比喻直接当作 "人们当下缺什么" 的结论，但原文这一句是描述 "许多人对友谊的看法"，不是 "人们追求个人成功时发生的结果"。
B) 答非所问They have no soul to confide in. 与 A 同套路，把 a reliable soul to confide in 这一对友谊作用的描绘当作题干所问的 "结果"，但原文这是 "友谊在人们眼中的样子"，不是 "追求成功时发生的事"。
C) 偷换概念They cannot find reliable friends. 说 "找不到可靠朋友"，但原文 we lose friends 强调的是 "主动放弃/丢失现有朋友"，与 "找不到" 是两种不同动作；原文从未说过 "找不到"。
D) ✓ 正确在四项中，只有 They ignore their ties with friends. 与预判 "失去/疏远朋友" 在主谓宾三层都吻合，故选 D。
  • we lose friends along the way↔They ignore their ties with friends.
P1

However, more often than not, in our eagerness to seek individual success, we lose friends along the way and that has become part of the process.

然而，多数情况下，在我们急于追求个人成功的过程中，我们沿途失去了朋友，而这已经成为这一过程的一部分。

47

What does the author advise people to do in their pursuit of radical individualism?

作者建议人们在追求激进个人主义时做什么?

  • A)Reexamine its detrimental effects.重新审视其有害影响。
  • B)Shrug off the loss of their loved ones.对失去所爱之人不以为意。
  • C)Avoid the chaos and turmoil involved.避免其中涉及的混乱和动荡。
  • D)Remove aggressive obstacles in the way.清除道路上咄咄逼人的障碍。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确在四项中，只有 Reexamine its detrimental effects. 同时覆盖了 "再审视" 与 "危害" 两个语义成分，故选 A。
  • reevaluate the harm of radical, individual freedom↔Reexamine its detrimental effects.
B) 与原文相反Shrug off the loss of their loved ones. 是 we use this excuse to shrug off losing people who we once thought to be loved ones 里被作者批判的 "错误做法"，并非作者建议。
C) 无中生有Avoid the chaos and turmoil involved. 把 The chaos and never-ending turmoil that accompanies everyday life 这一对生活状态的描述当作作者建议，但原文只是把它列为 "我们丢友谊的借口"，作者既未说要避免也未说要拥抱。
D) 偷换概念Remove aggressive obstacles in the way. 把 an aggressive obstacle in the pursuit of stable friendships 的名词搭配换皮成 "清除前进路上的强劲障碍"，但作者要清除的不是抽象意义上的 "前进路上的障碍"——他要重新评估的是极端个人主义之害。
P3

We must reevaluate the harm of radical, individual freedom.

我们必须重新评估极端个人自由的危害。

48

What is American society's assumption of personal achievement?

美国社会对个人成就的假设是什么?

  • A)It is to be duly celebrated and highly commended.它应当得到适当的庆祝和高度的赞扬。
  • B)It is a sure way to fulfill a person's dream.它是实现个人梦想的可靠途径。
  • C)It revolves around an individualistic culture.它围绕个人主义文化展开。
  • D)It reflects a person's exceptional endeavor.它反映了一个人非凡的努力。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 答非所问It is to be duly celebrated and highly commended. 把 Society tends to celebrate personal achievement and commend it 的 "社会的做法" 直接当成 "对个人成就的预设"，但题干问的是 the assumption 这个预设本身的内容，不是社会怎么做。
B) 无中生有It is a sure way to fulfill a person's dream. 说 "个人成就是实现梦想的可靠途径"，原文从未将个人成就与 "实现梦想" 直接挂钩；原文涉及 dream 只出现在 threatened the dream of many Americans 中，且是普及医保的对立面，与个人成就的预设无关。
C) 偷换概念It revolves around an individualistic culture. 把 Many of us have fallen victim to an individualistic culture 的上位归类（属于个人主义文化）当作预设本身，但题干问的是 "对个人成就所持的预设"，不是 "个人成就属于哪种文化"。
D) ✓ 正确四项中只有 It reflects a person's exceptional endeavor. 准确捕捉 "成功背后是个人非凡努力" 这一预设，故选 D。
  • winning or succeeding is a consequence of working harder than the next person↔It reflects a person's exceptional endeavor.
P5

Society tends to celebrate personal achievement and commend it based on the assumption that winning or succeeding is a consequence of working harder than the next person.

社会倾向于赞美个人成就，并基于一种预设来推崇它，这种预设是：获胜或成功是比别人更努力工作的结果。

49

What is preached in many success stories?

许多成功故事中宣扬的是什么?

  • A)Attaching importance to academic gains on the road to success.重视通往成功道路上的学业收获。
  • B)Investing one's time in work instead of connecting with friends.把时间投入工作而不是与朋友交往。
  • C)Trying to strengthen friendships in one's pursuit of personal success.在追求个人成功的过程中努力加强友谊。
  • D)Excelling in academic performance to get into a prestigious college.在学业表现上出类拔萃以进入名牌大学。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴Attaching importance to academic gains on the road to success. 来自 Don't worry about losing friendships, just focus on study and getting into college，但这是 "高中时听到的话"（作者的个人经历），紧接其后才提到 "a sentiment that is not unique to my experience. It's preached in many success stories"——题干问的是 "在 many success stories 中被宣扬的"，即作者随后展开的演讲家观点，不是高中老师的话。
B) ✓ 正确四项中只有 Investing one's time in work instead of connecting with friends. 同时覆盖 "投入工作" 与 "放弃朋友联系" 两层，故选 B。
  • Invest time in your relationships? Invest time in your work instead.↔Investing one's time in work instead of connecting with friends.
C) 与原文相反Trying to strengthen friendships in one's pursuit of personal success. 是作者自己在 P8 P9 提出的反向建议，不是 success stories 所宣扬的内容；many success stories 宣扬的恰恰是放弃友谊。
D) 以偏概全Excelling in academic performance to get into a prestigious college. 同样源自 focus on study and getting into college 这句高中老师的话，且把 college 升级为 prestigious college（名校）属于无中生有；不属于 many success stories 所宣扬的内容。
P6

Motivational speakers will push their audience to be complacent to the inevitable end of losing friends. Spend too much time with friends? You're losing time better utilized on your achievements. Invest time in your relationships? Invest time in your work instead.

励志演讲者会推动听众坦然接受 "失去朋友" 这一不可避免的结局。和朋友花太多时间？你正在浪费本可用于个人成就的时间。把时间投入到人际关系上？不如把时间投入到工作上。

50

What can we infer from the passage regarding the pursuit of success?

从文章中我们可以推断出关于追求成功的什么?

  • A)No one can succeed without pushing limits.没有人能不突破极限而取得成功。
  • B)No one can afford to neglect close friends.没有人能够承受忽视亲密朋友的后果。
  • C)Collectivism is superior to individualism.集体主义优于个人主义。
  • D)Success experts often lead people to ruin.成功学专家常常把人们引向毁灭。
解析
B（推断题）
A) 过度推断No one can succeed without pushing limits. 借 Push limits 一词夸大为 "没有人不突破极限就能成功"，但原文仅说 "是的，去突破极限" 作为肯定后退一步的让步话，并未声明 "不突破极限就不能成功"。
B) ✓ 正确在四项中，只有 No one can afford to neglect close friends. 是从 P8 P9 两处强表态合理延伸一步得到的结论，故选 B。
  • We cannot be complacent with losing friendships because they are our anchors.↔No one can afford to neglect close friends.
  • having people to support and love you on the way is far more important than any end result↔No one can afford to neglect close friends.
C) 过度推断Collectivism is superior to individualism. 把 Radical individualism elevates the freedom of the individual over the collective 与 universal healthcare, an example of collectivism 加工成 "集体主义比个人主义优越" 的全称比较，但原文只反对 "极端" 个人主义，并未抬高集体主义本身。
D) 过度推断Success experts often lead people to ruin. 来自 contrary to all the self-proclaimed success experts，作者只是 "与所谓的成功专家相反"，并未宣称 "成功专家把人引向毁灭"——ruin 一词是文外强化。
P8

We cannot be complacent with losing friendships because they are our anchors. Yes, pursue success. Challenge yourself. Push limits. But contrary to all the self-proclaimed success experts, doing it alone does not make you more successful or any better than someone who achieved it with the help of others.

我们不能对失去友谊袖手旁观，因为朋友是我们的锚。是的，去追求成功，挑战自己，突破极限。但与所有自封的成功专家所言相反，独自一人完成它，并不会让你更成功，也并不比那些借助他人之力成功的人更高一筹。
