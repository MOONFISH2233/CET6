范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 168 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

While striving for the Chinese Dream, young people enjoy more opportunities to realize their self-worth. As the nation advances, doors that were once closed are swinging open for the younger generation. Specifically, the rapid growth of technology, education and entrepreneurship offers a broad stage on which youth can turn their talents into real contributions.

These opportunities become meaningful only when young people seize them actively. Take my cousin as an example: instead of chasing a comfortable office job, she founded a start-up that brings online courses to rural children. For instance, thousands of students now learn from teachers they could never have met, and she has found genuine value in her work. In this way, personal ambition and national progress reinforce each other.

In a word, the Chinese Dream is not a distant slogan but a living platform that lets every young person shine. As long as we combine individual effort with the tide of the times, we can both realize our self-worth and push the country forward.
