原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

During his acceptance speech for the Nobel Peace Prize, Martin Luther King Jr. said, "I believe that unarmed truth and unconditional love will have the final word in reality."

马丁·路德·金在诺贝尔和平奖获奖演说中说：“我相信，手无寸铁的真理和无条件的爱终将在现实中占据主导地位。”

P2

More than five decades later, our society is still struggling to separate truth from fantasy, fact from fiction. From disputes over the climate change to debates about how slavery is taught, America is at a crossroads. And the battle for equity is at the heart of these vital conversations.

五十多年过去了，我们的社会仍在努力将真相与幻想、事实与虚构区分开来。从围绕气候变化的争议，到关于如何教授奴隶制历史的辩论，美国正处在十字路口。而争取公平是这些重要对话的核心。

P3

Study after study shows that inequity has had appalling consequences. A 2020 report by Citi indicated that "Black students could have increased their lifetime incomes $90 – $113 billion" if equity issues related to higher education had been adequately addressed over the past two decades.

一项又一项研究表明，不平等已经造成了骇人的后果。花旗集团在2020年的一份报告中指出，如果过去二十年间与高等教育相关的公平问题得到充分解决，“黑人学生一生的收入可能会增加900亿至1130亿美元”。

P4

This statistic represents a flood of issues we've failed to confront as a nation. It also highlights one truth: We're missing out on valuable opportunities to bridge the differences that divide us. However, by honoring King's legacy, we can get our country back on the path toward true equity.

这一统计数字反映出一系列我们作为一个国家未能面对的问题。它还凸显了一个事实：我们错失了弥合分歧、跨越隔阂的宝贵机会。然而，通过继承金的遗志，我们可以让国家重新走上实现真正公平的道路。

P5

Students — and adults for that matter — simply cannot understand the conflicts we have today without understanding what caused them in the first place. That means we have a responsibility to help them make connections between US history and current events. It also means giving them the tools they need to address inequity and bias in their own lives. Adherence to these principles, along with a commitment to truthful dialogue, can help every student better appreciate how far we've come and more clearly see the roads we have yet to travel.

学生，以及成年人，也无法在不了解当初起因的情况下，真正理解我们今天所面临的冲突。这意味着我们有责任帮助他们建立美国历史与当今事件之间的联系。这也意味着要为他们提供所需的工具，使他们能够处理自己生活中的不平等与偏见。坚持这些原则，并致力于开展真实的对话，可以帮助每一名学生更好地认识我们已经走了多远，也更清楚地看见我们仍有待走过的道路。

P6

With more truthful analysis of the need for more qualified, diverse educators, we can improve our recruiting, training, and retention strategies for schools across the country.

通过更加真实地分析对更多合格且多元化教育工作者的需求，我们可以改进全国各地学校的招聘、培训和留任策略。

P7

Unfortunately, our deeply rooted divisions are delaying these advancements. A recent survey found that 77 percent of Americans believe the nation is "more divided than ever before". But reports of increased polarization shouldn't persuade us to look away from one another. Instead, they should compel us to address our shared issues head-on.

遗憾的是，我们根深蒂固的分歧正在延缓这些进步。最近的一项调查发现，77%的美国人认为这个国家“比以往任何时候都更加分裂”。但关于两极分化加剧的报告不应使我们选择彼此回避。相反，这些报告应促使我们正面解决共同面临的问题。

P8

We are not, as some would suggest, living in a "post-racial society". We simply cannot afford to keep our heads buried in the sand when it comes to racial inequity. Unless we get honest about intolerance, bias, and how these social diseases impact the ways we treat one another, injustice will continue to haunt us. But our children and our grandchildren deserve better.

正如一些人所说的那样，我们并不是生活在一个“后种族社会”中。涉及种族不平等时，我们根本无法继续把头埋在沙子里。除非我们诚实面对不宽容、偏见，以及这些社会痼疾如何影响我们彼此对待的方式，否则不公正将继续纠缠着我们。但我们的子孙后代值得拥有更好的未来。

P9

On this Martin Luther King Day and beyond, let's commit to working together to disrupt the status quo. Let's continue to build a better, more inclusive nation; and in the words of Martin Luther King Jr., "A society at peace with itself, a society that can live with its conscience."

在这个马丁·路德·金纪念日以及今后的日子里，让我们承诺共同努力，打破现状。让我们继续建设一个更美好、更包容的国家；用马丁·路德·金的话说，建设“一个与自身和平相处、能够与自己的良知共存的社会”。

解析逐题详解

51

What does the author think about today's America?

作者对当今美国有何看法?

  • A)It is struggling to transform fiction into reality.美国正努力将虚构变为现实。
  • B)It has been the focus of many vital conversations.美国一直是许多重要对话的焦点。
  • C)It still has a long way to go before equity can be truly realized.美国在真正实现公平之前还有很长的路要走。
  • D)It has got to a point where people disagree on nearly every issue.美国已经到了人们在几乎每个问题上都存在分歧的地步。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反struggling to transform fiction into reality 的字面义是「努力把虚构变成现实」，与 struggling to separate truth from fantasy（努力分清真相与幻象）方向相反——原文是要分开两者，不是把虚构变成现实。
B) 张冠李戴It has been the focus of many vital conversations 把美国当成「对话的焦点」；但 the battle for equity is at the heart of these vital conversations 里说的是「公平之战是这些对话的核心」，焦点是 equity，不是美国。
C) ✓ 正确预判「离真正公平还远」与 still has a long way to go before equity can be truly realized 一一对应，且呼应 still struggling——锁定 C。
  • get our country back on the path toward true equity↔still has a long way to go before equity can be truly realized
  • our society is still struggling to separate truth from fantasy↔still has a long way to go
D) 以偏概全disagree on nearly every issue 把分歧泛化到「几乎每一个议题」；原文只举了 climate change 与 how slavery is taught 等具体议题，且作者关心的核心是 equity，没有「几乎每个议题都有分歧」的论断。
P4

However, by honoring King's legacy, we can get our country back on the path toward true equity.

然而，只要传承（马丁·路德·）金的精神遗产，我们就能让国家重新回到通向真正公平的道路上。

52

What do we learn from the passage about black students in the past 20 years?

从文章中我们了解到过去20年中黑人学生的什么情况?

  • A)They have been unfairly treated regarding higher education.他们在高等教育方面受到了不公平的对待。
  • B)They have been battling hard to address equity issues.他们一直在努力奋斗以解决公平问题。
  • C)They have boosted their lifetime incomes by billions of dollars.他们使自己的终身收入增加了数十亿美元。
  • D)They have come to see the consequences of racial discrimination.他们已经认识到种族歧视的后果。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判「过去二十年高等教育对黑人学生不公」与 unfairly treated regarding higher education 一一对应——锁定 A。
  • if equity issues related to higher education had been adequately addressed over the past two decades↔unfairly treated regarding higher education
  • Black students could have increased their lifetime incomes $90 – $113 billion↔They have been unfairly treated
B) 无中生有battling hard to address equity issues 把「被动遭遇不公」换成「主动奋力解决公平问题」；原文只说公平问题如果被妥善处理黑人学生本可多挣钱，并未说他们自己在奋斗解决。
C) 与原文相反They have boosted their lifetime incomes by billions of dollars 用现在完成时陈述「已经多挣了」；原文是 could have increased 虚拟语气，含义是「本可以但实际没有」——选项把虚拟当作了现实。
D) 偷换概念come to see the consequences of racial discrimination 把话题偷换成「看清种族歧视的后果」；原文谈的是高等教育公平问题与黑人学生的收入损失，并未提及他们「认识到歧视后果」这一心理变化。
P3

A 2020 report by Citi indicated that "Black students could have increased their lifetime incomes $90 – $113 billion" if equity issues related to higher education had been adequately addressed over the past two decades.

花旗集团 2020 年的一份报告指出：如果过去二十年里高等教育中的公平问题得到了妥善处理，黑人学生本可以多挣 900 亿到 1130 亿美元的终身收入。

53

How can Americans bridge the differences that divide them?

美国人如何能够弥合分裂他们的分歧?

  • A)By dealing with the thorny issues confronting them.通过处理他们面临的棘手问题。
  • B)By seizing every chance to enhance racial harmony.通过抓住每个机会来增进种族和谐。
  • C)By continuing to pursue Martin Luther King Jr.'s cause.通过继续追求Martin Luther King Jr.的事业。
  • D)By striving to identify exceedingly valuable opportunities.通过努力识别极其宝贵的机会。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 答非所问dealing with the thorny issues confronting them 描述的是「直面棘手问题」。原文 address our shared issues head-on 这一表述是作者对 polarization 的回应，并非他给出的「弥合分歧」路径；定位句 P4 给出的路径只有 honoring King's legacy。
B) 无中生有seizing every chance to enhance racial harmony 把答案拉到「抓住一切机会促进种族和谐」。原文只谈 equity、bridge differences、honor King's legacy，没有 racial harmony 的提法，也没有 every chance 这种全称量化。
C) ✓ 正确预判「继承金的精神遗产」与 By continuing to pursue Martin Luther King Jr.'s cause 一一对应——锁定 C。
  • by honoring King's legacy↔continuing to pursue Martin Luther King Jr.'s cause
  • in the words of Martin Luther King Jr., "A society at peace with itself, a society that can live with its conscience."↔Martin Luther King Jr.'s cause
D) 偷换概念striving to identify exceedingly valuable opportunities 截取了 missing out on valuable opportunities 中的 valuable opportunities，但原文的语义是「我们正在错失机会」，并非「努力识别极有价值的机会」；选项把「错过」扭成「努力寻找」。
P4

However, by honoring King's legacy, we can get our country back on the path toward true equity.

然而，只要传承（马丁·路德·）金的精神遗产，我们就能让国家重新回到通向真正公平的道路上。

54

How do most Americans view America as a nation according to a recent survey?

根据最近的一项调查,大多数美国人如何看待美国这个国家?

  • A)It is compelled to address its historical social issues.美国被迫解决其历史性的社会问题。
  • B)It is delaying advancements toward racial equality.美国正在推迟朝着种族平等的进步。
  • C)It has kept itself from polarization.美国使自己免于两极分化。
  • D)It has become increasingly split.美国已经变得越来越分裂。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴compelled to address its historical social issues 借用了 they should compel us to address our shared issues head-on 的措辞，但那句话是作者对 polarization 报告的回应，不是 most Americans 在调查中表达的看法；本题问的是 most Americans 的看法。
B) 张冠李戴delaying advancements toward racial equality 借用 our deeply rooted divisions are delaying these advancements，但原文里拖延进步的主语是 our deeply rooted divisions（根深蒂固的分裂），且这是作者的判断而非调查结论；本题问的是调查里美国人怎么看美国。
C) 与原文相反has kept itself from polarization 意为「避免了两极分化」。原文 reports of increased polarization 明确说「两极分化在加剧」，且 77% 的美国人认为国家更分裂——选项与调查结论方向相反。
D) ✓ 正确预判「国家越来越分裂」与 has become increasingly split 一一对应——锁定 D。
  • more divided than ever before↔increasingly split
  • 77 percent of Americans believe↔It has become increasingly split
P7

A recent survey found that 77 percent of Americans believe the nation is "more divided than ever before".

一项近期调查发现，77% 的美国人认为这个国家「前所未有地分裂」。

55

What does the author suggest Americans do?

作者建议美国人做什么?

  • A)Ensure the well-being of their children and grandchildren.确保他们的子孙后代的福祉。
  • B)Make joint efforts to tackle the nation's equity issues.共同努力解决国家的公平问题。
  • C)Restrain their intolerance and discrimination.克制他们的不宽容和歧视。
  • D)Restore social order for a harmonious nation.恢复社会秩序以建立和谐的国家。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 答非所问Ensure the well-being of their children and grandchildren 借用 our children and our grandchildren deserve better，但那句是说「下一代值得更好」——是作者的忧虑动机，不是他给读者的具体建议；选项把动机当成了行动。
B) ✓ 正确预判「联手打破现状」与 Make joint efforts to tackle the nation's equity issues 一一对应——锁定 B。
  • let's commit to working together to disrupt the status quo↔Make joint efforts to tackle the nation's equity issues
  • the path toward true equity↔the nation's equity issues
C) 偷换概念Restrain their intolerance and discrimination 把答案换成「克制偏狭与歧视」。原文 Unless we get honest about intolerance, bias 要求的是「对偏狭与偏见保持诚实」——是承认与谈论，而不是 restrain（克制压抑）。
D) 偷换概念Restore social order for a harmonious nation 借用末段 build a better, more inclusive nation 的「建设国家」色彩，但 restore social order（恢复社会秩序）原文从未提及；原文是「建设更包容」，不是「恢复秩序」。
P9

On this Martin Luther King Day and beyond, let's commit to working together to disrupt the status quo.

在马丁·路德·金日及今后，让我们一起承诺携手努力，打破现状。
