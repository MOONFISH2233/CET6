原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

One of the great successes of the Republican Party in recent decades is the relentless propagation of a simple formula for economic growth: tax cuts.

近几十年来，共和党取得的一项巨大成功，就是坚持不懈地传播一个关于经济增长的简单公式：减税。

P2

The formula doesn't work, but that has not affected its popularity. And while the cult of tax cuts has attracted many critics, it lacks for obvious rivals.

这个公式并不奏效，但这并未影响它的受欢迎程度。尽管减税狂热吸引了许多批评者，却没有明显的替代方案。

P3

Democratic politicians have tended to campaign on helping people left behind by economic growth. When Democrats do talk about encouraging economic growth, they often sound like Republicans.

民主党政治人物往往以帮助那些被经济增长落在后面的人为竞选主张。当民主党人确实谈到促进经济增长时，他们听起来常常像共和党人。

P4

This is not just a political problem for Democrats; it is an economic problem for the United States. The nation needs a better story about the drivers of economic growth. The painful lessons of recent decades point to a promising candidate: higher wages.

这不仅是民主党面临的政治问题，也是美国面临的经济问题。美国需要对经济增长的驱动因素有一种更好的阐释。近几十年令人痛苦的教训指向了一个有希望的候选因素：更高的工资。

P5

Raising the wages of American workers ought to be the priority of economic policymakers. We'd all be better off paying less attention to quarterly updates on the growth of the nation's gross domestic product (GDP) and focusing instead on the growth of workers' paychecks.

提高美国工人的工资应当成为经济政策制定者的优先事项。如果少关注国内生产总值（GDP）增长的季度更新，转而关注工人工资收入的增长，我们所有人的境况都会更好。

P6

Set aside, for the moment, the familiar argument for higher wages: fairness. The argument here is that higher wages can fuel the engine of economic growth.

暂且把支持提高工资的那个耳熟能详的理由放在一边：公平。这里的论点是，更高的工资能够为经济增长的引擎提供动力。

P7

Perhaps the most famous illustration of the benefits is the story of Henry Ford's decision in 1914 to pay $5 a day to workers on his Model T assembly lines. He did it to increase production—he was paying a premium to maintain a reliable workforce. The unexpected benefit was that Ford's factory workers became Ford customers, too.

关于提高工资所带来益处的最著名例证，或许就是这样一个故事：1914年，亨利·福特决定给福特T型车装配线上的工人每天5美元的工资。他这样做是为了提高产量——他支付高于常规的工资，以维持一支可靠的劳动力队伍。意想不到的好处是，福特工厂的工人也成了福特汽车的顾客。

P8

The same logic still holds: Consumption drives the American economy, and workers who are paid more can spend more.

同样的逻辑至今仍然成立：消费推动着美国经济，而工资更高的工人能够进行更多消费。

P9

Mainstream economists insist that it is impossible to order up a sustainable increase in wages because compensation levels reflect the unerring judgment of market forces.

主流经济学家坚持认为，不可能人为安排工资持续上涨，因为薪酬水平反映了市场力量无误的判断。

P10

The conventional wisdom held that productivity growth was the only route to higher wages. Through that lens, efforts to negotiate higher wages were counterproductive. Minimum-wage laws would raise unemployment because there was only so much money in the wage pool, and if some people got more, others would get none.

传统观念认为，生产率增长是提高工资的唯一途径。从这一角度看，争取提高工资的努力会适得其反。最低工资法会导致失业率上升，因为工资总额是有限的，如果一些人得到更多，其他人就会一无所获。

P11

It was in the context of this worldview that it became popular to argue that tax cuts would drive prosperity. Rich people would invest, productivity would increase, wages would rise.

正是在这种世界观的背景下，认为减税能够带来繁荣的观点开始流行。富人会进行投资，生产率会提高，工资会随之上涨。

P12

In the real world, things are more complicated. Wages are influenced by a tug of war between employers and workers, and employers have been winning. One clear piece of evidence is the widening gap between productivity growth and wage growth since roughly 1970. Productivity has more than doubled; wages have lagged far behind.

在现实世界中，事情更加复杂。工资受到雇主与工人之间拉锯的影响，而雇主一直占据上风。一个明确的证据是，大约自1970年以来，生产率增长与工资增长之间的差距不断扩大。生产率已经增长了一倍多，而工资却远远落后。

P13

A focus on wage growth would provide an antidote(矫正方法) to the attractive simplicity of the belief in the magical power of tax cuts.

关注工资增长，将成为对减税具有神奇力量这一信念所具备的诱人简洁性的一剂矫正药。

解析逐题详解

46

Why does the formula of tax cuts remain popular though ineffective?

为什么减税方案虽然无效却依然流行?

  • A)Its critics' voice has not been heard throughout the country.批评者的声音未能在全国范围内得到倾听。
  • B)There seem to be no other options available to replace it.似乎没有其他可用的选项来取代它。
  • C)The cult of tax cuts has been relentlessly propagated by all policymakers.减税崇拜已被所有政策制定者不懈地宣传。
  • D)There appears to be a misunderstanding of the formula among the public.公众似乎对该方案存在误解。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 无中生有原文只说 the cult of tax cuts has attracted many critics——招致很多批评，没有说这些批评"未被全国听见"。
B) ✓ 正确B 项是 it lacks for obvious rivals 的同义重写，与预判完全吻合。
  • it lacks for obvious rivals↔There seem to be no other options available to replace it.
C) 以偏概全原文 One of the great successes of the Republican Party 明确只是共和党在传播，选项扩大为 all policymakers（所有政策制定者）。
D) 无中生有原文只说该公式 doesn't work, but that has not affected its popularity，并未说大众"误解"了这个公式。
P2

And while the cult of tax cuts has attracted many critics, it lacks for obvious rivals.

尽管对减税的崇拜招致了许多批评，但它（在政策市场上）没有明显的替代品。

47

What does the author think is a more effective measure for driving economic growth in the U.S.?

作者认为推动美国经济增长更有效的措施是什么?

  • A)Aiding people left behind by economic growth.帮助那些在经济增长中被落下的人。
  • B)Prioritizing the growth of the nation's GDP.优先考虑国家GDP的增长。
  • C)Increasing the compensation for labor.增加劳动报酬。
  • D)Introducing even more extensive tax cuts.引入更大规模的减税。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴Democratic politicians have tended to campaign on helping people left behind by economic growth. 这是作者描述民主党的做法，紧接着 P4 就把它否定为"政治问题"。
B) 与原文相反We'd all be better off paying less attention to quarterly updates on the growth of the nation's gross domestic product (GDP)——作者明确说应少关注 GDP 增长。
C) ✓ 正确C 项是 Raising the wages of American workers 的逐字同义改写，与预判完全吻合。
  • Raising the wages of American workers↔Increasing the compensation for labor.
D) 与原文相反全文要驳斥的就是减税公式（tax cuts），作者不可能反过来主张更大规模减税。
P5

Raising the wages of American workers ought to be the priority of economic policymakers.

提高美国工人的工资，应当是经济政策制定者的首要任务。

48

What is the logic underlying the author's viewpoint?

作者观点背后的逻辑是什么?

  • A)The growth of workers' paychecks ultimately boosts the nation's economy.工人薪资的增长最终会推动国家经济。
  • B)Paying a premium to maintain a reliable workforce attracts more customers.支付溢价以维持可靠的劳动力会吸引更多顾客。
  • C)Consumption stimulates the desire for higher wages.消费刺激了对更高工资的渴望。
  • D)Familiar arguments for higher wages are outdated.关于提高工资的常见论据已经过时。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确A 项用 ultimately 把 P8 的两句话压成一句因果，与预判的"加薪→消费→经济增长"完全一致。
  • workers who are paid more can spend more↔The growth of workers' paychecks ultimately boosts the nation's economy.
  • Consumption drives the American economy↔ultimately boosts the nation's economy
B) 偷换概念He did it to increase production—he was paying a premium to maintain a reliable workforce. 是福特出于产能目的支付溢价；The unexpected benefit was that Ford's factory workers became Ford customers, too. 中"工人变顾客"是意外结果，并非"支付溢价"直接导向"吸引更多顾客"。
C) 与原文相反原文 workers who are paid more can spend more 是高工资 → 高消费，选项反成"消费刺激加薪欲望"，因果链方向倒置。
D) 过度推断Set aside, for the moment, the familiar argument for higher wages: fairness.——作者只是说"暂且搁置"公平这一旧论据，并未说它过时。
P8

Consumption drives the American economy, and workers who are paid more can spend more.

消费驱动着美国经济，而薪酬越高的工人能消费得越多。

49

What is the basis for higher wages according to the conventional wisdom?

根据传统观念,提高工资的依据是什么?

  • A)Fairness in distribution.分配的公平性。
  • B)Increase in productivity.生产率的提高。
  • C)The priority of economic policymakers.经济政策制定者的优先事项。
  • D)The unerring judgment of market forces.市场力量的准确判断。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴Set aside, for the moment, the familiar argument for higher wages: fairness. 中"公平"是作者列出的另一种加薪理由（且被搁置），与 conventional wisdom 无关。
B) ✓ 正确B 项就是 productivity growth 的同义改写，与预判直接对应。
  • productivity growth↔Increase in productivity.
C) 张冠李戴Raising the wages of American workers ought to be the priority of economic policymakers. 是作者本人的主张，不是传统观点对加薪基础的判断。
D) 张冠李戴Mainstream economists insist that ... compensation levels reflect the unerring judgment of market forces. 是主流经济学家的判断，且原文中"市场力量的无误判断"是指工资水平的决定方式，不是加薪的依据。
P10

The conventional wisdom held that productivity growth was the only route to higher wages.

传统观点认为，生产率的提高是通往更高工资的唯一途径。

50

What do we learn about things in the real world in America for the past 50 years or so?

关于美国过去50年左右的现实世界情况,我们了解到什么?

  • A)People have failed to see a corresponding increase in wages and in productivity.人们未能看到工资和生产率的相应增长。
  • B)People have been disheartened by the widening gap between the haves and have-nots.人们对贫富差距的扩大感到沮丧。
  • C)People have witnessed a tug of war between Republicans and Democrats over tax cuts.人们目睹了共和党和民主党就减税问题展开的拉锯战。
  • D)People have seen the link disappearing between productivity and workers' well-being.人们看到生产率与工人福祉之间的联系正在消失。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确A 项用 failed to see a corresponding increase 表达"生产率翻倍而工资落后"的整体含义，与预判完全吻合。
  • Productivity has more than doubled; wages have lagged far behind.↔People have failed to see a corresponding increase in wages and in productivity.
  • the widening gap between productivity growth and wage growth↔a corresponding increase in wages and in productivity
B) 无中生有原文 a tug of war between employers and workers 是劳资博弈，不是贫富分化（haves and have-nots）；且原文未提任何人 disheartened（沮丧）。
C) 偷换概念原文 a tug of war between employers and workers 是劳资双方的拉锯战，而非两党的争斗；且原文未说民主党与共和党在减税上拉锯。
D) 过度推断Productivity has more than doubled; wages have lagged far behind. 说的是"差距扩大"，并未说"联系消失（link disappearing）"——差距大 ≠ 联系断；且 well-being（福祉）也比 wages 范围更大。
P12

In the real world, things are more complicated. Wages are influenced by a tug of war between employers and workers, and employers have been winning. One clear piece of evidence is the widening gap between productivity growth and wage growth since roughly 1970. Productivity has more than doubled; wages have lagged far behind.

在现实世界里，情况更复杂。工资受到雇主与工人之间拉锯战的影响，而雇主一直在胜出。一个明显的证据是，自 1970 年前后以来，生产率增长与工资增长之间的差距越来越大：生产率已经翻了一倍多，而工资远远落在后面。
