A

A) On the hills of central Kenya, almost lime-green with the sparkle of tea bushes in the sunlight, farmers know all about climate change. "The rainy season is no longer predictable," says one. "When it is supposed to rain it doesn't, then it all comes at once." Climate change is an issue that will affect everyone on the planet. For Africans its consequences will be particularly bitter: whereas other regions were able to grow rich by burning coal and oil, Africa will pay much of the human price without having enjoyed the benefits. "Africa only represents 2% of global greenhouse-gas emissions but it is the continent that is expected to suffer the most from climate impacts," says Mafalda Duarte, who runs the World Bank's $8bn Climate Investment Funds.

在肯尼亚中部的山丘上，阳光下的茶树闪烁着近乎酸橙绿的光芒，农民们对气候变化了如指掌。“雨季已经无法预测了，”一位农民说，“该下雨的时候不下，之后却一下子全下来了。”气候变化是一个将影响地球上每个人的问题。对非洲人来说，其后果尤其苦涩：其他地区能够通过燃烧煤炭和石油变得富裕，而非洲没有享受到这些好处，却将承担相当大的人类代价。“非洲只占全球温室气体排放量的2%，但预计它将成为受气候影响最严重的大陆，”负责世界银行80亿美元气候投资基金的玛法尔达·杜阿尔特说。

B

B) Although there are huge uncertainties as to the precise impacts of climate change, enough is known to say that global warming represents one of the main threats to Africa's prosperity. Parts of the continent are already warming much more quickly than the average: temperatures in southern Africa have increased by about twice the global rate over the past 50 years. Even if the world were to cut emissions enough to keep global warming below 1.5℃, heat-waves would intensify in Africa and diseases would spread to areas not currently affected. Farming would also be hit hard. About 40% of the land now used to grow maize(玉米) would no longer be suitable for it. Overall, it is estimated that maize yields would fall by 18-22%.

尽管气候变化的确切影响存在巨大的不确定性，但已有足够的认识可以表明，全球变暖是非洲繁荣面临的主要威胁之一。非洲部分地区的变暖速度已经远远快于平均水平：过去50年间，非洲南部的气温升幅约为全球升温速度的两倍。即使世界各国削减排放，使全球变暖幅度保持在1.5℃以下，非洲的热浪也会加剧，疾病会扩散到目前尚未受影响的地区。农业也将受到严重冲击。目前用于种植玉米的土地中，约有40%将不再适合种植玉米。总体而言，预计玉米产量将下降18%至22%。

C

C) Africa is particularly vulnerable, in part because it is already struggling to feed itself and it will have to vastly increase yields and productivity if it is to put food on the plates of a fast-growing population, even without climate change. The UN's Food and Agriculture Organisation reckons that by 2050 global food production would have to rise by about 70% over its level of 2009 to meet demand from a population that is growing in numbers and appetite. Much of this new demand will be in Africa. Yet the continent already imports about $50bn-worth of food a year and that figure is expected to more than double over the next five years. Self-sufficiency is not Africa's goal, but the fact that it spends more money importing food than it does buying capital goods suggests it has room for improvement.

非洲尤其脆弱，部分原因在于它已经在努力养活自己；即使没有气候变化，若要满足一个人口和食欲都快速增长的社会的粮食需求，非洲也必须大幅提高产量和生产率。联合国粮食及农业组织估计，到2050年，全球粮食产量必须比2009年的水平提高约70%，才能满足人口数量和食欲不断增长所带来的需求。新增需求中有很大一部分将来自非洲。然而，非洲目前每年进口价值约500亿美元的食品，预计未来五年这一数字将增加一倍以上。自给自足并不是非洲的目标，但它进口食品所花的钱多于购买资本货物所花的钱，这表明其仍有改进空间。

D

D) Finding out why is not hard. Most farms are tiny, ploughed by hand and reliant on rain. More than half of Africa's people make their living from farming. Although its total harvest has climbed over the past few decades, this is mainly because there are more people farming more land. But in many places there is no spare land to farm. Plots in Rwanda are so small that you could fit 250 of them onto the average American farm. And although output per worker has improved by more than half over the past 30 years in Africa, that is still far behind the 2.5 times improvement in Asia. Yields of maize are generally less than two tonnes per hectare, a fifth the level in America.

要弄清原因并不难。大多数农场规模很小，靠人工耕作，并且依赖降雨。非洲一半以上的人口以务农为生。尽管过去几十年间，非洲的粮食总产量有所增加，但这主要是因为从事农业的人更多了，耕种的土地也更多了。然而，许多地方已经没有多余的土地可供耕种。卢旺达的地块小得惊人，美国一个普通农场的面积可以容纳250个这样的地块。尽管过去30年间，非洲每名农业劳动者的产出提高了超过一半，但这仍远远落后于亚洲产出提高2.5倍的水平。非洲的玉米单产通常不到每公顷两吨，只有美国水平的五分之一。

E

E) The low productivity of African farmers is reflected in national economic statistics—despite absorbing so much labour, farming generates just 15% of GDP. "They can't even feed their families," says Jennifer Blanke, a vice-president of the African Development Bank in charge of agriculture. "Farm productivity hasn't improved in many parts of Africa for 100 years."

非洲农民的低生产率反映在国民经济统计数据中：尽管农业吸纳了如此多的劳动力，但其创造的国内生产总值仅占15%。“他们甚至养活不了自己的家人，”负责农业事务的非洲开发银行副行长詹妮弗·布兰克说。“非洲许多地区的农业生产率已经有100年没有提高了。”

F

F) One reason is that in the first few decades of independence, many African governments neglected farming as they focused on industrialising their economies. Others damaged it by pushing down the prices that state monopolies paid for their crops in order to subsidise workers in cities with cheap food. Ghana taxed cocoa(可可粉) exports so heavily that production collapsed by half between the 1960s and 1980s, despite a jump in the global price of cocoa. Yet over the past two decades or so governments and donors have begun to look again at farming as a way of providing jobs for the 13 million young people entering the workforce each year. Much of the focus has been on getting small farmers to use fertiliser and, more important, better seeds. The results can be impressive. Improved varieties of sorghum(高梁), for instance, can produce a crop that is 40% larger than the usual variety. Infrastructure is important. A World Bank irrigation project in Ethiopia helped farmers increase their potato harvest from about 8 tonnes per hectare to 35 tonnes.

其中一个原因是，在独立后的最初几十年里，许多非洲政府专注于经济工业化，忽视了农业。另一些政府则通过压低国营垄断企业支付给农民的作物价格来损害农业，以便用廉价食品补贴城市工人。加纳对可可出口征收了过高的税，导致20世纪60年代至80年代间可可产量减少了一半，尽管同期全球可可价格大幅上涨。然而，在过去大约20年里，政府和捐助者开始重新把农业视为一种为每年进入劳动力市场的1300万年轻人提供就业的途径。许多工作重点放在推动小农使用化肥，以及更重要的优良种子上。成效可能十分显著。例如，改良品种的高粱产量可以比普通品种高出40%。基础设施也很重要。世界银行在埃塞俄比亚实施的一项灌溉项目帮助农民将马铃薯收成从每公顷约8吨提高到35吨。

G

G) Better techniques help, too. Small coffee farmers in Kenya are able to increase their incomes by 40% by following a few simple guidelines on caring for their bushes, such as trimming all but three of their stems. Many of their neighbours do not follow the advice, because it seems counter-intuitive. More stems ought to lead to more coffee beans, they say. Yet after seeing those following the advice get bigger harvests for a season or two, many others start doing the same.

更好的技术也会有所帮助。肯尼亚的小型咖啡种植户只需遵循几条关于照料咖啡树的简单指导，例如只保留三根枝干、剪去其余枝干，就能将收入提高40%。他们的许多邻居并不听从这些建议，因为这似乎违背直觉。他们说，枝干越多，咖啡豆应该就越多。然而，在看到听从建议的人连续一两个季节获得更大的收成后，许多人也开始照做。

H

H) One way of spreading knowledge is to link farms to big buyers of their harvests. When Diageo, a British drinks giant, built a brewery in western Kenya, it wanted to use local crops to make a beer cheap enough to compete with illicit home brew. It organised farmers into groups, improved supply chains for them to get seeds and fertiliser and then agreed to buy their grain. It now provides a market to about 17,000 farmers. Across the region it has doubled its use of local raw material to about 80% over five years, says John O'Keeffe, who runs its Africa business.

传播知识的一种方式，是将农场与大型农产品采购商联系起来。当英国饮料巨头帝亚吉欧在肯尼亚西部建造一家啤酒厂时，它希望利用当地作物酿造一种价格足够低廉、能够与非法家庭酿酒竞争的啤酒。该公司把农民组织起来，改善供应链，帮助他们获得种子和化肥，随后同意收购他们的谷物。如今，它为约1.7万名农民提供了市场。负责其非洲业务的约翰·奥基夫说，五年来，该公司在整个地区对当地原材料的使用比例已经翻了一番，达到约80%。

I

I) An even more important change is the move from traditional farming to building businesses that can profitably bring technology and investment to small farmers. Taita Ngetich, a young Kenyan, was studying engineering when he wanted to earn a little money on the side. He scraped together 20,000 Kenyan shillings (about $200) to plant tomatoes. Everything went wrong. The crop was attacked by pests. "Then there was a massive flood that swallowed all our capital," he says. Mr Ngetich persevered by looking into buying a greenhouse to protect his plants from bugs and rain. The cheapest ones cost more than $2,500 each, so he designed his own for half the price. Soon neighbouring farmers started placing orders with him, and now his firm, Illuminum Greenhouses, has sold more than 1,400 greenhouses that provide livelihoods to about 6,000 people. The business does not stop there; he also supplies fertiliser, high-quality seedlings and smart sensors that increase yields.

一个更为重要的变化，是从传统农业转向建立能够将技术和投资以有利可图的方式带给小农户的企业。年轻的肯尼亚人泰塔·恩盖蒂奇当时正在学习工程学，想挣一点外快。他东拼西凑了2万肯尼亚先令（约合200美元）种植西红柿。一切都出了问题。作物遭到了害虫侵袭。“后来，一场大洪水吞没了我们所有的本金，”他说。恩盖蒂奇没有放弃，而是开始研究购买温室，以保护作物免受虫害和雨水侵袭。最便宜的温室每座也要2500多美元，于是他自行设计了一种，价格只有一半。很快，邻近的农民开始向他下订单。如今，他的公司Illuminum Greenhouses已经售出1400多座温室，为约6000人提供了生计。该公司的业务并不止于此；它还供应化肥、高质量幼苗和能够提高产量的智能传感器。

J

J) Illuminum's success shows how technology can help even small farms become more productive. Because such a large share of Africa's population earns a living from agriculture, even small improvements in productivity can lift the incomes of millions of people. But over the longer run small-scale farming can go only so far, especially in the face of climate change and population pressure.

Illuminum的成功表明，技术甚至能够帮助小型农场提高生产率。由于非洲有如此大比例的人口依靠农业谋生，即使生产率只有小幅提升，也能增加数百万人的收入。但从长远来看，小规模农业能够发挥的作用终究有限，尤其是在气候变化和人口压力面前。

K

K) "If we really want to lift people out of poverty we have to finance projects that will get them an income of at least $100 a month so that they can pay for health care and education," says Mr Ngetich. "Projects that give them an extra $2 a month from growing beans or maize aren't going to get them there." Getting those big jumps will need better jobs in factories and cities.

“如果我们真的想让人们摆脱贫困，就必须资助那些能让他们每月至少获得100美元收入的项目，这样他们才能支付医疗和教育费用，”恩盖蒂奇先生说。“那些通过种植豆类或玉米每月只让他们多赚2美元的项目，不可能把他们带到那个水平。”要实现如此大幅度的提升，就需要在工厂和城市中提供更好的工作。

Everyone in the world will have to bear the consequences of climate change, especially Africans.

世界上每个人都将承受气候变化的后果,尤其是非洲人。

答案：A 段
锚点全人类共担、非洲尤其要承受气候后果
定位A 段 Climate change is an issue that will affect everyone on the planet. For Africans its consequences will be particularly bitter. 气候变化是一个会影响地球上每个人的议题，但对非洲人来说，其后果将尤其苦涩。
改写Everyone in the world will have to bear the consequences of climate change↔Climate change is an issue that will affect everyone on the planet everyone in the world = everyone on the planet；bear consequences = be affected especially Africans↔For Africans its consequences will be particularly bitter especially = particularly（程度副词同义）
辨邻B B 段 global warming represents one of the main threats to Africa's prosperity 是具体威胁论证，与题干 Everyone in the world will have to bear the consequences 的「全体共担」总论调性不对位。 global warming represents one of the main threats to Africa's prosperity

Improvement in farm output per worker in Africa falls far short of that in Asia.

非洲每个工人的农业产出改善远远落后于亚洲。

答案：D 段
锚点非洲人均产出提升远落后于亚洲
定位D 段 And although output per worker has improved by more than half over the past 30 years in Africa, that is still far behind the 2.5 times improvement in Asia. 尽管过去 30 年里非洲的人均产出提升了一半以上，这仍远落后于亚洲的 2.5 倍提升。
改写Improvement in farm output per worker in Africa↔output per worker has improved by more than half over the past 30 years in Africa 动词句 → 名词化短语 falls far short of that in Asia↔far behind the 2.5 times improvement in Asia fall far short of = far behind

In the long term, the potential for small farms in Africa to increase productivity is quite limited, especially owing to the warming climate and a growing population.

从长远来看,非洲小型农场提高生产力的潜力相当有限,特别是由于气候变暖和人口增长。

答案：J 段
锚点长期看小农增产潜力有限
定位J 段 But over the longer run small-scale farming can go only so far, especially in the face of climate change and population pressure. 但从更长远看，小规模农业能走的也就这么远，尤其是面对气候变化和人口压力的时候。
改写In the long term, the potential for small farms in Africa to increase productivity is quite limited↔over the longer run small-scale farming can go only so far long term = longer run；quite limited = can go only so far especially owing to the warming climate and a growing population↔especially in the face of climate change and population pressure owing to = in the face of；warming climate ≈ climate change；growing population ≈ population pressure
辨邻I I 段 Illuminum Greenhouses, has sold more than 1,400 greenhouses that provide livelihoods to about 6,000 people 给的是「小农通过科技获得收益」的正面案例，与题干 the potential for small farms in Africa to increase productivity is quite limited 的「潜力有限」断言方向相反，是 42 题的归属段而非本题。 An even more important change is the move from traditional farming to building businesses that can profitably bring technology and investment to small farmers K K 段 Projects that give them an extra $2 a month from growing beans or maize aren't going to get them there 给的是收入门槛论证，没有「长期看 + 气候变化 + 人口」这套与题干 especially owing to the warming climate and a growing population 完全对位的双因表述。 Getting those big jumps will need better jobs in factories and cities
