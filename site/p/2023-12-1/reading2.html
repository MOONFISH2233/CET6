原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Journal editors decide what gets published and what doesn't, affecting the careers of other academics and influencing the direction that a field takes. You'd hope, then, that journals would do everything they can to establish a diverse editorial board, reflecting a variety of voices, experiences, and identities.

期刊编辑决定哪些内容发表、哪些内容不发表，这影响着其他学者的职业生涯，也影响着一个领域的发展方向。因此，人们希望期刊能够竭尽所能建立多元化的编辑委员会，体现各种不同的声音、经历和身份。

P2

Unfortunately a new study in Nature Neuroscience makes for disheartening reading. The team finds that the majority of editors in top psychology and neuroscience journals are male and based in the United States: a situation that may be amplifying existing gender inequalities in the field and influencing the kind of research that gets published.

遗憾的是，《自然·神经科学》发表的一项新研究读来令人沮丧。研究团队发现，顶尖心理学和神经科学期刊的大多数编辑都是男性，且任职于美国：这种情况可能正在加剧该领域现有的性别不平等，并影响哪些类型的研究能够发表。

P3

Men were found to account for 60% of the editors of psychology journals. There were significantly more male than female editors at each level of seniority, and men made up the majority of editors in over three quarters of the journals. Crucially, the proportion of female editors was significantly lower than the overall proportion of women psychology researchers.

研究发现，男性占心理学期刊编辑的60%。在每个资历级别中，男性编辑人数都显著多于女性编辑，而且在超过四分之三的期刊中，男性占编辑的多数。至关重要的是，女性编辑所占比例显著低于女性心理学研究人员的总体比例。

P4

The differences were even starker in the neuroscience journals: 70% of editors were male, and men held the majority of editorial positions in 88% of journals. In this case, the proportion of female editors was not significantly lower than the proportion of female researchers working in neuroscience—a finding that reveals enduring gender disparities in the field more broadly.

神经科学期刊中的差异更加明显：70%的编辑是男性，男性在88%的期刊中占据了多数编辑职位。在这种情况下，女性编辑所占比例并未显著低于从事神经科学研究的女性研究人员所占比例——这一发现揭示了该领域更广泛且持续存在的性别差异。

P5

Based on their results, the team concludes that "the ideas, values and decision-making biases of men are overrepresented in the editorial positions of the most recognized academic journals in psychology and neuroscience."

根据研究结果，研究团队得出结论：“在心理学和神经科学领域最受认可的学术期刊编辑职位中，男性的思想、价值观和决策偏见所占比重过大。”

P6

Gender inequality in science is often attributed to the fact that senior academics are more likely to be male, because historically science was male-dominated: it's argued that as time goes on and more women rise to senior roles, the field will become more equal. Yet this study showed that even the junior roles in psychology journals tended to be held disproportionately by men, despite the fact that there are actually more female than male junior psychology faculty.

科学界的性别不平等常常被归因于资深学者更有可能是男性，因为从历史上看，科学一直由男性主导：人们认为，随着时间推移，越来越多的女性晋升到高级职位，这一领域将变得更加平等。然而，这项研究表明，心理学期刊中的初级职位往往也由男性不成比例地担任，尽管事实上，初级心理学教师中女性人数多于男性。

P7

This implies that a lack of female academics is not the problem. Instead, there are structural reasons that women are disadvantaged in science. Women receive lower salaries and face greater childcare demands, for instance, which can result in fewer publications and grants—the kinds of things that journals look for when deciding who to appoint. Rather than simply blaming the inequality of editorial boards on tradition, we should be actively breaking down these existing barriers.

这意味着，女性学者数量不足并不是问题所在。相反，科学界存在使女性处于不利地位的结构性原因。例如，女性的薪资较低，还要承担更多的育儿责任，这可能导致她们发表的论文和获得的资助更少，而这些正是期刊在决定任命谁时所看重的因素。我们不应仅仅把编辑委员会的不平等归咎于传统，而应积极消除这些现存的障碍。

P8

A lack of diversity among journal editors also likely contributes to psychology's WEIRD problem. If journal editors are largely men from the United States, then they will probably place higher value on papers that are relevant to Western, male populations, whether consciously or not.

期刊编辑缺乏多样性也很可能助长了心理学的WEIRD问题。如果期刊编辑主要是来自美国的男性，那么无论是有意还是无意，他们很可能会更加重视那些与西方男性群体相关的论文。

解析逐题详解

51

What would we expect an editorial board of an academic journal to exhibit in view of its important responsibilities?

鉴于学术期刊编委会的重要职责,我们期望它展现出什么?

  • A)Insight.洞察力。
  • B)Expertise.专业知识。
  • C)Integrity.诚信。
  • D)Diversity.多样性。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 无中生有全文没有任何句子提到「洞察力」相关概念，Insight 是从职责一词主观联想出来的属性。
B) 偷换概念原文谈的是编辑的「身份多样性」而非「专业能力」；编辑工作虽要求专业，但全文没有任何一句强调专业能力，Expertise 是属性偷换。
C) 无中生有全文未出现「诚信／正直」相关讨论，Integrity 同样是从「重要职责」一词联想出的主观属性。
D) ✓ 正确预判语义「多元」与 Diversity 完全同义，其它三项均无对应原文支撑，锁定 D。
  • diverse editorial board↔Diversity
P1

You'd hope, then, that journals would do everything they can to establish a diverse editorial board, reflecting a variety of voices, experiences, and identities.

那么，你会希望期刊竭尽所能去建立一个多元化的编委会，从而反映各种各样的声音、经验与身份。

52

What do we learn from the findings of a new study in Nature Neuroscience?

我们从Nature Neuroscience上一项新研究的发现中了解到什么?

  • A)The majority of top psychology and neuroscience journals reflect a variety of voices, experiences and identities.大多数顶级心理学和神经科学期刊反映了多样化的声音、经历和身份。
  • B)The editorial boards of most psychology and neuroscience journals do influence the direction their field takes.大多数心理学和神经科学期刊的编委会确实影响着其领域发展的方向。
  • C)The editorial boards of the most important journals in psychology and neuroscience are male-dominated.心理学和神经科学领域最重要期刊的编委会由男性主导。
  • D)The majority of editors in top psychology and neuroscience journals have relevant backgrounds.顶级心理学和神经科学期刊的大多数编辑具有相关背景。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反reflect a variety of voices, experiences and identities 是 P1 中作者「期望」的状态，而 P2 恰恰指出现实并非如此——选项把「期待」当成「现实」，方向完全反了。
B) 答非所问do influence the direction their field takes 借用 influencing the direction that a field takes，但那是 P1 描述「编辑职责」的总论，不是 P2 的「研究发现」；题干问 findings of a new study，本选项答的是 P1 的背景，与 52 题问的范围错位。
C) ✓ 正确「顶级=最重要」「以男为主=男性主导」两组同义替换都成立，C 是定位句的精确同义复述。
  • the majority of editors in top psychology and neuroscience journals are male↔The editorial boards of the most important journals in psychology and neuroscience are male-dominated.
  • top psychology and neuroscience journals↔the most important journals in psychology and neuroscience
D) 无中生有全文没有任何句子谈及编辑的「相关背景／资质」是否充足；P2 谈的是性别和国籍分布，不是专业相关性，have relevant backgrounds 凭空冒出。
P2

The team finds that the majority of editors in top psychology and neuroscience journals are male and based in the United States: a situation that may be amplifying existing gender inequalities in the field and influencing the kind of research that gets published.

研究团队发现，顶级心理学与神经科学期刊的大多数编辑是男性，且在美国工作——这种状况可能正在放大该领域既有的性别不平等，并影响着哪些研究得以发表。

53

What fact does the author highlight concerning the gender differences in editors of psychology journals?

关于心理学期刊编辑的性别差异,作者强调了什么事实?

  • A)There were quite a few female editors who also distinguished themselves as influential psychology researchers.有相当多的女性编辑也作为有影响力的心理学研究者脱颖而出。
  • B)The number of female editors was simply disproportionate to that of women engaged in psychology research.女性编辑的数量与从事心理学研究的女性数量根本不成比例。
  • C)The proportion of female editors was increasingly lower at senior levels.女性编辑的比例在高级职位上越来越低。
  • D)There were few female editors who could move up to senior positions.很少有女性编辑能够晋升到高级职位。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 无中生有原文只对比「女编辑占比」与「女研究者占比」，并未说有「兼任出色研究者的女编辑」；distinguished themselves as influential psychology researchers 是凭空添加的属性。
B) ✓ 正确唯一刻画「女编辑占比 vs 女研究者占比」这一比例对照的选项是 B；其它选项要么换了对比对象，要么换了讨论范围。
  • the proportion of female editors was significantly lower than the overall proportion of women psychology researchers↔The number of female editors was simply disproportionate to that of women engaged in psychology research.
  • the overall proportion of women psychology researchers↔that of women engaged in psychology research
C) 过度推断The proportion of female editors was increasingly lower at senior levels. 把原文「各级别男编辑都更多」的事实曲解为「越往高级越少」的递进趋势；原文 There were significantly more male than female editors at each level of seniority 只说「每一级都更多」，并没有给出「越往上越严重」的递进关系。
D) 偷换概念原文没有讨论「女编辑能否升迁」这一职业流动问题；few female editors who could move up to senior positions 把性别比例话题换成升迁话题，方向错位。
P3

Crucially, the proportion of female editors was significantly lower than the overall proportion of women psychology researchers.

至关重要的是，女性编辑的占比显著低于心理学领域整体女性研究者的占比。

54

What can we infer from the conclusion drawn by the team of the new study on the basis of their findings?

从新研究团队基于其发现得出的结论中,我们可以推断出什么?

  • A)Women's views are underrepresented in the editorial boards of top psychology and neuroscience journals.女性的观点在顶级心理学和神经科学期刊的编委会中代表性不足。
  • B)Male editors of top psychology and neuroscience journals tend to be biased against their female colleagues.顶级心理学和神经科学期刊的男性编辑往往对其女性同事存在偏见。
  • C)Male researchers have enough representation in the editorial boards to ensure their publications.男性研究者在编委会中有足够的代表性来确保他们的论文发表。
  • D)Female editors have to struggle to get women's research articles published in academic journals.女性编辑必须努力争取才能让女性的研究文章在学术期刊上发表。
解析
A（推断题）
A) ✓ 正确在「男过度代表」基础上仅走一步就到「女代表不足」，A 是唯一这样做的；其它选项要么走第二步（偏见、歧视），要么换了话题。
  • the ideas, values and decision-making biases of men are overrepresented↔Women's views are underrepresented in the editorial boards of top psychology and neuroscience journals.
  • the editorial positions of the most recognized academic journals in psychology and neuroscience↔the editorial boards of top psychology and neuroscience journals
B) 过度推断原文 decision-making biases of men 指的是「决策中的偏见模式被过度呈现」这一统计事实；be biased against their female colleagues 则把它推到「男编辑刻意歧视女同事」的人际行为层面，多走了一步。
C) 答非所问题干问「能推断出什么」，ensure their publications 谈的是「男编辑的发表得到保障」，原文没有任何句子谈论这种保障机制；且本研究的论调是批评不平等而非赞颂男性发表优势，与原文立场相反。
D) 无中生有原文从未谈论女编辑为发表女性研究而「斗争」，struggle to get women's research articles published 是凭空虚构出来的情节。
P5

Based on their results, the team concludes that "the ideas, values and decision-making biases of men are overrepresented in the editorial positions of the most recognized academic journals in psychology and neuroscience."

基于研究结果，研究团队总结道：「在最受认可的心理学与神经科学学术期刊的编辑岗位上，男性的想法、价值观与决策偏见被过度代表。」

55

What does the author suggest we do instead of simply blaming the inequality of editorial boards on tradition?

作者建议我们做什么,而不是简单地将编委会的不平等归咎于传统?

  • A)Strike a balance between male and female editors.在男性和女性编辑之间取得平衡。
  • B)Increase women's employment in senior positions.增加女性在高级职位上的任职。
  • C)Enlarge the body of female academics.扩大女性学者的群体。
  • D)Implement overall structural reforms.实施全面的结构性改革。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 以偏概全Strike a balance between male and female editors 只触及「编辑性别比例」这一表层结果，与作者主张的「拆除导致不平等的结构性原因」相比，停留在症状而非根因；原文反对的就是「只盯比例不动结构」的浅层对策。
B) 答非所问Increase women's employment in senior positions 借的是 P6 「初级岗位也男性偏多」这一对照性数据；但 P6 是作者用来「驳斥传统论」的论据，并非建议；55 题问的是「P7 作者建议什么」。
C) 与原文相反原文 This implies that a lack of female academics is not the problem. 已明确否定「女学者太少」是问题；Enlarge the body of female academics 恰恰反过来主张「扩充女学者队伍」，方向完全相反。
D) ✓ 正确把「主动拆除结构性壁垒」概括为「实施整体结构性改革」，是 D 与原文唯一吻合的同义改写；其它选项要么停留在「男女比例」「升迁」等局部对策，要么误把原因当对策。
  • actively breaking down these existing barriers↔Implement overall structural reforms.
  • structural reasons that women are disadvantaged in science↔structural reforms
P7

Instead, there are structural reasons that women are disadvantaged in science. Women receive lower salaries and face greater childcare demands, for instance, which can result in fewer publications and grants—the kinds of things that journals look for when deciding who to appoint. Rather than simply blaming the inequality of editorial boards on tradition, we should be actively breaking down these existing barriers.

实际上，是结构性的原因让女性在科学界处于不利地位。例如，女性薪资更低、育儿负担更重，这会导致她们论文与基金更少——而这些恰恰是期刊在选拔编辑时所看重的。与其简单地把编委会的不平等推给传统，不如主动去拆除这些既有的壁垒。
