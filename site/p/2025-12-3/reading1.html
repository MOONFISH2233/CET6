原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Mindfulness has been shown to have a number of meaningful health benefits — it can help reduce anxiety and promote healing in those suffering from chronic illness.

研究表明，正念具有多项显著的健康益处——它有助于减轻焦虑，促进慢性病患者的康复。

P2

The practice is based on an insight first described in ancient Buddhist texts that human beings have the capacity to observe experience without being caught up in it. This means simply that it is possible to observe ourselves having a craving, or a happy thought, or even a scary emotion, without reacting in the moment in a way that amplifies the feeling or sends the mind spiraling off into thinking about old memories or anticipating events.

这种练习建立在古代佛教典籍中最早阐述的一种洞见之上，即人类有能力观察自身的体验，而不被其牵着走。这简单地意味着，我们可以观察自己正在产生欲望、思绪愉快，甚至感到恐惧，却不在当下做出会强化这种感受的反应，也不让头脑陷入回忆往事或预想未来的思绪之中。

P3

This practice of mindfulness can help calm the mind and the body as we learn not to react to experience with likes and dislikes or judgments of good and bad. It does not make us cold or indifferent but more fully present.

随着我们学会不以喜欢和不喜欢，或好与坏的评判来回应体验，正念练习能够帮助我们平静心灵和身体。它不会使我们变得冷漠或无动于衷，反而会让我们更加充分地活在当下。

P4

One of the challenges of practicing mindfulness in our contemporary world is that there has been a profound transformation in human attention. The artist Jenny Odell argues that in our "attention economy" human attention has been transformed into a commodity that big corporations buy and sell. This economy rests on a technological revolution of mobile phones and social media that makes it possible for corporations to target our attention with their content at every moment.

在当今世界练习正念所面临的挑战之一，是人类的注意力已经发生了深刻的转变。艺术家珍妮·奥德尔认为，在我们的“注意力经济”中，人类的注意力已经被转化为一种由大公司买卖的商品。这种经济建立在手机和社交媒体的技术革命之上，使公司能够随时利用其内容来吸引我们的注意力。

P5

The devices most people use are a perpetual diversion from the present moment. The result is that it can feel as though our ability to focus, and be fully present, has been stolen. But mindfulness can help us resist the attention economy and relish the things that make life special, like being together with those we love.

大多数人使用的设备不断把我们从当下分散开来。结果是，我们可能会觉得自己集中注意力并充分活在当下的能力被夺走了。但正念能够帮助我们抵制注意力经济，享受那些使生活变得特别的事物，比如与我们所爱的人相聚。

P6

Most mindfulness research focuses on the individual benefits of the practice, but some scholars assert that we can not only practice mindfulness for ourselves but also practice it for others. It can help us build stronger, healthier relationships.

大多数关于正念的研究都集中于这种练习对个人的益处，但一些学者认为，我们不仅可以为自己练习正念，也可以为他人练习正念。它能够帮助我们建立更牢固、更健康的人际关系。

P7

The sad truth is that living in the attention economy, most of us have become bad listeners. However, just as it is possible to watch ourselves having an experience without reacting, it's possible to watch another person have an experience without getting tied up in reactivity and judgment.

令人悲哀的事实是，生活在注意力经济中的我们大多数人都已经不善于倾听。然而，正如我们可以观察自己正在经历某种体验而不做出反应一样，我们也可以观察另一个人正在经历某种体验，而不陷入情绪反应和评判之中。

P8

The gift of mindfulness is a practice of listening with compassion to others describe their experiences. To give this gift means putting away your phone, turning off social media, and setting aside other common distractions. It means practicing being fully present in another person's presence and listening to them with complete attention without judgment, while resisting the urge to make the interaction about you.

正念的馈赠，是带着同情心倾听他人描述自己的经历。要送出这份礼物，就意味着收起手机，关闭社交媒体，并把其他常见的干扰放到一边。这意味着练习在他人面前完全保持当下，以全部注意力、不带评判地倾听对方，同时克制住把这次互动变成谈论自己的冲动。

P9

If we judge the value of gifts based on their price, this gift may seem worthless, but in a distracted world, it is a precious one.

如果我们根据礼物的价格来判断其价值，那么这份礼物可能显得一文不值；但在一个注意力分散的世界里，它却是一份珍贵的礼物。

解析逐题详解

46

What do we learn from the passage about practicing mindfulness?

从文章中我们了解到关于练习正念的什么内容?

  • A)It triggers our mind to reflect on past experiences or anticipate future events.它触发我们的思维去反思过去的经历或预期未来的事件。
  • B)It focuses our attention on the present moment without making judgments.它将我们的注意力集中在当下时刻而不做评判。
  • C)It concentrates our mind on our personal feelings, happy or scary.它将我们的思维集中在个人感受上,无论是快乐的还是恐惧的。
  • D)It helps our mind to stay calm when facing judgments from others.它帮助我们的思维在面对他人的评判时保持平静。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反原文 without … sends the mind spiraling off into thinking about old memories or anticipating events 明确把「回想旧事 / 预期未来」放在否定结构里，是正念要避免的，A 把它说成正念的作用，恰好与原文相反。
B) ✓ 正确预判落在「观察当下、不卷入、不评判」三点上，focuses our attention on the present moment without making judgments 把前两点合在一句里全覆盖，是定位句的紧贴改写。
  • observe ourselves having a craving, or a happy thought, or even a scary emotion, without reacting in the moment↔focuses our attention on the present moment
  • we learn not to react to experience with likes and dislikes or judgments of good and bad↔without making judgments
C) 偷换概念原文 observe ourselves having a craving, or a happy thought, or even a scary emotion, without reacting in the moment 强调观察情绪而不卷入，C 项却说「把注意力集中在个人情绪上」，把「观察」偷换成「集中」，两者一冷静一沉浸，语义错位。
D) 张冠李戴原文谈的是「自己不对体验作好坏评判」（judgments of good and bad），并未涉及「他人评判我们」，D 项把评判主体从「自我」换成「他人」，无原文依据。
P2

This means simply that it is possible to observe ourselves having a craving, or a happy thought, or even a scary emotion, without reacting in the moment in a way that amplifies the feeling or sends the mind spiraling off into thinking about old memories or anticipating events.

这意味着，我们可以观察自己产生渴望、愉快念头甚至恐惧情绪，而无需在当下作出反应、不放大该情绪，也不让心思滑向旧时记忆或对未来事件的预期。

47

What do we see in today's "attention economy" according to Jenny Odell?

根据Jenny Odell的说法,我们在当今的"注意力经济"中看到了什么?

  • A)Focus on technological advances continuously updates products and services.对技术进步的关注持续更新着产品和服务。
  • B)People's attention is turned into something that can be bought and sold.人们的注意力变成了可以被买卖的东西。
  • C)Businesses profit from selling mobile phones and revolutionizing social media.企业通过销售手机和革新社交媒体获利。
  • D)People tend to purchase products and services without careful thinking.人们倾向于不加仔细思考就购买产品和服务。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念原文 This economy rests on a technological revolution of mobile phones and social media that makes it possible for corporations to target our attention with their content at every moment 说技术革命让公司能持续抢占注意力，没说技术让产品/服务持续更新，A 项偷换了技术革命的作用。
B) ✓ 正确选项 B 几乎是 Odell 原话的一对一改写，主语、动词、对象、属性四要素全对齐，是 according to 型细节题里最干净的同义替换。
  • human attention has been transformed into a commodity that big corporations buy and sell↔People's attention is turned into something that can be bought and sold
C) 偷换概念原文 buy and sell 的宾语是 human attention（人的注意力），C 项把宾语偷换成 mobile phones and social media，是把抽象对象偷换成具体技术，正好踩中考生「具象更易接受」的认知偏误。
D) 无中生有原文谈的是公司买卖「人的注意力」，并未谈消费者本身是否冲动消费；D 项关于「人们购买商品不细想」属凭空生造。
P4

The artist Jenny Odell argues that in our "attention economy" human attention has been transformed into a commodity that big corporations buy and sell.

艺术家 Jenny Odell 论述道：在我们的「注意力经济」中，人的注意力已经被转化成大公司买卖的商品。

48

What happens with the prevalent use of mobile phones or social media?

手机或社交媒体的普遍使用会发生什么?

  • A)They keep distracting users from the here and now.它们不断使用户从此时此地分心。
  • B)They render users incapable of resisting temptation.它们使用户无法抵抗诱惑。
  • C)They rob users of their ability to observe experience.它们剥夺了用户观察体验的能力。
  • D)They tempt users to endorse the attention economy.它们诱使用户认同注意力经济。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确A 项两个核心语义点（keep distracting、from the here and now）与原文 perpetual diversion、from the present moment 一一对应，是定位句的紧贴改写。
  • The devices most people use are a perpetual diversion from the present moment↔They keep distracting users from the here and now
B) 过度推断原文 But mindfulness can help us resist the attention economy 谈的是正念能帮我们抵抗，B 项却说设备让用户「彻底失去抵御诱惑的能力」，把「分心」夸大为「丧失抵抗力」，无原文依据。
C) 张冠李戴原文 our ability to focus, and be fully present, has been stolen 谈被偷走的是「专注、全在当下」的能力，而 capacity to observe experience 才是 observe experience 的能力，C 项把两者混在一起，张冠李戴。
D) 无中生有原文只说设备让人分心、注意力被卖，没说设备诱使用户主动「支持/认同」注意力经济，D 项凭空多出一个「用户主动认同」的环节。
P5

The devices most people use are a perpetual diversion from the present moment.

大多数人使用的这些设备是一种让人持续偏离当下的干扰。

49

What benefit can practicing mindfulness bring to us according to some scholars?

根据一些学者的说法,练习正念能给我们带来什么好处?

  • A)It prevents us from making arbitrary judgments about others.它防止我们对他人做出武断的评判。
  • B)It shifts our attention from material things to spiritual pursuits.它将我们的注意力从物质事物转移到精神追求。
  • C)It is conducive to our forging better interpersonal relationships.它有助于我们建立更好的人际关系。
  • D)It is helpful in regaining our ability to enjoy what we have in life.它有助于恢复我们享受生活中所拥有之物的能力。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴「不作评判」是 P3 谈 mindfulness 个体效用时的内容，而 Q49 问的是 some scholars 的观点；把 P3 的句子嫁接到学者名下属于张冠李戴，且 prevent us from making arbitrary judgments about others 也夸大了原文「自己不评判体验」的限定。
B) 无中生有原文没有把好处归结为「从物质转向精神追求」，B 项凭空生造一组对立维度。
C) ✓ 正确C 项把学者观点收束句 help us build stronger, healthier relationships 完整改写成 conducive to our forging better interpersonal relationships，主体、动作、对象逐项对齐。
  • help us build stronger, healthier relationships↔conducive to our forging better interpersonal relationships
D) 张冠李戴「重拾享受生活的能力」对应 relish the things that make life special, like being together with those we love，是作者自己的论述而非 some scholars 的观点；嫁接到学者名下属于张冠李戴。
P6

Most mindfulness research focuses on the individual benefits of the practice, but some scholars assert that we can not only practice mindfulness for ourselves but also practice it for others. It can help us build stronger, healthier relationships.

多数正念研究关注其个人层面的益处，但一些学者论断：正念不仅可为己练，也可为他人而练；它能帮我们建立更稳固、更健康的人际关系。

50

What does the author suggest we do in another person's presence?

作者建议我们在他人面前做什么?

  • A)Share each other's experience frankly and compassionately.坦诚而富有同情心地分享彼此的经历。
  • B)Pay full attention to establishing close relationships with them.全神贯注于与他们建立亲密关系。
  • C)Be attentive listeners while refraining from making judgments.做专注的倾听者同时避免做出评判。
  • D)Remind them of the distraction from mobile phones and social media.提醒他们注意来自手机和社交媒体的干扰。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反原文 resisting the urge to make the interaction about you 明确要求克制把谈话拉回到「自己」，强调单向倾听对方；A 项却说 share each other's experience 双向分享，与原文要求方向相反。
B) 偷换概念原文要求把全部注意力放在对方的体验上，目的是「以正念之心送出倾听这一礼物」（practicing being fully present in another person's presence）；B 项却把目的偷换成 establishing close relationships with them——这是 Q49 的话题，且原文未把它定为这一场景的目的。
C) ✓ 正确C 项把作者建议的两项核心动作（with complete attention、without judgment）都直接对应到选项的两个分句，是同义改写最紧贴的一项。
  • listening to them with complete attention without judgment↔Be attentive listeners while refraining from making judgments
D) 无中生有原文要求自己 putting away your phone, turning off social media, and setting aside other common distractions，即「自己」放下手机，并未要求去提醒对方；D 项把动作主体从「自己」偷换到「提醒他人」，无原文依据。
P8

It means practicing being fully present in another person's presence and listening to them with complete attention without judgment, while resisting the urge to make the interaction about you.

这意味着练习在另一个人面前全然临在，全神贯注地倾听他们，不作评判，同时克制把对话焦点拉回到自己身上的冲动。
