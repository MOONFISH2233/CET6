范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 162 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Living in an increasingly competitive world, college students should better prepare themselves to confront various challenges. This preparation is no longer optional but essential, for those who wait passively will inevitably be left behind.

To begin with, students ought to equip themselves with solid knowledge and practical skills rather than merely chasing high marks. For instance, in today's fierce job market, employers value candidates who can communicate, cooperate and solve real problems. What is more, developing a resilient mindset matters just as much. I still remember failing my first internship interview; instead of giving up, I reflected on my weaknesses, practised repeatedly, and finally secured a position the next semester. That experience taught me that challenges are conquered by those who keep improving.

Therefore, it is high time that college students stopped drifting and started planning ahead. By strengthening both our abilities and our willpower, we can turn the pressure of competition into a driving force and stride confidently into the future.
