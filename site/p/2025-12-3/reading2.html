原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

The other day I had to log into a service I hadn't used before. Since I was a new user, the website decided that it needed to check I wasn't a robot and so set me a Captcha test to determine whether the user is a person or a machine.

前几天，我不得不登录一个以前从未使用过的服务。由于我是新用户，网站认定有必要确认我不是机器人，于是给我设置了一项验证码测试，以判断用户是人还是机器。

P2

I was presented with an image of a roadside scene over which was laid a grid. My "challenge" was to click on each cell in the grid that contained a traffic sign. I did so, a bit irritated. Then I was presented with another image and another grid — also with a request to identify road signs. Like a lamb, I complied, after which the website accepted my input.

我看到的是一幅道路旁场景的图片，上面覆盖着一个网格。我的“挑战”是点击网格中每一个包含交通标志的单元格。我照做了，心里有些恼火。接着，网站又给我展示了另一幅图片和另一个网格，同样要求我找出道路标志。我像只待宰的羔羊一样照办了，之后网站接受了我的输入。

P3

And then I realised what I had been doing was adding to a dataset for training the machine-learning software that guides self-driving cars. So, to gain access to an automated service that will benefit financially from my input, I first have to do some unpaid labor to help improve the performance of autonomous vehicles.

随后我意识到，我刚才所做的一切，实际上是在为训练自动驾驶汽车所使用的机器学习软件添加数据集。也就是说，为了获得一项会从我的输入中获得经济利益的自动化服务，我首先必须做一些无偿劳动，帮助提升自动驾驶汽车的性能。

P4

Neat, eh? But note also the delicious additional irony that the Captcha is described as an "automated Turing test". The Turing test was conceived, you may recall, as a way of enabling humans to determine whether a machine could respond in such a way that one couldn't tell whether it was a human or a robot. So we have wandered into a world in which machines make us do tricks to prove that we are humans!

很妙吧？但还要注意其中那种令人回味的额外讽刺：验证码被称为“自动图灵测试”。你可能还记得，图灵测试的构想是让人类能够判断一台机器是否能以某种方式作出回应，使人无法分辨它究竟是人还是机器人。因此，我们已经误入了这样一个世界：机器让我们表演各种把戏，以证明我们是人类！

P5

The strangest aspect of this unparalleled shift is how under-discussed it has been. The metaphor of the boiling frog comes to mind. Have we become so subtly conditioned by digital technology that we don't see what's been happening to us? Have we been conditioned to accept a world governed by "smart" tech, trading convenience to the point where we become a bit like machines ourselves?

这一史无前例的转变最奇怪之处，在于人们几乎没有讨论过它。人们不禁会想到“温水煮青蛙”的比喻。我们是否已经受到数字技术如此悄无声息的影响，以至于看不见它一直在对我们做什么？我们是否已经习惯于接受一个由“智能”技术支配的世界，不断用便利进行交换，直到我们自己也变得有点像机器？

P6

In a recent startling and thoughtful book, two scholars — Brett Frischmann, a law professor, and Evan Selinger, a philosopher — argue that the answer to that question is "yes".

在最近出版的一本令人震撼且发人深省的书中，两位学者——法学教授Brett Frischmann和哲学家Evan Selinger——认为，对这个问题的答案是“是的”。

P7

And they're right. There's nothing technophobic(技术恐惧的) about that. We shape our tools and then our tools shape us. Technology is supposed to provide tools that serve human ends. But, as the machine-learning Captcha demonstrates, a significant proportion of digital tech now sees (and uses) humans as means to ends that are not ours. In the process, they reduce us to the status of cheery rats running on treadmills(踏车) designed by people who do not have our interests at heart. So back to the frog metaphor. Are we smart enough to jump out before it's too late? You don't even have to Google it to know the answer.

他们说得对。这并不意味着技术恐惧。我们塑造工具，然后工具又塑造我们。技术本应提供服务于人类目标的工具。但是，正如机器学习验证码所展示的那样，如今相当大一部分数字技术把人类视为并利用为实现并不属于我们的目标的手段。在这一过程中，它们把我们降低成一群兴高采烈地在跑步机上奔跑的老鼠，而这些跑步机是由那些根本不把我们的利益放在心上的人设计的。所以，让我们回到青蛙的比喻。我们是否足够聪明，能在为时已晚之前跳出去？你甚至不必用Google搜索，也能知道答案。

51

How did the author respond to the Captcha test?

作者是如何回应验证码测试的?

  • A)He clicked on the cells randomly.他随机点击了单元格。
  • B)He accepted the challenge readily.他欣然接受了挑战。
  • C)He completed it confidently.他自信地完成了测试。
  • D)He worked on it submissively.他顺从地完成了测试。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 偷换概念clicked on the cells randomly 说作者随机点，但原文 My "challenge" was to click on each cell in the grid that contained a traffic sign. I did so 显示作者是按规则点含交通标志的格子，并不是随机。
B) 与原文相反accepted the challenge readily 说"欣然接受"，但原文 I did so, a bit irritated 明示作者是带着不快接受的，二者情绪极性相反。
C) 无中生有completed it confidently 说"自信地完成"，但原文从未描写作者的信心，只说他不情愿地照办；自信纯属添加。
D) ✓ 正确预判要的就是"顺从地照做"，He worked on it submissively. 是唯一把这层顺从语气保留下来的选项。
  • Like a lamb, I complied↔submissively
P2

Like a lamb, I complied, after which the website accepted my input.

我像一只羔羊一样照办了，之后网站接受了我的输入。

52

What is ironic about the Captcha test?

验证码测试的讽刺之处是什么?

  • A)A machine can respond to the test as well as humans do.机器能像人类一样回应测试。
  • B)A machine can outperform humans in object identification.机器在物体识别方面的表现能超过人类。
  • C)A test designed to test machines is now used by machines to test humans.一项原本设计用来测试机器的测试现在被机器用来测试人类。
  • D)A test conceived to tell a human from a robot can now do tricks humans can't.一项原本用于区分人类和机器人的测试现在能做人类做不到的事情。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 答非所问A machine can respond to the test as well as humans do 是"机器答得和人一样好"，但原文讽刺的是"角色反转"，不是机器表现优秀。
B) 无中生有A machine can outperform humans in object identification 说"机器在物体识别上胜过人类"，但原文没做这种性能比较，纯属添加。
C) ✓ 正确预判要的就是"测机器→测人"的角色反转，A test designed to test machines is now used by machines to test humans. 一句话把这一组对调说清楚。
  • The Turing test was conceived, you may recall, as a way of enabling humans to determine whether a machine could respond↔A test designed to test machines
  • machines make us do tricks to prove that we are humans↔now used by machines to test humans
D) 张冠李戴A test conceived to tell a human from a robot can now do tricks humans can't 说"测试现在能耍出人耍不出的把戏"，主体被换成了"测试"，但原文是 machines make us do tricks——机器让我们耍把戏，主体错位。
P4

But note also the delicious additional irony that the Captcha is described as an "automated Turing test". The Turing test was conceived, you may recall, as a way of enabling humans to determine whether a machine could respond in such a way that one couldn't tell whether it was a human or a robot. So we have wandered into a world in which machines make us do tricks to prove that we are humans!

但还要注意一个绝妙的额外讽刺：Captcha 被称作"自动化的图灵测试"。你或许记得，图灵测试当初的设想，是让人类来判断机器能否做出让人分不清是人还是机器人的回应。结果，我们如今竟漂入了这样一个世界——机器反过来让我们耍把戏，以证明我们是人类！

53

Why does the author use the metaphor of the boiling frog?

作者为什么使用温水煮青蛙的比喻?

  • A)To show that humans are unwilling to trade convenience for control by smart tech.为了表明人类不愿意用便利换取对智能技术的控制。
  • B)To show that humans are unaware of the potential danger brought by smart tech.为了表明人类没有意识到智能技术带来的潜在危险。
  • C)To question whether humans are conditioned to accept digital tech.为了质疑人类是否习惯于接受数字技术。
  • D)To question whether humans are as vulnerable as the boiling frog.为了质疑人类是否像被煮的青蛙一样脆弱。
解析
B（推断题）
A) 与原文相反humans are unwilling to trade convenience for control by smart tech 说人"不愿"用便利换控制，但原文 trading convenience to the point where we become a bit like machines ourselves 显示人正在交换，不是不愿；极性翻转。
B) ✓ 正确温水煮青蛙寓意的核心就是"察觉不到"，To show that humans are unaware of the potential danger brought by smart tech. 同时覆盖"潜在危险"与"未察觉"两层，最贴合作者意图。
  • Have we become so subtly conditioned by digital technology that we don't see what's been happening to us?↔humans are unaware of the potential danger brought by smart tech
  • so subtly conditioned by digital technology↔potential danger brought by smart tech
C) 偷换概念To question whether humans are conditioned to accept digital tech 说作者"质疑"人是否被驯化，但原文虽用反问，立场是倾向于"已被驯化"，并不是中立质疑。
D) 偷换概念To question whether humans are as vulnerable as the boiling frog 把比喻意图框成"人是否像青蛙一样脆弱"，但温水煮青蛙寓意的核心是"察觉不到"而非"脆弱"，焦点跑偏。
P5

The metaphor of the boiling frog comes to mind. Have we become so subtly conditioned by digital technology that we don't see what's been happening to us?

脑海里浮现出"温水煮青蛙"的比喻。我们是不是已经被数字技术悄悄驯化，以至于看不到自己身上正在发生什么？

Why does the author think that Brett Frischmann and Evan Selinger are right?

作者为什么认为Brett Frischmann和Evan Selinger是对的?

  • A)A large part of digital tech is now making use of humans to meet its own ends.很大一部分数字技术现在正在利用人类来实现其自身目的。
  • B)Humans are now fast locked in a race against digital tech to control the world.人类现在正与数字技术展开一场争夺世界控制权的竞赛。
  • C)Digital tech can serve human needs in ways previously thought impossible.数字技术能以以前认为不可能的方式满足人类需求。
  • D)Digital tech is now doing thinking while humans are doing repetitive work.数字技术现在在进行思考,而人类在做重复性工作。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判要的是"技术把人当工具达成自身目的"，A large part of digital tech is now making use of humans to meet its own ends. 同时覆盖"相当大一部分"+"利用人"+"达成自身目的"三个要点，与原文逐一吻合。
  • a significant proportion of digital tech↔A large part of digital tech
  • now sees (and uses) humans as means to ends that are not ours↔now making use of humans to meet its own ends
B) 无中生有Humans are now fast locked in a race against digital tech to control the world 说"人在与数字技术赛跑争夺世界控制"，但原文从未把人与技术描绘成赛跑或争控关系。
C) 与原文相反Digital tech can serve human needs in ways previously thought impossible 在赞美技术服务人类的能力，但作者 But 之后说的恰是相反——技术正反过来利用人。
D) 过度推断Digital tech is now doing thinking while humans are doing repetitive work 把人机的工作分工说成"技术思考、人重复劳动"，原文虽然提到作者做了 some unpaid labor，但并未把它对立到"技术负责思考"的层面。
P7

And they're right. There's nothing technophobic about that. We shape our tools and then our tools shape us. Technology is supposed to provide tools that serve human ends. But, as the machine-learning Captcha demonstrates, a significant proportion of digital tech now sees (and uses) humans as means to ends that are not ours.

他们说得没错。这并不是什么技术恐惧。我们塑造工具，反过来工具也塑造我们。技术本应提供服务于人类目的的工具。但是，正如机器学习版 Captcha 所展示的那样，相当一部分数字技术如今把人类当作（并加以利用为）实现非人类目的之手段。

55

What does the author imply by asking "Are we smart enough to jump out before it's too late?" at the end of the passage?

作者在文章结尾问"我们是否足够聪明,能在为时已晚之前跳出来?"这暗示了什么?

  • A)It is not sensible to expect digital tech to take humans' interest to heart.期望数字技术将人类利益放在心上是不明智的。
  • B)It is hard to say whether technology or humans will get the upper hand.很难说技术还是人类会占上风。
  • C)We may have to search the web to get the answer to the question.我们可能必须搜索网络才能得到这个问题的答案。
  • D)We may not awake in time to the danger digital tech poses to us.我们可能无法及时意识到数字技术对我们构成的危险。
解析
D（推断题）
A) 答非所问It is not sensible to expect digital tech to take humans' interest to heart 在评判"指望技术为人着想是否明智"，对象是技术；但题干反问的主语是 we——问的是人是否够聪明，主体偏移。
B) 偷换概念It is hard to say whether technology or humans will get the upper hand 把作者立场说成"难说人机谁占上风"，但原文反问 + 反讽尾句强烈暗示作者倾向悲观，并非中立。
C) 与原文相反We may have to search the web to get the answer to the question 字面理解了 You don't even have to Google it——把"不用搜也知道"反向解成"必须搜才知道"。
D) ✓ 正确预判要的是"恐怕来不及醒悟"，We may not awake in time to the danger digital tech poses to us. 把"反问 → 否定断言"与"温水煮青蛙 → 数字技术的危险"两层一起兜住，是唯一贴着原文走一步的推断。
  • Are we smart enough to jump out before it's too late?↔We may not awake in time to the danger digital tech poses to us
  • Have we become so subtly conditioned by digital technology that we don't see what's been happening to us?↔the danger digital tech poses to us
P7

So back to the frog metaphor. Are we smart enough to jump out before it's too late? You don't even have to Google it to know the answer.

那么回到青蛙的比喻。我们够聪明，能在为时已晚之前跳出来吗？这个问题，你甚至都不用上网搜就能知道答案。
