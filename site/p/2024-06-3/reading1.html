原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Sarcasm and jazz have something surprisingly in common: You know them when you hear them. Sarcasm is mostly understood through tone of voice, which is used to portray the opposite of the literal words. For example, when someone says, "Well, that's exactly what I need right now," their tone can tell you it's not what they need at all.

讽刺和爵士乐有一个令人意外的共同点：听到它们时，你就能辨认出来。讽刺主要通过语气来理解，语气被用来表达与字面意思相反的含义。例如，当有人说：“嗯，这正是我现在需要的东西。”他的语气可能会告诉你，那根本不是他所需要的。

P2

Most frequently, sarcasm highlights an irritation or is, quite simply, mean.

讽刺最常见的作用是凸显恼怒，或者说得简单些，就是刻薄。

P3

If you want to be happier and improve your relationships, cut out sarcasm. Why? Because sarcasm is actually hostility disguised as humor.

如果你想变得更快乐并改善人际关系，那就戒掉讽刺。为什么？因为讽刺实际上是伪装成幽默的敌意。

P4

Despite smiling outwardly, many people who receive sarcastic comments feel put down and often think the sarcastic person is rude, or contemptible. Indeed, it's not surprising that the origin of the word sarcasm derives from the Greek word "sarkazein" which literally means "to tear or strip the flesh off." Hence, it's no wonder that sarcasm is often preceded by the word "cutting" and that it hurts.

尽管表面上带着微笑，许多听到讽刺话语的人却感到受辱，常常认为讽刺者粗鲁或令人鄙夷。事实上，sarcasm 一词源自希腊语“sarkazein”，其字面意思是“撕裂或剥去皮肉”，这并不令人意外。因此，讽刺常常被“尖刻”一词修饰，也难怪它会令人感到受伤。

P5

What's more, since actions strongly determine thoughts and feelings, when a person consistently acts sarcastically it may only serve to heighten their underlying hostility and insecurity. After all, when you come right down to it, sarcasm can be used as a subtle form of bullying—and most bullies are angry, insecure, or cowardly.

此外，由于行为会强烈影响思想和感受，一个人如果一贯以讽刺的方式行事，可能只会加剧其潜在的敌意和不安全感。毕竟，归根结底，讽刺可以被用作一种隐蔽的欺凌形式，而大多数欺凌者都愤怒、缺乏安全感或怯懦。

P6

Alternatively, when a person stops voicing negative comments, especially sarcastic ones, they may soon start to feel happier and more self-confident. Also, other people in their life benefit even more because they no longer have to hear the emotionally hurtful language of sarcasm.

相反，当一个人不再说消极的话，尤其是不再说讽刺的话时，他们可能很快就会开始感到更加快乐，也更加自信。此外，他们生活中的其他人获益更多，因为他们不再需要听到在情感上具有伤害性的讽刺言语。

P7

Now, I'm not saying all sarcasm is bad. It may just be better used sparingly—like a potent spice in cooking. Too much of the spice, and the dish will be overwhelmed by it. Similarly, an occasional dash of sarcastic wit can spice up a chat and add an element of humor to it. But a big or steady serving of sarcasm will overwhelm the emotional flavor of any conversation and can taste very bitter to its recipient.

我并不是说所有的讽刺都不好。或许少用一些会更好，就像烹饪中使用强烈的香料一样。香料放得太多，整道菜都会被它的味道压过。同样，偶尔来一点讽刺性的机智，可以为聊天增添情趣和幽默元素。但大剂量或持续不断的讽刺，会压过任何谈话的情感基调，让听者尝到十分苦涩的滋味。

P8

So, tone down the sarcasm and work on clever wit instead, which is usually without any hostility and thus more appreciated by those you're communicating with. In essence, sarcasm is easy while true, harmless wit takes talent.

所以，收敛一些讽刺，转而运用巧妙的机智，因为机智通常不带有敌意，因此更容易得到交流对象的欣赏。从本质上说，讽刺很容易，而真正无害的机智则需要才华。

P9

Thus, the main difference between wit and sarcasm is that, as already stated, sarcasm is often hostility disguised as humor. It can be intended to hurt and is often bitter and biting. Witty statements are usually in response to someone's unhelpful remarks or behaviors, and the intent is to untangle and clarify the issue by emphasizing its absurdities. Sarcastic statements are expressed in a cutting manner; witty remarks are delivered with undisguised and harmless humor.

因此，机智与讽刺之间的主要区别在于，正如前文所述，讽刺常常是伪装成幽默的敌意。它可能意在伤人，而且往往尖酸刻薄、咄咄逼人。机智的话语通常是针对某人无益的言论或行为作出的回应，其目的在于通过强调其中的荒谬之处，使问题变得清楚明白并得到理顺。讽刺的话语以尖刻的方式表达；机智的评论则以不加掩饰而又无害的幽默方式呈现。

解析逐题详解

46

Why does the author say sarcasm and jazz have something surprisingly in common?

作者为什么说讽刺和爵士乐有某种令人惊讶的共同点?

  • A)Both are recognized when heard.两者在被听到时都能被识别出来。
  • B)Both have exactly the same tone.两者有完全相同的语调。
  • C)Both mean the opposite of what they appear to.两者的含义都与其表面意思相反。
  • D)Both have hidden in them an evident irritation.两者都隐藏着明显的恼怒情绪。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确Both are recognized when heard. 是 You know them when you hear them. 的标准被动同义改写，与预判完全一致。
  • You know them when you hear them.↔Both are recognized when heard.
B) 无中生有原文只说"sarcasm 借 tone of voice 传达"，从未说 sarcasm 与 jazz 拥有相同语气。
C) 张冠李戴原文 Sarcasm is mostly understood through tone of voice, which is used to portray the opposite of the literal words. 把"言反其意"只归于 sarcasm，与 jazz 无关。
D) 张冠李戴Most frequently, sarcasm highlights an irritation 只说 sarcasm 反映 irritation，与 jazz 无关。
P1

Sarcasm and jazz have something surprisingly in common: You know them when you hear them.

讽刺和爵士乐有一个出人意料的共同点：你一听就能认出它们。

47

How do many people feel when they hear sarcastic comments?

许多人在听到讽刺性评论时有什么感受?

  • A)They feel hostile towards the sarcastic person.他们对讽刺者感到敌意。
  • B)They feel belittled and disrespected.他们感到被贬低和不被尊重。
  • C)They feel a strong urge to retaliate.他们感到强烈的报复冲动。
  • D)They feel incapable of disguising their irritation.他们感到无法掩饰自己的恼怒。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴原文 sarcasm is actually hostility disguised as humor 把 hostility 归于讽刺者，不是接收者的感受。
B) ✓ 正确They feel belittled and disrespected. 恰是 feel put down 的双词同义改写，与预判一致。
  • feel put down↔They feel belittled and disrespected.
  • think the sarcastic person is rude, or contemptible↔disrespected
C) 无中生有原文从未提及接收者想报复，文章主张反而是"停掉讽刺"以缓和关系。
D) 与原文相反Despite smiling outwardly 恰恰是说接收者表面在笑——即"能掩饰"，与 incapable of disguising 相反。
P4

Despite smiling outwardly, many people who receive sarcastic comments feel put down and often think the sarcastic person is rude, or contemptible.

尽管表面带笑，许多人在听到讽刺的话时仍会感觉被贬低，并常常认为讽刺他们的人粗鲁或可鄙。

48

What happens when a person consistently acts sarcastically?

当一个人持续以讽刺方式行事时会发生什么?

  • A)They feel their dignity greatly heightened.他们感到自己的尊严大大提高。
  • B)They feel increasingly insecure and hostile.他们感到越来越不安全和充满敌意。
  • C)They endure hostility under the disguise of humor.他们在幽默的伪装下忍受敌意。
  • D)They taste bitterness even in pleasant interactions.他们即使在愉快的互动中也感到苦涩。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反原文加深的是 hostility/insecurity 等负面情绪，而非 dignity（尊严）；正相反，长期讽刺者被视为隐性霸凌者，尊严下滑。
B) ✓ 正确They feel increasingly insecure and hostile. 把"程度加深"用 increasingly 还原，把名词敌意/不安全感转回形容词，恰是预判同义。
  • heighten their underlying hostility and insecurity↔They feel increasingly insecure and hostile.
C) 张冠李戴原文 hostility disguised as humor 的施事是讽刺者本人；这里把"在 humor 伪装下被迫忍受 hostility"按到讽刺者身上，搞反了角色。
D) 偷换概念原文 can taste very bitter to its recipient 的 bitter 是接收者的感觉，且语境是"过量讽刺"，从未说"持续讽刺者在愉快互动中也尝苦"。
P5

when a person consistently acts sarcastically it may only serve to heighten their underlying hostility and insecurity.

当一个人一贯地讽刺人，这种行为只会加深他内心潜藏的敌意与不安全感。

49

What does the author say about people quitting sarcastic comments?

作者对人们停止讽刺性评论有何看法?

  • A)It makes others happier and more self-confident.这让其他人更快乐、更自信。
  • B)It restrains them from being irritating and bullying.这约束他们不再恼人和欺凌。
  • C)It benefits not only themselves but also those around them.这不仅有益于他们自己,也有益于周围的人。
  • D)It shields them from negative comments and outright hostility.这使他们免受负面评论和公然敌意的影响。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 以偏概全原文 happier and more self-confident 的主语是讽刺者本人，A 只说让 others 更乐更自信，丢掉了"自己"那一半。
B) 无中生有原文虽在 can be used as a subtle form of bullying 提及 bullying，但 P6 描述停讽效果时根本没出现"约束自己别再霸凌"的说法。
C) ✓ 正确It benefits not only themselves but also those around them. 用 not only...but also 同时呼应 may soon start to feel happier 与 other people in their life benefit even more，与预判一致。
  • they may soon start to feel happier and more self-confident↔not only themselves
  • other people in their life benefit even more↔but also those around them
D) 与原文相反原文是"停讽者不再向外发讽刺、让他人不必听到"，并非"屏蔽讽刺者本人免受外界负面话语"，方向相反。
P6

Alternatively, when a person stops voicing negative comments, especially sarcastic ones, they may soon start to feel happier and more self-confident. Also, other people in their life benefit even more because they no longer have to hear the emotionally hurtful language of sarcasm.

反过来说，当一个人不再说讽刺等负面话语时，他自己很快会更快乐、更自信；而且，他生活中的其他人受益更多，因为他们再也不必听到讽刺这种伤人的话。

50

What is the chief difference between a speaker's wit and sarcasm?

说话者的机智和讽刺之间的主要区别是什么?

  • A)Their clarity.它们的清晰度。
  • B)Their appreciation.它们的欣赏程度。
  • C)Their emphasis.它们的侧重点。
  • D)Their intention.它们的意图。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 以偏概全untangle and clarify the issue 只出现在 wit 一侧；讽刺侧从未谈到 clarity 这一维度。
B) 偷换概念原文 more appreciated by those you're communicating with 谈的是"听者是否欣赏"，是结果而非区别本身。
C) 以偏概全by emphasizing its absurdities 中 emphasizing 仅是 wit 实现意图的手段，不是对比双方共有的维度。
D) ✓ 正确Their intention. 把原文 sarcasm 的 be intended to hurt 与 wit 的 intent is to untangle and clarify 抽象为共同维度 intention，与预判一致。
  • It can be intended to hurt↔Their intention.
  • the intent is to untangle and clarify the issue↔Their intention.
P9

Thus, the main difference between wit and sarcasm is that, as already stated, sarcasm is often hostility disguised as humor. It can be intended to hurt and is often bitter and biting. Witty statements are usually in response to someone's unhelpful remarks or behaviors, and the intent is to untangle and clarify the issue by emphasizing its absurdities.

于是，机智与讽刺的主要区别在于：讽刺常是"敌意伪装成幽默"，可能意在伤人；而机智之语意在厘清问题、点出其荒谬之处。
