原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Variability is crucially important for learning new skills. Consider learning how to serve in tennis. Should you always practise serving from the exactly same location on the court, aiming at the same spot? Although practising in more variable conditions will be slower at first, it will likely make you a better tennis player in the end. This is because variability leads to better generalisation of what is learned.

可变性对于学习新技能至关重要。以学习如何在网球比赛中发球为例。你是否应该总是在球场上完全相同的位置练习发球，并瞄准同一个点？虽然在更加多变的条件下练习起初会比较慢，但最终很可能会使你成为更优秀的网球运动员。这是因为可变性会使所学内容得到更好的泛化。

P2

This principle is found in many domains, including speech perception and learning categories. For instance, infants will struggle to learn the category "dog" if they are only exposed to Chihuahuas, instead of many different kinds of dogs.

这一原则存在于许多领域，包括语音感知和类别学习。例如，如果婴儿接触到的只有吉娃娃，而不是许多不同种类的狗，那么他们将很难学会“狗”这一类别。

P3

"There are over ten different names for this basic principle," says Limor Raviv, the senior investigator of a recent study. "Learning from less variable input is often fast, but may fail to generalise to new stimuli."

最近一项研究的高级研究员利莫尔·拉维夫说：“这一基本原则有十多个不同的名称。根据可变性较小的输入进行学习通常速度较快，但可能无法泛化到新的刺激上。”

P4

To identify key patterns and understand the underlying principles of variability effects, Raviv and her colleagues reviewed over 150 studies on variability and generalisation across fields, including computer science, linguistics, categorisation, visual perception and formal education.

为了确定关键模式并理解可变性效应背后的基本原则，拉维夫和她的同事们对来自计算机科学、语言学、分类、视觉感知和正规教育等领域的150多项有关可变性和泛化的研究进行了综述。

P5

The researchers discovered that, across studies, the term variability can refer to at least four different kinds of variability, such as set size and scheduling. "These four kinds of variability have never been directly compared—which means that we currently don't know which is most effective for learning," says Raviv.

研究人员发现，在不同的研究中，“可变性”一词至少可以指四种不同类型的可变性，例如集合大小和安排方式。拉维夫说：“这四种可变性从未被直接比较过，这意味着我们目前还不知道哪一种对学习最有效。”

P6

The impact of variability depends on whether it is relevant to the task or not. But according to the 'Mr. Miyagi principle', practising seemingly unrelated skills may actually benefit learning of other skills.

可变性的影响取决于它是否与任务相关。但根据“宫城先生原则”，练习看似无关的技能实际上可能有益于其他技能的学习。

P7

But why does variability impact learning and generalisation? One theory is that more variable input can highlight which aspects of a task are relevant and which are not.

但是，为什么可变性会影响学习和泛化呢？一种理论认为，可变性更大的输入能够凸显任务中哪些方面是相关的，哪些方面则不相关。

P8

Another theory is that greater variability leads to broader generalisations. This is because variability will represent the real world better, including atypical(非典型的) examples.

另一种理论认为，更大的可变性会带来更广泛的泛化。这是因为可变性能够更好地呈现现实世界，包括非典型的例子。

P9

A third reason has to do with the way memory works: when training is variable, learners are forced to actively reconstruct their memories.

第三个原因与记忆的运作方式有关：当训练具有可变性时，学习者会被迫主动重建自己的记忆。

P10

"Understanding the impact of variability is important for literally every aspect of our daily life. Beyond affecting the way we learn language, motor skills, and categories, it even has an impact on our social lives," explains Raviv. "For example, face recognition is affected by whether people grew up in a small community or in a larger community. Exposure to fewer faces during childhood is associated with diminished face memory."

拉维夫解释说：“了解可变性的影响对于我们日常生活的每一个方面都确实很重要。除了影响我们学习语言、运动技能和类别的方式之外，它甚至还会影响我们的社交生活。例如，人脸识别会受到人们是在小型社区还是较大社区中长大的影响。童年时期接触较少的人脸与面孔记忆能力减弱有关。”

P11

"We hope this work will spark people's curiosity and generate more work on the topic," concludes Raviv. "Our paper raises a lot of open questions. Can we find similar effects of variability beyond the brain, for instance, in the immune system?"

拉维夫总结道：“我们希望这项工作能够激发人们的好奇心，并推动开展更多有关这一主题的研究。我们的论文提出了许多尚未解决的问题。我们能否在大脑之外发现类似的可变性效应，例如在免疫系统中？”

解析逐题详解

51

What does the passage say about infants learning the category "dog" if they are exposed to Chihuahuas only?

如果婴儿只接触吉娃娃,文章对婴儿学习"狗"这一类别有何说法?

  • A)They will encounter some degree of difficulty.他们会遇到一定程度的困难。
  • B)They will try to categorise other objects first.他们会先尝试对其他物体进行分类。
  • C)They will prefer Chihuahuas to other dog species.他们会更喜欢吉娃娃而非其他犬种。
  • D)They will imagine Chihuahuas in various conditions.他们会想象吉娃娃在各种情况下的样子。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判“会有学习困难”与 encounter some degree of difficulty 在语义上严格对应，无需推断，直接锁定 A。
  • struggle to learn the category "dog"↔encounter some degree of difficulty
B) 无中生有They will try to categorise other objects first 在 P2 找不到任何对应——原文只说他们 struggle to learn“狗”，未说他们会先去分类别的东西。
C) 偷换概念They will prefer Chihuahuas to other dog species 把原文“只接触到吉娃娃”曲解为“偏好吉娃娃”——原文谈的是输入受限导致学习困难，与个人偏好无关。
D) 张冠李戴They will imagine Chihuahuas in various conditions 把第 1 段“在多变条件下练习”的概念硬塞到婴儿身上——原文中婴儿并不会主动 imagine，“in various conditions”是上一段网球训练的语境。
P2

For instance, infants will struggle to learn the category "dog" if they are only exposed to Chihuahuas, instead of many different kinds of dogs.

举例来说，如果婴儿只接触吉娃娃，而不是接触多种不同种类的狗，他们将很难学会“狗”这一类别。

52

What does Raviv say about the four different kinds of variability?

Raviv对四种不同类型的变异性有何说法?

  • A)Which of them is most relevant to the task at hand is to be confirmed.其中哪一种与手头的任务最相关有待确认。
  • B)Why they have an impact on learning is far from being understood.它们为何对学习产生影响远未被理解。
  • C)Why they have never been directly compared remains a mystery.它们为何从未被直接比较过仍是一个谜。
  • D)Which of them is most conducive to learning is yet to be identified.其中哪一种最有利于学习尚待确定。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 偷换概念Which of them is most relevant to the task at hand is to be confirmed 把原文的 most effective for learning（对学习最有效）偷换为 most relevant to the task at hand（对手头任务最相关）——relevant to the task 是第 6 段的话题，不是第 5 段 Raviv 引语的内容。
B) 答非所问Why they have an impact on learning is far from being understood 是关于“机制为何”的论断，不是 Raviv 在第 5 段就四种 variability 所说的话；况且第 7–9 段恰恰给出了三种 theory，“远未被理解”与原文也不符。
C) 过度推断Why they have never been directly compared remains a mystery 把原文 These four kinds of variability have never been directly compared 的事实（“未被比较”）扭曲成“为何未被比较仍是谜”——原文从未说“为何未被比较”是个谜。
D) ✓ 正确无论是“目前不知→尚待确认”的含蓄否定，还是“最有效→最有助益”的形容词替换，都精准落在 D 项措辞上，锁定 D。
  • we currently don't know which is most effective for learning↔Which of them is most conducive to learning is yet to be identified
  • most effective for learning↔most conducive to learning
P5

"These four kinds of variability have never been directly compared—which means that we currently don't know which is most effective for learning," says Raviv.

Raviv 说：“这四种 variability 从未被直接比较过——这意味着我们目前还不知道哪种对学习最有效。”

53

How does one of the theories explain the importance of variability for learning new skills?

其中一种理论如何解释变异性对学习新技能的重要性?

  • A)Learners regard variable training as typical of what happens in the real world.学习者认为变化训练是现实世界中发生情况的典型代表。
  • B)Learners receiving variable training are compelled to reorganise their memories.接受变化训练的学习者被迫重组其记忆。
  • C)Learners pay attention to the relevant aspects of a task and ignore those irrelevant.学习者关注任务的相关方面并忽略不相关的方面。
  • D)Learners focus on related skills instead of wasting time and effort on unrelated ones.学习者专注于相关技能而不是在不相关技能上浪费时间和精力。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反Learners regard variable training as typical of what happens in the real world 把 variability will represent the real world better, including atypical examples 颠倒——原文是“多变训练能更好地代表真实世界（包含非典型例子）”，并非“学习者把多变训练视为典型”。
B) ✓ 正确条件状语从句→v-ing 定语、被动词组→被动词组、动词短语→动词短语三层替换齐全，正确项 B 与定位句构成一对一映射。
  • learners are forced to actively reconstruct their memories↔Learners receiving variable training are compelled to reorganise their memories
  • when training is variable↔receiving variable training
C) 偷换概念Learners pay attention to the relevant aspects of a task and ignore those irrelevant 把 more variable input can highlight which aspects of a task are relevant and which are not 偷换主语——原文是“多变输入会突显相关/不相关方面”，并非“学习者主动关注/忽略”。
D) 与原文相反Learners focus on related skills instead of wasting time and effort on unrelated ones 把 practising seemingly unrelated skills may actually benefit learning of other skills 的意思颠倒——原文是“练习看似无关的技能也能助益”，选项却说“专注相关技能而不浪费在无关技能上”，恰恰相反。
P9

A third reason has to do with the way memory works: when training is variable, learners are forced to actively reconstruct their memories.

第三个原因与记忆的运作方式有关：当训练具有多变性时，学习者被迫主动地重构他们的记忆。

What does the passage say about face recognition?

文章对面孔识别有何说法?

  • A)People growing up in a small community may find it easy to remember familiar faces.在小社区长大的人可能会发现记住熟悉面孔很容易。
  • B)Face recognition has a significant impact on literally every aspect of our social lives.面孔识别对我们社会生活的几乎每个方面都有重大影响。
  • C)People growing up in a large community can readily recognise any individual faces.在大社区长大的人能够轻易识别任何个体面孔。
  • D)The size of the community people grow up in impacts their face recognition ability.人们成长所在社区的规模影响其面孔识别能力。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反People growing up in a small community may find it easy to remember familiar faces 给“小社区”贴上“易记熟人脸”的标签，但 Exposure to fewer faces during childhood is associated with diminished face memory 恰恰说“接触少→记忆减弱”，与 A 的方向相反；且原文没区分 familiar faces。
B) 张冠李戴Face recognition has a significant impact on literally every aspect of our social lives 把“variability 影响每一方面”安到 face recognition 头上——原文 Understanding the impact of variability is important for literally every aspect of our daily life 的主语是 variability，不是 face recognition。
C) 过度推断People growing up in a large community can readily recognise any individual faces 用 any individual faces 把原文中性的“识别能力受影响”夸大为“能轻松识别任何个人面孔”，是绝对化推断。
D) ✓ 正确D 既保留了“社区规模→识别能力”的因果，也保留了“人群”层面的中性陈述，没有像 A/C 那样把因果方向具体化、特例化，最贴近原文。
  • face recognition is affected by whether people grew up in a small community or in a larger community↔The size of the community people grow up in impacts their face recognition ability
P10

For example, face recognition is affected by whether people grew up in a small community or in a larger community.

例如，面孔识别会受到人们是在小社区还是大社区长大这一因素的影响。

What does Raviv hope to do with their research work?

Raviv希望通过其研究工作做什么?

  • A)Highlight which aspects of a task are relevant and which are not to learning a skill.强调任务的哪些方面与学习技能相关、哪些方面不相关。
  • B)Use the principle of variability in teaching seemingly unrelated skills in education.在教育中运用变异性原则教授看似无关的技能。
  • C)Arouse people's interest in variability and stimulate more research on the topic.激发人们对变异性的兴趣并促进该主题的更多研究。
  • D)Apply the principle of variability to such fields of study as the immune system.将变异性原则应用于免疫系统等研究领域。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 答非所问Highlight which aspects of a task are relevant and which are not to learning a skill 是 more variable input can highlight which aspects of a task are relevant and which are not 中第 1 个理论的措辞，并非 Raviv 在末段表达的研究愿景。
B) 答非所问Use the principle of variability in teaching seemingly unrelated skills in education 取自 practising seemingly unrelated skills may actually benefit learning of other skills 的 Mr. Miyagi 原则，也并非 Raviv 末段表达的愿景。
C) ✓ 正确C 把原文两个并列愿望 spark...curiosity 与 generate...work 都同义改写并对齐，没有遗漏其中任何一个，符合“并列动作全保留”原则。
  • spark people's curiosity↔Arouse people's interest in variability
  • generate more work on the topic↔stimulate more research on the topic
D) 过度推断Apply the principle of variability to such fields of study as the immune system 把 Can we find similar effects of variability beyond the brain, for instance, in the immune system? 这一开放性问题当成 Raviv 的具体计划——原文是疑问句、是抛出问题，并非“打算应用到免疫系统”。
P11

"We hope this work will spark people's curiosity and generate more work on the topic," concludes Raviv.

Raviv 总结说：“我们希望这项工作能激发人们的好奇心，并产生更多关于这一主题的研究。”
