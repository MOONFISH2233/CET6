范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 170 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in today’s world. As study, work and daily life move online, the ability to use digital tools wisely has become a basic survival skill rather than an optional advantage.

This importance is easy to feel in real situations. During the pandemic, all my courses were shifted to online platforms, and only classmates who could navigate video meetings, submit assignments and search for reliable sources kept up with the pace. Those who struggled with the technology quickly fell behind, not because they were less clever, but because they lacked the digital skills the new setting demanded. The same gap appears in the job market, where employers now expect graduates to handle data, communicate online and tell trustworthy information from rumours.

Therefore, digital literacy is no longer a bonus but a necessity. By actively learning to process information, protect our online safety and keep updating our skills, we can turn technology into a real advantage instead of a barrier.
