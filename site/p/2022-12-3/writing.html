范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 166 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Today increasing importance is being attached to cultivating college students' team spirit. Both universities and future employers have realized that few achievements today are the work of a single hand, so this quality truly deserves our attention.

There are convincing reasons behind this trend. First, almost every modern task, from a laboratory experiment to a business start-up, demands that people with different strengths pool their efforts; without cooperation, even talented individuals can accomplish little. Second, working in a team teaches students to communicate patiently, respect different opinions, and share responsibility, all of which are qualities that textbooks alone can never provide. A student who once relied only on personal effort, for instance, may finally learn to trust others after completing a group project together.

For these reasons, team spirit should never be treated as something optional. Colleges ought to design more group tasks, and students themselves should step out of their comfort zones and embrace cooperation, because it is teamwork that turns scattered abilities into real strength.
