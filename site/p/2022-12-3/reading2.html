原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Scientists have created by accident an enzyme(酶) that breaks down plastic drinks bottles. The breakthrough could help solve the global plastic pollution crisis by enabling for the first time the full recycling of bottles.

科学家们意外地制造出了一种能够分解塑料饮料瓶的酶。这一突破有望通过首次实现饮料瓶的完全回收，帮助解决全球塑料污染危机。

P2

The new research was spurred by the discovery in 2016 of the first bacterium that had naturally evolved to eat plastic at a waste dump in Japan. Scientists have now revealed the detailed structure of the crucial enzyme produced by the bug.

这项新研究源于2016年在日本一处垃圾倾倒场发现的第一种能够自然进化出以塑料为食能力的细菌。如今，科学家们已经揭示了这种细菌产生的关键酶的详细结构。

P3

An international team then adjusted the enzyme to see how it had evolved, but tests showed they had accidentally made the molecule even better at breaking down the plastic used for drinks bottles. "What actually turned out was we improved the enzyme, which was a bit of a shock," said head researcher Prof. McGeehan, at the University of Portsmouth, UK.

随后，一个国际团队对这种酶进行了调整，以了解它是如何进化的，但测试显示，他们意外地使这种分子分解饮料瓶所用塑料的能力变得更强了。“实际结果是，我们改进了这种酶，这着实让人震惊，”英国朴次茅斯大学的首席研究员McGeehan教授说。

P4

Currently, the enzyme takes a few days to start breaking down the plastic, far faster than the centuries it takes in the oceans, but the researchers are optimistic this can be speeded up even further and become a viable large-scale process.

目前，这种酶需要几天时间才能开始分解塑料，这已经远快于塑料在海洋中需要数百年才能分解的速度，但研究人员乐观地认为，这一过程还可以进一步加快，并成为一种可行的大规模处理方式。

P5

"What we are hoping to do is use this enzyme to turn this plastic back into its original components, so we can literally recycle it back to plastic," said McGeehan. "It means we won't need to dig up any more oil and, fundamentally, it should reduce the amount of plastic in the environment."

“我们希望利用这种酶将塑料还原成其最初的成分，从而真正把它重新制成塑料，实现回收利用，”McGeehan说。“这意味着我们不再需要开采更多石油，而且从根本上说，这应该会减少环境中的塑料数量。”

P6

About 1 million plastic bottles are sold each minute around the globe and, with just 14% recycled, many end up in the oceans where they have polluted even the remotest parts, harming marine life and potentially people who eat sea food. "Plastic is incredibly resistant to degradation," said McGeehan. "It is one of these wonder materials that has been made a little bit too well."

全球每分钟售出约100万个塑料瓶，而其中只有14%得到回收，许多塑料瓶最终流入海洋，污染了甚至最偏远的地区，危害海洋生物，也可能伤害食用海产品的人类。“塑料极其耐降解，”McGeehan说。“它是这类被制造得有些过于完美的神奇材料之一。”

P7

Currently those bottles that are recycled can only be turned into opaque fibres for clothing or carpets, while the new enzyme indicates a way to recycle old clear plastic bottles back into new clear plastic bottles.

目前，回收的塑料瓶只能被制成不透明的服装纤维或地毯纤维，而这种新酶则显示出一条途径，可以将旧的透明塑料瓶重新制成新的透明塑料瓶。

P8

"You are always up against the fact oil is cheap, so plastic is cheap," said McGeehan. "It is so easy for manufacturers to generate more of that stuff, rather than even try to recycle, but I believe there is a public interest here: perception is changing so much that companies are starting to look at how they can properly recycle these bottles."

“你始终要面对这样一个事实：石油价格低廉，所以塑料也便宜，”McGeehan说。“制造商很容易生产更多这种材料，甚至无需尝试回收，但我认为这里存在公共利益：人们的观念正在发生巨大变化，因此企业开始考虑如何真正回收这些塑料瓶。”

P9

Prof. Adisa Azapagic, at the University of Manchester in the UK, agreed the enzyme could be useful but added: "A full life-cycle assessment would be needed to ensure that the technology does not solve one environmental problem—waste—at the expense of others, including additional greenhouse gas emissions."

英国曼彻斯特大学的Adisa Azapagic教授同意这种酶可能会有用，但她补充说：“还需要进行完整的生命周期评估，以确保这项技术不会以增加其他环境问题为代价来解决某一个环境问题——废弃物问题，其中包括额外的温室气体排放。”

解析逐题详解

51

What do we learn from the passage about an enzyme scientists have created?

从文章中我们了解到科学家创造的一种酶有什么特点?

  • A)It was identified during a lab experiment accident.它是在一次实验室实验事故中被发现的。
  • B)It may make full recycling of plastic bottles a reality.它可能使塑料瓶的完全回收成为现实。
  • C)It was a breakthrough made with persistent efforts.它是通过持续努力取得的突破。
  • D)It may initiate a radical reform in plastic industry.它可能引发塑料行业的根本性变革。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念identified during a lab experiment accident 偷换了原文 created by accident——原文意思是"偶然创造出"，A 项却说成"在一次实验事故中被识别（identified）出来"，identified（识别）≠ created（创造），且 lab experiment accident（实验事故）也无原文支撑。
B) ✓ 正确B 项既覆盖"完全回收"又保留"可能性"语气，与定位句 could help…by enabling for the first time the full recycling of bottles 严格对应，是定位句的同义改写。
  • enabling for the first time the full recycling of bottles↔may make full recycling of plastic bottles a reality
  • the full recycling of bottles↔full recycling of plastic bottles
C) 与原文相反breakthrough made with persistent efforts 与原文 created by accident 直接相反；同段还点明 a bit of a shock 进一步坐实"意外性"。
D) 无中生有initiate a radical reform in plastic industry 中的 radical reform（根本性改革）在原文中无任何对应句；原文谈的是"帮助解决污染"和"完全回收"，并未谈及行业改革。
P1

The breakthrough could help solve the global plastic pollution crisis by enabling for the first time the full recycling of bottles.

这一突破有望首次实现塑料瓶的完全回收，从而帮助解决全球塑料污染危机。

52

What does the passage say about the bug that produces the important enzyme?

关于产生这种重要酶的微生物,文章说了什么?

  • A)It has a natural ability to consume plastics.它具有消耗塑料的天然能力。
  • B)It is a bacterium that reproduces at a high rate.它是一种高速繁殖的细菌。
  • C)It is essential to the recycling of plastic bottles.它对塑料瓶的回收至关重要。
  • D)It has a chemical structure unknown to scientists.它具有科学家未知的化学结构。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确A 项把定位句的动词陈述（自然进化出去吃塑料）改写为名词陈述（具有自然消化塑料的能力），是该细菌核心特征的客观转述。
  • naturally evolved to eat plastic↔natural ability to consume plastics
B) 无中生有reproduces at a high rate（高速繁殖）在原文中没有任何对应句；原文只说该细菌"自然进化"且"吃塑料"，没有谈论其繁殖速度。
C) 偷换概念essential to the recycling of plastic bottles 把所问对象（bug 本身的属性）偷换成"对回收的必要性"——这是对其衍生用途的评价，而非对 bug 本身的描述；且原文也未声称 bug 对回收"必不可少"。
D) 与原文相反chemical structure unknown to scientists 与原文 Scientists have now revealed the detailed structure of the crucial enzyme produced by the bug. 相反；原文明确"已揭示详细结构"，选项却说"科学家不知道"。
P2

The new research was spurred by the discovery in 2016 of the first bacterium that had naturally evolved to eat plastic at a waste dump in Japan.

这项新研究的契机是 2016 年在日本一处垃圾场发现的首个自然进化出能吃塑料能力的细菌。

53

By adjusting the enzyme produced by the bug, the scientists_______.

通过调整该微生物产生的酶,科学家们_______。

  • A)made it more effective by chance偶然使它变得更有效
  • B)discovered an extraordinary chemical发现了一种非凡的化学物质
  • C)altered its basic molecular composition改变了它的基本分子组成
  • D)found its evolutionary process sped up发现它的进化过程加快了
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确A 项 made it more effective 复述 even better at breaking down，by chance 复述 accidentally，正好等值改写定位句两个核心要素。
  • accidentally made the molecule even better at breaking down the plastic↔made it more effective by chance
B) 无中生有discovered an extraordinary chemical 中 extraordinary chemical 在原文无出处；研究者操作的是已知酶分子，并非"发现一种新化学物质"。
C) 过度推断altered its basic molecular composition 中 basic molecular composition（基本分子组成）属于结构层级更深的概念，原文只说 adjusted the enzyme（调整酶），未说改变其"基本组成"。
D) 偷换概念found its evolutionary process sped up 中 sped up（加速）与原文 to see how it had evolved（观察其如何进化）不一致；原文目的是观察，并未发现进化过程被加速。
P3

An international team then adjusted the enzyme to see how it had evolved, but tests showed they had accidentally made the molecule even better at breaking down the plastic used for drinks bottles.

国际团队随后调整了该酶以观察其进化过程，但实验表明他们意外地让这一分子分解饮料瓶塑料的能力变得更强了。

54

What does Prof. McGeehan say about the recycling of plastic bottles?

McGeehan教授关于塑料瓶回收说了什么?

  • A)Manufacturers are implementing it on an increasingly larger scale.制造商正在越来越大的规模上实施它。
  • B)It generates huge business opportunities for plastic manufacturers.它为塑料制造商创造了巨大的商业机会。
  • C)It has aroused persistent interest among the general public.它在公众中引起了持续的兴趣。
  • D)Manufacturers are beginning to explore ways of doing it.制造商正开始探索实施它的方法。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 过度推断implementing it on an increasingly larger scale 中 implementing on a larger scale（大规模实施）远超 starting to look at（开始研究）的程度——原文是观念转变后的初始阶段，并未谈到任何实施规模。
B) 无中生有generates huge business opportunities for plastic manufacturers 中 huge business opportunities（巨大商业机会）原文未提；McGeehan 谈的是企业态度转变，不是回收带来的商机。
C) 偷换概念aroused persistent interest among the general public 中 persistent interest（持续 / 长期兴趣）与原文 perception is changing so much（观念正在巨大变化）不一致——原文说"正在变化"，C 说"已激起且持续"；且讨论对象是公众而非回收行为本身，与题干 about the recycling of plastic bottles 错位。
D) ✓ 正确D 项把"才刚开始"和"探索如何回收"两个核心信息都对齐原文，是定位句的精准同义改写。
  • companies are starting to look at how they can properly recycle these bottles↔Manufacturers are beginning to explore ways of doing it
  • companies↔Manufacturers
P8

perception is changing so much that companies are starting to look at how they can properly recycle these bottles.

公众观念变化如此之大，以至于企业正开始研究如何妥善回收这些瓶子。

55

What is Prof. Adisa Azapagic's advice concerning the application of the enzyme?

Adisa Azapagic教授对这种酶的应用有什么建议?

  • A)Developing technologies to address greenhouse gas emissions.开发应对温室气体排放的技术。
  • B)Considering the extra cost involved in producing the enzyme.考虑生产这种酶所涉及的额外成本。
  • C)Assessing its possible negative impact on the environment.评估它对环境可能产生的负面影响。
  • D)Studying the full life cycle of the enzyme as the first step.将研究该酶的完整生命周期作为第一步。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 偷换概念Developing technologies to address greenhouse gas emissions 中 developing technologies 与原文 assessment（评估）不同——原文建议"评估"，并非"开发新技术"去解决排放问题。
B) 无中生有Considering the extra cost involved in producing the enzyme 中 extra cost in producing the enzyme（生产酶的额外成本）原文无出处；Adisa Azapagic 的建议是评估环境影响而非经济成本。
C) ✓ 正确C 项的 Assessing 对应 assessment，possible negative impact on the environment 对应 not solve…at the expense of others, including additional greenhouse gas emissions；正答完整覆盖建议的两个语义要点。
  • does not solve one environmental problem—waste—at the expense of others, including additional greenhouse gas emissions↔Assessing its possible negative impact on the environment
  • A full life-cycle assessment would be needed↔Assessing
D) 偷换概念Studying the full life cycle of the enzyme as the first step 中 the full life cycle of the enzyme（酶本身的生命周期）与原文 A full life-cycle assessment（针对技术应用的全生命周期评估）所指不同——原文评估的是技术对环境的全周期影响，并非"酶的寿命周期"；另外 as the first step（作为第一步）原文亦无对应。
P9

A full life-cycle assessment would be needed to ensure that the technology does not solve one environmental problem—waste—at the expense of others, including additional greenhouse gas emissions.

需要进行一次完整的生命周期评估，以确保该技术不会以制造其他环境问题（包括额外的温室气体排放）为代价来解决一个环境问题（废弃物）。

重点词本篇重点词▾
