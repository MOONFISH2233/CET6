范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 162 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Nowadays more and more people take delight in offering help to the needy. This heart-warming trend, in my view, reflects both a growing sense of social responsibility and the genuine happiness that giving brings to the giver.

There are solid reasons behind this phenomenon. On the one hand, lending a hand to those in difficulty strengthens the bonds that hold a community together, and a single act of kindness often inspires others to follow. On the other hand, helping people is deeply rewarding in itself. I still remember one winter when my classmates and I spent a weekend teaching migrant children in a shabby rented room. We gained far more warmth from their bright smiles than we ever gave, and that experience convinced me that generosity enriches both sides.

Therefore, we should never underestimate the power of a helping hand. If each of us is willing to reach out to those in need, our society will surely grow kinder, warmer and more united.
