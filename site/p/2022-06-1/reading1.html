原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Selective colleges and universities in the U.S. are under fire for being too elite and too expensive, and for not training graduates for the world of work. Such charges ignore the fact that these institutions continue to prepare students for success in their work, for thoughtful engagement in civic life, for lifelong learning, and for understanding the world and those with whom they live.

美国的高选择性学院和大学因过于精英化、学费过于昂贵，以及没有为毕业生进入职场做好培训而受到抨击。这些指责忽视了这样一个事实：这些学校仍在继续帮助学生为事业上的成功、对公民生活的理性参与、终身学习，以及理解他们所处的世界和与他们共同生活的人做好准备。

P2

These colleges and universities must be doing something right. Applications are at record highs, and their financial aid programs make them more accessible than ever. This model of education has long played a central role in creating opportunity, driving economic growth, and spurring innovation.

这些学院和大学一定做对了什么。申请人数创下历史新高，而它们的助学金项目使其比以往任何时候都更容易进入。这种教育模式长期以来一直在创造机会、推动经济增长和促进创新方面发挥着核心作用。

P3

Yet, there is growing skepticism about the value of this model. The recent tax reform bill is a wake-up call that our strongest colleges and universities are under assault by some in government. The initial proposals would have made education unaffordable for many by taxing tuition waivers for graduate students and ending deductions for student loan interest. Thankfully, these provisions were ultimately stripped from the bill, but lawmakers let stand a new tax on the investment income of some colleges and universities.

然而，人们对这种模式的价值日益持怀疑态度。最近的税制改革法案敲响了警钟，表明我们最优秀的学院和大学正受到政府中一些人的攻击。最初的提案会通过对研究生的学费减免征税以及取消学生贷款利息抵扣，使许多人无力承担教育费用。所幸，这些条款最终被从法案中删除，但立法者却保留了针对部分学院和大学投资收入的新税。

P4

While these attacks are motivated by misguided ideas, we need to do a better job of explaining why these claims are false and why what we do is valuable. We cannot take for granted that any of this is obvious.

尽管这些攻击源于错误的观念，但我们需要更好地解释为什么这些说法是错误的，以及我们所做的事情为何具有价值。我们不能想当然地认为这些道理都是显而易见的。

P5

It is often said that elite colleges and universities do not train students, particularly those who study the liberal arts, for the workforce. But this can be refuted by scholarly research. The data are clear: a liberal arts education is great career preparation, both for excellent lifetime earnings and for satisfaction with the work. This education develops the skills of critical thinking, rigorous analysis of data and facts, communication with the written and spoken word, understanding of cultural differences and issues, and the ability to keep learning. In fact, liberal arts graduates do extremely well in every imaginable field.

人们常说，精英学院和大学没有为学生，尤其是学习文科的学生，进入职场做好培训。但学术研究可以驳斥这一说法。数据十分明确：文科教育是极好的职业准备，既能带来优厚的终身收入，也能提高对工作的满意度。这种教育培养批判性思维能力、对数据和事实进行严谨分析的能力、通过书面和口头语言进行交流的能力、理解文化差异和相关问题的能力，以及持续学习的能力。事实上，文科毕业生在所有可以想象的领域都表现得非常出色。

P6

Access to an education at selective colleges and universities is now more available than ever to low- and middle-income families. We have built endowments from donations by alumni(校友) and parents who understand and appreciate our mission to provide access and opportunity, and a significant portion of the returns from these endowments is used to fund financial aid.

如今，低收入和中等收入家庭比以往任何时候都更有机会接受高选择性学院和大学的教育。我们依靠校友和家长的捐赠建立了捐赠基金；他们理解并认可我们提供机会和入学机会的使命，而这些捐赠基金的投资回报中有相当大一部分被用于资助助学金。

P7

Ironically, the new tax on endowments drains financial aid funds from the very schools most able to offer opportunity to those who have earned a spot but cannot otherwise afford this education. Beyond the virtue of access to those who have earned a place at these schools, the diversity of economic backgrounds enhances the education and experience of all of our students.

具有讽刺意味的是，这项针对捐赠基金的新税削减了那些最有能力为有资格入学却无力承担这种教育费用的人提供机会的学校的助学金资金。除了让那些凭自身努力获得这些学校入学资格的人能够接受教育这一优点之外，不同经济背景的多样性也提升了我们所有学生所接受的教育及其经历。

解析逐题详解

46

What fact does the author emphasize concerning selective colleges and universities?

关于选择性学院和大学,作者强调了什么事实?

  • A)They have been ignoring the training of graduates for the world of work.它们一直忽视对毕业生进入职场的培训。
  • B)They have been doing well in ensuring their students a successful future.它们一直在确保学生拥有成功未来方面做得很好。
  • C)They have been constantly attacked for being too elite and too expensive.它们一直因过于精英化和过于昂贵而不断受到抨击。
  • D)They have been actively engaged in civic life beyond the school campus.它们一直积极参与校园之外的公民生活。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反They have been ignoring the training of graduates for the world of work 把“忽视”这一动作扣到了院校头上，正好与原文相反——原文是说指控者忽视了事实，并不是院校忽视培养。
B) ✓ 正确预判说“院校仍在为学生未来成功做准备”，B 项 doing well in ensuring their students a successful future 正是这一表述的同义替换，故选 B。
  • these institutions continue to prepare students for success in their work↔doing well in ensuring their students a successful future
  • Such charges ignore the fact that↔They have been doing well
C) 答非所问They have been constantly attacked for being too elite and too expensive 复述的是 Selective colleges and universities in the U.S. are under fire for being too elite and too expensive 这一事实——但这是别人对院校的指控，不是作者要强调的 fact；题干问 What fact does the author emphasize。
D) 偷换概念They have been actively engaged in civic life beyond the school campus 把原文中“院校培养学生参与公民生活”的对象（学生）偷换成了院校本身参与公民生活，主体被替换。
P1

Such charges ignore the fact that these institutions continue to prepare students for success in their work, for thoughtful engagement in civic life, for lifelong learning, and for understanding the world and those with whom they live.

这些指控忽视了一个事实：这些院校仍在持续地为学生做准备——为他们的事业成功、公民生活的深入参与、终身学习，以及理解世界与共处之人做准备。

47

What does the author say in arguing for the model of education in the U.S.?

作者在论证美国教育模式时说了什么?

  • A)It has contributed substantially to the nation's overall development.它对国家的整体发展做出了重大贡献。
  • B)It has succeeded in maintaining sustainable financial aid programs.它成功维持了可持续的财政资助项目。
  • C)It has given priority to innovative programs for graduate studies.它优先考虑研究生学习的创新项目。
  • D)It has played a central role in attracting international applicants.它在吸引国际申请者方面发挥了核心作用。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确三项并列贡献可统一抽象为 the nation's overall development，A 项正是这一上位概括，故选 A。
  • creating opportunity, driving economic growth, and spurring innovation↔contributed substantially to the nation's overall development
  • has long played a central role↔contributed substantially
B) 偷换概念succeeded in maintaining sustainable financial aid programs 把“该模式的整体贡献”偷换成了“维持可持续的资助项目”——原文 their financial aid programs make them more accessible than ever 只是说援助项目使学校更可及，没说“可持续维持”，更不是作者为之辩护的核心论据。
C) 无中生有given priority to innovative programs for graduate studies 把 spurring innovation 中的 innovation 拼接成“研究生项目优先”，但原文从未谈及研究生项目优先级，是凭空捏造。
D) 无中生有played a central role in attracting international applicants 套用了原文 played a central role 的句式，却把宾语换成了 attracting international applicants（吸引国际申请人），但原文从未提国际申请人，是用熟悉句式包装的新内容。
P2

This model of education has long played a central role in creating opportunity, driving economic growth, and spurring innovation.

这一教育模式长期以来在创造机会、推动经济增长与激发创新方面扮演着核心角色。

48

What do we learn about the initial proposals concerning the recent tax reform bill?

关于近期税改法案的初步提案,我们了解到什么?

  • A)They would have stripped many students of life's chances.它们本会剥夺许多学生的人生机会。
  • B)They would have deducted graduate student loan interest.它们本会扣除研究生学生贷款利息。
  • C)They would have added to many students' financial burden.它们本会增加许多学生的经济负担。
  • D)They would have increased the number of tuition waivers.它们本会增加学费减免的数量。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 偷换概念would have stripped many students of life's chances 把“加重经济负担”夸大为“剥夺人生机会”——原文 unaffordable for many 只到“许多人负担不起”，未到“剥夺人生机会”的程度，且 life's chances 范围过宽。
B) 与原文相反would have deducted graduate student loan interest 把原文 ending deductions for student loan interest 中的“取消抵扣”反过来说成“抵扣”，方向完全相反。
C) ✓ 正确unaffordable for many 与 added to many students' financial burden 在虚拟语气下完全等值，故选 C。
  • would have made education unaffordable for many↔would have added to many students' financial burden
  • taxing tuition waivers for graduate students and ending deductions for student loan interest↔financial burden
D) 与原文相反would have increased the number of tuition waivers 与原文 taxing tuition waivers 相反——原文是对学费减免征税（变相缩减或削弱），选项却说“增加学费减免数量”，方向相反。
P3

The initial proposals would have made education unaffordable for many by taxing tuition waivers for graduate students and ending deductions for student loan interest.

最初的提议本来会通过对研究生学费减免征税、取消学生贷款利息抵扣，使许多人负担不起教育。

49

What do the data show about elite colleges and universities?

数据显示精英学院和大学有什么特点?

  • A)Their graduates lack the rigor required for doing statistical analysis.它们的毕业生缺乏进行统计分析所需的严谨性。
  • B)Their students prove to be inadequately prepared for their future careers.它们的学生被证明对未来职业准备不足。
  • C)Their focus on research is conducive to developing students' critical thinking.它们对研究的关注有助于培养学生的批判性思维。
  • D)Their liberal arts education enables graduates to excel in whatever field they are in.它们的博雅教育使毕业生能够在任何领域中表现出色。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反Their graduates lack the rigor required for doing statistical analysis 与原文 rigorous analysis of data and facts（严谨的数据与事实分析能力）正相反——原文说博雅教育培养这种严谨，选项却说他们缺乏。
B) 与原文相反Their students prove to be inadequately prepared for their future careers 与原文 a liberal arts education is great career preparation 正相反——原文说“出色的职业准备”，选项说“准备不充分”。
C) 无中生有Their focus on research is conducive to developing students' critical thinking 借用了 scholarly research 和 critical thinking 两个原文词，拼出“院校以研究为重心、研究有助于培养批判性思维”——但原文只说博雅教育培养批判性思维，并未说研究是其重心，更未谈“研究→批判性思维”的因果。
D) ✓ 正确预判“在任何领域都表现出色”，D 项 excel in whatever field they are in 是其精准同义替换，故选 D。
  • liberal arts graduates do extremely well in every imaginable field↔Their liberal arts education enables graduates to excel in whatever field they are in
  • a liberal arts education is great career preparation↔Their liberal arts education enables graduates to excel
P5

The data are clear: a liberal arts education is great career preparation, both for excellent lifetime earnings and for satisfaction with the work. ... In fact, liberal arts graduates do extremely well in every imaginable field.

数据很清楚：博雅教育是出色的职业准备，既能带来优厚的终身收入，也能带来工作满意度。……事实上，博雅毕业生在每一个可以想象的领域都表现极佳。

50

What is an advantage of providing financial aid for students?

为学生提供经济资助有什么好处?

  • A)Every student can choose the institution they wish to attend.每个学生都可以选择他们希望就读的院校。
  • B)All students can benefit from a diversified student population.所有学生都能从多元化的学生群体中受益。
  • C)All students will be able to earn a place on university campus.所有学生都能够在大学校园获得一席之地。
  • D)Less privileged students will be more competitive at elite schools.弱势学生在精英学校将更具竞争力。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 无中生有Every student can choose the institution they wish to attend 谈“学生可以自由选择学校”，原文从未涉及“选校自由”这一层，是凭空捏造。
B) ✓ 正确原文明确说 all of our students 都从多样性中获益，与 B 项 All students can benefit 完全对位，故选 B。
  • the diversity of economic backgrounds enhances the education and experience of all of our students↔All students can benefit from a diversified student population
  • the diversity of economic backgrounds↔a diversified student population
C) 偷换概念All students will be able to earn a place on university campus 把原文 those who have earned a spot（那些已凭实力争得席位的人）扩大为“全体学生都能争得席位”——原文说的是 access 给了已经“earned”的人，并不是让所有人都能 earn。
D) 无中生有Less privileged students will be more competitive at elite schools 谈“弱势学生在精英校竞争力更强”，原文只说资助让他们有 access、让全体学生从多样性中获益，并未对比“是否更具竞争力”。
P7

Beyond the virtue of access to those who have earned a place at these schools, the diversity of economic backgrounds enhances the education and experience of all of our students.

除了让那些凭实力争得席位的人获得入学机会这一价值之外，经济背景的多样性还提升了所有学生的教育与体验。
