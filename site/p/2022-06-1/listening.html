There are probably teams you've worked with but you never want to work with again. But there must have also been other teams that you would prize reuniting with professionally. In other words, your team had vitality. Vitality comes about when the ties people form with their fellow team members are such that they stay connected even after the team breaks up.

2

What characteristics of a team make its members more likely to stay in contact despite no longer working together? This question has been answered recently in a study published in a business journal.

团队具有什么样的特征，才能使其成员即使不再一起工作，也更有可能保持联系？最近，一项发表于商业期刊的研究回答了这个问题。

3

One of the two key factors the research team discovered is sameness. Specifically, sharing the same gender or ethnic origin. The more members of a team share similar demographics, the more inclined they will be to remain associates long after the team has served its purpose. After ties are established, similarity strengthens them. As a result, they regard these individuals with greater trust and mutual understanding, which motivates them to seek further opportunities for collaboration. In effect, people tend to create stronger and longer-lasting connections with similar others. Someone who looks and sounds different from us may have the resources we need to be more successful. Yet, we find them to be significantly less credible simply because they are different. If you are a fierce advocate of workplace diversity, you'll no doubt be horrified by such a revelation.

研究团队发现的两个关键因素之一是相似性，具体来说，就是拥有相同的性别或族裔背景。一个团队成员的人口统计特征越相似，他们就越倾向于在团队完成使命很久之后仍保持同事关系。联系建立之后，相似性会进一步巩固这种联系。因此，他们会更加信任并相互理解这些人，这促使他们寻求进一步合作的机会。实际上，人们往往会与相似的人建立更牢固、更持久的联系。与我们外貌和声音不同的人，或许拥有帮助我们取得更大成功所需的资源。然而，仅仅因为他们与众不同，我们就会发现自己明显不那么信任他们。如果你是职场多元化的坚定倡导者，毫无疑问会对这样的发现感到震惊。

4

The second factor identified by the researchers is the quality of the relationships among the team members. The more they trust one another, share the same goals and depend on each other for the achievement of those goals, the stronger their chances of maintaining their connections, despite no longer working as one team. Teams with quality relationships have a shared belief that it's safe to take risks with each other, and their members are obliged to share the workload and help out.

研究人员确定的第二个因素是团队成员之间关系的质量。他们彼此越信任，目标越一致，并且越依赖彼此来实现这些目标，即使不再作为一个团队工作，他们维持彼此联系的可能性就越大。关系良好的团队拥有一种共同信念，即彼此合作承担风险是安全的；其成员也有义务分担工作量并互相提供帮助。

  • A)It empowers them to cope with irrational emotions.它使他们有能力应对非理性情绪。
  • B)It will guarantee the profits from their investments.它将保证他们的投资获利。

D（细节题）

It also takes confidence to invest properly. Instead of making investing decisions with your emotions, financial planners advise that you should leave your investments alone and focus on a long-term investment plan.

【题目】 How do millionaires react when they fail in their investment?

  • A)They count on others to take the responsibility.他们指望别人承担责任。
  • B)They change their investment strategy in time.他们及时改变投资策略。
  • C)They think they themselves are to blame.他们认为自己应该受到责备。
  • D)They persist rather than get discouraged.他们坚持下去而不是气馁。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反 A 说他们 count on others to take the responsibility，盗用了原文的 count on anyone else 和 responsibility，但原文明明是"don't count on anyone else"——加了否定。A 把原文的否定句正过来用，与原文相反。
B) 未提及 B 说他们 change their investment strategy in time（及时改投资策略），原文谈的是"失利时不怪别人/担责"，从没说他们会"改策略"，属凭常识脑补的未提及项。

Millionaires don't count on anyone else to make them rich, and they don't blame anyone else if they fall short.

百万富翁不指望别人让自己变富，达不到目标时也不怪罪任何人。
