原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

When a group of Australians was asked why they believed climate change was not happening, about 36% said it was "common sense", according to a report published last year by the Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organization. This was the most popular reason for their opinion, with only 11% saying their belief that climate change was not happening was based on scientific research.

根据澳大利亚联邦科学与工业研究组织去年发布的一份报告，当被问及为什么相信气候变化并未发生时，约36%的澳大利亚人回答说这是“常识”。这是他们持有这一观点的最普遍理由，只有11%的人表示，他们认为气候变化并未发生的信念是基于科学研究的。

P2

But what do we mean by an appeal to common sense? Presumably it's an appeal to rationality of some sort that forms the basis of more complex reasoning. The appeal to common sense, however, is usually nothing more than an appeal to thinking that just feels right, but what feels right to one person may not feel right to another. Whether it feels right is usually a reflection of the world view and ideologies we have internalised, and that frames how we interact with new ideas. When new ideas are in accord with what we already believe, they are more readily accepted. When they are not, they, and the arguments that lead to them, are more readily rejected.

但是，我们所说的诉诸常识究竟是什么意思？想必它是诉诸某种理性，而这种理性构成了更复杂推理的基础。然而，诉诸常识通常不过是诉诸一种让人感觉正确的思考方式，但对一个人来说感觉正确的事情，对另一个人来说未必如此。事情是否感觉正确，通常反映了我们内化的世界观和意识形态，而它们决定了我们如何与新观念互动。当新观念与我们原有的信念相一致时，我们更容易接受它们；当它们不一致时，我们则更容易拒绝这些观念以及导向它们的论证。

P3

We often mistake this automatic compatibility testing of new ideas with existing beliefs as an application of common sense, but, in reality, it is more about judging than thinking. As Nobelist Daniel Kahneman notes in Thinking, Fast and Slow, when we arrive at conclusions in this way, the outcomes also feel true, regardless of whether they are. We are not psychologically well equipped to judge our own thinking.

我们常常把这种将新观念与现有信念进行自动兼容性检验的过程误认为是在运用常识，但实际上，这更多的是在评判，而不是在思考。正如诺贝尔奖得主丹尼尔·卡尼曼在《思考，快与慢》中所指出的，当我们以这种方式得出结论时，无论结论是否正确，结果都会让人感觉真实。我们在心理上并不具备充分评判自己思维的能力。

P4

We are also highly susceptible to a range of cognitive biases such as giving preference to the first things that come to mind when making decisions or giving weight to evidence.

我们还很容易受到各种认知偏差的影响，例如在作出决定或评估证据时，偏爱最先想到的事情，或赋予某些证据更大的分量。

P5

One way we can check our internal biases and inconsistencies is through the social verification of knowledge, in which we test our ideas in a rigorous and systematic way to see if they make sense not just to us, but to other people. The outstanding example of this socially shared cognition is science.

检验我们的内在偏见和不一致之处的一种方法，是通过知识的社会验证来进行检验，在这一过程中，我们以严谨且系统的方式检验自己的观念，看看它们是否不仅对我们自己说得通，对其他人是否也说得通。这种社会共享认知的杰出范例就是科学。

P6

That does not mean that individuals are not capable of excellent thinking, nor does it mean no individual is rational. But the extent to which individuals can do this on their own is a function of how well integrated they are with communities of systematic inquiry in the first place. You can't learn to think well by yourself.

这并不意味着个人没有能力进行出色的思考，也不意味着没有任何个人是理性的。但个人能够独自做到这一点的程度，取决于他们起初与系统探究共同体的融合程度。你无法独自学会良好地思考。

P7

In matters of science at least, those who value their common sense over methodological, collaborative investigation imagine themselves to be more free in their thinking, unbound by involvement with the group, but in reality they are tightly bound by their capabilities and perspectives. We are smarter together than we are individually, and perhaps that's just common sense.

至少在科学问题上，那些把常识置于有方法的协作性调查之上的人，以为自己在思考时更加自由，不受参与群体的束缚，但实际上，他们受到自身能力和视角的严格限制。我们共同思考时比各自单独思考时更加聪明，而这或许就是常识。

解析逐题详解

51

What does the author intend to show by citing the findings from the report published last year?

作者引用去年发表的报告中的研究发现意图说明什么?

  • A)People seldom appeal to rationality in their thinking.人们在思考时很少诉诸理性。
  • B)It is often the case that truth lies in the hands of a few.真理往往掌握在少数人手中。
  • C)Common sense and science are the two sides of a coin.常识和科学是一枚硬币的两面。
  • D)Few people know if climate change is really happening.很少有人知道气候变化是否真的在发生。
解析
A（例证目的题）
A) ✓ 正确作者列调查只是"由头"，紧跟的转折句把"common sense"等同于"feels right"而非 rationality；合起来等于说"人们很少真的诉诸理性"，与 People seldom appeal to rationality in their thinking 直接对接。
  • about 36% said it was "common sense" ... only 11% saying their belief that climate change was not happening was based on scientific research↔People seldom appeal to rationality in their thinking
  • The appeal to common sense, however, is usually nothing more than an appeal to thinking that just feels right↔appeal to rationality in their thinking
B) 无中生有原文谈"大多数人凭感觉判断"，并未提及 truth lies in the hands of a few 这种"真理掌握在少数人手中"的命题。
C) 与原文相反作者把"常识"与"科学"对立，并贬低前者；选项却说二者是 two sides of a coin"一体两面"，与原文的对立关系正相反。
D) 答非所问Few people know if climate change is really happening 谈的是"人们是否知道气候变化"，而题干问的是作者引用调查的目的——目的在论证"常识非理性"，不在论证"人们对气候变化的认知"。
P2

But what do we mean by an appeal to common sense? Presumably it's an appeal to rationality of some sort that forms the basis of more complex reasoning. The appeal to common sense, however, is usually nothing more than an appeal to thinking that just feels right.

但我们说"诉诸常识"究竟意味着什么？大概是诉诸某种作为更复杂推理基础的理性吧。然而，所谓"诉诸常识"，往往不过是诉诸"感觉对"的想法罢了。

52

What is the appeal to common sense according to the author?

根据作者的观点,诉诸常识是什么?

  • A)It is the basis for the internalisation of individuals' ideologies.它是个体意识形态内化的基础。
  • B)It is a series of conceptions formulated from complex reasoning.它是一系列通过复杂推理形成的概念。
  • C)It is collective wisdom that helps people interact with new ideas.它是帮助人们与新观念互动的集体智慧。
  • D)It is something subjective based on what one perceives to be right.它是基于个人对正确事物的认知的主观性内容。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反原文是"内化的意识形态"决定"是否感觉对"——意识形态是因、感觉是果；选项却把诉诸常识当成 basis for the internalisation of individuals' ideologies，把因果颠倒过来。
B) 与原文相反原文 Presumably it's an appeal to rationality of some sort that forms the basis of more complex reasoning 是作者先假设的"理想状态"，紧随 however, is usually nothing more than 被否定；formulated from complex reasoning 恰是被否定的那一面。
C) 张冠李戴原文从未说 common sense 是 collective wisdom"集体智慧"；作者后文反而把集体智慧的代表给了"science"而非 common sense，是典型张冠李戴。
D) ✓ 正确预判"凭感觉对的主观判断"与 something subjective based on what one perceives to be right 在两个语义点上完全对齐：subjective ↔ feels right 因人而异；perceives to be right ↔ feels right。
  • an appeal to thinking that just feels right↔something subjective based on what one perceives to be right
  • what feels right to one person may not feel right to another↔something subjective
P2

The appeal to common sense, however, is usually nothing more than an appeal to thinking that just feels right, but what feels right to one person may not feel right to another. Whether it feels right is usually a reflection of the world view and ideologies we have internalised.

然而所谓"诉诸常识"，往往不过是诉诸"感觉对"的想法罢了，可一个人觉得对，另一个人未必觉得对。是否觉得对，通常只是我们已经内化的世界观和意识形态的投射。

53

What does Daniel Kahneman think is the problem of testing new ideas with existing beliefs?

Daniel Kahneman认为用现有信念检验新观念存在什么问题?

  • A)It may lead to incorrect judgment.它可能导致错误的判断。
  • B)It makes no use of common sense.它没有运用常识。
  • C)It fails to correct mistakes through serious reasoning.它无法通过严肃的推理纠正错误。
  • D)It can produce psychologically unacceptable outcomes.它可能产生心理上无法接受的结果。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确两条语义在 P3 里共同作用：判断而非思考、结果感觉真但不一定真——合起来就是"该过程会带来错误判断"，对应 It may lead to incorrect judgment。
  • the outcomes also feel true, regardless of whether they are↔It may lead to incorrect judgment
  • it is more about judging than thinking↔incorrect judgment
B) 答非所问Kahneman 谈的是该过程的"问题"，不是评论它"用没用 common sense"；makes no use of common sense 把话题从"问题是什么"偷换为"是否使用了常识"。
C) 过度推断原文只说该过程产生的"感觉真"未必真，并未谈到能不能"通过严肃推理修正错误"——这是基于原文延展出去的额外命题。
D) 偷换概念psychologically unacceptable outcomes 把原文 not psychologically well equipped（人在心理上没有能力）偷换成"心理上无法接受的结果"——一个谈"能力"，一个谈"接受度"，貌似都有 psychologically 实则不同。
P3

As Nobelist Daniel Kahneman notes in Thinking, Fast and Slow, when we arrive at conclusions in this way, the outcomes also feel true, regardless of whether they are. We are not psychologically well equipped to judge our own thinking.

正如诺贝尔奖得主丹尼尔·卡尼曼在《思考，快与慢》中所言，当我们以这种方式得出结论时，结果"感觉上是真的"，至于它们到底是不是真的并不重要。我们在心理上并不擅长审视自己的思考过程。

54

What can we do to be less susceptible to cognitive biases?

我们能做些什么来减少对认知偏差的易感性?

  • A)Give equal weight to evidence of both sides in a conflict.对冲突双方的证据给予同等重视。
  • B)Provide convincing examples in developing an argument.在展开论证时提供有说服力的例证。
  • C)Establish socially shared cognition via scientific methods.通过科学方法建立社会共享认知。
  • D)Avoid inconsistencies when addressing controversial issues.在处理有争议的问题时避免不一致。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 无中生有P4 只提到我们对证据有偏向，Give equal weight to evidence of both sides in a conflict 把它包装成"双方等量赋权"作为对策；原文从未给出这一对策。
B) 无中生有原文谈"用科学的社会共享认知去检验自己的想法"，并未提及在论证中 Provide convincing examples in developing an argument"举有说服力的例子"这一具体写作技巧。
C) ✓ 正确预判"通过社会化检验、典型即科学"与 Establish socially shared cognition via scientific methods 在两个核心点上同义对齐——前半部分是手段，后半部分是手段的载体。
  • through the social verification of knowledge↔Establish socially shared cognition
  • we test our ideas in a rigorous and systematic way↔via scientific methods
D) 偷换概念Avoid inconsistencies when addressing controversial issues 把 internal biases and inconsistencies里的 inconsistencies 搬走，却换上一个原文没谈的 controversial issues 场景；并且对策从"通过社会化检验来检查"偷换成"直接避免不一致"。
P5

One way we can check our internal biases and inconsistencies is through the social verification of knowledge, in which we test our ideas in a rigorous and systematic way to see if they make sense not just to us, but to other people. The outstanding example of this socially shared cognition is science.

我们能够检验自身内在偏差与矛盾的一种方式，是通过对知识的社会化检验：以严格、系统的方式检验我们的想法，看它们不仅对自己说得通，对别人也是否说得通。这种社会共享认知最杰出的例子就是科学。

55

What message does the author try to convey at the end of the passage?

作者在文章结尾试图传达什么信息?

  • A)Multiple perspectives stimulate people's interest in exploring the unknown.多元视角激发人们探索未知的兴趣。
  • B)Individuals can enhance their overall capabilities by interacting with others.个体可以通过与他人互动提升整体能力。
  • C)Individuals should think freely to break from the restrictions of common sense.个体应自由思考以打破常识的束缚。
  • D)Collaborative efforts can overcome individuals limitations in scientific inquiry.协作努力可以克服个体在科学探究中的局限性。
解析
D（主旨题）
A) 无中生有末段从未谈及"多元视角能激发人们对未知的兴趣"——stimulate people's interest in exploring the unknown 是被自造出的概念，与原文金句无关。
B) 以偏概全Individuals can enhance their overall capabilities by interacting with others 把范围窄化为"提升个人综合能力"，遗漏了原文的"科学探究"语境与"克服局限"这一关键含义；末段强调的是"集体优于个人"，不是"通过互动让个人变强"。
C) 与原文相反末段批评的正是"以为自由地思考、不被群体束缚"的态度（imagine themselves to be more free in their thinking, unbound by involvement with the group）；Individuals should think freely to break from the restrictions of common sense 反而把"自由思考"当作积极建议——立场正好相反。
D) ✓ 正确末段三层语义（个人受限—协作探究—集体更聪明）全部对应到 Collaborative efforts can overcome individuals limitations in scientific inquiry 中，是覆盖最完整的选项。
  • We are smarter together than we are individually↔Collaborative efforts can overcome individuals limitations
  • methodological, collaborative investigation↔Collaborative efforts ... in scientific inquiry
  • in reality they are tightly bound by their capabilities and perspectives↔individuals limitations
