【参考范文 · 179 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Nowadays more and more people choose to live an environmentally friendly lifestyle. This shift is no longer a slogan but a set of daily habits that reshape how we eat, travel and consume.

Several concrete practices explain this trend. To begin with, many commuters now favour buses, subways or shared bikes over private cars, which cuts carbon emission and eases traffic. What is more, sorting rubbish into recyclable and kitchen waste has become routine in my own neighbourhood, so that plastic and paper can be recycled rather than buried. Take myself as an example: since I started carrying a cloth bag and a reusable bottle, I have thrown away far less single-use plastic every week. Small as these acts seem, they add up to a sustainable way of living.

As a result, an environmentally friendly lifestyle benefits both individuals and the planet. It saves resources, keeps the air cleaner and reminds us that a green future depends on the choices we make today. I firmly believe that when everyone takes one small step, the earth will thank us tomorrow.
