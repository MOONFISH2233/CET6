范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 168 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

College provides a great opportunity for students to explore various possibilities and find the right path for themselves. Unlike the rigid routine of high school, the campus offers a wide range of courses, clubs and internships that allow us to test different interests and gradually discover where our true passion lies.

Take my own case as an example. When I entered university, I was convinced that I would become an accountant, simply because my parents thought it was stable. However, after joining a campus debate club and taking an elective in journalism, I realized how much I enjoyed writing and public speaking. That single semester of trying new things completely reshaped my plan, and I eventually switched my major to communication.

In my view, such freedom to experiment is exactly what makes college so valuable. Instead of clinging to a fixed idea, students should seize every chance to explore, because it is only through trial and error that we can truly find the path that suits us best.
