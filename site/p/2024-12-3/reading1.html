原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

There are hundreds of personality quizzes online that assert they can ascertain whether the right or left half of your brain is dominant. Left-brained people are supposedly logical and excel at language and math while right-brained people are more imaginative, emotionally intelligent and skilled with spatial reasoning. There's just one problem: That's not how brains work.

网上有数百种性格测试，声称能够判断你是大脑左半球还是右半球占主导。据说左脑型的人具有逻辑性，擅长语言和数学；而右脑型的人更富有想象力，情商更高，也更擅长空间推理。问题只有一个：大脑并不是这样运作的。

P2

Popular science enthusiasts sort of took this idea and ran with it, and it's become woven in popular culture now, and it's not going away.

科普爱好者可以说是抓住了这个观点大肆发挥，如今它已经融入流行文化，而且不会消失。

P3

Despite this enduring belief, there's no such thing as being "right-brained" or "left-brained". Whether you're someone who tends more towards creativity or logic has nothing to do with one hemisphere of your brain being dominant over the other. But the actual science of how the two halves of our brains work together is sometimes stranger than fiction.

尽管这种观念一直存在，但根本不存在所谓“右脑型”或“左脑型”的人。一个人更偏向创造性还是逻辑性，与其大脑的某个半球是否凌驾于另一个半球之上毫无关系。不过，我们大脑的两个半球如何协同工作，其真实科学原理有时比虚构作品还要奇特。

P4

The human brain is divided into two hemispheres, the left and right. In all vertebrate(脊椎的) animals, the right hemisphere controls the left side of the body and vice versa. And scientists have long known, thanks to the behaviors of patients who suffered brain injuries, that different areas of the brain do different things.

人类大脑分为左、右两个半球。在所有脊椎动物中，右半球控制身体的左侧，反之亦然。科学家还早已通过那些遭受脑损伤的患者的行为了解到，大脑的不同区域承担着不同的功能。

P5

But many scientists struggled with this idea, because the very suggestion that the left and right halves of the brain operate differently disrupted the idea that nature tends toward perfect symmetry.

但是，许多科学家难以接受这一观点，因为仅仅是暗示大脑的左右两半运作方式不同，就冲击了自然趋向完美对称这一观念。

P6

Work by neuroscientists(神经科学家) has revealed the importance of different hemispheres of the brain for different activities. However, their research quickly saw some misinterpretations in the general public: Some presumed creative people must be right-brained and logical people left-brained. It is proven that not only is personality unrelated to the different halves of the brain, but people aren't really right- or left-brained to begin with. The idea that we have left-dominant people and right-dominant people, and that this is related to personality, is categorically false. That's never been supported in the neuroscience community. Neuroscientists don't believe that and never have.

神经科学家的研究揭示了大脑不同半球对于不同活动的重要性。然而，他们的研究很快在公众中引发了一些误解：有些人想当然地认为，富有创造力的人一定是右脑型，而有逻辑性的人则是左脑型。事实证明，性格不仅与大脑的不同半球无关，而且人们从一开始就并不真正属于右脑型或左脑型。认为人群中存在左脑占主导者和右脑占主导者，并且这与性格有关，这种说法完全错误。神经科学界从未支持过这一观点。神经科学家不相信这一点，也从来没有相信过。

P7

What scientists learned is that there are really important differences between the left hemisphere and the right hemisphere. It's just that they have nothing to do with personality or whether cognitive strategy is more logical or free spirited or creative. While researchers have shown the limitations of how the hemispheres of our brains influence our lives, it's not difficult to understand the appeal of such ideas. People are endlessly fascinated by themselves and their friends, and the subtle differences in how people think about the world are really meaningful to them. When you come up with an online quiz that tells us something about ourselves, we're drawn to that. It's irresistible. But you have to take it with an enormous grain of salt.

科学家了解到，左右两个大脑半球之间确实存在非常重要的差异。只是这些差异与性格无关，也与一个人的认知策略究竟更具逻辑性、更自由奔放还是更有创造力无关。尽管研究人员已经揭示了大脑半球对我们生活影响的局限性，但这类观点的吸引力并不难理解。人们始终对自己和朋友着迷，而人们思考世界的方式存在的细微差异，对他们而言确实意义重大。当你设计一个在线测试，告诉我们一些关于自己的事情时，我们就会被它吸引。这种诱惑难以抗拒。但你必须对它持极大的保留态度。

解析逐题详解

46

What do numerous personality quizzes online claim they are able to do?

众多在线性格测试声称它们能够做什么?

  • A)Distinguish between the two hemispheres of one's brain.区分一个人大脑的两个半球。
  • B)Determine whether one is left-brained or right-brained.判断一个人是左脑型还是右脑型。
  • C)Tell if one is more of a linguist or of a mathematician.判断一个人更像语言学家还是数学家。
  • D)Ascertain how one's brain performs different tasks.确定一个人的大脑如何执行不同任务。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念Distinguish between the two hemispheres of one's brain 说的是"区分大脑的两个半球"——这是解剖事实而非测试声称的本事，与原文承诺的"判断哪边占主导"不同。
B) ✓ 正确B 项把定位句改写成"判断一个人是左脑型还是右脑型"，与预判一致，且严格落在 P1 首句的字面承诺范围内。
  • ascertain whether the right or left half of your brain is dominant↔Determine whether one is left-brained or right-brained
C) 答非所问Tell if one is more of a linguist or of a mathematician 取自 excel at language and math 的延伸描述，是"左脑型人"的衍生特征，而非测试本身宣称要做的事。
D) 张冠李戴Ascertain how one's brain performs different tasks 的内容来自 different areas of the brain do different things，那是科学界的事实，不是"测试"声称要做的；动词 Ascertain 虽与原文同词，但宾语错配。
P1

There are hundreds of personality quizzes online that assert they can ascertain whether the right or left half of your brain is dominant.

网络上有成百上千的性格测试，宣称它们能够查明你的左半脑还是右半脑占主导。

47

What does the author say is sometimes stranger than fiction?

作者说什么有时比小说更离奇?

  • A)How one hemisphere of the brain impacts creativity.大脑的一个半球如何影响创造力。
  • B)How the two halves of our brains work alternately.我们大脑的两半如何交替工作。
  • C)How the two hemispheres of our brains cooperate.我们大脑的两个半球如何合作。
  • D)How one half of the brain dominates the other.大脑的一半如何主导另一半。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 偷换概念How one hemisphere of the brain impacts creativity 关注"某一个半脑对创造力的影响"，定位句强调的是"两半协同"，主体数量不符；creativity 也是从前文带过来的旁支话题。
B) 偷换概念How the two halves of our brains work alternately 把 work together 改成 work alternately（轮流工作），意思完全不同——一个是同时合作，一个是交替使用。
C) ✓ 正确C 项把 "halves…work together" 替换成 "hemispheres…cooperate"，是定位句的等值改写。
  • the two halves of our brains work together↔the two hemispheres of our brains cooperate
D) 与原文相反How one half of the brain dominates the other 正是文章一开头就否定的"左右脑主导说"，与定位句乃至全文立场相反。
P3

But the actual science of how the two halves of our brains work together is sometimes stranger than fiction.

但关于我们大脑两个半球如何协同运作的真实科学，有时比小说还要离奇。

48

Why did many scientists have difficulty endorsing the idea that different areas of the brain do different things?

为什么许多科学家难以认可大脑不同区域做不同事情这一观点?

  • A)It contradicts the assumption that the two hemispheres of the brain are symmetrical.它与大脑两个半球对称的假设相矛盾。
  • B)It dismisses the view that the universe has been evolving in a consistent manner.它否定了宇宙一直以一致方式演化的观点。
  • C)It is in conflict with the suggestion that the left and right halves of the brain work together.它与大脑左右两半共同工作的说法相冲突。
  • D)It disrupts the idea that the right hemisphere of the brain controls the left side of the body.它打破了大脑右半球控制身体左侧的观点。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确A 项把"动摇自然对称预设"这一动作改写为"与左右脑对称的预设相矛盾"，二者在本语境下指代同一观念，定位句即等于 A 项。
  • disrupted the idea that nature tends toward perfect symmetry↔contradicts the assumption that the two hemispheres of the brain are symmetrical
B) 无中生有It dismisses the view that the universe has been evolving in a consistent manner 把对象从"自然对称"扩展为"宇宙演化方式"，原文未涉及宇宙演化的话题。
C) 张冠李戴It is in conflict with the suggestion that the left and right halves of the brain work together 把矛盾对象说成"两半脑协同"，但原文中科学家排斥的不是"协同"，而是"对称"预设；况且"协同"是 P3 的肯定观点，本身不会被科学家抗拒。
D) 张冠李戴It disrupts the idea that the right hemisphere of the brain controls the left side of the body 取自 P4 的解剖事实，是脊椎动物的客观规律，不可能被科学家抗拒；动词 disrupts 虽与原文同词，宾语却换错。
P5

But many scientists struggled with this idea, because the very suggestion that the left and right halves of the brain operate differently disrupted the idea that nature tends toward perfect symmetry.

但许多科学家难以接受这个观点，因为"大脑左右半球运作方式不同"这一论断本身就动摇了"自然界趋向完美对称"的观念。

49

What belief have neuroscientists long rejected according to the passage?

根据文章,神经科学家长期以来拒绝接受什么观点?

  • A)There are left-dominant people and right-dominant people with different personalities.存在具有不同性格的左脑主导型和右脑主导型的人。
  • B)There are noticeable differences between the left and right hemispheres of the brain.大脑左右半球之间存在明显差异。
  • C)One's personality is hardly related to the different halves of the brain.一个人的性格与大脑的不同半球几乎无关。
  • D)Different areas of the brain are responsible for different activities.大脑的不同区域负责不同的活动。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确A 项就是定位句被 categorically false / never been supported 否定的那个具体观念，与预判一致。
  • we have left-dominant people and right-dominant people, and that this is related to personality↔There are left-dominant people and right-dominant people with different personalities
B) 与原文相反There are noticeable differences between the left and right hemispheres of the brain 是 there are really important differences between the left hemisphere and the right hemisphere 明确肯定的事实，神经科学界并不拒斥它。
C) 与原文相反One's personality is hardly related to the different halves of the brain 正是作者本人和神经科学界所持的立场（人格与左右脑无关），不是他们"长期拒绝"的观念。
D) 与原文相反Different areas of the brain are responsible for different activities 是 different areas of the brain do different things 的同义改写，是科学界共识，不可能被"长期拒绝"。
P6

The idea that we have left-dominant people and right-dominant people, and that this is related to personality, is categorically false. That's never been supported in the neuroscience community. Neuroscientists don't believe that and never have.

"存在左脑主导型人和右脑主导型人、并且这与人格有关"这一观念是完全错误的。神经科学界从未支持过这种说法。神经科学家不相信这种说法，也从未相信过。

50

What are we advised to do with an online quiz that tells us something about ourselves?

对于告诉我们一些关于自己信息的在线测试,我们被建议做什么?

  • A)Challenge its authority.质疑其权威性。
  • B)Scrutinize its originality.审查其原创性。
  • C)Evaluate its popularity.评估其受欢迎程度。
  • D)Question its reliability.质疑其可靠性。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 过度推断Challenge its authority 暗示要去"挑战其权威性"，但原文只是建议保留态度、不轻信结论，并未要求与之对抗。
B) 无中生有Scrutinize its originality 让人去审查"原创性"，原文丝毫未涉及测试是否原创的问题。
C) 答非所问Evaluate its popularity 评的是"测试的流行程度"，与作者给读者的"个人如何对待结论"的建议完全错位。
D) ✓ 正确把习语 take it with an enormous grain of salt 还原为字面意 question its reliability，与预判一致。
  • take it with an enormous grain of salt↔Question its reliability
P7

When you come up with an online quiz that tells us something about ourselves, we're drawn to that. It's irresistible. But you have to take it with an enormous grain of salt.

当出现一份告诉我们自身某些情况的网络测试时，我们会被吸引，难以抗拒。但你必须对它持极大的保留态度。
