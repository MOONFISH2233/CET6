原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Some people in the US have asserted that forgiving student loan debt is one way to stimulate the economy and give assistance to those in need. One government proposition is to eliminate $10,000 of debt for 'economically distressed' students. Some in US Congress have gone so far as to suggest forgiving up to $50,000 in debt per student borrower, but does forgiving student debt necessarily correlate to helping the economically disadvantaged?

美国有些人声称，免除学生贷款债务是刺激经济并向有需要者提供帮助的一种方式。一项政府提议是免除“经济困难”学生1万美元的债务。美国国会中的一些人甚至建议免除每名学生借款人最多5万美元的债务，但免除学生债务是否必然意味着帮助经济上处于不利地位的人呢？

P2

The answer is no. This policy is just giving money away to universities and the most affluent students in attendance. Federal Reserve data reveals that the highest-income 40 percent of households owe approximately 60 percent of outstanding student debt, while the lowest 40 percent owe just under 20 percent. This could be due to a combination of factors: students from high-income households are more likely to go to expensive colleges, less likely to receive financial aid, and more likely to have high incomes post-graduation. Plus, the majority of student debt is held by graduate degree earners, who earn approximately 25 percent more than their undergraduate counterparts. Clearly, giving free reign to banks to forgive student debt is a step in the wrong direction.

答案是否定的。这项政策只是在把钱送给大学和在校就读的最富裕学生。美联储数据显示，收入最高的40%的家庭约负有60%的未偿学生债务，而收入最低的40%的家庭负有的债务略低于20%。这可能是多种因素共同作用的结果：来自高收入家庭的学生更有可能就读昂贵的大学，较不可能获得助学金，并且毕业后更有可能拥有高收入。此外，大多数学生债务由拥有研究生学位的人持有，而他们的收入约比本科毕业生高25%。显然，任由银行免除学生债务是朝着错误方向迈出的一步。

P3

Other proposals for broader, long-term student loan plans have some fundamental problems. One idea is to cancel student debt only for undergraduate degrees and for students making less than $125,000.

其他关于更广泛、长期学生贷款计划的提议存在一些根本性问题。其中一种想法是，只为本科生以及收入低于12.5万美元的学生取消学生债务。

P4

This attempts to address the fact that Congress' previously mentioned student loan forgiveness plan largely helps out the wealthy, but is an adverse incentive for universities to keep raising tuition and for students to choose to major in low-earning degree programs. Colleges have no reason to make their programs more affordable if they believe students will just take out more debt. And, students will feel more comfortable making the irresponsible decision to go tens of thousands of dollars in debt to major in impractical or idealistic subjects if they know their loans will be forgiven.

这一做法试图解决国会此前提到的学生贷款免除计划主要帮助富人的问题，但它会对大学继续提高学费以及学生选择就读低收入专业形成不利激励。如果大学认为学生只会借更多债务，就没有理由让其课程更加负担得起。而且，如果学生知道贷款将被免除，他们在选择不切实际或理想主义的专业时，就会更安心地作出背负数万美元债务这一不负责任的决定。

P5

This is especially concerning given the pandemic(大流行病) has rendered a college education practically worthless. Students are paying tens of thousands of dollars per year to live at home and be lectured on the Internet. Do we really want to tell colleges that they can get away with providing below-average service for an outrageous cost?

鉴于大流行病已经使大学教育实际上变得毫无价值，这尤其令人担忧。学生每年支付数万美元，却只能住在家里听网络讲课。我们真的想告诉大学，它们可以以高得离谱的费用提供低于平均水平的服务，却不必承担后果吗？

P6

In the case of any of these student debt plans, working-class Americans who chose not to or could not afford to go to college will be subsidizing the education of the professional class. Plumbers and retail workers will be paying for the degrees of doctors and lawyers.

在这些学生债务计划中的任何一种情况下，那些选择不上大学或无力负担大学费用的工薪阶层美国人，都将为专业阶层的教育提供补贴。管道工和零售业工人将为医生和律师的学位买单。

P7

The US government's effort to help those in debt is commendable but is this really the solution that will help the poor financially recover?

美国政府帮助负债者的努力值得肯定，但这真的是能够帮助穷人实现经济复苏的解决方案吗？

解析逐题详解

46

Why do some people advocate forgiving student loan debt?

为什么有些人主张免除学生贷款债务?

  • A)They assert it will narrow the gap between the wealthy and the poor.他们断言这将缩小富人和穷人之间的差距。
  • B)They believe it will benefit both the economy and the underprivileged.他们相信这将对经济和弱势群体都有利。
  • C)They claim it will eliminate economic distress among college students.他们声称这将消除大学生的经济困境。
  • D)They think the cost of education is the responsibility of the government.他们认为教育费用是政府的责任。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 过度推断原文只说 give assistance to those in need，并未说 narrow the gap between the wealthy and the poor——"帮助困难者" 不等于 "缩小贫富差距"。
B) ✓ 正确预判要求同时覆盖 "经济" 与 "弱势群体"，B 用 both the economy and the underprivileged 并列结构精准对应定位句的并列宾语，是唯一双满足项。
  • stimulate the economy↔benefit ... the economy
  • give assistance to those in need↔benefit ... the underprivileged
C) 偷换概念原文 economically distressed 只是 "被免债 1 万美元" 的资格描述，并未声称免债能 eliminate economic distress among college students。
D) 无中生有原文未出现 the cost of education is the responsibility of the government 这一表述，主张方只谈 "免债" 这一具体动作，未上升到 "教育成本应由政府承担" 的责任归属。
P1

Some people in the US have asserted that forgiving student loan debt is one way to stimulate the economy and give assistance to those in need.

美国一些人主张：免除学生贷款债务是刺激经济、为困难人群提供援助的一种方式。

47

What do we learn from the Federal Reserve data?

从联邦储备系统的数据中我们了解到什么?

  • A)Approximately 60% of student debt remains unpaid.大约60%的学生债务仍未偿还。
  • B)Cancelling student debt benefits wealthy families most.取消学生债务使富裕家庭受益最多。
  • C)Forgiving student debt provides little benefit to universities.免除学生债务对大学几乎没有好处。
  • D)Low-income families owe the biggest amount of student debt.低收入家庭拖欠的学生债务金额最大。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念原文 60% 指 outstanding student debt 中由高收入家庭持有的比例，并非 Approximately 60% of student debt remains unpaid——"未偿" 在原文里是 outstanding（仍欠着），但 60% 修饰的是高收入家庭占比而非未偿率。
B) ✓ 正确数据要证明 "消债是给富人送钱"，B 把这一结论用 benefits wealthy families most 复述，是唯一锁定 "受益者方向" 的选项。
  • giving money away to universities and the most affluent students in attendance↔Cancelling student debt benefits wealthy families most
  • the highest-income 40 percent of households owe approximately 60 percent of outstanding student debt↔wealthy families
C) 与原文相反原文 giving money away to universities 明示大学正是免债的受益方，C 项却说 Forgiving student debt provides little benefit to universities，与原文截然相反。
D) 与原文相反原文 the lowest 40 percent owe just under 20 percent 明确说低收入家庭只欠不到 20%，D 项称 Low-income families owe the biggest amount of student debt，方向完全反向。
P2

This policy is just giving money away to universities and the most affluent students in attendance. Federal Reserve data reveals that the highest-income 40 percent of households owe approximately 60 percent of outstanding student debt, while the lowest 40 percent owe just under 20 percent.

该政策不过是把钱白送给大学和在读的最富有学生。美联储数据显示：收入最高的 40% 家庭欠下了大约 60% 的未偿学贷，而收入最低的 40% 仅欠不到 20%。

48

What does the author say students are likely to do if they know they needn't repay their loans?

作者认为如果学生知道他们不需要偿还贷款,他们可能会做什么?

  • A)They will choose to study subjects without considering their job prospects.他们会选择学习科目而不考虑就业前景。
  • B)They will be free to pursue their goals without being burdened financially.他们将能够自由追求自己的目标而不会有经济负担。
  • C)They will over-borrow and live beyond their means.他们会过度借贷并超出自己的经济能力生活。
  • D)They will be able to enroll in expensive universities.他们将能够入读学费昂贵的大学。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确把 impractical / idealistic 翻译为 "不考虑就业" 这一行为机制，是定位句到 A 项的唯一等值路径；其余三项均跳出此语义域。
  • major in impractical or idealistic subjects↔study subjects without considering their job prospects
  • major in↔study subjects
B) 与原文相反原文将这一行为定性为 the irresponsible decision（不负责任的决定），B 项却美化为 be free to pursue their goals without being burdened financially（自由追求目标、不受经济负担），态度方向完全相反。
C) 偷换概念原文谈的只是 major in impractical or idealistic subjects 选专业行为；C over-borrow and live beyond their means 把话题扩到 "超支度日" 的生活方式，越界。
D) 无中生有原文未提学生因免债 enroll in expensive universities 报昂贵学校的行为，借贷增多与 "昂贵大学" 没有原文支持。
P4

And, students will feel more comfortable making the irresponsible decision to go tens of thousands of dollars in debt to major in impractical or idealistic subjects if they know their loans will be forgiven.

并且，如果学生知道自己的贷款将被免除，他们会更安心地做出不负责任的决定——为了主修不实用或理想化的专业而背上数万美元的债务。

49

What does the author imply about colleges offering online education?

作者对提供在线教育的大学有何暗示?

  • A)They cannot get away with the serious consequences.它们无法逃脱严重的后果。
  • B)They have suffered greatly from the current pandemic.它们在当前疫情中遭受了巨大损失。
  • C)The tuition they charge is not justified by the quality of their service.它们收取的学费与其服务质量不相称。
  • D)The tuition they charge has surged outrageously during the pandemic.它们在疫情期间收取的学费飙升得令人发指。
解析
C（推断题）
A) 过度推断原文反问句 Do we really want to tell colleges that they can get away with ... ? 表达 "作者认为大学不该逃过去"，但 A 项 They cannot get away with the serious consequences 直接把 "逃不掉" 当作论点，作者只是质问、并未陈述其事实结果，且 "serious consequences" 系原文未提的后果维度。
B) 张冠李戴原文 Students are paying tens of thousands of dollars per year，受害方是学生，B 却把 "受害" 戴给大学：They have suffered greatly from the current pandemic，主语与原文相反。
C) ✓ 正确反问句的反义即 "学费不应换来低于平均的服务"，C 用 not justified by the quality of their service 把这一态度客观化，是唯一锁定 "价质不匹配" 的选项。
  • below-average service for an outrageous cost↔The tuition they charge is not justified by the quality of their service
  • outrageous cost↔the tuition they charge
D) 无中生有原文只说学费 "离谱"（outrageous），未说它 has surged outrageously during the pandemic 在大流行期间 "飙涨"；时间维度的涨幅描述无原文支撑。
P5

Students are paying tens of thousands of dollars per year to live at home and be lectured on the Internet. Do we really want to tell colleges that they can get away with providing below-average service for an outrageous cost?

学生每年花费数万美元只为待在家里、通过网络听课。我们真的愿意告诉大学：它们可以一边收取离谱的费用、一边提供低于平均水平的服务，还安然无恙吗？

50

What will happen if any of the proposed student debt plans is implemented?

如果任何一项拟议的学生债务计划得以实施,将会发生什么?

  • A)Plumbers and retail workers will have a chance of becoming professionals.水管工和零售工人将有机会成为专业人士。
  • B)Working-class students will have increasing access to subsidized education.工薪阶层学生将越来越多地获得补贴教育。
  • C)Blue-collar workers will have to bear the cost of educating would-be high-earners.蓝领工人将不得不承担培养未来高收入者的教育成本。
  • D)A growing number of students will be able to earn degrees in medicine and law.越来越多的学生将能够获得医学和法律学位。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反原文谈的是 working-class Americans 要补贴别人，而非 "获得成为专业阶层的机会"；A Plumbers and retail workers will have a chance of becoming professionals 把方向反过来，让他们成为受益人。
B) 与原文相反原文 working-class Americans who chose not to or could not afford to go to college 强调工薪阶层不上大学，B 说 Working-class students will have increasing access to subsidized education，把不上大学的工薪阶层反向描述为 "上大学且获补贴"。
C) ✓ 正确预判要求保留 "蓝领→白领" 的财政方向，C 用 Blue-collar workers 与 would-be high-earners 把双方上位化后保留方向，是唯一忠实复述者。
  • Plumbers and retail workers↔Blue-collar workers
  • will be paying for the degrees of doctors and lawyers↔will have to bear the cost of educating would-be high-earners
D) 答非所问原文谈的是 "谁为这些学位埋单" 的财政分配，而非 A growing number of students will be able to earn degrees in medicine and law 这种 "医法学位人数增加" 的供给变化，未对应题干 What will happen 所问的方案后果。
P6

In the case of any of these student debt plans, working-class Americans who chose not to or could not afford to go to college will be subsidizing the education of the professional class. Plumbers and retail workers will be paying for the degrees of doctors and lawyers.

无论这些学生贷款方案的哪一种实施，那些选择不上大学或负担不起大学的工薪阶层美国人都将在补贴专业阶层的教育。管道工和零售工将为医生和律师的学位埋单。
