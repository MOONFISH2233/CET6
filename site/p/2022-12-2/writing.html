范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 180 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

In an era of information explosion, it is vitally important to develop the ability to think critically and make rational choices. Every day we are flooded with news, advertisements and social-media posts, yet not all of them are trustworthy. Only those who can sift truth from falsehood are able to stay clear-headed.

There are two compelling reasons why this ability matters. First, information today is a mixture of facts and rumours, and a careless reader can easily be misled. Take online rumours during public emergencies for example: people who forward them without checking often spread panic. Second, the cost of a wrong choice has risen sharply; whether we are selecting a course, a product or a career, rational judgement based on reliable evidence saves us from regret.

Therefore, we should never swallow information passively. By questioning the source, comparing different views and weighing the evidence before us, we can turn the endless flood of information into a real advantage rather than a heavy burden. In the end, critical thinking is not an optional skill but a survival tool for the modern mind.
