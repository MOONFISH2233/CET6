原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

If there's one rule that most parents cling to in the confusing, fast-changing world of kids and media, it's "No screens before age 2."

在儿童与媒体这个令人困惑且变化迅速的世界里，如果说有一条大多数父母都紧紧遵守的规则，那就是“2岁以前不得接触屏幕”。

P2

As of today, that rule has been thrown out the window.

从今天起，这条规则被彻底抛到一边了。

P3

The American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP), which first issued that recommendation back in 1999, has extensively updated and revised its guidelines for children and adolescents to reflect new research and new habits.

美国儿科学会（AAP）于1999年首次发布了这一建议，如今已根据新的研究成果和新的生活习惯，对儿童和青少年的相关指南进行了大幅更新和修订。

P4

The new guidelines, especially for very young children, shift the focus from WHAT is on the screen to WHO else is in the room. And in doing so, they raise some intriguing points about the future of learning from media.

新的指南，尤其是针对幼儿的指南，将关注重点从屏幕上播放的内容转向了房间里还有谁。这样一来，它们也提出了一些关于未来如何通过媒体学习的引人深思的问题。

P5

For babies younger than 18 months, AAP still says no screens at all are the best idea—with one notable exception: Live video chat. Surveys indicate that families already popularly believe that "Face time doesn't count", or at least that the benefit of virtual visits with grandparents or other relatives outweighs the potential cost of exposing babies to the laptop or smartphone.

对于不满18个月的婴儿，AAP仍然认为完全不接触屏幕是最好的做法，但有一个值得注意的例外：实时视频聊天。调查显示，许多家庭已经普遍认为“Face time不算在内”，或者至少认为，与祖父母或其他亲属进行虚拟探访所带来的益处，超过了让婴儿接触笔记本电脑或智能手机可能造成的影响。

P6

The AAP doesn't cite positive evidence that infants actually get something out of this kind of "conversation", the way that they clearly do from live social interaction. But there's some observational research that infants as young as six months old are emotionally engaged by playing live peekaboo(躲猫猫) with Grandma online.

AAP没有引用积极证据证明，婴儿确实能从这种“对话”中获得什么，就像他们显然能够从实时的社会互动中获得益处一样。不过，有一些观察性研究表明，年仅6个月的婴儿在网上与奶奶进行实时躲猫猫游戏时，也会投入情感。

P7

For infants and toddlers(学步儿童), ages 15 months to 2 years old, there's limited evidence from a couple of very small studies that they can learn new words from educational media, if and only if parents are watching alongside them, repeating what the video says and/or drawing attention to what is on the screen. In other words, treating a video or an app like a picture book is the best bet.

对于15个月至2岁的婴幼儿，目前有几项规模很小的研究提供了有限证据，表明他们能够从教育媒体中学会新词，但前提是父母必须陪在旁边观看，重复视频中的话，和／或引导孩子注意屏幕上的内容。换句话说，把视频或应用程序当作图画书来使用，是最有可能奏效的方法。

P8

The flip side of this is that many studies have actually shown poorer language skills correlated with earlier solo viewing of "educational" videos. There's also research that shows language delays in children who watch more TV and start watching earlier. In both cases, the problem seems to be media replacing interaction with people. For this reason, the new AAP guideline has changed from "avoid all screens under age 2" to "avoid solo media use in this age group."

与此相反，许多研究实际上显示，较早独自观看“教育类”视频与较差的语言能力相关。还有研究表明，观看电视较多且开始观看年龄较早的儿童会出现语言发育迟缓。在这两种情况下，问题似乎都在于媒体取代了人与人之间的互动。因此，新的AAP指南已将“2岁以下避免接触所有屏幕”改为“这一年龄段应避免独自使用媒体”。

P9

For preschoolers ages 2 to 5, there's more evidence that they have the ability to transfer knowledge from screens to the real world, including early literacy and math. For this age group, AAP recommends no more than an hour a day of screen use. And, just as with younger children, they want care-givers to take part in screen time: "Co-view with your children, help children understand what they are seeing, and help them apply what they learn to the world around them."

对于2至5岁的学龄前儿童，有更多证据表明，他们具备将从屏幕上获得的知识迁移到现实世界中的能力，包括早期读写能力和数学能力。对于这一年龄段，AAP建议每天使用屏幕的时间不超过1小时。与年龄更小的儿童一样，他们也希望照护者参与孩子的屏幕使用时间：“与孩子一起观看，帮助孩子理解他们所看到的内容，并帮助他们将所学知识运用到周围的世界中。”

解析逐题详解

51

What do we learn about the "No screens under 2" rule?

关于"2岁以下禁止屏幕"规则我们了解到什么?

  • A)It has met more and more resistance from parents.它遭到了越来越多家长的抵制。
  • B)It has proved helpful to children's healthy growth.它已被证明有助于儿童的健康成长。
  • C)It confuses parents with regard to kids' education.它使家长在孩子教育方面感到困惑。
  • D)It has been discarded in line with recent research.它已根据最新研究被废弃。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 无中生有met more and more resistance from parents 在原文中找不到对应；rule that most parents cling to 反而显示家长一直紧抱旧规则，与"抵制"相反。
B) 无中生有proved helpful to children's healthy growth 在原文中无对应；原文只是说家长一直奉行该规则，并未给出"该规则被证明对孩子健康有益"的证据。
C) 张冠李戴confuses parents with regard to kids' education 把"令家长困惑"扣在旧规则头上；但原文 in the confusing, fast-changing world of kids and media 中 confusing 形容的是"kids and media 这个世界"，不是 No screens under 2 这条规则——这是典型的张冠李戴。
D) ✓ 正确预判落点"被弃用/依据新研究"两个要素，正好分别对应 discarded 与 in line with recent research，D 是定位句与紧邻背景句的整合同义替换。
  • that rule has been thrown out the window↔It has been discarded
  • extensively updated and revised its guidelines... to reflect new research↔in line with recent research
P2

As of today, that rule has been thrown out the window.

从今天起，那条规则已被扔出窗外（即被彻底弃用）。

52

What do the new AAP guidelines advocate?

美国儿科学会的新指南倡导什么?

  • A)Young children should be accompanied by parents during screen time.幼儿在屏幕时间应由家长陪伴。
  • B)Parents should be emotionally involved in their children's upbringing.家长应在情感上参与孩子的养育。
  • C)Young children should watch videos carefully selected by parents.幼儿应观看由家长精心挑选的视频。
  • D)Parents should protect their children from too much media exposure.家长应保护孩子免受过多媒体接触。
解析
A（主旨题）
A) ✓ 正确预判要点"父母陪同孩子用屏"在 P7 P9 至少三处同义表述同时成立，A 项一句话覆盖这条主线，是新指南的整体主张。
  • if and only if parents are watching alongside them↔accompanied by parents during screen time
  • Co-view with your children↔accompanied by parents during screen time
  • they want care-givers to take part in screen time↔accompanied by parents during screen time
B) 过度推断emotionally involved in their children's upbringing 把范围从"屏幕时间陪伴"扩到"整体育儿过程"；本文限定话题在"屏幕/媒体使用"，不涉及通用育儿建议。
C) 与原文相反videos carefully selected by parents 强调父母"挑选视频内容"，与 shift the focus from WHAT is on the screen to WHO else is in the room 明确告示的方向相反——新指南要的是 WHO（有谁陪），不是 WHAT（挑什么内容）。
D) 以偏概全protect their children from too much media exposure 偏向"控制屏幕时间总量"，确实贴近 P8 与 P9 的"一小时上限/避免独看"，但只是 P9 的局部要点之一；它没覆盖贯穿全文的 co-view 主线，作为整体主张以偏概全。
53

What do families think of live video chat according to surveys?

根据调查,家庭如何看待实时视频聊天?

  • A)It should not be regarded as screen time.它不应被视为屏幕时间。
  • B)It helps babies to develop their verbal skills.它有助于婴儿发展语言技能。
  • C)It is not as harmful as playing games on laptops.它不像在笔记本电脑上玩游戏那样有害。
  • D)It is a good substitute for video viewing.它是视频观看的良好替代品。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判落点是"家庭把视频聊天排除在屏幕时间之外"，A 恰好是这句话的书面化、完整化版本，是定位句的标准同义替换。
  • "Face time doesn't count"↔It should not be regarded as screen time
B) 张冠李戴原文 P5 没有说视频聊天帮助婴儿"发展语言能力"；emotionally engaged 给出的也是"情感投入"而非语言；P7 谈到 learn new words 但前提是 educational media+父母伴看，与 live video chat 是不同话题。
C) 无中生有not as harmful as playing games on laptops 把视频聊天与"在笔记本上玩游戏"作比较，但原文 P5 只比较"虚拟探访的好处"与"接触手机的潜在代价"，从未把 video chat 与 playing games 放一起比较。
D) 无中生有a good substitute for video viewing 把视频聊天说成"看视频的好替代品"，但 P5 只说家庭认为视频聊天"不算屏幕时间"，并未在视频聊天与看视频之间建立替代关系。
P5

Surveys indicate that families already popularly believe that "Face time doesn't count", or at least that the benefit of virtual visits with grandparents or other relatives outweighs the potential cost of exposing babies to the laptop or smartphone.

调查显示，许多家庭已经普遍相信"视频聊天不算（屏幕时间）"，或者至少认为与祖父母等亲属虚拟相见的好处大于让婴儿接触电脑或智能手机的潜在代价。

54

What do researchers find about kids solo viewing educational videos?

研究人员发现儿童独自观看教育视频有什么结果?

  • A)It arouses their interest in language learning.它激发了他们对语言学习的兴趣。
  • B)It works no better than reading picture books.它并不比阅读图画书效果更好。
  • C)It hampers their development of language skills.它妨碍了他们语言技能的发展。
  • D)It helps them acquire independent learning skills.它帮助他们获得自主学习技能。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反arouses their interest in language learning 原文未提兴趣，且原文方向是"独看伴随更差语言能力"，与"激发兴趣"方向相反。
B) 偷换概念works no better than reading picture books 把 treating a video or an app like a picture book is the best bet 错读为"二者效果一样"；原文是"把视频当绘本来读才是最佳做法"，并未说"独看视频和读绘本一样"。
C) ✓ 正确原文 poorer skills correlated with solo viewing 等同于"独看与语言能力变差相关"，C 的 hampers 正是这一负向相关的动词化表达。
  • poorer language skills correlated with earlier solo viewing of "educational" videos↔It hampers their development of language skills
D) 无中生有helps them acquire independent learning skills 把"独看"硬扯成"获得自主学习能力"；原文不仅未提及自主学习，反而把 solo 与负向后果绑定。
P8

The flip side of this is that many studies have actually shown poorer language skills correlated with earlier solo viewing of "educational" videos.

另一方面，许多研究实际上已经表明，过早地独自观看"教育"视频与较差的语言能力相关。

55

What does the author say about preschoolers ages 2 to 5?

作者关于2至5岁学龄前儿童说了什么?

  • A)They can understand pretty well what they see on the screen.他们能够很好地理解在屏幕上看到的内容。
  • B)They can learn on their own without much parental guidance.他们可以在没有太多父母指导的情况下自主学习。
  • C)They can make use of educational videos to develop digital literacy.他们可以利用教育视频培养数字素养。
  • D)They can relate what they learn on the screen to real life situations.他们能够将在屏幕上学到的内容与现实生活情境联系起来。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 偷换概念understand pretty well what they see on the screen 与原文 help children understand what they are seeing 中的"理解"措辞相近，但原文这句是 AAP 对父母的"建议"——要父母帮孩子理解，并非"孩子已经能理解"的事实结论。
B) 与原文相反learn on their own without much parental guidance 与原文 they want care-givers to take part in screen time 直接相反——原文强调要父母参与，B 项说不需要父母指导。
C) 偷换概念develop digital literacy 把原文 early literacy and math 中的"早期识字"偷换为"数字素养"——early literacy 指儿童的识字、阅读能力，与 digital literacy（数字素养/媒介使用能力）不是一回事。
D) ✓ 正确预判要点"屏上所学应用到现实"，D 用 relate to real life situations 准确还原 transfer to the real world，是定位句的同义替换。
  • transfer knowledge from screens to the real world↔relate what they learn on the screen to real life situations
P9

For preschoolers ages 2 to 5, there's more evidence that they have the ability to transfer knowledge from screens to the real world, including early literacy and math.

对于 2 到 5 岁的学龄前儿童而言，有更多证据表明他们能够把从屏幕上学到的知识迁移到现实世界，包括早期识字与数学能力。
