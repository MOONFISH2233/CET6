原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Research is meant to benefit society by raising public awareness and creating products and innovations that enhance development. For research to serve its full purpose, the results must leave the confines of research laboratories and academic journals.

研究旨在通过提高公众意识以及创造促进发展的产品和创新成果来造福社会。要使研究充分发挥其作用，研究成果必须走出研究实验室和学术期刊的范围。

P2

Findings effectively communicated can go a long way to serve the interests of the public. They can help address social injustices or improve treatments offered to patients.

得到有效传播的研究发现能够在很大程度上服务于公众利益。它们可以帮助解决社会不公问题，或改善为患者提供的治疗方案。

P3

Many researchers seem to be content with sharing the results of their studies in academic journals or at conferences. But few journals allow everybody to read the findings. Even articles freely available are usually written in academic language incomprehensible to the average reader.

许多研究人员似乎满足于在学术期刊或会议上分享研究成果。但很少有期刊允许所有人阅读这些研究发现。即使是可以免费获取的文章，通常也采用普通读者难以理解的学术语言撰写。

P4

For researchers in the tenure-track system, their main goal is winning tenure, which in part can be achieved by getting a number of papers published in prominent journals. Pressures like this mean community-level outreach is not prioritised.

对于处于终身教职轨道体系中的研究人员来说，他们的主要目标是获得终身教职，而这在一定程度上可以通过在知名期刊上发表一定数量的论文来实现。这样的压力意味着社区层面的推广工作不会被优先考虑。

P5

Many researchers lack the writing skills to describe their results to a general audience. They may also worry about whether the public will understand their findings, or about findings being used to influence controversial policies. These concerns cause some researchers to shy away from communicating their findings outside the academic community.

许多研究人员缺乏向普通受众描述研究成果的写作技能。他们也可能担心公众是否能够理解自己的研究发现，或者担心研究发现会被用于影响有争议的政策。这些顾虑导致一些研究人员不愿在学术界之外传播自己的研究成果。

P6

Propagating research findings beyond academic publications is particularly crucial for addressing certain social discrepancies. It can help families, communities, healthcare providers, policymakers, government agencies and other stakeholders to understand and respond to crises that plague society.

将研究发现传播到学术出版物之外，对于解决某些社会差异尤为重要。这样可以帮助家庭、社区、医疗服务提供者、政策制定者、政府机构及其他利益相关者理解并应对困扰社会的危机。

P7

The benefits of sharing findings flow both ways. Engaging with other researchers and the public can lead to unexpected new connections and new ideas that could suggest fruitful new directions for research.

分享研究发现带来的益处是双向的。与其他研究人员和公众开展交流，可能会促成意想不到的新联系和新想法，并为研究指明富有成效的新方向。

P8

To benefit both researchers and the communities, the need to find innovative, accessible ways to share the work cannot be overstated.

为了使研究人员和社区双方都受益，寻找创新且易于获取的方式来分享研究成果的重要性无论如何强调都不为过。

P9

Institutions and funding organisations should support more researchers to publish in open access journals so that the public doesn't have to pay to read them. Institutions and researchers should invest in partnerships that expand capacity for sharing results more broadly.

机构和资助组织应支持更多研究人员在开放获取期刊上发表论文，使公众无需付费即可阅读。机构和研究人员应投资于合作关系，以扩大更广泛分享研究成果的能力。

P10

Furthermore, ethics committees should make it mandatory for researchers to share their results with the public. Every research participant should opt in or out of receiving results, as part of the process of giving informed consent.

此外，伦理委员会应规定研究人员必须向公众分享研究成果。作为知情同意过程的一部分，每位研究参与者都应选择是否接收研究结果。

P11

There could be misunderstanding of the findings presented by the researcher because of technical terms. But this can be resolved by researchers engaging the services of professional writers or communication officers to help with translating their study into more accessible language and share it widely with media outlets and the public.

由于技术术语的存在，研究人员所呈现的研究发现可能会被误解。但研究人员可以聘请专业写作者或传播官员，将其研究转化为更易理解的语言，并将其广泛分享给媒体和公众，从而解决这一问题。

P12

Sharing results with the people who are most affected by them makes us better researchers and ensures that our work can be used to improve people's lives. Institutions and collaborators must recognise the value of doing so.

与最受研究成果影响的人分享研究结果，会使我们成为更好的研究人员，并确保我们的工作能够用于改善人们的生活。机构和合作者必须认识到这样做的价值。

解析逐题详解

46

How can research serve its full purpose according to the author?

根据作者的观点,研究如何才能充分发挥其作用?

  • A)With researchers being aware of public interests.研究人员意识到公众利益
  • B)With its findings published in prominent journals.研究成果发表在知名期刊上
  • C)With researchers creating products that enhance social development.研究人员创造促进社会发展的产品
  • D)With its findings properly communicated beyond the academic circle.研究成果在学术圈之外得到恰当传播
D（细节题）
A) 无中生有With researchers being aware of public interests 说"研究者要意识到公众利益"，原文从未谈论"研究者的觉察"这一前提，P1 强调的是"传播"而非"觉察"。
B) 与原文相反With its findings published in prominent journals 把"发表在顶级期刊上"当作答案，但 P1 恰恰指出"必须离开学术期刊"——发表在顶级期刊只是 P4 中讲到的研究者评职称的手段，与"研究发挥充分作用"相反。
C) 张冠李戴With researchers creating products that enhance social development 把 creating products and innovations that enhance development 这句"研究的目标之一"挪用来作为"研究发挥作用的方式"——这是研究的目的描述，不是 serve its full purpose 的条件。
D) ✓ 正确选项 With its findings properly communicated beyond the academic circle 用同义替换还原了预判的"研究结果必须走出学术圈"，是定位句的标准转述。
  • the results must leave the confines of research laboratories and academic journals↔With its findings properly communicated beyond the academic circle
P1

For research to serve its full purpose, the results must leave the confines of research laboratories and academic journals.

要让研究充分发挥其作用，研究结果必须走出实验室和学术期刊的范围。

47

Why do ordinary readers find it difficult to access the results of researchers' studies?

为什么普通读者难以获取研究人员的研究结果?

  • A)They cannot understand the academic language used for reporting these results.他们无法理解报告这些结果时使用的学术语言
  • B)They feel intimidated by the jargon researchers use to describe their findings.他们对研究人员用来描述其发现的术语感到畏惧
  • C)They do not attend conferences where these results are freely available.他们不参加可以免费获取这些结果的会议
  • D)They have few chances to locate the journals that publish these findings.他们几乎没有机会找到发表这些研究成果的期刊
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确选项 They cannot understand the academic language used for reporting these results 是定位句"X 对 Y 不可理解"到"Y 不能理解 X"的标准视角转换，与预判完全吻合。
  • academic language incomprehensible to the average reader↔They cannot understand the academic language used for reporting these results
B) 无中生有They feel intimidated by the jargon researchers use to describe their findings 中 feel intimidated（感到畏惧）原文从未提及；jargon（行话）原文也未出现这个词。
C) 偷换概念They do not attend conferences where these results are freely available 把 freely available 从"免费可读"偷换为"会议上免费提供"；原文是说"少数期刊允许人人阅读，即便免费可读也用艰涩语言"，不是说在 conferences 上免费提供。
D) 答非所问They have few chances to locate the journals that publish these findings 说"普通读者找不到这些期刊"，但原文 But few journals allow everybody to read the findings 说的是"少数期刊允许所有人阅读"——即"读到"的问题，而非"找到"的问题；且题干问的是 difficult to access the results（读懂结果），不是 difficult to locate the journals（找到期刊）。
P3

Even articles freely available are usually written in academic language incomprehensible to the average reader.

即便那些免费可读的文章，通常也是用普通读者无法理解的学术语言写成。

48

What is one of the reasons some researchers won't prioritise communicating their findings to the public?

一些研究人员不会优先考虑向公众传播其研究成果的原因之一是什么?

  • A)They can thrive on the papers published.他们可以凭借发表的论文获得成功
  • B)Their top consideration is to win tenure他们的首要考虑是获得终身教职
  • C)Their main goal is gaining recognition in their field.他们的主要目标是在其领域获得认可
  • D)They have to struggle to reach out to the community.他们必须努力接触社区
B（细节题）
A) 答非所问They can thrive on the papers published 说"他们靠发表的论文茁壮成长"——题干问的是"为什么不优先对外传播的原因"，A 描述的是"发文的结果/好处"，而非"不愿对外传播的原因"，且 thrive on 的积极语义与原文"压力"对应不上。
B) ✓ 正确选项 Their top consideration is to win tenure 是定位句 their main goal is winning tenure 几乎逐词的同义替换，且与预判"首要目标是 tenure"完全一致。
  • their main goal is winning tenure↔Their top consideration is to win tenure
C) 无中生有Their main goal is gaining recognition in their field 把研究者目标改成"在领域内获得认可"——原文 their main goal is winning tenure 明确写的是 winning tenure（拿到终身教职），不是 recognition；这是把因果中段的"靠发文"过度泛化为"求认可"。
D) 与原文相反They have to struggle to reach out to the community 说"他们必须努力对外传播"，但原文 community-level outreach is not prioritised 是说"不优先做"——不是"努力做"，方向完全相反。
P4

For researchers in the tenure-track system, their main goal is winning tenure, which in part can be achieved by getting a number of papers published in prominent journals. Pressures like this mean community-level outreach is not prioritised.

对处在终身教职轨制度中的研究者来说，他们的主要目标是获得终身教职，而获得终身教职部分可以通过在顶级期刊上发表若干论文实现。诸如此类的压力意味着面向社区层面的对外传播不被优先考虑。

49

How can sharing findings benefit researchers themselves?

分享研究成果如何使研究人员自身受益?

  • A)By helping them to identify new research directions.帮助他们确定新的研究方向
  • B)By enabling them to understand crises plaguing society.使他们能够理解困扰社会的危机
  • C)By enabling them to effectively address social discrepancies.使他们能够有效解决社会差异
  • D)By helping them to forge ties with government agencies.帮助他们与政府机构建立联系
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确选项 By helping them to identify new research directions 是定位句 could suggest fruitful new directions for research 的同义改写，且 By Ving 结构正好回应题干 How。
  • could suggest fruitful new directions for research↔By helping them to identify new research directions
B) 张冠李戴By enabling them to understand crises plaguing society 借用了 respond to crises that plague society 的措辞——但 P6 讲的是"分享有助于其他主体（家庭、社区、政策制定者等）理解危机"，受益者并非"研究者自身"；与题干 researchers themselves 错位。
C) 张冠李戴By enabling them to effectively address social discrepancies 借用 addressing certain social discrepancies 的措辞——同样是 P6 讲对社会的好处，而非对研究者自身的好处；与题干 themselves 错位。
D) 无中生有By helping them to forge ties with government agencies 说"帮助他们与政府机构建立关系"——原文虽提过 government agencies（P6 列举受益方时），但从未把"与政府机构结盟"作为研究者自身的受益方式。
P7

Engaging with other researchers and the public can lead to unexpected new connections and new ideas that could suggest fruitful new directions for research.

与其他研究者及公众的互动可能催生意想不到的新联系和新想法，进而可能指明富有成果的新研究方向。

50

Why are researchers advised to engage the services of professional writers or communication officers?

为什么建议研究人员聘请专业作家或传播官员的服务?

  • A)To satisfy ethics committees' mandatory requirements of researchers.满足伦理委员会对研究人员的强制性要求
  • B)To translate their study into languages accessible to readers overseas.将他们的研究翻译成海外读者可理解的语言
  • C)To make their publications correctly understood by the public.使他们的出版物被公众正确理解
  • D)To render their findings acceptable by prominent journals.使他们的研究成果被知名期刊接受
C（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴To satisfy ethics committees' mandatory requirements of researchers 把 ethics committees should make it mandatory 这一伦理委员会的强制要求挪用来作为聘请写手的目的；但 P10 与 P11 是两个独立建议——伦理委员会强制研究者分享是一回事，聘请写手帮助翻译是另一回事，两者目的不同。
B) 偷换概念To translate their study into languages accessible to readers overseas 把 more accessible language 偷换为 languages accessible to readers overseas（让海外读者读懂）——原文从未提及 overseas / readers abroad 等概念，只说"通俗易懂"，不涉及外语翻译。
C) ✓ 正确选项 To make their publications correctly understood by the public 是 P11 整段问题（误解）与解决（翻译成通俗语言）的目的合成，与预判完全吻合。
  • to help with translating their study into more accessible language↔To make their publications correctly understood by the public
  • There could be misunderstanding of the findings presented by the researcher because of technical terms.↔correctly understood by the public
D) 与原文相反To render their findings acceptable by prominent journals 说"让顶级期刊接受研究"——这是 P4 中谈到的另一动机（评 tenure 时投顶级期刊），且 P1 明确批判"留在期刊里就发挥不了作用"；聘请写手的目的恰恰是"走出期刊面向公众"，方向相反。
P11

But this can be resolved by researchers engaging the services of professional writers or communication officers to help with translating their study into more accessible language and share it widely with media outlets and the public.

但这可以通过研究者聘请专业写手或传播官来解决，让他们帮助把研究"翻译"成更通俗易懂的语言，并广泛地向媒体和公众分享。
