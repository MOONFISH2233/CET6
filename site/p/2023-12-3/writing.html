范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 167 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

With their valuable skills and experience, elderly people can continue to make significant contributions to society. Far from being a burden, seniors possess a lifetime of professional knowledge and practical wisdom that younger generations simply have not had time to acquire.

This potential shows itself in countless ways. Take retired engineers and doctors as an example: many of them return to their former posts as consultants, training newcomers and solving problems that puzzle less experienced staff. In my own community, retired teachers volunteer to run free evening classes, while elderly craftsmen pass down traditional skills that would otherwise disappear. Such efforts prove that age brings not decline but a rich resource waiting to be tapped.

Therefore, we should stop regarding old age merely as a time of rest and instead recognize its true worth. If society creates more opportunities for the elderly to share their expertise, everyone benefits. Only by valuing and drawing on their skills and experience can we build a wiser and more harmonious community.
