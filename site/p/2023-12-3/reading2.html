原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Spiders make their presence felt in late August and through early autumn. This is the mating season of some of the most common varieties, when male house spiders come out of hidden corners to look for females, and garden spiders reach adult size and spin their most dazzling webs.

蜘蛛在八月下旬至初秋期间开始显现踪迹。这是一些最常见蜘蛛品种的交配季节，此时雄性家蛛会从隐蔽的角落里出来寻找雌蛛，而园蛛则长到成体大小，结出最为炫目的蛛网。

P2

Yet while the spider is a familiar fixture of nursery poems or songs and Halloween decorations, its relationship with humans is complicated. Fear of spiders is common and has serious impacts on the lives of sufferers. Its prevalence appears unrelated to any rational assessment of risk. Spiders in the UK are almost all harmless. Farmland species perform valuable ecosystem services, by preying on insects that are our competitors for crops. But they are a constant source of human anxieties—with a cultural association with witches and wickedness dating back to the middle ages.

然而，尽管蜘蛛是童谣或歌曲以及万圣节装饰中常见的形象，但它与人类的关系十分复杂。害怕蜘蛛十分普遍，并且会严重影响患者的生活。这种恐惧的普遍程度似乎与对风险的理性评估毫无关系。英国的蜘蛛几乎都无害。农田中的蜘蛛以那些与我们争夺作物的昆虫为食，发挥着重要的生态系统服务作用。但它们始终是人类焦虑的一个来源——它们与女巫和邪恶之间的文化关联可以追溯到中世纪。

P3

Does this perhaps explain, in part, the lack of data about how spiders are faring in our age of ecological crisis? British butterflies are the most studied group of insects in the world, due to the long tradition of collecting and observing them. But spider conservationists point out that it was only in the 1980s that the classification of house spiders was properly sorted out. And while information about insect populations is gathered by experiments that measure the numbers hitting windscreens or traps, there have been few attempts to count spiders.

这是否可以部分解释在我们这个生态危机时代，关于蜘蛛生存状况的数据为何如此匮乏？英国蝴蝶是世界上受到研究最多的昆虫类群，这要归功于人们长期以来收集和观察蝴蝶的传统。但蜘蛛保护者指出，直到20世纪80年代，家蛛的分类才得到妥善厘清。而且，关于昆虫种群的信息是通过测量撞上挡风玻璃或落入捕捉器的昆虫数量的实验收集的，至于统计蜘蛛数量，人们却几乎没有进行过尝试。

P4

The huge reductions in the numbers of flying insects can only mean a reduction in spiders' food supply. A recent landmark study identified a 75% fall in insect populations between 1989 and 2016, with pesticide use thought to be to blame along with the destruction of wild areas for development. This means the overall picture for spiders is worrying, as it is for most creatures. But conservationists are most concerned about those varieties that are threatened due to habitat loss and fragmentation, which makes it impossible for them to migrate.

飞行昆虫数量的大幅减少只能意味着蜘蛛食物来源的减少。最近一项具有里程碑意义的研究发现，1989年至2016年间，昆虫种群数量下降了75%；人们认为，除野生区域因开发而遭到破坏外，农药的使用也是罪魁祸首。这意味着蜘蛛的总体状况令人担忧，大多数生物的情况也是如此。但保护工作者最担心的是那些因栖息地丧失和破碎化而受到威胁的品种，因为这使它们无法迁徙。

P5

Of around 650 spider species regularly recorded in the UK, the majority thrive in marshes and wasteland. Conservation efforts, often led by determined individuals, have helped some species to recover by reintroducing them to new areas. With rewilding now firmly on the environmental policy agenda, the hope is that in future, spiders will be enabled to migrate by themselves, adapting to climate change by moving along wildlife corridors.

在英国经常被记录到的大约650种蜘蛛中，大多数在沼泽地和荒地中繁衍生息。保护工作往往由意志坚定的个人带头开展，通过将一些物种重新引入新的区域，帮助它们恢复种群。随着再野化如今已稳居环境政策议程，希望未来蜘蛛能够自行迁徙，沿着野生动物廊道移动，以适应气候变化。

P6

It seems unlikely that spiders will ever attract the same level of human enthusiasm as bees, birds or butterflies, in spite of their unique status as nature's spinners. But as they reveal themselves in all their splendour this autumn, it would be a good thing if more animal lovers recognised the ways in which spiders are simply terrific.

尽管蜘蛛作为大自然的独特织网者享有特殊地位，但它们似乎不太可能像蜜蜂、鸟类或蝴蝶那样吸引人类的热情。然而，当它们在今年秋天展现出全部绚丽风采时，如果有更多动物爱好者认识到蜘蛛本身有多么了不起，那将是一件好事。

解析逐题详解

51

What do we learn about spiders in the UK since the middle ages?

关于英国的蜘蛛,我们了解到自中世纪以来发生了什么?

  • A)They have been generally misconceived.它们普遍被误解了。
  • B)They have adversely impacted crop growth.它们对农作物生长产生了不利影响。
  • C)They have been a constant reminder of bad luck.它们一直是厄运的持续提醒。
  • D)They have made their presence felt when spinning webs.它们在织网时让人感受到了它们的存在。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判落在蜘蛛被文化误解上，A They have been generally misconceived 用 misconceived 概括了原文女巫、邪恶等不实联想，与预判完全对应，是定位句的高度抽象同义替换。
  • a cultural association with witches and wickedness↔generally misconceived
  • its prevalence appears unrelated to any rational assessment of risk↔misconceived
B) 与原文相反B 选项 They have adversely impacted crop growth 称蜘蛛对农作物生长有不利影响，但原文 Farmland species perform valuable ecosystem services, by preying on insects that are our competitors for crops 明确说农田蜘蛛吃掉与人类争粮的昆虫，是有利的；B 把有利说成有害，与原文相反。
C) 偷换概念C 选项 They have been a constant reminder of bad luck 说蜘蛛是坏运气的提醒，但原文说的是与女巫、邪恶（witches and wickedness）的文化关联，witches/wickedness 是邪恶/巫术，不等于坏运气（bad luck）；二者是不同的文化概念，C 偷换了概念。
D) 答非所问D 选项 They have made their presence felt when spinning webs 取自首段 Spiders make their presence felt in late August and through early autumn，讲的是晚夏初秋的自然现象，与题干 since the middle ages 的时间范围毫无关系；属答非所问。
P2

But they are a constant source of human anxieties—with a cultural association with witches and wickedness dating back to the middle ages.

但它们一直是人类焦虑的来源——与女巫和邪恶的文化关联可追溯至中世纪。

52

What have spiders been associated with in the UK for centuries?

在英国,蜘蛛几个世纪以来一直与什么相关联?

  • A)Harm.伤害。
  • B)Evil.邪恶。
  • C)Suffering.痛苦。
  • D)Aggression.攻击性。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念A Harm 意为有形的伤害，对应原文应是 spiders 对人造成的实质损害；但原文 Spiders in the UK are almost all harmless 明确说英国蜘蛛几乎都无害——蜘蛛被关联的是文化上的邪恶，而不是实际伤害；A 偷换概念。
B) ✓ 正确由预判 与巫术与邪恶相关联，选项 B Evil 把 witches 与 wickedness 两个并列宾语用一个上位词 Evil 同时归纳，是定位句的精准同义替换。
  • wickedness↔Evil
  • witches↔Evil
C) 张冠李戴C Suffering 指痛苦/折磨，原文 Fear of spiders is common and has serious impacts on the lives of sufferers 出现 sufferers 一词——但 sufferers 描述的是怕蜘蛛的人所承受的影响，而题干问蜘蛛被关联的对象，主体错位；C 张冠李戴。
D) 无中生有D Aggression 指攻击性，原文从未把蜘蛛与攻击性联系起来——焦虑、恐惧、邪恶都不等于攻击；这是文中没有的概念，属无中生有。
P2

But they are a constant source of human anxieties—with a cultural association with witches and wickedness dating back to the middle ages.

但它们一直是人类焦虑的来源——与女巫和邪恶的文化关联可追溯至中世纪。

53

What partly accounts for the reduction in spiders' food supply?

什么在一定程度上解释了蜘蛛食物供应的减少?

  • A)The long tradition of collecting insects.收集昆虫的悠久传统。
  • B)Fast reproduction of their competitors.它们的竞争者快速繁殖。
  • C)Chemicals used for killing insects.用于杀灭昆虫的化学物质。
  • D)The extinction of a lot of wildlife大量野生动物的灭绝。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 答非所问A The long tradition of collecting insects 取自 British butterflies are the most studied group of insects in the world, due to the long tradition of collecting and observing them，那里讲的是蝴蝶被研究多的原因，与蜘蛛食物减少没有任何因果关系；A 答非所问。
B) 无中生有B Fast reproduction of their competitors 称蜘蛛竞争者快速繁殖导致食物减少；但原文 preying on insects that are our competitors for crops 说的是昆虫是人类的庄稼竞争者，主体是人类的庄稼竞争者，并非蜘蛛的竞争者；且原文从未提及 fast reproduction；属无中生有。
C) ✓ 正确由预判 杀虫剂，C Chemicals used for killing insects 是 pesticide 的标准释义，是定位句最直接的同义替换，无任何外加信息。
  • pesticide use↔Chemicals used for killing insects
  • thought to be to blame↔Chemicals used for killing insects
D) 过度推断D The extinction of a lot of wildlife 称大量野生动物灭绝；原文说的是 the destruction of wild areas for development——破坏野生区（栖息地），不是野生动物灭绝；二者范围与程度都不同，D 过度推断。
P4

A recent landmark study identified a 75% fall in insect populations between 1989 and 2016, with pesticide use thought to be to blame along with the destruction of wild areas for development.

近期一项标志性研究发现，1989 到 2016 年间昆虫种群下降了 75%，元凶是杀虫剂使用以及为开发而破坏野生区域。

54

What does the passage say is conservationists' biggest worry?

文章说保护主义者最大的担忧是什么?

  • A)A variety of spiders are threatened due to pollution of marshes and wasteland.由于沼泽和荒地的污染,多种蜘蛛受到威胁。
  • B)Certain species of spiders are endangered due to loss of their natural homes.由于失去自然栖息地,某些蜘蛛物种濒临灭绝。
  • C)An increasing number of spiders are being killed by deadly pesticides.越来越多的蜘蛛正被致命的杀虫剂杀死。
  • D)More and more spider species are found losing their ability to migrate.越来越多的蜘蛛物种被发现正在失去迁徙能力。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 无中生有A A variety of spiders are threatened due to pollution of marshes and wasteland 称蜘蛛因沼泽与荒地受污染而受威胁；但原文 the majority thrive in marshes and wasteland 说蜘蛛多在沼泽与荒地繁衍，从未提到污染；A 用了原文出现的 marshes and wasteland 字眼，但污染（pollution）是无中生有。
B) ✓ 正确由预判 栖息地丧失威胁某些物种，B Certain species of spiders are endangered due to loss of their natural homes 与原文 those varieties、are threatened、habitat loss 三处词一一对应，是定位句的高度同义替换。
  • those varieties↔Certain species of spiders
  • are threatened↔are endangered
  • habitat loss and fragmentation↔loss of their natural homes
C) 答非所问C An increasing number of spiders are being killed by deadly pesticides 称越来越多蜘蛛被杀虫剂直接杀死；但原文 pesticide use thought to be to blame 说杀虫剂杀的是昆虫（飞虫）、进而影响蜘蛛食物，并非杀虫剂直接杀蜘蛛；C 答非所问，且与题干 biggest worry 不符。
D) 过度推断D More and more spider species are found losing their ability to migrate 称越来越多物种正失去迁徙能力；原文 habitat loss and fragmentation, which makes it impossible for them to migrate 说的是栖息地丧失与碎片化导致无法迁徙——无法迁徙是栖息地丧失的结果，而题干问的是最担忧的原因/对象；D 把结果当作主担忧，且加入了原文没有的 more and more（递增趋势），属过度推断。
P4

But conservationists are most concerned about those varieties that are threatened due to habitat loss and fragmentation, which makes it impossible for them to migrate.

但保护者最担忧的是那些因栖息地丧失与碎片化而受威胁的物种，这种碎片化使得它们无法迁徙。

55

What wish does the author express close to the end of the passage?

作者在文章接近结尾处表达了什么愿望?

  • A)More people would recognise spiders' unique status in the ecosystem.更多人会认识到蜘蛛在生态系统中的独特地位。
  • B)People would show greater enthusiasm for spiders than for butterflies.人们对蜘蛛表现出比对蝴蝶更大的热情。
  • C)There would be sufficient corridors for spiders to move along.会有足够的通道供蜘蛛沿着移动。
  • D)There would be more people appreciating spiders' splendour.会有更多人欣赏蜘蛛的壮丽。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 偷换概念A More people would recognise spiders' unique status in the ecosystem 称作者希望更多人认识到蜘蛛在生态系统中的独特地位；但原文 in spite of their unique status as nature's spinners 说蜘蛛的独特地位是 纺织者，而非 生态系统中的地位；且 unique status 出现在 in spite of 让步状语里，不是作者愿望句的内容；A 偷换了概念。
B) 与原文相反B People would show greater enthusiasm for spiders than for butterflies 称作者希望人们对蜘蛛比对蝴蝶更热情；但原文 It seems unlikely that spiders will ever attract the same level of human enthusiasm as bees, birds or butterflies 是作者承认蜘蛛 不太可能 像蜜蜂/鸟/蝴蝶那样受欢迎，这是事实判断而非作者愿望——B 把作者认为不可能的对照句反向当成愿望，与原文相反。
C) 答非所问C There would be sufficient corridors for spiders to move along 称作者希望有足够的廊道供蜘蛛通行；但 wildlife corridors 出现在 spiders will be enabled to migrate by themselves, adapting to climate change by moving along wildlife corridors，属于上一段保护政策的内容；题干已限定 close to the end of the passage，C 位置不对、答非所问。
D) ✓ 正确由预判 更多人能欣赏到蜘蛛的精彩，D There would be more people appreciating spiders' splendour 用 more people 概括 animal lovers，用 appreciating spiders’ splendour 概括 recognised the ways in which spiders are simply terrific——一一对应，是定位句的精准同义替换。
  • more animal lovers↔more people
  • recognised the ways in which spiders are simply terrific↔appreciating spiders' splendour
P6

But as they reveal themselves in all their splendour this autumn, it would be a good thing if more animal lovers recognised the ways in which spiders are simply terrific.

但当它们在这个秋天展现出全部精彩时，如果有更多动物爱好者能认识到蜘蛛之妙，那真是一件好事。
