原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

As interdependent beings we cannot thrive independent of others. We like to work with people who are like-minded, with whom we share a common interest, or who also believe in a cause we are serious about. This is evident in how we have organized ourselves into various collective groups such as nations, regions, communities, organizations, and families.

作为相互依存的存在，我们无法脱离他人而蓬勃发展。我们喜欢与志趣相投的人一起工作，与他们共享共同的兴趣，或者他们也相信我们所认真对待的事业。这一点从我们将自己组织成各种集体群体中便可明显看出，例如国家、地区、社区、组织和家庭。

P2

Does the group you belong to have a strong collective consciousness or shared beliefs? Is there interdependence among its members? Do you consider this group a significant part of your existence? If your answers are affirmative, then you are experiencing a micro version of solidarity.

你所属的群体是否具有强烈的集体意识或共同信念？其成员之间是否存在相互依存关系？你是否认为这个群体是你生命中重要的一部分？如果你的回答是肯定的，那么你正在经历一种微观层面的团结。

P3

What is solidarity? Its simplest definition in the dictionary is: "… a unifying opinion, feeling, purpose or interest among a group of people."

什么是团结？词典中最简单的定义是：“一群人之间统一的意见、感情、目标或利益。”

P4

In this increasingly diverse society we live in, solidarity takes on a complex and multifaceted character as it forms bonds of interdependence among unique individuals living in a fast-changing environment and working within a complex division of labor.

在我们所生活的这个日益多元化的社会中，团结呈现出复杂而多面的特征，因为它在生活于快速变化环境中的独特个体之间建立起相互依存的纽带，而这些个体又在复杂的劳动分工体系中工作。

P5

One facet of solidarity fits well with the principles of communitarianism(社群主义). In this setting, individuals give up their own interests to serve the common good. However, its strict adherence to social cohesiveness and exclusiveness restricts expression and practice of individual differences and preferences. Consequently, individuality and autonomy are sacrificed to maintain the unity of the group.

团结的一个方面与社群主义的原则十分契合。在这种情况下，个人放弃自身利益，以服务于共同利益。然而，对社会凝聚力和排他性的严格坚持，限制了个人差异和偏好的表达与实践。因此，为了维护群体的统一，个性和自主性被牺牲了。

P6

Solidarity is also viewed as a reaction to the extreme emphasis on individualism and freedom of choice. It goes against the liberal idea of unrestricted individuals who see society as a hindrance to their freedom to make choices and to act according to what is good for them regardless of how these will affect others.

团结也被视为对极端强调个人主义和选择自由的一种反应。它反对自由主义关于不受约束的个人的理念，这种理念认为社会妨碍了个人作出选择并按照对自己有利的方式行事，而不管这些选择和行为将如何影响他人。

P7

But despite its many facets, social theorists have viewed solidarity as a key factor in bringing about order and progress in society. They believe that for human society to survive as it becomes more diverse, more new bonds need to be formed based on the differences. In the light of this, they regard a genuine solidarity as more than just shallow relationships based on common interests. Solidarity is not just a product of strategic calculations of how much it will benefit the self. It is not an exclusive class founded on the you-and-me-against-the-world principle. Rather, it is inclusive and is not forced. Acting out of solidarity means supporting and standing up for each other — in recognition that one's fate is in the fate of the other. It is a bond characterized by cooperative practices and strengthened by the concern for the well-being of others or the common good. It fights for the protection of human dignity and listens to and acts on the cries of the most vulnerable.

但是，尽管团结具有许多不同的层面，社会理论家仍将其视为促成社会秩序与进步的关键因素。他们认为，随着人类社会变得更加多元化，要使其得以存续，就需要基于差异建立更多新的纽带。鉴于此，他们认为真正的团结不仅仅是建立在共同利益之上的浅层关系。团结并不只是对某种行为将使自我获益多少进行战略计算的产物。它也不是建立在“你我对抗全世界”原则之上的排他性阶层。相反，它具有包容性，并非强迫形成。出于团结而行动，意味着相互支持、彼此挺身而出——承认一个人的命运与他人的命运息息相关。团结是一种以合作实践为特征的纽带，并因对他人福祉或共同利益的关切而得到加强。它为保护人的尊严而斗争，倾听最脆弱群体的呼声，并对此采取行动。

解析逐题详解

46

What does the author say about human beings?

作者对人类有何看法?

  • A)They cannot prosper without cooperating with each other.他们若不相互合作就无法繁荣发展。
  • B)They share a common interest and believe in a just cause.他们有共同的利益并相信正义的事业。
  • C)They think and behave alike in different social groups.他们在不同社会群体中的思维和行为方式相似。
  • D)They cannot gain independence without fighting for it.他们若不为之抗争就无法获得独立。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确They cannot prosper without cooperating with each other. 把 cannot thrive independent of others 改写为"必须合作才能兴旺"，正是预判内容的同义转述。
  • thrive↔prosper
  • cannot thrive independent of others↔cannot prosper without cooperating with each other
B) 偷换概念They share a common interest and believe in a just cause. 取自 we share a common interest, or who also believe in a cause we are serious about，但原文这是"喜欢和谁一起工作"的举例，不是对 human beings 的总体陈述；且 cause 被偷换为 just cause（正义事业）。
C) 过度推断They think and behave alike in different social groups. 声称"在不同群体中想法行为都一样"，原文只说人喜欢和"like-minded"的人共事，并未说人在所有群体中思想行为都相同。
D) 与原文相反They cannot gain independence without fighting for it. 谈"独立要靠斗争争取"，但原文 we cannot thrive independent of others 强调的是"无法独立"而非"为独立而战"，话题相反。
P1

As interdependent beings we cannot thrive independent of others.

作为相互依赖的存在，我们无法脱离他人独自繁荣兴盛。

47

What do we learn about solidarity in an increasingly diverse society?

关于日益多元化社会中的团结,我们了解到什么?

  • A)It gives rise to a complicated division of labor.它引发了复杂的劳动分工。
  • B)It involves many aspects with great complexity.它涉及许多具有高度复杂性的方面。
  • C)It brings individuals together as environment changes.它随着环境变化将个体凝聚在一起。
  • D)It embraces the characteristics of different individuals.它包容不同个体的特征。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 强加因果It gives rise to a complicated division of labor. 说 solidarity 导致了复杂分工，但原文 working within a complex division of labor 是说 solidarity 发生"在"复杂分工"之内"，分工是背景而非结果。
B) ✓ 正确involves many aspects with great complexity 同时覆盖原文的 complex 和 multifaceted 两个核心修饰语，且未做任何额外延伸。
  • takes on a complex and multifaceted character↔involves many aspects with great complexity
  • takes on↔involves
C) 以偏概全It brings individuals together as environment changes. 把"形成相互依赖纽带"窄化为"把个体聚拢"，且偷换了主线——题干问的是"我们关于 solidarity 在多元社会中学到什么"，正确答案应概括其"特征"，C 选了一处细节当主答。
D) 偷换概念It embraces the characteristics of different individuals. 说 solidarity"接纳"个体差异，但原文只说它在 unique individuals 之间形成纽带，并未说它"接纳"或"包含"个体特征。
P4

In this increasingly diverse society we live in, solidarity takes on a complex and multifaceted character as it forms bonds of interdependence among unique individuals living in a fast-changing environment and working within a complex division of labor.

在我们所处的这个日益多元化的社会里，solidarity 呈现出复杂而多面的特征，因为它在快速变化的环境中、在复杂的分工内部，于各不相同的个体之间形成了相互依赖的纽带。

48

What would we witness in the setting of communitarianism?

在社群主义的背景下我们会看到什么?

  • A)Ruling of social norms and ridding of individuality.社会规范的统治和个性的消除。
  • B)Emphasis on the adherence to individual differences.强调坚持个体差异。
  • C)Sacrifice of personal interests for the common good.为了公共利益而牺牲个人利益。
  • D)Elimination of individualism and personal choices.消除个人主义和个人选择。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 以偏概全Ruling of social norms and ridding of individuality. 把"个体在某情境下放弃个人利益"夸大为"社会规范统治、个性被彻底清除"，原文虽提到 individuality 被牺牲，但用的是 sacrificed（让位）而非 ridding（彻底清除）。
B) 与原文相反Emphasis on the adherence to individual differences. 说社群主义强调坚持个体差异，但原文 strict adherence to social cohesiveness and exclusiveness restricts expression and practice of individual differences 明说它限制了个体差异的表达，完全相反。
C) ✓ 正确Sacrifice of personal interests for the common good. 是对 individuals give up their own interests to serve the common good 的完整、保守的名词化改写，与预判完全吻合。
  • individuals give up their own interests↔Sacrifice of personal interests
  • to serve the common good↔for the common good
D) 过度推断Elimination of individualism and personal choices. 用 Elimination（彻底消除）描述社群主义的效果，但原文只说 individuality and autonomy are sacrificed（让位/牺牲），并未达到"彻底消除"的程度。
P5

In this setting, individuals give up their own interests to serve the common good.

在这种情境下，个人放弃自身利益以服务于共同利益。

49

What does solidarity entail according to the passage?

根据文章,团结意味着什么?

  • A)A limit to personal differences.对个人差异的限制。
  • B)An opposition to individualism.对个人主义的反对。
  • C)A hindrance to social advancement.对社会进步的阻碍。
  • D)An inevitable end to liberal pursuits.自由主义追求的必然终结。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴A limit to personal differences. 来自 restricts expression and practice of individual differences，那是讲社群主义的代价，不是讲 solidarity 整体的内涵；题干问的是 solidarity entail 什么。
B) ✓ 正确An opposition to individualism 是 a reaction to the extreme emphasis on individualism 最直接的同义改写，与预判一致。
  • a reaction to the extreme emphasis on individualism↔An opposition to individualism
  • the extreme emphasis on individualism and freedom of choice↔individualism
C) 张冠李戴A hindrance to social advancement. 把 solidarity 说成"社会进步的障碍"，但原文 see society as a hindrance to their freedom to make choices 是说"自由派认为社会是其自由的障碍"，hindrance 的主语应为 society 而非 solidarity；并且 P7 明说 solidarity 是 order and progress 的关键。
D) 过度推断An inevitable end to liberal pursuits. 说 solidarity 会"不可避免地终结"自由派追求，但原文只说它是对极端个人主义的 reaction（反对/回应），并未说会"终结"或必然成功，且 inevitable 加重了语气。
P6

Solidarity is also viewed as a reaction to the extreme emphasis on individualism and freedom of choice.

solidarity 也被视为对极端强调个人主义与选择自由的一种反应（反对）。

50

What do social theorists think of solidarity?

社会理论家如何看待团结?

  • A)It is especially relevant to the most vulnerable.它与最弱势群体尤其相关。
  • B)It is most effective in protecting human dignity.它在保护人类尊严方面最为有效。
  • C)It is essential to progress and order in society.它对社会的进步与秩序至关重要。
  • D)It is indispensable to avoiding shallow relationships.它对于避免肤浅关系不可或缺。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 以偏概全It is especially relevant to the most vulnerable. 取自 listens to and acts on the cries of the most vulnerable，原文这是对 solidarity 内涵的一种延伸描述，且"especially relevant"是干扰项的额外断言；题干问的是社会理论家对 solidarity 的整体看法，而非"它最关乎谁"。
B) 过度推断It is most effective in protecting human dignity. 用 most effective（最有效）做绝对化判断，但原文 It fights for the protection of human dignity 仅说它"为人类尊严而战"，并未与其他事物比较"最有效"。
C) ✓ 正确essential to progress and order in society 是 a key factor in bringing about order and progress in society 的标准同义改写，与预判完全对应。
  • a key factor in bringing about order and progress in society↔essential to progress and order in society
  • bringing about order and progress in society↔progress and order in society
D) 偷换概念It is indispensable to avoiding shallow relationships. 说 solidarity 对"避免肤浅关系"必不可少，但原文 they regard a genuine solidarity as more than just shallow relationships based on common interests 是说"真正的 solidarity 不仅仅是肤浅关系"，是在界定 solidarity 本身，而非说它用于"避免肤浅关系"。
P7

But despite its many facets, social theorists have viewed solidarity as a key factor in bringing about order and progress in society.

但是，尽管 solidarity 有诸多面向，社会理论家们仍把它视为推动社会秩序与进步的关键因素。
