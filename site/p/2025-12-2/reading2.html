原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

People who repeatedly give unwanted advice can be well-meaning and genuinely want to help. Others might do this out of a sense of self-worth around the ability to influence others. One study found that people with a high tendency to seek power were more likely to give advice than those with an opposite tendency.

反复提出他人不需要的建议的人可能是出于好意，也是真心想要帮忙。另一些人这样做，可能是因为他们从影响他人的能力中获得了自我价值感。一项研究发现，与具有相反倾向的人相比，权力寻求倾向较强的人更有可能提出建议。

P2

Under the guise(外表) of unselfishness, people may be driven to give you unwanted advice because it makes them feel powerful or in control. They may not be fully aware of this drive, however.

人们可能以无私为幌子，实际上是因为提出你不需要的建议会让他们感到自己有力量或能够掌控局面，才会这样做。不过，他们可能并没有充分意识到这种驱动力。

P3

These people may also display a problematic degree of emotional vulnerability, becoming upset very quickly, and taking a long time to calm down. It is possible that their emotions were only validated in childhood when they were at their loudest, encouraging them to adopt responses to discomfort that are excessive in most situations.

这些人还可能表现出程度较高且有问题的情绪脆弱性，很快变得不安，并且需要很长时间才能平静下来。他们的情绪或许只有在童年时期表现得最为激烈时才会得到认可，这促使他们养成了在大多数情况下都过度应对不适的方式。

P4

When someone is giving advice in order to make themselves feel more powerful, there is underlying anxiety to their behavior that recipients of the advice tend to notice. It can be tempting in this situation to react harshly to the advice giver and to accuse them of being manipulative, but this approach might produce an undesired result.

当一个人提出建议是为了让自己感到更有力量时，他们的行为背后存在一种焦虑，而接受建议的人往往能够察觉到这一点。在这种情况下，人们很容易严厉地回应提出建议的人，并指责他们是在操纵他人，但这种做法可能会产生不理想的结果。

P5

If the act of giving advice is contributing actively to someone's feelings of self-worth, an outright(直率的) rejection may be perceived as a threat, activating their fight-or-flight response, possibly causing them to double down on their validation-seeking behavior, or leading to a larger conflict.

如果提出建议这一行为正在积极地促成某人的自我价值感，那么直率的拒绝可能会被视为一种威胁，激活他们的战或逃反应，可能使他们进一步强化寻求认可的行为，或者导致更大的冲突。

P6

The key is to validate without over-identifying. You can let them know that you have heard them and appreciate where they are coming from without taking on the potentially damaging narrative that you could not have gotten by without their help. You had better say something like, "Thanks for the idea. I have my own plan for handling this, but I really appreciate your perspective and will take it into consideration. Can I let you know when I need help in the future?"

关键在于给予认可，但不要过度认同。你可以让他们知道你听到了他们的话，也理解他们的出发点，同时不要接受那种可能有害的说法，即没有他们的帮助你就无法解决问题。你最好这样说：“谢谢你的建议。我有自己的计划来处理这件事，但我真的很感谢你的看法，也会考虑你的意见。以后我需要帮助时，可以告诉你吗？”

P7

If you have trouble setting boundaries tactfully, prioritize working on your own ability to self-regulate. As uncomfortable as it may be to continuously receive, if you can respond with compassion, the situation will likely defuse much faster.

如果你难以以圆 tact 的方式设定界限，就应优先提升自己的自我调节能力。即使持续接受这些建议可能令人感到不适，只要你能够以同理心作出回应，局面很可能会更快缓和下来。

P8

Though it is usually not the intended outcome of giving unsolicited advice, many who receive it often feel stressed, offended, or simply annoyed by unwanted suggestions. Setting a boundary in this regard, if you feel you need one, is perfectly reasonable and something that can bring you increased emotional safety.

尽管提出未经请求的建议通常并非为了达到这样的结果，但许多接受建议的人常常会因这些不需要的建议而感到压力、受到冒犯，或者仅仅感到恼火。如果你觉得有必要，在这方面设定界限完全合理，而且这能让你获得更强的情绪安全感。

P9

It is best to set boundaries in a way that takes into account the individual's underlying reasons for doing what they are doing to avoid unnecessary conflict and more stress.

设定界限时，最好考虑到对方这样做背后的原因，以避免不必要的冲突和更多压力。

解析逐题详解

51

What is the finding of one study about people who keep giving unsolicited advice?

一项研究关于不断提供主动建议的人有什么发现?

  • A)They turn out to be well-meaning.他们原来是出于好意的。
  • B)They are genuinely kind-hearted.他们确实心地善良。
  • C)They are capable of influencing others.他们有能力影响他人。
  • D)They are inclined to crave for power.他们倾向于渴望权力。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 答非所问They turn out to be well-meaning 取自首句 can be well-meaning，但那只是 give advice 的一种可能动机的铺垫，并非“研究的发现”。
B) 答非所问They are genuinely kind-hearted 改写自 genuinely want to help，同样位于铺垫句，不是研究结论。
C) 偷换概念They are capable of influencing others 把 self-worth around the ability to influence others 中“自我价值感来自影响他人的能力”误读为“具备影响他人能力”，且这同样不在 study 结论里。
D) ✓ 正确四项中只有 inclined to crave for power 与预判“求权欲强者更爱给建议”形成 1:1 同义对应。
  • a high tendency to seek power↔inclined to crave for power
P1

One study found that people with a high tendency to seek power were more likely to give advice than those with an opposite tendency.

一项研究发现，求权欲望强的人比求权欲望相反（低）的人更倾向于给出建议。

52

What is the problem with people who are driven to give you unsolicited advice?

那些被驱使向你提供主动建议的人有什么问题?

  • A)They may be quick to upset others.他们可能很快就会让别人不高兴。
  • B)They may be emotionally vulnerable.他们可能在情感上很脆弱。
  • C)They want their voice to be the loudest.他们想让自己的声音最响亮。
  • D)They respond to discomfort excessively.他们对不适过度反应。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴They may be quick to upset others 把原文中“自己易激动”扭曲为“使他人易激动”——动作发出者由 these people 变成对他人施加影响。
B) ✓ 正确预判的“情绪脆弱”在四项中只与 emotionally vulnerable 对应；A/C/D 都来自定位句的修饰部分。
  • a problematic degree of emotional vulnerability↔emotionally vulnerable
C) 偷换概念They want their voice to be the loudest 改自 their emotions were only validated in childhood when they were at their loudest，那是对成因的猜测且发生在童年，并非“现在的问题”本身。
D) 答非所问They respond to discomfort excessively 取自 adopt responses to discomfort that are excessive in most situations，那是脆弱情绪导致的“反应方式”，是表现/后果，而题干问的是“问题本身”。
P3

These people may also display a problematic degree of emotional vulnerability, becoming upset very quickly, and taking a long time to calm down.

这些人还可能表现出某种程度上有问题的情绪脆弱性——很快就情绪激动，且久久不能平复。

53

What is the advice receiver tempted to do when noticing the advice giver's underlying anxiety?

当注意到建议提供者潜在的焦虑时,建议接受者会倾向于做什么?

  • A)Reveal the undesired result of their approach.揭示他们的方法所带来的不良结果。
  • B)Refuse their advice in an undisguised manner.以不加掩饰的方式拒绝他们的建议。
  • C)Bring their real motives to the surface.将他们的真实动机揭露出来。
  • D)Accuse them of trying to control others.指责他们试图控制他人。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 无中生有Reveal the undesired result of their approach 把原文 this approach might produce an undesired result 中“己方做法的结果”硬塞为“揭露对方做法的结果”，且 reveal 这一动作原文从未出现。
B) 张冠李戴Refuse their advice in an undisguised manner 取自 an outright rejection 的 outright（直率的），属于 Q54 区间内容；与 Q53 问的“被诱使做什么”无关。
C) 无中生有Bring their real motives to the surface 谈“把真实动机暴露出来”，原文虽提到 They may not be fully aware of this drive，但从未说被诱使去把动机带到台面上。
D) ✓ 正确在 to react harshly 与 to accuse them of being manipulative 两个候选动作里，只有 Accuse them of trying to control others 与后者形成精确同义改写。
  • accuse them of being manipulative↔Accuse them of trying to control others
P4

It can be tempting in this situation to react harshly to the advice giver and to accuse them of being manipulative, but this approach might produce an undesired result.

在这种情况下，人很容易被诱使去强硬回怼劝告者并指责他们在操控自己，但这种做法可能导致不希望出现的结果。

54

How is the advice giver likely to react to the rejection of their advice?

建议提供者对他们的建议被拒绝可能会有什么反应?

  • A)They may regard it as a threat.他们可能会将其视为一种威胁。
  • B)They may modify their behavior.他们可能会改变自己的行为。
  • C)They get ready for a larger conflict.他们为更大的冲突做好准备。
  • D)They begin to doubt their self-worth.他们开始怀疑自己的自我价值。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判“视为威胁”在四项中只与 They may regard it as a threat 形成 1:1 同义对应；B/C/D 均取自定位句末尾的分词链或脱离原文。
  • may be perceived as a threat↔They may regard it as a threat
B) 与原文相反They may modify their behavior 暗示劝告者会调整行为；原文却是 double down on their validation-seeking behavior——加倍重复原行为，方向相反。
C) 过度推断They get ready for a larger conflict 描述劝告者“准备好”打更大架；原文是 possibly...leading to a larger conflict，只是分词链中可能的下游结果，并非劝告者主动准备。
D) 与原文相反They begin to doubt their self-worth 说劝告者开始怀疑自己；原文方向相反——他们的 self-worth 被威胁后，会更努力地寻求认可（double down on validation-seeking），而不是怀疑。
P5

If the act of giving advice is contributing actively to someone's feelings of self-worth, an outright rejection may be perceived as a threat, activating their fight-or-flight response, possibly causing them to double down on their validation-seeking behavior, or leading to a larger conflict.

如果给建议这一行为正主动地构建着对方的自我价值感，那么一次直率的拒绝可能被对方视为威胁，激活其“战或逃”反应，可能导致他们加倍寻求认可，或引发更大的冲突。

55

What should the advice receiver do to avoid unnecessary conflict?

建议接受者应该怎么做才能避免不必要的冲突?

  • A)Concede the advice is perfectly reasonable.承认该建议是完全合理的。
  • B)Give their reasons for not taking the advice.给出他们不采纳该建议的理由。
  • C)Understand why the advice giver offers the advice.理解建议提供者为什么要提供这个建议。
  • D)Explain their chosen way of handling the situation.解释他们所选择的处理情况的方式。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴Concede the advice is perfectly reasonable 让步承认建议合理，是放弃自己立场；原文是劝考者“自己设界限是 perfectly reasonable”，主语完全不同。
B) 偷换概念Give their reasons for not taking the advice 让 receiver 说出自己的拒绝理由；原文范本话术 I have my own plan for handling this 谈的是“我自有方案”，并未要求陈述拒绝理由，更不是 P9 给出的总策略。
C) ✓ 正确预判“顾及对方深层动机”在四项中只与 Understand why the advice giver offers the advice 形成 1:1 同义对应。
  • takes into account the individual's underlying reasons for doing what they are doing↔Understand why the advice giver offers the advice
D) 以偏概全Explain their chosen way of handling the situation 描述 receiver 解释自己的处理方式，对应 I have my own plan for handling this 的示范话术之一，但题干问的是“避免冲突的做法”——文末 P9 的总结才是答案。
P9

It is best to set boundaries in a way that takes into account the individual's underlying reasons for doing what they are doing to avoid unnecessary conflict and more stress.

最好以一种顾及当事人行为背后深层动机的方式来设界限，以避免不必要的冲突和更多压力。
