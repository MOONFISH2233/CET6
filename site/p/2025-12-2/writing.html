范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 152 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

It is believed that teachers can exert a profound influence on their students' academic pursuit and personal development. Having spent years in classrooms, I firmly agree that a dedicated teacher shapes not only what we learn but also who we become.

On the one hand, teachers guide our academic growth. My high-school chemistry teacher, Mr. Li, turned a subject I once dreaded into my favourite one. By designing vivid experiments and patiently answering my endless questions, he inspired me to pursue science and eventually major in it at university. On the other hand, teachers mould our character. Through his fairness and honesty, Mr. Li taught me to value integrity over easy shortcuts, a lesson that still steers my decisions today.

In conclusion, a good teacher enlightens the mind and cultivates the heart at the same time. Their influence, though quiet and gradual, reaches far beyond examinations and often lasts a whole lifetime.
