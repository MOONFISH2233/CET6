5

In our evolutionary past, it was a very good thing that our attention could be easily seized by negative information, since it might well indicate a dangerous risk to our own survival.   • A)Whether they actually signify a substantial change.它们是否真正标志着实质性的改变。
  • B)Whether they effectively protect animals at large.它们是否能有效地保护整体上的动物。

  • D)Whether they can be regarded as ethical.它们是否可以被视为符合伦理的。

D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反 录音说 This signifies a change——「这标志着一种改变」是肯定语气、已成定论，恰恰不是 unclear 的对象；A a substantial change 盗用了 change 原词，却把已确定的事说成尚不明确，正话反说。
B) 答非所问 录音整体讲 animal-free/animal welfare，但说 unclear 的那句问的是 ethical，不是 protect animals at large；B 借了全篇的「动物」话题来冒充存疑对象，原文没把「是否真护动物」列为不明确之事。
C) 原词陷阱 录音只在开头报告名 fashion and sustainability report 出现过 sustainability，从未说「是否可持续」尚不明确；C sustainable 盗用了这个开头出现的原词来答非所问。

However, it is unclear whether these new products can be considered ethical.

然而，这些新产品是否能被视为合乎道德/伦理，尚不明确。

信号However 转折后接 unclear。录音先说 This signifies a change，紧接 However, it is unclear whether these new products can be considered ethical——转折词 However 加存疑词 unclear 双重信号叠加，转折之后才是真意，存疑的对象 ethical 就在这句里。

【题目】 What is one of the facts stated by the speaker?

说话者陈述的事实之一是什么?

  • A)The era we live in is the most peaceful in history.我们生活的时代是历史上最和平的。
  • B)The world has seen more violence in recent years.近年来世界上发生了更多暴力事件。
  • C)The environmental welfare has worsened in the world.世界环境福祉已经恶化。
  • D)The belief is less prevalent that the world is going to hell.认为世界正在走向毁灭的观念不那么普遍了。
解析
A（细节题）
B) 与原文相反 选项说 more violence in recent years，但录音明说 violence of all kinds in steep decline（各类暴力急剧下降），方向正好相反，是把'下降'听反成'增多'。
C) 与原文相反 选项称 environmental welfare has worsened，但录音说每一项 environmental welfare…has improved rather than deteriorated（改善而非恶化），用 worsened 偷换了 improved，方向反了。
D) 未提及 选项说'认为世界要完蛋的信念不那么流行了'，但录音恰恰描述这种悲观信念很普遍（everything was terrible, and that the world was going to hell、第 3 段多数英法人觉得世界在变坏），从没说它在减弱，属凭空生造。

We are living in history's most peaceful era, with violence of all kinds in steep decline. Those are the facts.

我们正生活在历史上最和平的时代，各类暴力都在急剧下降。这些就是事实。

信号'Those are the facts' 直接点明这是事实。演讲者紧接着用 Those are the facts. 给前面一串陈述盖章为'事实'，这就是答案信号；其中 history's most peaceful era 正对应题干要的那个 fact。
替换history's most peaceful era↔the most peaceful in history同义改写：词序调换、词性微调
排除B)与原文相反选项说 more violence in recent years，但录音明说 violence of all kinds in steep decline（各类暴力急剧下降），方向正好相反，是把'下降'听反成'增多'。 借用原文：violence of all kinds in steep decline C)与原文相反选项称 environmental welfare has worsened，但录音说每一项 environmental welfare…has improved rather than deteriorated（改善而非恶化），用 worsened 偷换了 improved，方向反了。 借用原文：Every single measure of material and environmental welfare…has improved rather than deteriorated D)未提及选项说'认为世界要完蛋的信念不那么流行了'，但录音恰恰描述这种悲观信念很普遍（everything was terrible, and that the world was going to hell、第 3 段多数英法人觉得世界在变坏），从没说它在减弱，属凭空生造。
定位P2 We are living in history's most peaceful era, with violence of all kinds in steep decline. Those are the facts. 我们正生活在历史上最和平的时代，各类暴力都在急剧下降。这些就是事实。

It is best explained as the result of various psychological biases that served a purpose for our evolutionary ancestors—but now, in a media-saturated era, constantly mislead us.

【题目】 What was a very good thing in our evolutionary past?

在我们的进化历史中什么是非常好的事情?

  • A)Spreading exciting news around us far and wide.把令人兴奋的消息广泛传播到我们周围。
  • B)Vacuuming up depressing or enraging stories.大量吸收令人沮丧或愤怒的故事。
  • C)Calculating dangerous risks to our survival.计算对我们生存的危险风险。
  • D)Paying attention to negative information.关注负面信息。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反 选项说 Spreading exciting news around us far and wide.，但原文说 Really good news…can be a lot harder to spot（好消息其实更难被注意到），且演讲者夸的是关注'坏'消息有生存价值，不是传播好消息，方向相反。
B) 原词陷阱 选项 Vacuuming up depressing or enraging stories. 几乎照搬原文 vacuum up depressing or enraging stories from across the globe，听到原词极想选；但原文说这是'当今超连接时代'我们对坏消息上瘾的恶果，而非'进化过去的好事'，时间与褒贬都对不上。
C) 偷换概念 选项说 Calculating dangerous risks to our survival.，原文有 a dangerous risk to our own survival，但说的是负面信息可能'意味着'生存危险，故关注它是好事，并未说去'计算'风险；把'感知威胁'偷换成'计算风险'。

In our evolutionary past, it was a very good thing that our attention could be easily seized by negative information, since it might well indicate a dangerous risk to our own survival.

在我们的进化过去，注意力容易被负面信息攫取是一件很好的事，因为这很可能意味着对我们自身生存的危险威胁。

信号原句直给 'a very good thing that…'。题干 a very good thing 在原文原样出现于 it was a very good thing that our attention could be easily seized by negative information，that 从句里的内容就是那件'好事'，即答案信号。
