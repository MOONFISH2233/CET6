原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Could you get by without using the internet for four and a half years? That's exactly what singer and actress Selena Gomez has done in a bid to improve her mental health.

你能在四年半的时间里不使用互联网而生活下去吗？这正是歌手兼演员赛琳娜·戈麦斯为了改善自己的心理健康所做的事情。

P2

She has spoken extensively about the relationship between her social media usage and her mental wellbeing, recalling feeling like "an addict" when she became Instagram's most followed user in 2016. "Taking a break from social media was the best decision that I've ever made for my mental health", says she. "The unnecessary hate and comparisons went away once I put my phone down."

她曾多次谈到使用社交媒体与心理健康之间的关系，并回忆说，2016年成为Instagram上粉丝最多的用户时，她感觉自己像“上瘾者”一样。“暂时离开社交媒体是我为心理健康所做过的最好的决定，”她说，“放下手机后，那些不必要的仇恨和攀比都消失了。”

P3

Ditching the web at large, however, is a far more subtle and complicated prospect. The increasing digitisation of our society means that everything from paying a gas bill to plotting a route to a friend's house and even making a phone call is at the mercy of your internet connection. Actively opting out of using the internet becomes a matter of privilege.

然而，彻底放弃互联网则是一个微妙得多、复杂得多的选择。社会日益数字化意味着，从支付燃气费、规划去朋友家的路线，甚至到拨打电话，一切都要受互联网连接的制约。主动选择不使用互联网，变成了一种只有有条件的人才能享有的特权。

P4

Ms Gomez's multi-millionaire status has allowed her to take the "social" out of social media, so she can continue to leverage her enormous fame while keeping the trolls(恶意挑衅的帖子) at bay. The fact that she's still the second most-followed woman on Instagram suggests it's entirely possible to maintain a significant web profile to promote various projects—by way of a dedicated team—without being exposed to the cruel comments, hate mail and rape or death threats.

戈麦斯女士数百万富翁的身份使她能够从社交媒体中去掉“社交”这一部分，因此她可以继续利用自己巨大的名气，同时把恶意挑衅的帖子拒之门外。她仍然是Instagram上粉丝数量第二多的女性，这一事实表明，通过一个专门的团队来运营，完全有可能在维持重要网络形象、推广各种项目的同时，不接触残酷的评论、仇恨邮件以及强奸或死亡威胁。

P5

It goes without saying that this is fundamentally different from how the rest of us without beauty deals and films to publicise use the likes of Instagram, TikTok and Twitter, but even the concept of a digital detox(戒瘾) requires having a device and connectivity to choose to disconnect from.

不言而喻，这与我们这些没有美容产品代言和电影需要宣传的人使用Instagram、TikTok和Twitter的方式有着根本区别，但即使是“数字戒毒”这一概念，也要求人们先拥有可以选择断开连接的设备和网络。

P6

The UK's digital divide has worsened over the past two years, leaving poorer families without broadband connections in their homes. Digital exclusion is a major threat to wider societal equality in the UK, so witnessing companies like Facebook championing the metaverse(元宇宙) as the next great frontier when school children are struggling to complete their homework feels particularly irritating.

过去两年，英国的数字鸿沟进一步恶化，使得一些较贫困的家庭家中没有宽带连接。数字排斥是对英国更广泛的社会平等构成的重大威胁，因此，当学龄儿童还在为完成家庭作业而苦苦挣扎时，看到Facebook这样的公司把元宇宙吹捧为下一个伟大前沿，尤其令人恼火。

P7

Consequently, it's worth bearing in mind that while deleting all social media accounts will undoubtedly make some feel infinitely better, many other people benefit from the strong sense of community that sharing platforms can breed.

因此，值得牢记的是，虽然删除所有社交媒体账户无疑会让一些人感觉好得多，但许多其他人则受益于分享平台能够培育的强烈社区感。

P8

Internet access will continue to grow in importance as we edge further towards web 3.0, and greater resources and initiatives are needed to provide the underprivileged with the connectivity they desperately need to learn, work and live. It's crucial that people who feel that social media is having a detrimental effect on their mental health are allowed to switch off and for those living in digital exclusion to be able to switch on in the first place.

随着我们进一步迈向Web 3.0，互联网接入的重要性将继续提高，因此需要更多资源和举措，为那些处境不利的人提供他们迫切需要的网络连接，以便学习、工作和生活。必须让那些认为社交媒体正在对其心理健康产生不利影响的人能够关掉网络，也必须让那些遭受数字排斥的人首先有能力接入网络。

解析逐题详解

46

What do we learn about singer and actress Selena Gomez in the past four and a half years?

在过去四年半里,我们对歌手兼演员Selena Gomez有何了解?

  • A)She has had worsening mental problems.她的心理问题日益恶化。
  • B)She has won Instagram's most followers.她赢得了Instagram最多的关注者。
  • C)She has refrained from using social media.她克制自己不使用社交媒体。
  • D)She has succeeded in a bid on the internet.她在互联网上的竞标中取得了成功。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反原文说她远离社媒是为改善心理健康（the best decision that I've ever made for my mental health），而 She has had worsening mental problems 把「为改善心理健康」反过来说成「心理问题加重」，与原文方向相反。
B) 偷换概念原文说她「在 2016 年成为 Instagram 关注最多的用户」（she became Instagram's most followed user in 2016），是过去某一时点的事实；She has won Instagram's most followers 用现在完成时把这件 2016 年的事说成「过去四年半内取得」，与题干 in the past four and a half years 不符。
C) ✓ 正确预判「远离社媒」与 She has refrained from using social media 完全吻合，且原文-选项之间的替换属于六级阅读高频同义替换模式。
  • Taking a break from social media was the best decision that I've ever made for my mental health↔She has refrained from using social media
D) 偷换概念原文 in a bid to improve her mental health 中 bid 意为「努力、尝试」（熟词僻义），整个短语意为「为改善心理健康所作的努力」；She has succeeded in a bid on the internet 把 bid 当作「竞拍」用，与原文意思完全两回事。
P2

Taking a break from social media was the best decision that I've ever made for my mental health

远离社交媒体是我为心理健康做过的最好的决定。

47

Why does actively opting out of using the internet become a matter of privilege?

为什么主动选择不使用互联网成为一种特权问题?

  • A)Most people find it subtle and complicated to give up using the internet.大多数人发现放弃使用互联网既微妙又复杂。
  • B)Most people can hardly ditch the web while avoiding hate and comparisons.大多数人在避免仇恨和比较的同时很难摆脱网络。
  • C)Most people can hardly get by without the internet due to growing digitisation.由于数字化程度不断提高,大多数人没有互联网几乎无法生活。
  • D)Most people have been seriously addicted to the web without being aware of it.大多数人在不知不觉中已经严重沉迷于网络。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 答非所问Most people find it subtle and complicated to give up using the internet 改写自 Ditching the web at large, however, is a far more subtle and complicated prospect，描述的是「难度」，并未回答题干「为何成为特权」。
B) 张冠李戴Most people can hardly ditch the web while avoiding hate and comparisons 把「避开仇恨与攀比」塞给「为何不上网是特权」这一问题；但 The unnecessary hate and comparisons went away once I put my phone down 讲的是 Gomez 远离社媒后的结果，与 Q47 的因果无关。
C) ✓ 正确预判的因果（数字化 → 离不开网 → 不上网成特权）与 Most people can hardly get by without the internet due to growing digitisation 一一对应。
  • The increasing digitisation of our society means that everything from paying a gas bill to plotting a route to a friend's house and even making a phone call is at the mercy of your internet connection↔Most people can hardly get by without the internet due to growing digitisation
  • Could you get by without using the internet for four and a half years?↔Most people can hardly get by without the internet
D) 无中生有Most people have been seriously addicted to the web without being aware of it 拿 like "an addict" 套到「普通人不自觉成瘾」上，但原文只说 Gomez 自己感觉像 addict，并没有「普通人不自觉成瘾」这一论断，且与 Q47 因果无关。
P3

The increasing digitisation of our society means that everything from paying a gas bill to plotting a route to a friend's house and even making a phone call is at the mercy of your internet connection. Actively opting out of using the internet becomes a matter of privilege.

我们社会日益数字化意味着——从交一笔煤气费、规划一条去朋友家的路线，甚至打一通电话——一切都受制于你的网络连接。主动选择不使用互联网由此变成一种特权。

48

Why does the author say "witnessing companies…feels particularly irritating"(Lines 2-4, Para. 6)?

作者为什么说"目睹各公司……感到特别恼火"(第6段第2-4行)?

  • A)The UK digital divide would further worsen due to the metaverse.英国的数字鸿沟会因元宇宙而进一步恶化。
  • B)The concept of the metaverse is believed to be still quite illusory.元宇宙的概念被认为仍然相当虚幻。
  • C)School children would be drawn farther away from the real world.学龄儿童会被拉得离现实世界更远。
  • D)Most families in the UK do not have stable broadband connections.英国大多数家庭没有稳定的宽带连接。
解析
A（推断题）
A) ✓ 正确预判「鼓吹元宇宙会加深数字鸿沟」与 The UK digital divide would further worsen due to the metaverse 完全对应，且推断只走一步，未越界。
  • The UK's digital divide has worsened over the past two years↔The UK digital divide would further worsen due to the metaverse
  • championing the metaverse(元宇宙) as the next great frontier when school children are struggling to complete their homework↔due to the metaverse
B) 无中生有The concept of the metaverse is believed to be still quite illusory 评价的是「元宇宙是否虚幻」，原文只用 the next great frontier 描述公司的宣传口径，并未对 metaverse 概念本身的「虚幻与否」作判断。
C) 答非所问School children would be drawn farther away from the real world 把焦点放在「学童被拉离现实世界」，但原文中学童的位置是 struggling to complete their homework——他们是因为没有宽带连作业都做不了，而不是被元宇宙拉离现实。
D) 以偏概全Most families in the UK do not have stable broadband connections 把原文 leaving poorer families without broadband connections in their homes 的「较贫困家庭」扩大为「大多数家庭」；同时这只是引发 irritating 的背景之一，而不是作者觉得 irritating 的核心原因。
P6

The UK's digital divide has worsened over the past two years, leaving poorer families without broadband connections in their homes. Digital exclusion is a major threat to wider societal equality in the UK, so witnessing companies like Facebook championing the metaverse(元宇宙) as the next great frontier when school children are struggling to complete their homework feels particularly irritating.

英国的数字鸿沟在过去两年里加剧，导致较贫困家庭家中没有宽带。数字排斥是英国社会整体公平的一大威胁，因此当学童正为完成作业犯难时，看到 Facebook 之类的公司还在把元宇宙鼓吹成下一个伟大前沿，就让人尤其感到刺目。

49

What is worth bearing in mind concerning social media platforms?

关于社交媒体平台,什么值得牢记?

  • A)They are conducive to promoting societal equality.它们有助于促进社会平等。
  • B)They help many people feel connected with others.它们帮助许多人感到与他人相连。
  • C)They provide a necessary device for a digital detox.它们为数字排毒提供了必要的设备。
  • D)They create a virtual community on the internet.它们在互联网上创建了一个虚拟社区。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴They are conducive to promoting societal equality 借用了上一段 wider societal equality in the UK 的高敏感词 societal equality，但原文是说「数字排斥威胁公平」，并未说社交媒体平台「促进」公平。
B) ✓ 正确预判的「连接感」和 They help many people feel connected with others 完美对应，且 They 指代 social media platforms / sharing platforms。
  • many other people benefit from the strong sense of community that sharing platforms can breed↔They help many people feel connected with others
C) 与原文相反They provide a necessary device for a digital detox 颠倒了原文逻辑：even the concept of a digital detox(戒瘾) requires having a device and connectivity to choose to disconnect from 说的是「做数字戒瘾本身就要先有设备和网络」，设备/网络是 digital detox 的前提，而非由社交媒体平台提供。
D) 偷换概念They create a virtual community on the internet 用 virtual community 偷换原文的 strong sense of community。原文强调的是「社群归属感」（用户主观感受），而 virtual community 指「线上社群」这一实体，重点偏离；并且原文用 breed（孕育）描述的是「感受」被催生，而非「社群」被创造。
P7

Consequently, it's worth bearing in mind that while deleting all social media accounts will undoubtedly make some feel infinitely better, many other people benefit from the strong sense of community that sharing platforms can breed.

因此，值得记住的是：虽然删除所有社交媒体账号无疑会让一些人感觉好得多，但许多其他人则从共享平台所孕育的强烈社群感中获益。

50

What does the author think is really important for those living in digital exclusion?

作者认为对于生活在数字排斥中的人们来说,什么才是真正重要的?

  • A)Having access to the internet.能够接入互联网。
  • B)Edging further towards web 3.0.进一步迈向web 3.0。
  • C)Getting more educational resources.获得更多教育资源。
  • D)Opening more social media accounts.开设更多社交媒体账户。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判「先能联网」与 Having access to the internet 完全对应，且与同段两处描述都能挂上。
  • for those living in digital exclusion to be able to switch on in the first place↔Having access to the internet
  • greater resources and initiatives are needed to provide the underprivileged with the connectivity they desperately need↔Having access to the internet
B) 偷换概念Edging further towards web 3.0 改自 Internet access will continue to grow in importance as we edge further towards web 3.0，但 edge further towards web 3.0 在原文是描述时代背景，而非「对数字排斥者真正重要的事」。
C) 以偏概全Getting more educational resources 把原文 greater resources and initiatives are needed to provide the underprivileged with the connectivity they desperately need to learn, work and live 中的 resources 缩到「教育资源」。原文 resources 指的是「用于提供网络连接的资源」，learn 只是 connectivity 的用途之一。
D) 与原文相反Opening more social media accounts 与全文论调（提醒社媒对心理健康有害、Gomez 主动远离社媒）相反；原文要那些 digital exclusion 的人 switch on 的是「网络连接」，并非「更多社媒账号」。
P8

It's crucial that people who feel that social media is having a detrimental effect on their mental health are allowed to switch off and for those living in digital exclusion to be able to switch on in the first place.

至关重要的是：让那些觉得社交媒体正在损害自己心理健康的人能够「关掉」，也要让那些深陷数字排斥的人首先能够「打开」。
