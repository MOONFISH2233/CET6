范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 177 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Nowadays parents are increasingly aware that allowing kids more freedom to explore and learn on their own helps foster their independence and boost their confidence. This shift in parenting reflects a valuable truth: children grow strongest not when every step is arranged for them, but when they are trusted to try, fail, and try again.

To begin with, freedom teaches independence in a way no lecture ever could. A child who is allowed to plan a weekend trip, cook a simple meal, or solve a quarrel with friends gradually learns to rely on his own judgment. Take my younger cousin, for example. When his mother stopped correcting his every homework mistake and let him check the answers himself, his grades slowly improved, and, more importantly, he began tackling new problems without fear. Each small success became a brick building his confidence.

In this way, granting children the room to explore freely is not a risk but a wise investment. As a result, they mature into adults who dare to think, decide, and stand on their own feet.
