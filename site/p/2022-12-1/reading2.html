原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Degradation of the world's natural resources by humans is rapidly outpacing the planet's ability to absorb the damage, a recent UN environmental study has found. The study concludes that without radical action the level of prosperity that millions of people in the developed world count on will be impossible to maintain or extend to poorer countries.

联合国最近的一项环境研究发现，人类对世界自然资源的破坏正迅速超过地球吸收这种损害的能力。该研究得出结论：如果不采取彻底的行动，发达国家数百万人所依赖的繁荣水平将无法维持，也不可能推广到较贫困的国家。

P2

Water scarcity is the curse of some of the poorest regions on Earth, leaving developing countries increasingly unable to feed themselves, and causing hardship for millions of people. There appears little prospect of this desperate situation being remedied without radical action being taken. Water resources are under increasing threat from population growth, climate change, rapid urbanization, rising levels of consumption, and the degradation of lands that previously provided a natural replenishment(补充) of water resources.

水资源短缺是地球上一些最贫困地区的祸根，使发展中国家越来越无力养活自己，并给数百万人带来困苦。如果不采取彻底的行动，这种绝望的状况似乎很难得到改善。人口增长、气候变化、快速城市化、消费水平上升，以及原本能够自然补充水资源的土地遭到退化，都使水资源面临日益严重的威胁。

P3

The rate of damage to the natural environment was found to increase globally, despite concerted efforts to persuade government to take measures to improve the condition. "If current trends continue, and the world fails to improve patterns of production and consumption, then the state of the world's environment will continue to decline," warned UN executive director Achim Steiner.

研究发现，尽管人们共同努力劝说政府采取措施改善环境状况，但自然环境遭受破坏的速度在全球范围内仍在加快。联合国执行主任阿希姆·施泰纳警告说：“如果当前趋势持续下去，而世界未能改善生产和消费模式，那么世界环境状况将继续恶化。”

P4

He said the tools for improving the environment for millions of people existed in developed countries, but were in danger of not being used.

他说，发达国家已经拥有改善数百万人民生活环境的手段，但这些手段有可能得不到使用。

P5

The study found that basic measures to tackle some of the key causes of environmental damage were still not being taken. These included measures to reduce air pollution; to control the damage to marine ecosystems, which can have a huge effect on fish stocks on which hundreds of millions of people depend; and to curb the degradation of land where modern agricultural methods were pursued without regard to the longer-term consequences.

研究发现，人们仍未采取解决环境破坏一些主要原因的基本措施。其中包括减少空气污染的措施；控制对海洋生态系统破坏的措施，因为这种破坏会对数亿人赖以生存的鱼类资源产生巨大影响；以及遏制土地退化的措施，因为现代农业方法的推行没有顾及长期后果。

P6

Despite the recent global agreement on cutting greenhouse gas emissions, global carbon output continues to rise. This will put a long-term strain on the ability of developing economies to feed their own people. Climate change is aggravated by the emissions of greenhouse gases from chemical and natural fertilisers used in agriculture which increased by more than a quarter between 2000 and 2010. Other problem areas identified in the report included glaciers, which provide vital water resources for millions of people, but which are shrinking as the climate warms.

尽管最近全球就削减温室气体排放达成了协议，但全球碳排放量仍在持续上升。这将长期削弱发展中经济体养活本国人民的能力。农业中使用的化学肥料和天然肥料所排放的温室气体使气候变化进一步加剧，而这些排放量在2000年至2010年间增加了四分之一以上。报告确定的其他问题领域还包括冰川，它们为数百万人提供至关重要的水资源，但随着气候变暖正在不断缩小。

P7

In rich countries, these problems have built up over decades and centuries while economic growth was pursued at the expense of the environment. Subsequent efforts to remedy the environment have met with partial success. But in developing countries, the path of future development has more potential to change, which has encouraged international institutions to devise more sustainable growth pathways that are supposed both to alleviate poverty and preserve the environment.

在富裕国家，这些问题是在数十年乃至数百年的经济增长过程中逐渐积累起来的，而经济增长是以牺牲环境为代价的。随后为改善环境所作的努力取得了部分成效。但在发展中国家，未来的发展道路更有可能发生改变，这促使国际机构制定更加可持续的增长路径，旨在既减轻贫困，又保护环境。

解析逐题详解

51

What is the major finding of the UN environmental study?

联合国环境研究的主要发现是什么?

  • A)Human activity has rendered Mother Earth almost uninhabitable.人类活动已使地球母亲几乎不适宜居住。
  • B)Humans are doing more damage to the earth than it can cope with.人类对地球造成的破坏超过了地球的承受能力。
  • C)Environmental problems have considerably weakened human prosperity.环境问题已大大削弱了人类的繁荣。
  • D)Environmental damage is more serious in developed countries these days.如今发达国家的环境破坏更为严重。
解析
B（主旨题）
A) 无中生有Mother Earth almost uninhabitable 说地球"几乎不宜居住"，原文只说破坏速度超过吸收能力，并未提到地球已濒临不宜居住的程度。
B) ✓ 正确预判"破坏速度超过地球承受力"在 Humans are doing more damage to the earth than it can cope with 中以 more...than it can cope with 完整复现，是定位句的标准同义替换。
  • Degradation of the world's natural resources by humans is rapidly outpacing the planet's ability to absorb the damage↔Humans are doing more damage to the earth than it can cope with
C) 偷换概念原文意思是"破坏超过吸收能力会使富国当前繁荣不可维持"——是"环境破坏会损害繁荣"的预言；Environmental problems have considerably weakened human prosperity 改成"环境问题已经显著削弱了人类繁荣"，把未来的预警偷换成已发生的事实。
D) 无中生有Environmental damage is more serious in developed countries these days 断言发达国家环境破坏更严重，原文从未做"发达国家 vs 发展中国家谁更严重"的横向比较，是无中生有的比较。
52

What is said about water scarcity in some of the poorest regions?

关于一些最贫困地区的水资源短缺问题,文中说了什么?

  • A)It is getting so serious that there is little hope of solution.问题变得如此严重以至于几乎没有解决的希望。
  • B)It largely accounts for their slow economic development.这在很大程度上是这些地区经济发展缓慢的原因。
  • C)It can hardly be relieved if no drastic measures are taken.如果不采取严厉措施,水资源短缺问题很难得到缓解。
  • D)It is primarily caused by the acceleration of climate change.水资源短缺问题主要是由气候变化加速造成的。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 过度推断原文是"不激进行动则无望"——前提是"如果不采取"，there is little hope of solution 把条件去掉、直接断言"几乎无解"，把有条件的判断推为无条件结论，过度推断。
B) 张冠李戴原文只说水资源短缺让发展中国家 increasingly unable to feed themselves, and causing hardship for millions of people，关心的是粮食与民生；It largely accounts for their slow economic development 转到"经济发展缓慢"上，原文未做此因果归因。
C) ✓ 正确预判"无激进行动→几乎无望解决"在 It can hardly be relieved if no drastic measures are taken 中以 hardly + if no...are taken 完整复刻，是定位句的标准同义重写。
  • There appears little prospect of this desperate situation being remedied without radical action being taken↔It can hardly be relieved if no drastic measures are taken
D) 以偏概全原文罗列了 population growth, climate change, rapid urbanization, rising levels of consumption, and the degradation of lands 五个并列威胁因素；It is primarily caused by the acceleration of climate change 单挑"气候加剧"为主因，原文从未做此排序。
P2

There appears little prospect of this desperate situation being remedied without radical action being taken.

如果不采取激进行动，这种绝望的状况几乎没有得到改善的前景。

53

What does Achim Steiner say about the environmental condition?

Achim Steiner对环境状况有何看法?

  • A)It will deteriorate worldwide.环境状况将在全球范围内恶化。
  • B)It is attracting global attention.环境状况正在引起全球关注。
  • C)It is being slowly remedied globally.环境状况正在全球范围内缓慢改善。
  • D)It will shrink the world's population.环境状况将导致世界人口减少。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判"环境将持续恶化"在 It will deteriorate worldwide 中以 will deteriorate 保留 will 未来时态，worldwide 保留全球范围，整句结构紧贴定位句。
  • the state of the world's environment will continue to decline↔It will deteriorate worldwide
B) 无中生有It is attracting global attention 说环境正吸引全球关注，原文 Steiner 引语只谈"将继续恶化"，未提全球对它的关注度。
C) 与原文相反It is being slowly remedied globally 说环境正在被缓慢修复，与原文 the rate of damage to the natural environment was found to increase globally 及 Steiner 引语"将继续恶化"完全相反。
D) 无中生有It will shrink the world's population 说环境状况会缩减世界人口，原文 Steiner 引语只字未提人口收缩。
P3

If current trends continue, and the world fails to improve patterns of production and consumption, then the state of the world's environment will continue to decline.

如果当前趋势继续下去，世界又无法改善生产与消费模式，那么全球环境状况将持续恶化。

54

What is the dilemma developing countries face?

发展中国家面临什么困境?

  • A)They cannot modernise farming without causing land degradation.这些国家无法在不造成土地退化的情况下实现农业现代化。
  • B)They cannot promote industrialisation without polluting waterways.这些国家无法在不污染水道的情况下推进工业化。
  • C)They cannot boost crop yields without causing greenhouse gas emissions.这些国家无法在不造成温室气体排放的情况下提高农作物产量。
  • D)They cannot catch up with rich countries without sacrificing the environment.这些国家无法在不牺牲环境的情况下赶上富裕国家。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴cannot modernise farming without causing land degradation 是"现代化农业导致土地退化"，原文第 5 段才提 curb the degradation of land where modern agricultural methods were pursued，但与本题第 6 段的"养人 vs 温室气体"两难是两个不同话题。
B) 无中生有cannot promote industrialisation without polluting waterways 说"工业化必然污染水道"，原文未谈发展中国家工业化的两难，也未谈水道污染。
C) ✓ 正确预判"养人靠提产、提产致排放"在 They cannot boost crop yields without causing greenhouse gas emissions 中以 cannot...without... 双重否定形式把目的与代价绑成两难，与原文两面对应。
  • This will put a long-term strain on the ability of developing economies to feed their own people↔boost crop yields
  • Climate change is aggravated by the emissions of greenhouse gases from chemical and natural fertilisers used in agriculture↔causing greenhouse gas emissions
D) 偷换概念cannot catch up with rich countries without sacrificing the environment 说"追赶富国必牺牲环境"，这是 P7 谈"富国曾以环境换增长"的引申、且原文 P7 的态度是发展中国家"有潜力改变路径"——选项把 P7 的论点反向化，又错放进本题两难。
P6

This will put a long-term strain on the ability of developing economies to feed their own people. Climate change is aggravated by the emissions of greenhouse gases from chemical and natural fertilisers used in agriculture which increased by more than a quarter between 2000 and 2010.

这（碳排放上升）将长期压制发展中经济体养活本国人口的能力。气候变化又被农业中化肥（化学与天然）所排放的温室气体所加剧——这类排放在 2000 到 2010 年间增长了超过四分之一。

55

What should developing countries do in their future development according to the passage?

根据文章,发展中国家在未来发展中应该怎么做?

  • A)They turn to developed countries for the assistance they need.这些国家应向发达国家寻求所需的援助。
  • B)They remedy environmental damage by slowing economic growth.这些国家应通过放缓经济增长来修复环境破坏。
  • C)They avoid damaging interference from international institutions.这些国家应避免来自国际机构的破坏性干预。
  • D)They improve people's livelihood without harming the environment.这些国家应在不损害环境的情况下改善人民生活。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 无中生有turn to developed countries for the assistance they need 说"向发达国家求援"，原文 P7 只说国际机构"设计了可持续路径"，未提向发达国家求援。
B) 与原文相反remedy environmental damage by slowing economic growth 说"靠放缓经济增长来修补环境"，但原文 P7 提的 sustainable growth pathways 是"既减贫又护环境"——发展与环保并行，不是减速换环保。
C) 与原文相反avoid damaging interference from international institutions 说"避开国际机构的破坏性干预"，但原文 P7 has encouraged international institutions to devise more sustainable growth pathways 中国际机构是建设性角色——选项把它颠倒成"破坏性"。
D) ✓ 正确预判"减贫与护环境兼顾"在 They improve people's livelihood without harming the environment 中以 improve...without harming 双动作并列承接 both...and 结构，是 P7 对策句的标准同义改写。
  • alleviate poverty↔improve people's livelihood
  • preserve the environment↔without harming the environment
P7

But in developing countries, the path of future development has more potential to change, which has encouraged international institutions to devise more sustainable growth pathways that are supposed both to alleviate poverty and preserve the environment.

但在发展中国家，未来发展的路径有更大的可改变潜力，这促使国际机构设计了更可持续的发展路径——既要减贫，又要保护环境。
