原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Many people associate their self-worth with their work. The more successful their career, the better they feel about themselves. Work-related self-esteem is therefore a worthy ideal to pursue with vigor, right? Well, not always. According to recent research, in which psychologists interviewed 370 full-time workers over a period of three weeks, the reality is a little more complicated. And it involves negative as well as positive consequences.

许多人把自我价值与工作联系在一起。事业越成功，他们对自己的感觉就越好。因此，努力追求与工作相关的自尊，应当是一个值得追求的理想，对吧？嗯，也不总是如此。根据近期的一项研究，心理学家在三周内采访了370名全职工作人员，现实情况要复杂一些，而且其中既有消极后果，也有积极后果。

P2

It's natural to be drawn towards pleasure and to step away from pain. In the workplace, if that pleasure comes from a triumph which swells our self-respect, people will try to repeat the accomplishment. But repeating that accomplishment is often not realistic, which can lead to severe negative emotional consequences when it doesn't reoccur. This form of motivation is widely regarded as a negative type of motivation. It can hinder other more positive motivation types, such as completing a task purely because it's fulfilling or enjoyable.

人们自然会被快乐吸引，并会避开痛苦。在工作场所，如果这种快乐来自一次令我们的自尊膨胀的胜利，人们就会试图重复这一成就。但重复这一成就往往并不现实，而当它未能再次出现时，便可能导致严重的负面情绪后果。这种形式的动机被普遍视为一种消极动机。它可能妨碍其他更积极的动机类型，例如仅仅因为完成一项任务会带来满足感或乐趣而去完成它。

P3

What consumes the employee instead is a pressing need to feel mighty and sure of themselves. They then take on only tasks and objectives which serve that ego-driven need. As a result, to avoid feelings of shame and worthlessness associated with failure, they extend themselves to such a degree that there's a subsequent adverse effect on their well-being. This internal pressure to succeed at all costs demands a lot of effort. It depletes their energy, culminating in disproportionate levels of damaging sentiment.

真正消耗员工的，是一种迫切想要感到强大并对自己充满信心的需要。于是，他们只承担那些能够满足这种受自我意识驱动的需要的任务和目标。结果，为了避免失败所带来的羞耻感和无价值感，他们过度透支自己，以至于随后对自身福祉产生不利影响。这种不惜一切代价取得成功的内在压力要求付出大量努力。它耗尽了他们的精力，最终导致有害情绪达到不成比例的程度。

P4

Those negative emotions mount into heightened anxiety, impacting their ability to make the most of their personal life. Their desire to avoid feeling inferior ends up making them feel inferior when it comes to their diminished capacity for friendship and leisure. They end up dissatisfied both at work and outside of it.

这些负面情绪不断累积，演变为更强烈的焦虑，影响他们充分利用个人生活的能力。他们想要避免感到低人一等，结果却因为自己交友和休闲能力的减弱而感到低人一等。最终，他们在工作内外都感到不满。

P5

But thankfully, for those people compelled almost entirely by this specific form of motivation, the news isn't all bad, or bad at all. The study also discovered several positive outcomes that can actually outweigh the harmful ones. Though these types of employees are motivated by the desire to avoid negative consequences, they are also motivated by the excitement of pursuing emotional rewards. This excitement makes pursuing goals enjoyable and stimulates pleasure and pride that would result from success. An effect of the positive motivation is that it neutralizes the existence of negative motivation.

但值得庆幸的是，对于那些几乎完全受这种特定形式的动机驱使的人来说，情况并非全然糟糕，甚至根本算不上糟糕。研究还发现了几项积极结果，而这些结果实际上可以超过有害结果。尽管这类员工受到避免负面后果的愿望驱动，但他们同样受到追求情感回报所带来的兴奋感驱动。这种兴奋感使追求目标变得愉快，并激发成功后将产生的快乐和自豪感。积极动机所产生的一个影响是，它抵消了消极动机的存在。

P6

Sure, it affects people's personal lives to what could be deemed an unhealthy extent, because leisure activities are often seen as a part of life that must be sacrificed to manage work and family demands. However, the way people feel about their work has less to do with whether they're motivated by the preservation of self-esteem but more with the fact that they're simply motivated.

当然，这确实在某种可能被视为不健康的程度上影响了人们的个人生活，因为为了应对工作和家庭的要求，休闲活动常常被视为生活中必须牺牲的一部分。然而，人们对工作的感受，与其说更多取决于他们是否受到维护自尊的驱动，不如说更多取决于他们确实受到了激励。

解析逐题详解

46

What does the author say about the pursuit of work-related self-esteem?

作者对追求与工作相关的自尊有何看法?

  • A)It may result in negative motivation.这可能导致负面动机。
  • B)It contributes to one's accomplishments.这有助于个人成就。
  • C)It can increase one's vigor as one keeps trying.随着不断尝试,这能增强个人活力。
  • D)It costs too much emotionally and psychologically.这在情感和心理上代价太高。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判的负面后果在 negative motivation 中被精确收口为研究真正定义的负面后果类型——动机层面的负面，故锁定 A。
  • involves negative as well as positive consequences↔It may result in negative motivation
B) 与原文相反It contributes to one's accomplishments 把 a worthy ideal to pursue with vigor 当成作者真实观点，但作者立刻用 not always 否定了这种乐观说法。
C) 偷换概念It can increase one's vigor as one keeps trying 把原文中描述追求方式的 with vigor 改造成追求会增加 vigor，混淆 vigor 作方式状语与作宾语的角色。
D) 过度推断It costs too much emotionally and psychologically 给出 too much 的程度判断，但首段仅说 involves negative as well as positive consequences，并未将代价定性为过高。
P1

Work-related self-esteem is therefore a worthy ideal to pursue with vigor, right? Well, not always. According to recent research, in which psychologists interviewed 370 full-time workers over a period of three weeks, the reality is a little more complicated. And it involves negative as well as positive consequences.

于是工作型自尊就是一个值得全力追求的理想，对吗？嗯，并非总是如此。根据一项让心理学家在三周内访谈 370 名全职员工的近期研究，现实要更复杂一些。它既牵涉正面后果，也牵涉负面后果。

47

What do employees tend to do in pursuing work-related self-esteem?

员工在追求与工作相关的自尊时往往会做什么?

  • A)Take on tasks well beyond their actual capabilities.承担远超自身实际能力的任务。
  • B)Strive to succeed at the expense of their well-being.以牺牲自身幸福为代价努力获得成功。
  • C)Resort to all means regardless of the consequences.不顾后果诉诸一切手段。
  • D)Exaggerate their sense of shame and worthlessness.夸大自己的羞耻感和无价值感。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 过度推断Take on tasks well beyond their actual capabilities 把 to such a degree that there's a subsequent adverse effect on their well-being 改写为超出实际能力，但原文只说付出过度，没说任务超出能力。
B) ✓ 正确预判中的拼命与损害自身两成分被 Strive to succeed 和 at the expense of their well-being 一一收拢，故锁定 B。
  • they extend themselves to such a degree that there's a subsequent adverse effect on their well-being↔Strive to succeed at the expense of their well-being
C) 与原文相反Resort to all means regardless of the consequences 说他们不顾后果，但原文 to avoid feelings of shame and worthlessness associated with failure 恰恰说他们极其在意后果。
D) 与原文相反Exaggerate their sense of shame and worthlessness 把 to avoid feelings of shame and worthlessness 改成夸大羞耻感，正话反说。
P3

As a result, to avoid feelings of shame and worthlessness associated with failure, they extend themselves to such a degree that there's a subsequent adverse effect on their well-being.

结果，为了躲开与失败相连的羞耻感和无价值感，他们把自己拉伸到这种程度，以至于随之而来的是对其身心健康的不利影响。

48

What do we learn about people over-concerned with work-related self-esteem?

关于过度关注与工作相关的自尊的人,我们了解到什么?

  • A)They may often feel inferior to their colleagues.他们可能经常觉得不如同事。
  • B)They cannot enjoy their personal life to the full.他们无法充分享受个人生活。
  • C)They are never satisfied with their achievements.他们对自己的成就从不满足。
  • D)They have their own view of friendship and leisure.他们对友谊和休闲有自己的看法。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念They may often feel inferior to their colleagues 把原文 feel inferior 的对照对象改成 colleagues，但原文比较对象是 diminished capacity for friendship and leisure，与同事无关。
B) ✓ 正确预判中的个人生活质量被削弱被 cannot enjoy their personal life to the full 完整收拢，故锁定 B。
  • impacting their ability to make the most of their personal life↔They cannot enjoy their personal life to the full
C) 过度推断They are never satisfied with their achievements 用 never 加 with their achievements 描述成就维度的不满，但原文说的是 dissatisfied both at work and outside of it，且未升级到 never 的极端否定。
D) 无中生有They have their own view of friendship and leisure 借 friendship and leisure 词面做诱饵，但原文只说他们对友谊与闲暇的能力削弱，从未说他们持独到看法。
P4

Those negative emotions mount into heightened anxiety, impacting their ability to make the most of their personal life. Their desire to avoid feeling inferior ends up making them feel inferior when it comes to their diminished capacity for friendship and leisure.

那些负面情绪累积成加剧的焦虑，影响他们充分享受个人生活的能力。他们想要避免自卑的渴望最终反而让他们在自己对友谊与闲暇的削弱能力面前感到自卑。

49

What is the good news we learn from the recent research?

我们从最近的研究中了解到的好消息是什么?

  • A)The pursuit of goals may turn out to be enjoyable and pleasant.追求目标可能会变得令人愉快和快乐。
  • B)The emotional rewards from goal pursuit are worth the pains taken.追求目标带来的情感回报值得付出的辛苦。
  • C)The negative consequences of goal pursuit can mostly be avoided.追求目标的负面后果大多可以避免。
  • D)The goal of swelling self-esteem can be achieved if one keeps trying.如果不断尝试,提升自尊的目标可以实现。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判中的追求带来享受被 enjoyable and pleasant 完整收拢，故锁定 A。
  • This excitement makes pursuing goals enjoyable and stimulates pleasure and pride↔The pursuit of goals may turn out to be enjoyable and pleasant
B) 无中生有The emotional rewards from goal pursuit are worth the pains taken 引入代价对价值的权衡关系，但原文 An effect of the positive motivation is that it neutralizes the existence of negative motivation 只说正面动机中和负面动机，未做代价值得的判断。
C) 过度推断The negative consequences of goal pursuit can mostly be avoided 把 neutralizes the existence of negative motivation 升级为多数可避免，但 neutralize 是抵消、不是避免，且原文未说大多数。
D) 与原文相反The goal of swelling self-esteem can be achieved if one keeps trying 与 repeating that accomplishment is often not realistic 直接冲突；原文明说重复成就并不现实。
P5

Though these types of employees are motivated by the desire to avoid negative consequences, they are also motivated by the excitement of pursuing emotional rewards. This excitement makes pursuing goals enjoyable and stimulates pleasure and pride that would result from success.

尽管这些员工受到躲避负面后果的渴望驱使，他们同时也受到追求情感奖赏所带来的兴奋的驱使。这种兴奋让追求目标变得令人享受，并激发出本会因成功而产生的愉悦与自豪。

50

What can we infer from the last paragraph?

我们可以从最后一段推断出什么?

  • A)Workers have to make sacrifices to preserve self-esteem.员工必须做出牺牲以维护自尊。
  • B)Self-esteem swells when workers are strongly motivated.当员工受到强烈激励时,自尊会提升。
  • C)Pursuit of goals affects people's personal lives to an unhealthy extent.追求目标在不健康的程度上影响了人们的个人生活。
  • D)People feel positive about their work as long as they are motivated.只要人们受到激励,他们就会对自己的工作感到积极。
解析
D（推断题）
A) 偷换概念Workers have to make sacrifices to preserve self-esteem 把 leisure activities are often seen as a part of life that must be sacrificed to manage work and family demands 中牺牲闲暇的目的改成 preserve self-esteem，目的错位。
B) 与原文相反Self-esteem swells when workers are strongly motivated 把因果说成动机强则自尊膨胀，但原文 has less to do with whether they're motivated by the preservation of self-esteem 反而弱化了自尊在因果链里的地位。
C) 以偏概全Pursuit of goals affects people's personal lives to an unhealthy extent 把 Sure, it affects people's personal lives to what could be deemed an unhealthy extent 这一让步分句当作尾段主结论，但 However 之后才是作者真正立场。
D) ✓ 正确预判中的有动机即正向感受被 as long as they are motivated 完整收拢，且 positive 与文章正向 good news 区段的态度一致，故锁定 D。
  • the way people feel about their work has less to do with whether they're motivated by the preservation of self-esteem but more with the fact that they're simply motivated↔People feel positive about their work as long as they are motivated
P6

However, the way people feel about their work has less to do with whether they're motivated by the preservation of self-esteem but more with the fact that they're simply motivated.

然而，人们对工作的感受真正取决于的，与其说是他们是否被维护自尊的渴望所驱动，不如说是他们到底有没有被驱动这件事本身。
