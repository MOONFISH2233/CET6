范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 177 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

People are now increasingly aware of the ‘digital gap’ or challenges the elderly face in a digital world. As technology reshapes daily life at a dizzying speed, many senior citizens find themselves left behind, struggling to keep pace with tools that younger generations take for granted.

This gap manifests itself in countless everyday scenes. For instance, an elderly man may stand helplessly before a restaurant that only accepts mobile payment, unable to scan the QR code. Similarly, when hospitals shift appointment booking online, or when a health code is required to enter public places, those unfamiliar with smartphones are effectively shut out. As a result, tasks that were once simple now become sources of anxiety and isolation.

In my view, no one should be excluded by the very technology meant to serve them. Therefore, families ought to patiently teach their parents digital skills, while service providers should retain user-friendly alternatives such as cash counters and manual registration. Only through such joint efforts can we bridge this gap and build a society that is inclusive of every generation.
