原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Why are we so worried about our careers? Partly it's to do with money, but there's a psychological aspect to our fears as well.

我们为什么如此担心自己的职业生涯？一部分原因与金钱有关，但我们的恐惧也有心理层面的因素。

P2

We worry because we suspect—not wrongly—that the world is full of a frightening sort of person ready to judge us ruthlessly and swiftly: a person we can call a snob.

我们之所以担心，是因为我们怀疑——而且这种怀疑并没有错——这个世界上充斥着一类令人恐惧的人，他们随时准备无情而迅速地评判我们：我们可以把这种人称为势利小人。

P3

A snob is anyone who takes a relatively small part of us and uses it to come to a rigid conclusion about how much of their attention we deserve. In the past, that might be your ancestry and royal connections. Nowadays, the snob cares about one thing only: what you do for a living.

势利小人是这样一种人：他们只取我们身上相对较小的一部分，便据此武断地判断我们值得得到他们多少关注。过去，这一部分可能是你的家世和与王室的关系。如今，势利小人只在意一件事：你以什么为生。

P4

This explains why the first question we will be asked in any new social context is 'What do you do?' and according to how we answer, snobs will either welcome us with broad smiles, or leave us in the cold.

这就解释了为什么在任何新的社交场合中，我们被问到的第一个问题都是“你是做什么的？”，而势利小人会根据我们的回答，要么满面笑容地欢迎我们，要么对我们冷眼相待。

P5

And that is why we are fired up by such a desperate urge to achieve and impress.

这也正是我们如此强烈地渴望取得成就并给人留下深刻印象的原因。

P6

Sometimes our behaviour is mistaken for greed and vanity, but it is more than this. A lot of our interest in fancy cars, jobs and houses has nothing to do with materialism. It has to do with a hunger for the respect and esteem that is only available in our societies through the acquisition of material goods. It isn't the goods themselves we seek, it is the love we stand to gain through our possession of them. The next time we see someone driving a Ferrari, we shouldn't condemn them for their greed, we should pity them for the intensity of their need for love from the world.

有时，我们的行为会被误认为是贪婪和虚荣，但事情并不止于此。我们对豪车、工作和房屋的浓厚兴趣，有很大一部分与物质主义毫无关系。它源于一种对尊重和敬重的渴望，而在我们的社会中，只有通过获得物质财富才能得到这些东西。我们追求的并不是物品本身，而是我们有望通过拥有它们获得的爱。下次我们看到有人驾驶法拉利时，不应因其贪婪而谴责他，而应同情他，因为他如此强烈地需要得到世人的爱。

P7

At the root of snobbery is a lack of imagination and confidence about how to decide who in the world is valuable. The snobs are brutally misguided and slavish in their beliefs about how the superior individuals can be identified. For snobs, it is the already acclaimed and already successful who are the only ones worthy of respect. There is no room in their timid regimented minds to imagine that someone might be clever, kind or good—and yet somehow have been overlooked entirely by society, their qualities lying hidden beneath an unfamiliar veil, and having as yet discovered no obvious outlet.

势利的根源在于，人们缺乏想象力和信心，不知道该如何判断谁在这个世界上有价值。势利小人关于如何辨认优秀之人的观念极其错误，而且盲目而奴性十足。对势利小人而言，只有那些已经声名显赫、已经取得成功的人才值得尊重。在他们胆怯而刻板的头脑中，没有空间去想象这样一种可能：某个人也许聪明、善良或优秀，却不知为何完全被社会忽视；他们的品质隐藏在一层陌生的面纱之下，至今尚未找到显而易见的施展途径。

P8

The true answer to snobbery is not to say that there is no such thing as a better or worse person, but to insist that better or worse exist in constantly unexpected places and carry none of the outward signs of distinction. And because we are such poor judges of the worth of others, our ultimate duty remains to be kind, good, curious and imaginative about pretty much everyone who ever crosses our path.

应对势利的真正答案，不是说世上不存在更好或更差的人，而是坚持认为，优秀与否总会出现在不断出人意料的地方，并且不带有任何外在的卓越标志。而且，由于我们并不善于判断他人的价值，我们最终的责任仍然是：对几乎所有曾与我们相遇的人，都保持善良、正直、好奇和富有想象力。

解析逐题详解

46

What gives rise to our worry about careers apart from money?

除了金钱之外，是什么引发了我们对职业的担忧?

  • A)Fear of being judged in a snobbish manner.害怕被以势利的方式评判。
  • B)The prospect of facing fault-finding managers.面对吹毛求疵的管理者的前景。
  • C)The ruthless way employees are often treated.员工经常受到无情对待的方式。
  • D)Fright at the difficulty in hunting for a job.对求职困难的恐惧。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判要的是「金钱之外的心理因 = 怕被评判」，A 项 Fear of being judged in a snobbish manner 把「心理因—怕—被评判—被势利者」四要素全部覆盖
  • a psychological aspect to our fears↔Fear of being judged in a snobbish manner
  • ready to judge us ruthlessly and swiftly: a person we can call a snob↔in a snobbish manner
B) 无中生有原文谈的是 a person we can call a snob，并未把恐惧对象限定为 fault-finding managers——经理只是势利者的一种可能，但原文没说
C) 偷换概念原文是「我们怕被人评判」（we are judged），主体是 we；选项 The ruthless way employees are often treated 主语变成 employees，且 treated 把视角换成了「被对待」，与原文 judge us ruthlessly 的「评判」含义不一致
D) 答非所问Fright at the difficulty in hunting for a job 谈的是「找工作难」，本质仍属经济或金钱因；而题干用 apart from money 明确把金钱因排除在外
P1

Partly it's to do with money, but there's a psychological aspect to our fears as well.

部分原因与金钱有关，但我们的恐惧还有一个心理层面。

47

What do we learn from the passage about today's snobs?

从文章中我们对当今的势利之人了解到什么?

  • A)They try hard to dig into a person's past.他们努力挖掘一个人的过去。
  • B)They draw a rigid conclusion about people.他们对人们得出僵化的结论。
  • C)They judge a person by their occupation.他们通过职业来评判一个人。
  • D)They tend to place people in a social context.他们倾向于将人置于社会语境中。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反原文说今天势利者只看职业；至于 dig into a person's past（挖某人的过去），对应的是 In the past, that might be your ancestry and royal connections——但那是说「过去势利者看祖宗」，不是「今天势利者挖过去」，正话反说
B) 答非所问A snob is anyone who takes a relatively small part of us and uses it to come to a rigid conclusion 是势利者的通用定义，不专属于今天；题问的是 today's snobs 与过去相比的独有特征，应取 cares about... what you do for a living
C) ✓ 正确预判核心是「只看职业」，C 项 They judge a person by their occupation. 把「只看」显性化为「以...为判断依据」，吻合
  • what you do for a living↔judge a person by their occupation
  • the snob cares about one thing only↔judge a person by
D) 偷换概念in any new social context is 'What do you do?' 是描写「在新社交场合问问题」的情境，不是「势利者把人放在某种社交语境里评判」；选项 tend to place people in a social context 把这一场景偷换成势利者的主动行为
P3

Nowadays, the snob cares about one thing only: what you do for a living.

如今，势利者只在乎一件事：你以何为生。

48

What does the author say about people's interest in material goods?

作者关于人们对物质商品的兴趣说了什么?

  • A)It is the cause for condemnation of their greed.这是谴责他们贪婪的原因。
  • B)It has a lot to do with the comforts they provide.这与物质商品提供的舒适度有很大关系。
  • C)It arouses pity rather than respect from the wealthy.这引起富人的怜悯而非尊重。
  • D)It arises from their craving for social recognition.这源于他们对社会认可的渴望。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反we shouldn't condemn them for their greed 明确反对「把对物质的兴趣等同于贪婪并加以谴责」；选项 It is the cause for condemnation of their greed 把「对物质兴趣」说成「谴责其贪婪的原因」，与作者立场相反
B) 无中生有the comforts they provide 指物质带来的「舒适」，但 P6 谈的是「通过物质获得尊重」，不是「物质提供舒适」——comfort 维度是选项凭空添加的
C) 张冠李戴原文 we should pity them for the intensity of their need for love 说「我们应怜悯豪车车主对爱的渴求」——施动者是「我们」，对象是「豪车车主」；选项 It arouses pity rather than respect from the wealthy 却说「（兴趣）引起富人的怜悯而非尊重」，施动者变成了 the wealthy、且把 respect 与 pity 错误对立
D) ✓ 正确预判的核心三要素：第一是「渴求」（hunger 对应 craving）、第二是对象是「社会层面的尊重」（respect and esteem 对应 social recognition）、第三是驱动了对物质的兴趣（arises from）。D 项三要素全中
  • a hunger for the respect and esteem↔their craving for social recognition
  • only available in our societies through the acquisition of material goods↔arises from
P6

It has to do with a hunger for the respect and esteem that is only available in our societies through the acquisition of material goods.

（人们对豪车好工作好房子的兴趣）与对尊重和敬意的渴求有关——而在我们的社会里，这种尊重只能通过占有物质来获得。

49

What kind of people do snobs deem worth respect and esteem?

势利之人认为什么样的人值得尊重和敬重?

  • A)Those with fame and fortune.那些拥有名望和财富的人。
  • B)Those with regimented minds.那些思想受到严格管束的人。
  • C)Those with intelligence and imagination.那些拥有智慧和想象力的人。
  • D)Those with qualities lying hidden in disguise.那些品质隐藏在伪装之下的人。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判核心是「已获得外在声名地位的人」，A 项 Those with fame and fortune 用名词 fame and fortune 浓缩了 acclaimed 与 successful 两个分词
  • the already acclaimed and already successful↔Those with fame and fortune
B) 张冠李戴There is no room in their timid regimented minds 中的 regimented minds 是描写势利者 their 的心智状态（胆怯、刻板），不是「被尊重的对象」；选项把势利者的特征张冠到「被尊重者」头上
C) 与原文相反At the root of snobbery is a lack of imagination and confidence 说势利者缺乏想象力；someone might be clever, kind or good—and yet somehow have been overlooked entirely by society 说「聪明、善良的人反而被社会忽略」——这部分人恰恰是势利者「看不到」的，而不是势利者尊敬的对象
D) 与原文相反their qualities lying hidden beneath an unfamiliar veil 中的「品质藏于陌生面纱之下」，主体仍是被社会忽略的、品质未显的人，正是势利者「看不见」而非「尊敬」的对象
P7

For snobs, it is the already acclaimed and already successful who are the only ones worthy of respect.

在势利者眼中，唯有那些已经获得赞誉、已经取得成功的人，才是值得尊重的对象。

50

What does the author imply we should do to avoid being snobbish?

作者暗示我们应该做什么来避免势利?

  • A)Be aware there has never been such a thing as a better or worse person.意识到从来就不存在更好或更差的人这回事。
  • B)Be kind to and curious about those who we happen to meet in our lives.对我们生活中碰巧遇到的人保持善意和好奇。
  • C)Realize that better or worse keeps changing in unexpected ways.认识到更好或更差以意想不到的方式不断变化。
  • D)Judge people on the basis of their distinctive character traits.根据人们独特的性格特征来评判他们。
解析
B（推断题）
A) 与原文相反原文 The true answer to snobbery is not to say that there is no such thing as a better or worse person 明确反对「无所谓好人坏人」这种说法；选项 there has never been such a thing as a better or worse person 却正是被作者明确否定的命题
B) ✓ 正确预判三要素：第一是态度（善良、好奇）、第二是对象（每一个相遇者）、第三是语气（建议、应该）。B 项以祈使句 Be kind to and curious about those who we happen to meet 三要素全中
  • to be kind, good, curious and imaginative about pretty much everyone who ever crosses our path↔Be kind to and curious about those who we happen to meet in our lives
  • our ultimate duty remains↔Be kind to...
C) 偷换概念better or worse exist in constantly unexpected places 说的是「好坏的人出现在意想不到的地方」——位置出乎意料；选项 better or worse keeps changing in unexpected ways 把它偷换成「好坏不断以意想不到的方式变化」——把空间分布偷换成动态变化
D) 与原文相反carry none of the outward signs of distinction 直白说「不带任何显赫的外在标志」；选项 Judge people on the basis of their distinctive character traits 却要求依据「显著的性格特征」判断人，与原文明确否定的方向一致
P8

And because we are such poor judges of the worth of others, our ultimate duty remains to be kind, good, curious and imaginative about pretty much everyone who ever crosses our path.

正因为我们其实并不善于判断他人的价值，所以我们最根本的义务始终是——对几乎每一个与我们相遇的人保持善良、温厚、好奇与想象力。
