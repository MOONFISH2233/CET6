原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Women have historically been paid less. But in the US in the 1980s, they began to catch up—fast. During that decade, the gender pay gap closed by about one percentage point a year. Had that trend continued, the gender wage gap would have been closed by 2017.

从历史上看，女性的薪酬一直低于男性。但在20世纪80年代的美国，她们开始迅速追赶。在那十年间，性别薪酬差距每年缩小约一个百分点。假如这一趋势延续下去，性别工资差距本应在2017年消除。

P2

But the trend didn't continue, and the gap remains yawning.

但这一趋势并未延续，差距依然十分悬殊。

P3

According to a new study from academics at Harvard, the stagnation can be put down, perhaps counterintuitively, to the introduction of state and federal family leave policies.

哈佛大学学者的一项新研究表明，造成这种停滞的原因，或许出人意料地在于州政府和联邦政府家庭休假政策的出台。

P4

The academics argue that during the 1990s, as governments began to introduce leave policies, it was mainly women who took advantage of them. Though the leave policies might have helped those women to stay in the workplace—instead of dropping out to have families—those who returned saw their wages had increased at lower rates than the men.

这些学者认为，在20世纪90年代，随着各国政府开始推行休假政策，主要是女性利用了这些政策。尽管休假政策可能帮助这些女性留在职场，而不是为了组建家庭而退出职场，但那些重返职场的女性发现，她们的工资增长速度低于男性。

P5

After family leave was introduced in the US, in fact, the rate of gender wage convergence fell to just 0.03 percentage points per year, and has remained there ever since.

事实上，在美国实行家庭休假政策后，男女工资趋同的速度降至每年仅0.03个百分点，此后一直保持在这一水平。

P6

Those monitoring the process towards salary equity at work have long watched as progress slowed in many countries around the world. In fact, that progress began to reverse during the pandemic(大流行病).

长期以来，关注职场薪酬平等进程的人们一直看到，世界上许多国家的进展正在放缓。事实上，这一进程在大流行病期间开始倒退。

P7

The gender pay gap is one of the most outstanding examples of that lack of parity(平等), and still exists just about everywhere. The motherhood penalty has become a shorthand for describing why: In many places, especially rich countries, women earn the same as men until they reach their childbearing years. Women who have children begin to see their salaries slip behind their male counterparts.

性别薪酬差距是这种不平等最突出的例子之一，而且几乎在世界各地都依然存在。“母职惩罚”已成为解释这一现象原因的简略说法：在许多地方，尤其是富裕国家，女性在进入生育年龄之前，收入与男性相同。有了孩子的女性，其薪资便开始落后于男性同事。

P8

Part of this is because women take on more of the unpaid labor at home, which can eat into time available for work and energy for career advancement. But it's also because mothers are passed over for raises and promotion, and because time out of the workplace sets women back, even if that time is taken voluntarily, and supported by company or government policy.

部分原因在于，女性在家中承担了更多无偿劳动，这可能会挤占她们用于工作的时间和职业发展的精力。但这也是因为母亲们在加薪和晋升方面被忽视了，还因为离开职场一段时间会使女性处于不利地位，即使这段时间是她们自愿离开，并且得到了公司或政府政策的支持。

P9

What would have happened if leave policies hadn't been introduced? The study doesn't go into that question, other than to say that if the 1980s trend continued, we would have been at parity by now.

如果当初没有出台休假政策，情况会怎样？除了指出如果20世纪80年代的趋势延续下去，我们现在本应已经实现平等之外，这项研究没有探讨这个问题。

P10

It's possible, however, that the journey towards wage parity would have stalled either way. If women's gains in the 1980s were made through the erasure of things like bias, once those less uncontrollable problems had been addressed, there would still have been an issue with women—who are the ones to bear children and take care of them in the early weeks because of biological factors like the ability to breastfeed, forcing them to take breaks, whether or not those breaks were mandated.

然而，通往工资平等的道路也有可能无论如何都会停滞不前。如果女性在20世纪80年代取得的进步是通过消除偏见等因素实现的，那么，一旦那些较不难解决的问题得到处理，女性仍然会面临一个问题：由于能够哺乳等生理因素，女性才是生育孩子并在孩子出生后的最初几周照料孩子的人，这迫使她们不得不休假，无论这些休假是否由政策规定。

解析逐题详解

51

What do we learn about the gender pay gap in the US during the 1980s?

关于20世纪80年代美国的性别薪酬差距,我们了解到什么?

  • A)It was being slowly closed.它正在缓慢缩小。
  • B)It was shrinking rapidly.它正在迅速缩小。
  • C)It started to yawn.它开始扩大。
  • D)It remained substantial.它仍然很大。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反It was being slowly closed 把原文的 fast 偷换成 slowly，方向完全相反。
B) ✓ 正确预判强调"差距在 1980 年代快速缩小"，It was shrinking rapidly 与之完全等值，且未引入任何额外含义，直接锁定 B。
  • they began to catch up—fast↔shrinking rapidly
  • closed by about one percentage point a year↔rapidly
C) 张冠李戴It started to yawn 借用 the gap remains yawning 制造关联，但 yawning 在原文描述的是 P2 之后趋势停滞后差距依旧巨大的状态，不是 1980 年代发生的事，属于张冠李戴。
D) 答非所问It remained substantial 描述的是 Women have historically been paid less 这种历史背景下的总体状态，并不针对题干所问的 1980 年代特征。
P1

But in the US in the 1980s, they began to catch up—fast. During that decade, the gender pay gap closed by about one percentage point a year.

但在美国 1980 年代，女性的薪酬开始追赶——而且速度很快。在那十年里，性别薪酬差距以每年约一个百分点的速度在缩小。

52

What happened with the introduction of state and federal family leave policies?

州和联邦家庭休假政策出台后发生了什么?

  • A)The process towards salary equity at work began to reverse.工作中薪酬平等的进程开始逆转。
  • B)The rate of gender wage convergence started to fall noticeably.性别工资趋同的速度开始明显下降。
  • C)The trend of women returning to work after childbirth started.女性产后重返工作的趋势开始出现。
  • D)The narrowing of the gender pay gap attracted more attention.性别薪酬差距的缩小引起了更多关注。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴The process towards salary equity at work began to reverse 借用 that progress began to reverse during the pandemic，但原文的"反转"发生在大流行期间，不是家庭休假政策引入之后。
B) ✓ 正确选项几乎逐词保留了 the rate of gender wage convergence...fell 的主干，只把后面的具体数字抽象化，是最高度等值的同义替换，直接锁定 B。
  • the rate of gender wage convergence fell to just 0.03 percentage points per year↔The rate of gender wage convergence started to fall noticeably
C) 无中生有The trend of women returning to work after childbirth started 借用 helped those women to stay in the workplace—instead of dropping out to have families，但原文只说政策让一些女性留下来，没有说"产后返岗成为新趋势"，且这不是题干"introduction后发生了什么"的核心结果。
D) 无中生有The narrowing of the gender pay gap attracted more attention 把"差距缩小"和"引起更多关注"拼接在一起，但原文从未提及关注度变化，是凭空捏造。
P5

After family leave was introduced in the US, in fact, the rate of gender wage convergence fell to just 0.03 percentage points per year, and has remained there ever since.

事实上，美国引入家庭休假政策之后，性别工资收敛率降至每年仅 0.03 个百分点，并自此一直停留在这个水平。

53

What partly accounts for the slip in women's salaries?

女性薪酬下滑的部分原因是什么?

  • A)The insufficient motivation women generally have for career advancement.女性在职业晋升方面普遍缺乏足够的动力。
  • B)The opportunities numerous women give up for pay raise and promotion.众多女性为加薪和晋升而放弃的机会。
  • C)The huge amounts of time and energy women spend taking care of the family.女性在照顾家庭上花费的大量时间和精力。
  • D)The lack of policy support from government and business corporations.政府和商业公司缺乏政策支持。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 无中生有The insufficient motivation women generally have for career advancement 把原因归结为女性自身"职业发展动力不足"，原文从未做出此种心理动机层面的归因。
B) 与原文相反The opportunities numerous women give up for pay raise and promotion 暗示女性"主动放弃"涨薪机会，但原文 mothers are passed over for raises and promotion 是被动语态——她们是"被跳过"，不是"主动放弃"。
C) ✓ 正确C 同时包含"时间和精力"与"照顾家庭"两个核心要素，对应定位句的两段含义，是定位句的高度同义改写，直接锁定 C。
  • women take on more of the unpaid labor at home↔taking care of the family
  • eat into time available for work and energy for career advancement↔The huge amounts of time and energy
D) 与原文相反The lack of policy support from government and business corporations 把原因归为"缺乏政策支持"，但原文恰恰相反：政策（family leave）已经引入并提供支持，问题反而出在政策支持的副作用上。
P8

Part of this is because women take on more of the unpaid labor at home, which can eat into time available for work and energy for career advancement.

部分原因在于女性在家承担了更多无偿劳动，这会侵蚀她们可用于工作的时间，以及用于职业发展的精力。

54

What does the new study say about wage parity?

新研究对薪酬平等是怎么说的?

  • A)It would have stalled if those controllable problems had not been addressed.如果这些可控问题没有得到解决,它就会停滞不前。
  • B)It would have halted if company and government had not worked together.如果企业和政府没有共同努力,它就会停滞不前。
  • C)It would have been achieved with the complete erasure of gender biases.随着性别偏见的完全消除,它本可以实现。
  • D)It would have been attained with the continuation of the 1980s trend.随着20世纪80年代趋势的延续,它本可以实现。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反It would have stalled if those controllable problems had not been addressed 借用 once those less uncontrollable problems had been addressed 制造对应，但原文是"问题已被解决之后还会有别的问题"，选项却说"如果没解决就会停滞"，方向颠倒。
B) 无中生有It would have halted if company and government had not worked together 把"公司和政府合作"作为条件，但原文从未提及二者协同与否，只是说休假可由 company or government policy 支持。
C) 过度推断It would have been achieved with the complete erasure of gender biases 借用 through the erasure of things like bias，但原文用了 things like bias 这种举例口吻，且只是 if 假设下的推测，并未说"完全消除偏见就能实现"。
D) ✓ 正确D 把虚拟条件 if the 1980s trend continued 和结果 would have been at parity 一一对应，无缺漏无外加，是定位句最严格的同义改写。
  • if the 1980s trend continued↔with the continuation of the 1980s trend
  • we would have been at parity by now↔It would have been attained
P9

The study doesn't go into that question, other than to say that if the 1980s trend continued, we would have been at parity by now.

这项研究没有深入探讨那个问题，只是说如果 1980 年代的趋势延续下去，我们现在本应已经实现了薪酬平等。

55

What prevents women from achieving parity with men in the final analysis?

归根结底,是什么阻止女性实现与男性的平等?

  • A)Ignoring biases against women in the workplace.忽视工作场所对女性的偏见。
  • B)Giving birth to children and taking care of them.生育和抚养孩子。
  • C)Failing to mandate breaks for childbirth and care.未能强制规定生育和照料的休假。
  • D)Lacking resources to address biological problems.缺乏解决生理问题的资源。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反Ignoring biases against women in the workplace 把原因归为"忽视偏见"，但原文 through the erasure of things like bias 恰是说偏见已被消除，且作者强调即便消除之后仍有问题——并非"忽视"导致。
B) ✓ 正确B 把末段长句中的两个核心动作（生育 + 照护）抽出并列，正好对应预判中的"生育和照顾孩子"两层根因，是最纯粹的同义改写，直接锁定 B。
  • who are the ones to bear children and take care of them in the early weeks↔Giving birth to children and taking care of them
C) 与原文相反Failing to mandate breaks for childbirth and care 把"不强制规定休假"作为最终原因，但原文 whether or not those breaks were mandated 明确指出强制与否都没用，最终原因不是强制不强制。
D) 无中生有Lacking resources to address biological problems 借用 biological factors like the ability to breastfeed，但原文从未说"缺乏资源去解决生物学问题"，且作者立场是生物学因素本身就难以绕开，不是资源问题。
P10

If women's gains in the 1980s were made through the erasure of things like bias, once those less uncontrollable problems had been addressed, there would still have been an issue with women—who are the ones to bear children and take care of them in the early weeks because of biological factors like the ability to breastfeed, forcing them to take breaks, whether or not those breaks were mandated.

如果 1980 年代女性的进步是通过消除偏见之类的东西取得的，那么一旦这些相对可控的问题被处理掉之后，女性身上仍然会存在一个问题——她们是承担生育以及在最初几周照料孩子的人，因为像哺乳能力这样的生理因素，迫使她们休假，无论这种休假是否被强制规定。
