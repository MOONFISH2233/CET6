范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 166 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

As social media is used more and more extensively, there is a growing awareness of the importance of using it properly and responsibly. Platforms such as WeChat and Weibo have become part of daily life, yet the way we behave online shapes both our own reputation and the wider community.

To use social media responsibly, we should verify information before sharing it and treat others with respect. This is because a single careless post can spread misinformation within minutes. A case in point is my own experience: last year I nearly forwarded a false rumour about a campus event, but a quick check revealed it was fake. Had I shared it, I would have misled hundreds of classmates.

Therefore, using social media properly is not a trivial habit but a genuine responsibility. If each of us pauses to think before posting, the online world can become a place of trust rather than confusion. Only in this way can we truly enjoy the benefits that technology offers.
