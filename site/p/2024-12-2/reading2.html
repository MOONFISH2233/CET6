原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Statements, like "beauty is in the eye of the beholder(观看者)", are rarely questioned. They've become so embedded in our consciousness that people think it's absurd to think otherwise. It might be useful, however, to at least push back on this assumption because people evaluate environment, situations, and people aesthetically. We may find that we still believe it is correct, but we may also find that there is a lot more to the situation than you suppose.

像“美在观看者的眼中”这样的说法很少受到质疑。它们已经如此深深地植入我们的意识，以至于人们认为持相反看法是荒谬的。然而，至少对这一假设提出质疑或许是有益的，因为人们会从审美角度评价环境、情境和他人。我们可能会发现自己仍然认为这一说法是正确的，但也可能会发现，事情远比你所设想的复杂得多。

P2

A recent study exploring aesthetic taste was published in Cognition. The results from this study show that people agree very much in their aesthetic evaluation of natural objects, but they disagree more about artifacts, or human-made objects.

一项探讨审美趣味的最新研究发表在《认知》杂志上。该研究的结果表明，人们在对自然物体进行审美评价时具有很高的一致性，但对于人工制品，即人造物体，意见分歧则更大。

P3

The study found that shared taste was most common for faces and natural landscapes, but least common among works of architecture and art. The hypothesis is the commonly pleasing features, like proportion or symmetry, are at work.

研究发现，共同审美趣味最常见于面孔和自然景观，而在建筑和艺术作品中最少见。其假设是，比例或对称性等通常能带来愉悦感的特征在其中发挥着作用。

P4

Preferences for natural scenes might be learned through life experiences. Factors like habitability, safety, and openness might be preferred as people develop. Some of the details of landscapes change, but the basics are more common, e.g., water, open spaces, and signs of care.

人们对自然景观的偏好可能是在生活经历中逐渐形成的。随着人们的成长，宜居性、安全性和开阔感等因素可能会成为人们偏好的对象。景观的某些细节会发生变化，但基本要素更加普遍，例如水域、开阔空间以及受到照料的迹象。

P5

Art and architecture, unlike natural spaces, do not have the same level of exposure. So, people do not have the same level of shared taste.

与自然空间不同，艺术和建筑并没有同等程度的接触机会。因此，人们也不会具有同等程度的共同审美趣味。

P6

It's possible that the lower amount of agreement in the shared taste of artifacts has to do more with elements of style, rather than "behavioral consequences".

人们在人工制品方面共同审美趣味的一致性较低，可能更多与风格元素有关，而不是与“行为后果”有关。

P7

All of the consequences seem reasonable (or at least possible), but I think there might be an interesting philosophical conclusion that they did not draw. It seems that there is an objective ground to our aesthetic preferences or evaluations. I am not saying this would mean beauty is completely objective, just that there could possibly be general objective principles at work, i.e., beauty is not simply in the eye of the beholder.

所有这些推论似乎都是合理的（或者至少是可能的），但我认为其中或许存在一个有趣的哲学结论，而他们没有得出这一结论。我们的审美偏好或评价似乎存在某种客观基础。我并不是说这意味着美完全是客观的，只是说，可能有一些普遍的客观原则在发挥作用；也就是说，美并不只是存在于观看者的眼中。

P8

Nature exhibits some of the universal aesthetic features, such as radiance, in a common way throughout the world. It's not exactly the same everywhere, but it is common. For example, a sunset is similar enough in different places to warrant almost universal appeal.

自然以一种在世界各地都很常见的方式呈现出某些普遍的审美特征，例如光辉。它并非在任何地方都完全相同，但这种特征具有普遍性。比如，不同地方的日落足够相似，因此几乎能够引起普遍的喜爱。

P9

Possible universal principles of beauty—such as proportion, fittingness, radiance, and others—are general categories, which allow for a wide array of embodiments. When people get involved in making artifacts in architecture or art, they apply these very general concepts in unique ways. But the way they applied the principle may not have universal appeal.

可能具有普遍性的美的原则，例如比例、合宜性、光辉以及其他原则，都是一般性的类别，因而可以有多种多样的具体体现。当人们参与建筑或艺术创作、制造人工制品时，他们会以独特的方式运用这些十分一般的概念。但他们运用这些原则的方式未必能获得普遍的认同。

P10

This is why it's important to experience diverse cultures and their artifacts because it opens us up to different ways of approaching or constructing beauty. And it is always good to question our assumptions.

这就是为什么体验不同的文化及其人工制品很重要，因为这会让我们接触到构思或创造美的不同方式。而且，质疑我们的假设总是有益的。

解析逐题详解

51

What does the passage say about the statement "beauty is in the eye of the beholder"?

文章对"情人眼里出西施"这一说法是如何评价的?

  • A)It has hardly ever been disputed.它几乎从未受到过质疑。
  • B)It can be interpreted aesthetically.它可以从美学角度进行解读。
  • C)Many people have found it absurd.许多人认为它很荒谬。
  • D)People have long been misled by it.人们长期以来一直被它误导。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判「几乎无人质疑」与 hardly ever been disputed 在频率与语义上完全等值，锁定 A。
  • rarely questioned↔hardly ever been disputed
B) 偷换概念It can be interpreted aesthetically 说「它可被审美地解读」，与原文 rarely questioned 完全无关；作者只说「无人质疑」，没说「可被审美解读」。
C) 与原文相反原文说「人们觉得 absurd to think otherwise」（反着想才荒谬），而 Many people have found it absurd 把「荒谬」直接扣给那句谚语本身，正话反说。
D) 无中生有People have long been misled by it 说「人们长期被它误导」，原文只说作者「想 push back 一下」，并未给该谚语贴「误导」标签。
P1

Statements, like "beauty is in the eye of the beholder(观看者)", are rarely questioned.

诸如「美在观看者的眼中」这样的说法，几乎从不受到质疑。

52

What does a recent study exploring aesthetic taste show?

最近一项探索审美品味的研究显示了什么?

  • A)Aesthetic tastes tend to differ from person to person.审美品味往往因人而异。
  • B)Natural beauty is rarely surpassed by artificial beauty.自然美很少被人工美所超越。
  • C)There is less consensus on objects shaped by human craft.人们对人工制品的共识较少。
  • D)There is general agreement on what is pleasing to the eye.人们对什么是赏心悦目的存在普遍共识。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 以偏概全Aesthetic tastes tend to differ from person to person 把原文「人造物上分歧」扩大成「审美口味本来就因人而异」，丢掉了原文「自然物上一致」这一半。
B) 偷换概念Natural beauty is rarely surpassed by artificial beauty 把研究讲成「自然美几乎不被人造美超越」，原文谈的是「评价一致性」，不是「美的优劣比较」。
C) ✓ 正确预判要求选项聚焦「人造物上分歧更多」，less consensus on objects shaped by human craft 完整对应该语义，锁 C。
  • they disagree more about artifacts, or human-made objects↔less consensus on objects shaped by human craft
D) 以偏概全There is general agreement on what is pleasing to the eye 只说「人们对什么好看普遍一致」，丢掉了原文「对人造物分歧更多」这关键一半，与研究的对比结论不符。
P2

The results from this study show that people agree very much in their aesthetic evaluation of natural objects, but they disagree more about artifacts, or human-made objects.

这项研究的结果显示，人们对自然物的审美评价高度一致，但对人造物（即人工制造的物品）则分歧更多。

53

Why do people differ in their aesthetic appraisal of artifacts?

为什么人们对人工制品的审美评价存在差异?

  • A)They come out in a great variety of designs.它们的设计种类繁多。
  • B)They lead to varying behavioral consequences.它们导致不同的行为后果。
  • C)They take up much less natural space than landscapes.它们占用的自然空间比风景少得多。
  • D)They come into people's view less often than natural spaces.它们进入人们视野的频率低于自然空间。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 答非所问They come out in a great variety of designs 谈的是「设计多样」，与原文给出的原因 exposure 完全无关；这是 P9 里 a wide array of embodiments 的话被移花接木过来。
B) 与原文相反P6 明说差异 has to do more with elements of style, rather than "behavioral consequences"——作者用 rather than 否定了 behavioral consequences；They lead to varying behavioral consequences 反而把被否定的那一支抬回来当原因。
C) 无中生有They take up much less natural space than landscapes 说人造物比自然景观「占用的自然空间更少」，原文从未做过这种空间面积比较；这是按生活常识替原文补造的对比。
D) ✓ 正确预判要求「人造物被看到的机会少」，与 come into people's view less often than natural spaces 完全等值，锁 D。
  • do not have the same level of exposure↔come into people's view less often than natural spaces
P5

Art and architecture, unlike natural spaces, do not have the same level of exposure. So, people do not have the same level of shared taste.

艺术与建筑（不像自然空间）没有同等程度的曝光度。因此，人们对它们的共同审美趣味也达不到同样的程度。

54

What does the example of sunset tell about nature?

日落的例子说明了自然的什么特点?

  • A)It creates a powerful appeal through its brilliant radiance.它通过灿烂的光辉产生强大的吸引力。
  • B)It displays aesthetic traits in a similar way the world over.它在世界各地以相似的方式展现美学特征。
  • C)It embodies beauty with a wide array of aesthetic features.它以广泛的美学特征体现美。
  • D)It establishes the principles of beauty in a number of ways.它以多种方式确立了美的原则。
解析
B（例证目的题）
A) 答非所问It creates a powerful appeal through its brilliant radiance 谈的是「落日靠光辉本身创造强烈吸引力」，是 sunset 例子的表层描写，不是例子要说明的论点（共通性）。
B) ✓ 正确预判要求「自然在全球以共通方式呈现审美特征」，It displays aesthetic traits in a similar way the world over 与论点句逐项匹配，锁 B。
  • Nature exhibits some of the universal aesthetic features, such as radiance, in a common way throughout the world↔It displays aesthetic traits in a similar way the world over
C) 张冠李戴It embodies beauty with a wide array of aesthetic features 借用 a wide array of embodiments 与 Possible universal principles of beauty 的短语，但 P9 谈的是「美的原则允许多样体现」，不是 sunset 例子要说明的内容。
D) 过度推断It establishes the principles of beauty in a number of ways 把「sunset 例证一种共通呈现方式」夸大成「确立了多个美学原则」，原文从未把 sunset 提到「确立原则」的高度。
P8

Nature exhibits some of the universal aesthetic features, such as radiance, in a common way throughout the world. It's not exactly the same everywhere, but it is common. For example, a sunset is similar enough in different places to warrant almost universal appeal.

自然界以共通的方式在世界各地呈现某些普遍的审美特征（比如光辉）。各处并不完全相同，但确实有共通之处。例如，落日在不同地方足够相似，因而几乎能引起普世的吸引力。

55

Why do artifacts not always hold universal appeal?

为什么人工制品并不总是具有普遍吸引力?

  • A)The appreciation of their beauty is not subject to objective standards.对其美的欣赏不受客观标准的约束。
  • B)The universal principle of beauty prevents a wide array of embodiments.美的普遍原则阻止了广泛的体现方式。
  • C)The way their creators apply the principle of beauty may not be pleasing to everybody.其创作者应用美的原则的方式可能无法取悦所有人。
  • D)Their creators interpret the universal principle of beauty each in their preferred way.其创作者各自以自己偏好的方式诠释美的普遍原则。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反The appreciation of their beauty is not subject to objective standards 说「人造物之美不受客观标准约束」，但作者在 P7 与 P9 都坚持存在 objective ground / Possible universal principles of beauty，A 与作者立场相反。
B) 与原文相反The universal principle of beauty prevents a wide array of embodiments 说原则「阻止」多样体现，原文恰恰说 are general categories, which allow for a wide array of embodiments——「允许」多样体现。
C) ✓ 正确「运用方式」+「未必人人喜欢」两个语义落点在 The way their creators apply the principle of beauty may not be pleasing to everybody 都得到对应，锁 C。
  • the way they applied the principle may not have universal appeal↔The way their creators apply the principle of beauty may not be pleasing to everybody
D) 以偏概全Their creators interpret the universal principle of beauty each in their preferred way 只说「创作者各自按偏好诠释原则」，并未传达「未必人人喜欢」这层「为何没有普遍吸引力」的因果；它只复述了 they apply these very general concepts in unique ways 的前半段，丢掉了 But 之后的关键否定。
P9

When people get involved in making artifacts in architecture or art, they apply these very general concepts in unique ways. But the way they applied the principle may not have universal appeal.

当人们投身于建筑或艺术中的人造物创作时，他们以独特的方式运用这些非常宽泛的概念。但他们运用原则的方式未必能引起所有人的共鸣。
