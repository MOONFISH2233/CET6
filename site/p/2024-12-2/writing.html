范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 164 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Nowadays, more and more students have realized the importance of self-discipline in their personal growth. In an age flooded with short videos and endless distractions, the ability to control oneself has become the very foundation of academic success and character building.

A vivid example is my roommate Li Ming. When we entered university, he set himself a firm rule: no phone before finishing the day's study plan. While others drifted from one game to another, his steady self-management allowed him to master a difficult programming course and win a national scholarship. His experience convinced me that talent alone rarely decides how far a person goes; it is perseverance and daily self-control that truly shape our future.

Therefore, I firmly believe that cultivating self-discipline deserves our full attention. Rather than blaming the environment for our failures, we should learn to say no to temptations and stick to our own plans. Only by mastering ourselves can we grow into the people we truly wish to become.
