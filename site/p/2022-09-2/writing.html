范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 181 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Today more and more people begin to realize the pleasures and joys of real-world social interaction. After years of staring at glowing screens, many of us have discovered that a warm handshake, a shared laugh or a face-to-face chat brings a satisfaction that no online message can replace.

My own experience convinces me of this. Last summer I put down my phone and joined a hiking club in my hometown. Instead of typing emojis, I talked, sweated and cooked with real people on the trail. When a stranger offered me water on a steep slope, I felt a genuine bond that no number of online 'likes' had ever given me. Those weekends left me happier and more energetic than a whole year of scrolling ever did.

Therefore, real-world social interaction feeds a need that technology alone cannot satisfy. It reminds us that we are living beings who thrive on presence, eye contact and shared moments. Let us step out of the virtual world more often and reconnect with the people around us, for it is there that true joy quietly grows.
