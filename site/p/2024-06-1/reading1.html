原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

It is irrefutable that employees know the difference between right and wrong. So why don't more employees intervene when they see someone exhibiting at-risk behavior in the workplace?

不可否认，员工知道是非对错。那么，为什么当他们看到有人在工作场所表现出可能导致事故的行为时，却没有更多员工出面干预呢？

P2

There are a number of factors that influence whether people intervene. First, they need to be able to see a risky situation beginning to unfold. Second, the company's culture needs to make them feel safe to speak up. And third, they need to have the communication skills to say something effectively.

有许多因素会影响人们是否进行干预。首先，他们需要能够看出危险情形正在逐渐发生。其次，公司的文化需要让他们感到可以安全地表达意见。第三，他们需要具备有效表达的沟通技巧。

P3

This is not strictly a workplace problem; it's a growing problem off the job too. Every day people witness things on the street and choose to stand idly by. This is known as the bystander effect—the more people who witness an event, the less likely anyone in that group is to help the victim. The psychology behind this is called diffusion of responsibility. Basically, the larger the crowd, the more people assume that someone else will take care of it—meaning no one effectively intervenes or acts in a moment of need.

这并不完全是工作场所的问题；在工作之外，这也日益成为一个严重的问题。人们每天都会目睹街头发生的事情，却选择袖手旁观。这被称为旁观者效应——目睹某一事件的人越多，群体中的任何人出手帮助受害者的可能性就越小。这背后的心理机制被称为责任分散。基本而言，人群越大，越多人会认为其他人会处理这件事，这意味着在需要帮助的时刻，没有人真正进行干预或采取行动。

P4

This crowd mentality is strong enough for people to evade their known responsibilities. But it's not only frontline workers who don't make safety interventions in the workplace. There are also instances where supervisors do not intervene either.

这种群体心态强烈到足以让人们逃避自己明知应承担的责任。但在工作场所不进行安全干预的并不只有一线员工，也有主管不进行干预的情况。

P5

When a group of employees sees unsafe behavior not being addressed at a leadership level it creates the precedent that this is how these situations should be addressed, thus defining the safety culture for everyone.

当一群员工看到领导层没有处理不安全行为时，就会形成一种先例，使人们认为这类情况就应该这样处理，从而为所有人确立安全文化。

P6

Despite the fact that workers are encouraged to intervene when they observe unsafe operations, this happens less than half of the time. Fear is the ultimate factor in not intervening. There is a fear of penalty, a fear that they'll have to do more work if they intervene. Unsuccessful attempts in the past are another strong contributing factor to why people don't intervene—they tend to prefer to defer that action to someone else for all future situations.

尽管人们被鼓励在发现不安全作业时进行干预，但实际发生这种干预的情况还不到一半。不进行干预的最终原因是恐惧：害怕受到惩罚，害怕一旦进行干预就不得不承担更多工作。过去不成功的尝试也是人们不进行干预的重要原因之一——对于今后遇到的所有类似情况，他们往往更愿意把采取行动的责任推给别人。

P7

On many worksites, competent workers must be appointed. Part of their job is to intervene when workers perform a task without the proper equipment or if the conditions are unsafe. Competent workers are also required to stop work from continuing when there's a danger.

在许多工作场所，必须指定合格人员。他们的工作之一，就是在工人未配备适当设备进行作业或作业条件不安全时进行干预。当存在危险时，合格人员还必须叫停作业，不得继续进行。

P8

Supervisors also play a critical role. Even if a competent person isn't required, supervisors need a broad set of skills to not only identify and alleviate workplace hazards but also build a safety climate within their team that supports intervening and open communication among them.

主管同样发挥着关键作用。即使不要求指定合格人员，主管也需要具备广泛的技能，不仅要能够识别和消除工作场所的危险，还要在团队内部营造一种安全氛围，支持成员之间进行干预和开放沟通。

P9

Beyond competent workers and supervisors, it's important to educate everyone within the organization that they are obliged to intervene if they witness a possible unsafe act, whether you're a designated competent person, a supervisor or a frontline worker.

除了合格人员和主管之外，重要的是要教育组织内的每一个人：无论你是指定的合格人员、主管还是一线员工，只要目睹了可能存在的不安全行为，就有义务进行干预。

解析逐题详解

46

What is one of the factors contributing to failure of intervention in face of risky behavior in the workplace?

在面对工作场所风险行为时,导致干预失败的因素之一是什么?

  • A)Slack supervision style.松懈的监督方式。
  • B)Unfavorable workplace culture.不利的工作场所文化。
  • C)Unforeseeable risk.不可预见的风险。
  • D)Blocked communication.受阻的沟通。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 无中生有P2 列举的三个因素中并无 Slack supervision style（监管松懈）。supervisors 在 P4 P8 才出现，且讨论的是是否亲自干预，与「监管风格松懈」无关。
B) ✓ 正确原文说「需要文化让人敢说」，预判答案是「文化不让人敢说」，B 项 Unfavorable workplace culture 正是这一缺失态的同义改写，因此锁定 B。
  • the company's culture needs to make them feel safe to speak up↔Unfavorable workplace culture
C) 与原文相反they need to be able to see a risky situation beginning to unfold 说的是「员工要能预见风险」，而 Unforeseeable risk 反过来说「风险无法预见」，与原文方向相反；且不在 P2 的三因素列表中。
D) 偷换概念they need to have the communication skills to say something effectively 说的是「需要具备沟通技能」，强调的是个人技能层面；而 Blocked communication 说「沟通被阻塞」，把「技能不足」偷换为「通道被堵」，对象完全不同。
P2

Second, the company's culture needs to make them feel safe to speak up.

第二，公司的文化需要让他们觉得发声是安全的。

47

What does the author mean by "diffusion of responsibility" (Para. 3)?

作者所说的"责任分散"(第3段)是什么意思?

  • A)The more people are around, the more they need to worry about their personal safety.周围的人越多,他们就越需要担心自己的人身安全。
  • B)The more people who witness an event, the less likely anyone will venture to目睹某一事件的人越多,任何人冒险的可能性就越小。
  • C)The more people idling around on the street, the more likely they need taking care of.在街上闲逛的人越多,他们就越有可能需要被照顾。
  • D)The more people are around, the less chance someone will step forward to intervene.周围的人越多,有人站出来干预的机会就越少。
解析
D（词义句意题）
A) 偷换概念they need to worry about their personal safety 把原文「无人干预他人」的话题偷换成「担心自身安全」，自身安全在 P3 里根本未提及。
B) 答非所问The more people who witness an event, the less likely anyone will venture to 后半句 venture to 后无宾语，句子残缺；即便补全也只是 the more people who witness an event, the less likely anyone in that group is to help the victim 的 bystander effect 释义，而题干问的是 diffusion of responsibility 这一更深层心理机制，对应的是 P3 后半句而非这句。
C) 偷换概念people idling around on the street 把原文 stand idly by 的「袖手旁观者」读成「街上闲逛的人」，并称他们「需要被照顾」，与 diffusion of responsibility 的心理机制无关。
D) ✓ 正确选项 D 的两段比较级与原文的两段比较级一一对应：人多→人多假定→更少人出手干预，与预判「越多越没人挺身而出」严丝合缝，故锁定 D。
  • the larger the crowd, the more people assume that someone else will take care of it↔The more people are around, the less chance someone will step forward to intervene
P3

Basically, the larger the crowd, the more people assume that someone else will take care of it—meaning no one effectively intervenes or acts in a moment of need.

基本上，人群越大，越多人就假定别人会处理这事——也就是说，在需要出手时反而无人有效干预或行动。

48

What happens when unsafe behavior at the workplace is not addressed by the leaders?

当工作场所的不安全行为没有被领导者处理时,会发生什么?

  • A)No one will intervene when they see similar behaviors.当人们看到类似行为时,没有人会进行干预。
  • B)Everyone will see it as the easiest way to deal with crisis.每个人都会把它视为处理危机最简单的方法。
  • C)Workers have to take extra caution executing their duties.工人在执行职责时必须格外谨慎。
  • D)Workers are left to take care of the emergency themselves.工人只能自己处理紧急情况。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确结合上下文（领导没干预+形成先例），先例的具体内容就是「对类似行为不干预」，选项 A 正是该先例的客观转述，故锁定 A。
  • it creates the precedent that this is how these situations should be addressed↔No one will intervene when they see similar behaviors
B) 过度推断原文只说形成「该这样处理」的先例，并未评价它是 the easiest way；且「危机处理」角度也是 crisis 强加的描述。
C) 无中生有Workers have to take extra caution 在原文中找不到根据，原文是说大家集体不干预，并非「工人变得更小心」。
D) 与原文相反Workers are left to take care of the emergency themselves 说工人会自己去处理紧急情况，而 P5 的逻辑是「没人去处理」，方向完全相反。
P5

When a group of employees sees unsafe behavior not being addressed at a leadership level it creates the precedent that this is how these situations should be addressed, thus defining the safety culture for everyone.

当一群员工看到领导层不处理不安全行为时，就会形成「这类情况就该这样处理」的先例，从而为所有人定义了安全文化。

What is the ultimate reason workers won't act when they see unsafe operations?

当工人看到不安全操作时不采取行动的根本原因是什么?

  • A)Preference of deferring the action to others.倾向于把行动推给其他人。
  • B)Anticipation of leadership intervention.期待领导层进行干预。
  • C)Fear of being isolated by coworkers.担心被同事孤立。
  • D)Fear of having to do more work.害怕必须做更多工作。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 答非所问Preference of deferring the action to others 对应 they tend to prefer to defer that action to someone else for all future situations，但这是 Unsuccessful attempts in the past（过去失败经验）的副产品，是 another strong contributing factor，不是 ultimate factor。
B) 无中生有Anticipation of leadership intervention 在 P6 没有依据。文中 P4 P5 提到 leadership 不干预，但谈的是「领导自己不出手」形成先例，而非「员工等领导干预」。
C) 无中生有Fear of being isolated by coworkers 中「被同事孤立」在 P6 罗列的几种 fear（penalty / do more work / unsuccessful attempts）中并无对应。
D) ✓ 正确原文罗列了两类 fear，选项 D 把第二类「怕多干活」原样收纳，与预判完全一致，故锁定 D。
  • a fear that they'll have to do more work if they intervene↔Fear of having to do more work
P6

Fear is the ultimate factor in not intervening. There is a fear of penalty, a fear that they'll have to do more work if they intervene.

恐惧是不干预的最终因素。存在对惩罚的恐惧，以及对「一旦干预就得做更多工作」的恐惧。

50

What is critical to ensuring workplace safety?

什么对于确保工作场所安全至关重要?

  • A)Workers be trained to operate their equipment properly.工人接受培训以正确操作设备。
  • B)Workers exhibiting at-risk behavior be strictly disciplined.表现出危险行为的工人受到严格惩戒。
  • C)Supervisors create a safety environment for timely intervention.监督者创造一个安全环境以便及时干预。
  • D)Supervisors conduct effective communication with frontline workers.监督者与一线工人进行有效沟通。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 无中生有Workers be trained to operate their equipment properly 中「培训工人正确操作设备」在原文中无对应；without the proper equipment 谈的是「没有合适设备」，与「培训操作设备」不是一回事。
B) 过度推断Workers exhibiting at-risk behavior be strictly disciplined 中「对有风险行为的工人进行严厉惩戒」，P6 只是说工人怕 penalty，不代表全文主张要 strictly discipline，原文并未把「严厉惩戒」作为确保安全的做法。
C) ✓ 正确主语对位（Supervisors↔Supervisors）、动作对位（build↔create）、对象对位（safety climate↔safety environment）、目的对位（supports intervening↔for timely intervention），与预判一致，故锁定 C。
  • build a safety climate within their team that supports intervening↔Supervisors create a safety environment for timely intervention
D) 以偏概全Supervisors conduct effective communication with frontline workers 抓住了 open communication，但漏掉原文核心 build a safety climate within their team that supports intervening——主管的关键不仅是沟通，而是建立支持干预的安全氛围；且 communication with frontline workers 把原文 open communication among them（团队内部沟通）偷换为「主管与一线工人之间的沟通」，对象错位。
P8

Supervisors also play a critical role. Even if a competent person isn't required, supervisors need a broad set of skills to not only identify and alleviate workplace hazards but also build a safety climate within their team that supports intervening and open communication among them.

主管也起着至关重要的作用。即便不需要专门的胜任人员，主管也需要一整套技能，既要能识别和消除职场隐患，也要在团队中营造支持干预和坦诚沟通的安全氛围。
