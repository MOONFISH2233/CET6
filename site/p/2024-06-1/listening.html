2

Two papers published today in the American Journal of Clinical Nutrition outline the case for and against using the concept of ultra-processed foods to help inform dietary guidelines beyond conventional food classification systems. The authors, Carlos Monteiro of the University of São Paulo and Arne Astrup of Novo Nordisk Foundation, will discuss the issue in a live virtual debate August 14th during NUTRITION 2024 LIVE ONLINE.

今天发表在《美国临床营养学杂志》上的两篇论文，分别阐述了支持和反对使用“超加工食品”这一概念的理由，以帮助在传统食品分类体系之外进一步制定膳食指南。论文作者、圣保罗大学的Carlos Monteiro和Novo Nordisk Foundation的Arne Astrup，将于8月14日在NUTRITION 2024 LIVE ONLINE期间举行的线上直播辩论中讨论这一问题。

3

The debate centers around a system developed by Monteiro and colleagues that classifies foods by their degree of industrial processing, ranging from unprocessed to ultra-processed. The system defines ultra-processed foods as those made using sequences of processes that extract substances from foods and alter them with chemicals in order to formulate the final product. Ultra-processed foods are characteristically designed to be cheap, tasty, and convenient; examples include soft drinks and candy, packaged snacks and pastries, ready-to-heat products, and reconstituted meat products.

这场辩论围绕Monteiro及其同事制定的一套体系展开，该体系根据食品的工业加工程度对其进行分类，范围从未加工食品到超加工食品。该体系将超加工食品定义为：通过一系列加工步骤制成的食品，在这些步骤中，先从食品中提取物质，再使用化学品对其进行改变，以配制出最终产品。超加工食品通常被设计成价格低廉、美味可口且方便食用；其中包括软饮料和糖果、包装零食和糕点、即热食品以及再制肉制品等。

4

Studies have linked consumption of ultra-processed foods—which are packed high in salt, sugar, and fat—with weight gain and an increased risk of chronic diseases, even after adjusting for the amount of salt, sugar, and fat in the diet. While the mechanisms behind these associations are not fully understood, Monteiro argues that the existing evidence is sufficient to justify discouraging consumption of ultra-processed foods in dietary recommendations and government policies.

研究表明，食用超加工食品会导致体重增加并提高患慢性疾病的风险；这类食品含有大量盐、糖和脂肪，即使在对膳食中盐、糖和脂肪的含量进行调整后，这些关联仍然存在。尽管这些关联背后的机制尚未被完全理解，Monteiro认为，现有证据已经足以支持在膳食建议和政府政策中劝阻人们食用超加工食品。

3

The shark. Sharks try to overpower opponents by forcing them to accept their solution to the conflict. They seek to achieve their goals at all costs. 泰迪熊型。泰迪熊希望得到他人的接纳和喜爱。它们认为，为了保持和谐，应当避免冲突，并相信讨论冲突会损害人际关系。它们放弃自己的目标，以维护这段关系。
