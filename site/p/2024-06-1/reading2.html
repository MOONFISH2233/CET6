原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

The term "environmentalist" can mean different things. It used to refer to people trying to protect wildlife and natural ecosystems. In the 21st century, the term has evolved to capture the need to combat human-made climate change.

“环保主义者”一词可以指不同的人。过去，它指的是试图保护野生动物和自然生态系统的人。在21世纪，这一词语的含义发生了变化，涵盖了应对人为造成的气候变化的需要。

P2

The distinction between these two strands of environmentalism is the cause of a split within the scientific community about nuclear energy.

这两种环保主义之间的区别，是科学界围绕核能问题产生分歧的原因。

P3

On one side are purists who believe nuclear power isn't worth the risk and the exclusive solution to the climate crisis is renewable energy. The opposing side agrees that renewables are crucial, but says society needs an amount of power available to meet consumers' basic demands when the sun isn't shining and the wind isn't blowing. Nuclear energy, being far cleaner than oil, gas and coal, is a natural option, especially where hydroelectric capacity is limited.

一方是信奉纯粹主义的人，他们认为核电的风险不值得承担，应对气候危机的唯一方案就是可再生能源。另一方则同意可再生能源至关重要，但表示，当阳光不照耀、风也不吹动时，社会仍需要有足够的电力来满足消费者的基本需求。核能比石油、天然气和煤炭清洁得多，因此是一种自然的选择，尤其是在水力发电能力有限的地方。

P4

Leon Clarke, who helped author reports for the UN's Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change, isn't an uncritical supporter of nuclear energy, but says it's a valuable option to have if we're serious about reaching carbon neutrality.

曾参与撰写联合国政府间气候变化专门委员会报告的莱昂·克拉克，并不是毫无保留地支持核能，但他说，如果我们认真对待实现碳中和这一目标，那么核能是一个值得保留的选择。

P5

"Core to all of this is the degree to which you think we can actually meet climate goals with 100% renewables," he said. "If you don't believe we can do it, and you care about the climate, you are forced to think about something like nuclear."

他说：“这一切的核心在于，你认为我们究竟有多大可能仅凭100%的可再生能源实现气候目标。如果你不相信我们能够做到，同时又关心气候问题，那么你就不得不考虑核能之类的方案。”

P6

The achievability of universal 100% renewability is similarly contentious. Cities such as Burlington, Vermont, have been "100% renewable" for years. But these cities often have small populations, occasionally still rely on fossil fuel energy and have significant renewable resources at their immediate disposal. Meanwhile, countries that manage to run off renewables typically do so thanks to extraordinary hydroelectric capabilities.

实现普遍的100%可再生能源同样存在争议。佛蒙特州的伯灵顿等城市，多年来一直实现了“100%可再生能源”。但这些城市往往人口较少，有时仍然依赖化石燃料能源，而且手头拥有丰富的可再生能源资源。与此同时，那些成功依靠可再生能源运行的国家，通常也是得益于非同寻常的水力发电能力。

P7

Germany stands as the best case study for a large, industrialized country pushing into green energy. Chancellor Angela Merkel in 2011 announced Energiewende, an energy transition that would phase out nuclear and coal while phasing in renewables. Wind and solar power generation has increased over 400% since 2010, and renewables provided 46% of the country's electricity in 2019.

德国是大型工业化国家推进绿色能源的最佳案例研究对象。德国总理安格拉·默克尔于2011年宣布实施“能源转型”，逐步淘汰核能和煤炭，同时逐步引入可再生能源。自2010年以来，风能和太阳能发电量增加了400%以上，2019年可再生能源占该国发电量的46%。

P8

But progress has halted in recent years. The instability of renewables doesn't just mean energy is often not produced at night, but also that solar and wind can overwhelm the grid during the day, forcing utilities to pay customers to use their electricity. Lagging grid infrastructure struggles to transport this overabundance of green energy from Germany's north to its industrial south, meaning many factories still run on coal and gas. The political limit has also been reached in some places, with citizens meeting the construction of new wind turbines with loud protests.

但近年来，进展已经停滞。可再生能源的不稳定性不仅意味着夜间经常无法发电，还意味着太阳能和风能在白天可能使电网超负荷，迫使公用事业公司付钱给用户，让他们使用这些电力。滞后的电网基础设施难以将过剩的绿色能源从德国北部输送到工业发达的南部，这意味着许多工厂仍然依靠煤炭和天然气运行。在一些地方，政治上的容忍度也已达到极限，居民以激烈的抗议回应新风力涡轮机的建设。

P9

The result is that Germany's greenhouse gas emissions have fallen by around 11.5% since 2010—slower than the EU average of 13.5%.

结果是，自2010年以来，德国的温室气体排放量下降了约11.5%，低于欧盟13.5%的平均降幅。

解析逐题详解

51

What accounts for the divide within the scientific community about nuclear energy?

是什么造成了科学界内部关于核能的分歧?

  • A)Attention to combating human-made climate change.对应对人为气候变化的关注。
  • B)Emphasis on protecting wildlife and natural ecosystems.对保护野生动物和自然生态系统的重视。
  • C)Evolution of the term "green energy" over the last century.过去一个世纪"绿色能源"一词的演变。
  • D)Adherence to different interpretations of environmentalism.对环境保护主义不同解释的坚持。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 以偏概全Attention to combating human-made climate change 只对应 P1 里 environmentalist 的"第二种含义"，不是"两种含义的差异"。
B) 以偏概全Emphasis on protecting wildlife and natural ecosystems 只对应 P1 里 environmentalist 的"第一种含义"，同样只覆盖单侧。
C) 偷换概念原文谈的是 The term "environmentalist" 在 21 世纪的演变，Evolution of the term "green energy" over the last century 把术语换成了 green energy、时间换成了 the last century。
D) ✓ 正确预判要求答案体现"对环保主义的不同理解→科学界分裂"这一因果链，Adherence to different interpretations of environmentalism 是四项中唯一精准覆盖该因果的表述。
  • The distinction between these two strands of environmentalism↔Adherence to different interpretations of environmentalism
P2

The distinction between these two strands of environmentalism is the cause of a split within the scientific community about nuclear energy.

这两种环保主义之间的区别，正是科学界在核能问题上发生分裂的原因。

52

What is the solution to energy shortage proposed by purists' opponents?

纯粹主义者的反对者提出的能源短缺解决方案是什么?

  • A)Relying on renewables firmly and exclusively.坚定且专门依赖可再生能源。
  • B)Using fossil fuel and green energy alternately.交替使用化石燃料和绿色能源。
  • C)Opting for nuclear energy when necessary.必要时选择核能。
  • D)Limiting people's non-basic consumption.限制人们的非基本消费。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反Relying on renewables firmly and exclusively 描述的恰恰是 purists 的立场，而非对立方——对立方主张的是核能补缺，绝不"独占"可再生能源。
B) 无中生有Using fossil fuel and green energy alternately 谈"化石燃料和绿色能源交替使用"，原文从未提出过"交替使用"这一方案，且对立方明确说核能 far cleaner than oil, gas and coal，即反对烧煤气油。
C) ✓ 正确预判结论"必要时启用核能补缺"在四项中只有 Opting for nuclear energy when necessary 精准匹配。
  • Nuclear energy, being far cleaner than oil, gas and coal, is a natural option, especially where hydroelectric capacity is limited↔Opting for nuclear energy when necessary
D) 无中生有Limiting people\'s non-basic consumption 谈"限制非基本消费"，原文只提 consumers\' basic demands——"满足基本需求"和"限制非基本消费"是两回事，后者属凭空引申。
P3

The opposing side agrees that renewables are crucial, but says society needs an amount of power available to meet consumers' basic demands when the sun isn't shining and the wind isn't blowing. Nuclear energy, being far cleaner than oil, gas and coal, is a natural option, especially where hydroelectric capacity is limited.

对立方认同可再生能源关键，但表示在阳光不足、无风时社会仍需一定量电力以满足消费者的基本需求。核能比油、气、煤都清洁得多，是自然之选，尤其在水电能力有限的地方。

53

What point does the author want to make with cities like Burlington as an example?

作者想以Burlington等城市为例说明什么观点?

  • A)It is controversial whether the goal of the whole world's exclusive dependence on renewables is attainable.全世界完全依赖可再生能源的目标是否可以实现是有争议的。
  • B)It is contentious whether cities with large populations have renewable resources at their immediate disposal.人口众多的城市是否拥有可直接利用的可再生资源是有争议的。
  • C)It is arguable whether cities that manage to run off renewables have sustainable hydroelectric capabilities.设法依靠可再生能源运行的城市是否拥有可持续的水力发电能力是有争议的。
  • D)It is debatable whether traditional fossil fuel energy can be done away with entirely throughout the world.传统化石燃料能源是否能在全世界范围内被完全淘汰是有争议的。
解析
A（例证目的题）
A) ✓ 正确预判结论包含三要素"全球范围、独占可再生、可达成性存疑"，A 是四项中唯一同时覆盖三要素的表述。
  • The achievability of universal 100% renewability is similarly contentious↔It is controversial whether the goal of the whole world\'s exclusive dependence on renewables is attainable
B) 偷换概念cities with large populations have renewable resources at their immediate disposal 谈"大人口城市是否有可再生资源"，原文 P6 只说 Burlington 这类"小人口"城市，并未把议题转向"大人口城市能否做到"。
C) 偷换概念原文 countries that manage to run off renewables typically do so thanks to extraordinary hydroelectric capabilities 只说"靠可再生运转的国家"通常水电禀赋强，并未质疑其"可持续性"；选项 sustainable hydroelectric capabilities 把客观描述偷换为"是否可持续"的争议。
D) 过度推断traditional fossil fuel energy can be done away with entirely throughout the world 谈"是否能在全球范围彻底废除化石燃料"，原文只论"是否能 100% 可再生"，并未直接讨论"彻底废除化石燃料"，两者虽相关但不等价。
P6

The achievability of universal 100% renewability is similarly contentious. Cities such as Burlington, Vermont, have been "100% renewable" for years.

全球 100% 可再生能源是否能实现同样有争议。像佛蒙特州 Burlington 这样的城市多年来一直号称"100% 可再生"。

54

What do we learn about Germany regarding renewable energy?

关于可再生能源,我们对德国有何了解?

  • A)It has increased its wind and solar power generation four times over the last two decades.在过去二十年里,它的风能和太阳能发电量增加了四倍。
  • B)It represents a good example of a major industrialized country promoting green energy.它代表了一个主要工业化国家推广绿色能源的良好范例。
  • C)It relies on renewable energy to generate more than half of its electricity.它依靠可再生能源产生超过一半的电力。
  • D)It has succeeded in reaching the goal of energy transition set by Merkel.它已成功达到默克尔设定的能源转型目标。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念原文 Wind and solar power generation has increased over 400% since 2010 是"自 2010 年以来"约 10 年，而 over the last two decades 是"过去二十年"——时间维度被夸大一倍。
B) ✓ 正确预判结论"大型工业国推进绿能的范本"与 B 在主谓宾每个成分上都做到同义替换，是最高质量的同义匹配。
  • Germany stands as the best case study for a large, industrialized country pushing into green energy↔It represents a good example of a major industrialized country promoting green energy
C) 偷换概念原文 renewables provided 46% of the country\'s electricity in 2019 是 46%，more than half 是">50%"——46% 显然不到一半，是数字偷换。
D) 与原文相反原文只说默克尔 2011 年宣布 Energiewende，未说德国"已成功达成"该目标；事实上 But progress has halted in recent years 直接表明进展停滞，说明远未达成。
P7

Germany stands as the best case study for a large, industrialized country pushing into green energy.

德国是大型工业化国家推进绿色能源的最佳案例。

55

What may be one of the reasons for Germany's progress having halted in recent years?

德国近年来进展停滞的原因之一可能是什么?

  • A)Its grid infrastructure's capacity has fallen behind its development of green energy.其电网基础设施的容量落后于其绿色能源的发展。
  • B)Its overabundance of green energy has forced power plants to suspend operation during daytime.其过剩的绿色能源迫使发电厂在白天暂停运营。
  • C)Its industrial south is used to running factories on conventional energy supplies.其工业化的南部习惯于使用传统能源供应运营工厂。
  • D)Its renewable energy supplies are unstable both at night and during the day.其可再生能源供应在夜间和白天都不稳定。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判结论"电网跟不上绿电规模"在四项中只有 A 精准命中，主语"grid infrastructure"与原文同名重合，谓语"has fallen behind"与 Lagging 同义。
  • Lagging grid infrastructure struggles to transport this overabundance of green energy↔Its grid infrastructure\'s capacity has fallen behind its development of green energy
B) 偷换概念原文 solar and wind can overwhelm the grid during the day, forcing utilities to pay customers to use their electricity 是"电网超负荷迫使电力公司付钱让顾客用电"，forced power plants to suspend operation during daytime 把"付钱让顾客用电"偷换为"电厂停运"，主语和动作均失实。
C) 偷换概念原文 many factories still run on coal and gas 是"由于电网无法把北部绿电输到南部"这一被动结果；Its industrial south is used to running factories on conventional energy supplies 把它说成"南部工业区习惯于用传统能源"——把客观限制偷换为主观习惯。
D) 偷换概念原文 The instability of renewables doesn\'t just mean energy is often not produced at night, but also that solar and wind can overwhelm the grid during the day 是"不仅夜里没电、白天还过剩"——表面看选项 D 似乎和原文一致，但 unstable both at night and during the day 把"夜间缺供+白天过剩"这一具体二元情况偷换为笼统的"昼夜都不稳定"，丢失了关键差异。
P8

Lagging grid infrastructure struggles to transport this overabundance of green energy from Germany's north to its industrial south, meaning many factories still run on coal and gas.

滞后的电网基础设施难以把过剩的绿电从德国北部输送到工业化南部，这意味着许多工厂仍靠煤气运转。
