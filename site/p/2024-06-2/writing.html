范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 174 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

Nowadays more and more college students have come to realize social practice and academic learning are equally important. Rather than treating them as rivals competing for limited time, an increasing number of us regard the two as partners that reinforce each other, and I firmly share this view.

Academic learning equips us with theories, while social practice tests and deepens them. Knowledge that never leaves the textbook easily fades, yet once applied, it truly takes root. Take my own experience as an example. After studying marketing principles for a whole semester, I joined a campus start-up and helped promote its products online. Only then did the abstract concepts suddenly make sense, and in return the real feedback from customers pushed me back to my books with sharper questions. Learning guided my practice, and practice enriched my learning.

Therefore, academic study and social practice are two wheels of the same cart, and neither can be spared. Only by letting them go hand in hand can college students grow into capable talents that society genuinely needs.
