原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

The "American Dream" promises that in the Land of Opportunity, any individual can climb the economic ladder and prosper through hard work and ambition alone. And yet, young Americans today are struggling to earn more than their parents did at the same age, and upward mobility in the US actually compares unfavourably to that of other industrialised nations.

“美国梦”承诺，在这片机遇之地，任何个人都可以仅凭勤奋和抱负攀登经济阶梯并实现富足。然而，如今的美国年轻人正努力争取在同龄时挣得比父母更多，而美国的向上流动性实际上比其他工业化国家更差。

P2

So why does the idea of the American Dream persist? A new study in the American Journal of Political Science identifies one factor that has been overlooked: the influence of reality TV.

那么，为什么美国梦这一理念仍然存在？《美国政治学杂志》发表的一项新研究指出了一个此前被忽视的因素：真人秀节目的影响。

P3

Reality shows have come to dominate US television over the past 20 years, notes Eunji Kim from Vanderbilt University. And the overwhelming majority of these have a "rags-to-riches" storyline: they feature ordinary Americans who work hard to achieve great economic success. And while these programmes are regularly among the most-watched shows, news broadcasts—which paint a more realistic view of the economic hardship faced by millions of Americans—get a much smaller proportion of the viewership.

范德比尔特大学的Eunji Kim指出，过去20年间，真人秀节目已经占据了美国电视节目的主导地位。其中绝大多数节目都有“从贫穷到富有”的故事线：节目展现普通美国人通过努力工作取得巨大的经济成功。尽管这些节目经常跻身收视率最高的节目之列，但新闻节目描绘了数百万美国人所面临的经济困境的更为真实的景象，却只获得了小得多的观众比例。

P4

Rags-to-riches stories are ubiquitous(无处不在的) on TV—but does watching these programmes actually convince people that economic mobility is easily attainable? To find out, Kim's team had participants watch a 5-minute clip from a reality show with a rags-to-riches storyline. Control participants watched a clip from a reality show that didn't have a rags-to-riches story. After watching the shows, participants rated how much they agreed with four statements relating to the American Dream.

电视上“从贫穷到富有”的故事无处不在——但观看这些节目真的会让人相信经济流动是很容易实现的吗？为了弄清这一点，Kim的团队让参与者观看一段时长5分钟、讲述“从贫穷到富有”故事的真人秀片段。对照组参与者观看了一段没有“从贫穷到富有”故事的真人秀片段。节目结束后，参与者对四项与美国梦相关的陈述进行认同程度评价。

P5

The results showed that those who'd watched a rags-to-riches clip did indeed have a significantly greater belief in the American Dream. Interestingly, when participants were separated by party affiliation, this effect was significant among Republicans but not Democrats, suggesting that the kind of messages implicit in these TV shows may play into people's existing socioeconomic beliefs.

结果显示，观看了“从贫穷到富有”片段的人确实对美国梦有明显更强的信念。有趣的是，当按照党派归属对参与者进行区分时，这一影响在共和党人中具有显著性，而在民主党人中则没有显著性，这表明这些电视节目所隐含的信息可能会迎合人们已有的社会经济观念。

P6

Kim also conducted a survey of 3,000 US residents. They also rated the extent to which they believed success in life is related to various internal factors (such as ambition) and external factors (such as family wealth). Finally, they read a list of TV programmes and indicated which they regularly watched.

Kim还对3000名美国居民进行了调查。他们还评价了自己在多大程度上认为人生成功与各种内在因素（如抱负）和外在因素（如家庭财富）有关。最后，他们阅读了一份电视节目清单，并指出自己经常观看其中哪些节目。

P7

Participants who were heavy viewers of rags-to-riches programmes or frequent viewers had a stronger belief in the American Dream than those who never watched such shows.

经常观看“从贫穷到富有”节目的参与者，比从不观看这类节目的参与者更坚信美国梦。

P8

Kim concludes that "rags-to-riches entertainment media are an important cultural force that promotes and perpetuates beliefs in upward mobility". And here's the problem: if people mistakenly believe that hard work is all that is needed for individuals to make a better life for themselves, they may be less supportive of policies that could actually combat inequality.

Kim总结道：“讲述‘从贫穷到富有’故事的娱乐媒体是一股重要的文化力量，它推动并延续了人们对向上流动性的信念。”而问题就在于：如果人们错误地认为，个人要想让自己的生活变得更好，只需要努力工作就够了，那么他们可能就不太支持那些实际上能够应对不平等的政策。

P9

"In this era of choice, entertainment media is what captures hearts and minds," Kim writes. "Its political consequences are anything but trivial".

Kim写道：“在这个选择多样的时代，娱乐媒体最能俘获人心、影响思想。它所产生的政治后果绝非无足轻重。”

解析逐题详解

46

What do we learn from the passage about young Americans of today?

从文章中我们了解到当今美国年轻人的什么情况?

  • A)They have greater ambitions than their parents.他们比父母有更大的抱负。
  • B)They find it difficult to achieve upward mobility.他们发现实现向上流动很困难。
  • C)They have overtaken their parents in terms of earnings.他们在收入方面已经超过了父母。
  • D)They envy the opportunities in other industrialised nations.他们羡慕其他工业化国家的机会。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 无中生有原文只说他们在收入上难超父辈，从未比较两代人的『抱负』。
B) ✓ 正确预判结论是『难以向上流动』，选项 B 几乎是这句话的字面化，二者构成最直接的同义替换。
  • struggling to earn more than their parents did at the same age↔find it difficult to achieve upward mobility
  • upward mobility in the US actually compares unfavourably↔upward mobility
C) 与原文相反原文说青年正在 struggling to earn more than their parents，意即尚未超过；C 选项却说 overtaken their parents in terms of earnings，恰好相反。
D) 过度推断原文只把美国向上流动与其他工业化国家比较得出『美国更差』，从未提及青年的情绪态度，更未说他们 envy。
P1

And yet, young Americans today are struggling to earn more than their parents did at the same age, and upward mobility in the US actually compares unfavourably to that of other industrialised nations.

然而，今天的美国年轻人正在艰难地试图挣得比父辈同龄时更多的收入，而且美国的向上流动性事实上比其他工业化国家逊色。

47

What does Kim's team find about reality TV shows in America?

Kim的团队对美国真人秀节目有什么发现?

  • A)They reinterpret the essence of the popular rags-to-riches culture.它们重新诠释了流行的白手起家文化的本质。
  • B)They urge people to achieve economic success through hard work.它们敦促人们通过努力工作获得经济成功。
  • C)They help strengthen people's conviction in the American Dream.它们帮助增强人们对美国梦的信念。
  • D)They feature ordinary Americans striving for social recognition.它们以普通美国人争取社会认可为特色。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 无中生有原文只说真人秀展示 rags-to-riches storyline 并验证其加深观众信仰，从未提到节目在『重新诠释』该文化的本质。
B) 偷换概念they feature ordinary Americans who work hard to achieve great economic success 的主语是节目所呈现的人物在努力，而非节目敦促观众去努力。
C) ✓ 正确预判为『真人秀加深美国梦信仰』，选项 C 把『加深』表达为 help strengthen，把『美国梦信仰』表达为 conviction in the American Dream，二者一一对应。
  • significantly greater belief in the American Dream↔help strengthen people’s conviction in the American Dream
D) 偷换概念原文明确写 great economic success，与选项的 social recognition 不是一回事。
P5

The results showed that those who'd watched a rags-to-riches clip did indeed have a significantly greater belief in the American Dream.

结果显示，那些看过『白手起家』短片的人确实显著加深了对美国梦的信仰。

48

What does the author say about news broadcasts in America?

作者对美国的新闻广播说了什么?

  • A)They attract far fewer viewers than reality TV.它们吸引的观众远少于真人秀节目。
  • B)They are bent on reporting the dark side of life.它们一心报道生活的阴暗面。
  • C)They stand in striking contrast with reality TV.它们与真人秀节目形成鲜明对比。
  • D)They focus on Americans' economic hardships.它们关注美国人的经济困境。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确预判结论是『观众远少于真人秀』，选项 A 把 smaller proportion 改写为 far fewer viewers，把对照对象明示为 reality TV，与定位句的对比关系完全一致。
  • get a much smaller proportion of the viewership↔attract far fewer viewers than reality TV
B) 过度推断paint a more realistic view of the economic hardship 只描述了新闻所呈现的内容，并未对新闻的意图作 be bent on（一心要、决意）这种主观倾向判断。
C) 偷换概念原文用 while 引出对比，但描述对象是『收视份额』，并未对新闻与真人秀做 striking contrast（鲜明反差）的整体定性评价。
D) 以偏概全paint a more realistic view of the economic hardship 是把经济困境作为更真实图景的一部分来呈现，并未说新闻 focus on（聚焦于）经济困境；况且 Q48 问的是作者对新闻的总评，而 D 只是从句里附带说明的内容。
P3

And while these programmes are regularly among the most-watched shows, news broadcasts—which paint a more realistic view of the economic hardship faced by millions of Americans—get a much smaller proportion of the viewership.

而尽管这些节目（真人秀）经常位列收视率最高的电视节目之列，新闻播报——它们描绘了数以百万计的美国人所面临的经济困境这一更现实的图景——只占据小得多的观众份额。

49

What can we infer from the passage about Republicans in general?

从文章中我们可以推断出共和党人总体上的什么情况?

  • A)They believe strongly in the American Dream.他们坚信美国梦。
  • B)They strive to climb the socio-economic ladder.他们努力攀登社会经济阶梯。
  • C)They have a very strong affiliation with their party.他们对自己的党派有很强的归属感。
  • D)They tend to watch more rags-to-riches TV shows.他们倾向于观看更多白手起家的电视节目。
解析
A（推断题）
A) ✓ 正确预判结论是『共和党人本就更信美国梦』，选项 A 是这一推断的标准表达。
  • this effect was significant among Republicans but not Democrats↔believe strongly in the American Dream
  • play into people’s existing socioeconomic beliefs↔believe strongly in the American Dream
B) 无中生有原文只比较了党派对美国梦的信念差异，从未提到任一党派的人在 socio-economic ladder 上的实际行动。
C) 偷换概念when participants were separated by party affiliation 中 party affiliation 只是『按党派分组』的研究操作变量，并未评价共和党人对自己政党的归属感强弱。
D) 过度推断原文只说看了 rags-to-riches 短片后，对美国梦信仰加深的效应在共和党人中显著；并未说共和党人比民主党人更常或更多地观看此类节目。
P5

Interestingly, when participants were separated by party affiliation, this effect was significant among Republicans but not Democrats, suggesting that the kind of messages implicit in these TV shows may play into people's existing socioeconomic beliefs.

有趣的是，当按党派分组时，这种效应在共和党人中显著、而在民主党人中不显著，这意味着真人秀里隐含的信息可能契合了人们既有的社会经济观念。

50

What is stated about people who believe in upward mobility?

关于相信向上流动的人,文中陈述了什么?

  • A)They are likely to blame the government for their plight.他们可能会因自己的困境而责怪政府。
  • B)They regard political consequences as anything but trivial.他们认为政治后果绝非微不足道。
  • C)They respect individuals striving to climb the social ladder.他们尊重努力攀登社会阶梯的个人。
  • D)They are less likely to approve of policies to fight inequality.他们不太可能赞成对抗不平等的政策。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 无中生有P8 只说他们更不支持反不平等政策，从未提到他们指责政府或抱怨自身困境。
B) 张冠李戴Its political consequences are anything but trivial 中代词 Its 指向 entertainment media；选项 B 把这一评价偷塞到『相信向上流动者』身上，属于张冠李戴。
C) 无中生有原文只说他们因为相信努力万能而更不支持反不平等政策，从未提到他们对其他奋斗者的态度（如尊敬）。
D) ✓ 正确预判结论『更不支持反不平等政策』与选项 D 完全等义，combat 与 fight 是六级阅读高频可互换词。
  • they may be less supportive of policies that could actually combat inequality↔less likely to approve of policies to fight inequality
P8

And here's the problem: if people mistakenly believe that hard work is all that is needed for individuals to make a better life for themselves, they may be less supportive of policies that could actually combat inequality.

问题就在这里：如果人们错误地以为只要努力工作就足以让个人过上更好的生活，他们就可能更不支持那些其实能对抗不平等的政策。
