自美国国家航空航天局（NASA）于1989年发表一篇论文，声称室内植物能够吸收污染物和有毒化学物质以来，企业和房主越来越多地投资于绿植，以帮助净化空气。然而，一项新的分析表明，要获得任何超过简单打开几扇窗户所能带来的益处，实际上可能需要每平方米摆放超过1000株植物。

2

The problem lies in the fact that NASA conducted their tests in sealed containers that do not simulate the conditions in most people's homes or offices. The space agency was primarily concerned about keeping the air fresh for astronauts cut off in biospheres or space stations and helping to combat "sick building syndrome", which had become a problem due to the super insulated and energy efficient offices of the late 1970s. By the early 1980s, workers regularly complained of skin rashes, sleeplessness, headaches, and allergies as they breathed in toxic chemicals from paints and plastics.

问题在于，NASA是在密封容器中进行测试的，而这种环境并不能模拟大多数人家中或办公室的实际情况。该航天机构主要关注的是如何为被隔绝在生物圈或空间站中的宇航员保持空气清新，以及帮助应对“病态建筑综合征”。由于20世纪70年代末的办公室采用了高度隔热且节能的设计，这种综合征逐渐成为一个问题。到了20世纪80年代初，员工经常抱怨出现皮疹、失眠、头痛和过敏症状，因为他们吸入了来自油漆和塑料的有毒化学物质。

3

NASA found that certain plants could remove chemicals from the air, and even today, garden centers recommend the plants for air cleaning properties. However, a new evaluation of dozens of studies spanning 30 years found that houseplants in a normal environment have little impact. In fact, natural ventilation is far better at cleaning the air. The researchers also calculated the clean air delivery rate for plants in the studies they analyzed and found that the rate at which plants disperse the compounds was well below the usual rate of air exchange in a normal building, caused by the movement of people coming and going, opening doors and windows.

NASA发现，某些植物能够去除空气中的化学物质，直到今天，园艺中心仍会因这些植物具有净化空气的功效而予以推荐。然而，一项对历时30年、涵盖数十项研究的新评估发现，在正常环境中，室内植物的影响微乎其微。事实上，自然通风在净化空气方面要有效得多。研究人员还计算了所分析研究中植物的清洁空气输送率，发现植物分散这些化合物的速率远低于普通建筑通常的空气交换速率；后者是由人员进出以及开关门窗等活动造成的。

4

Many of the studies did show a reduction in the concentration of volatile organic compounds over time, which is likely why people have seized on them to praise the air purifying virtues of plants. But the researchers' calculation showed it would take 10 to 1,000 plants per square meter of floor space to compete with the air cleaning capacity of a building's air handling system or even just a couple of open windows in a house. In contrast, NASA's sealed experiment recommended one pot plant per 100 square feet.

许多研究确实显示，挥发性有机化合物的浓度会随着时间推移而降低，这可能就是人们抓住这一点来赞美植物净化空气功效的原因。但研究人员的计算表明，要与建筑空气处理系统，甚至住宅中仅仅打开几扇窗户的空气净化能力相匹敌，每平方米地面面积需要摆放10至1000株植物。相比之下，NASA的密封实验建议每100平方英尺摆放一盆植物。

5

This is certainly an example of how scientific findings can be misleading or misinterpreted over time. But it's also a great example of how scientific research should continually reexamine and question findings to get closer to the ground truth of understanding what's actually happening.

这无疑说明，科学发现可能会随着时间推移而产生误导或被误解。但这也很好地体现了科学研究应当不断重新审视并质疑已有发现，从而更加接近对实际情况的真实理解。

【题目】 What does NASA's sealed experiment recommendation exemplify in scientists' pursuit of truth?

NASA的密封实验建议在科学家追求真理的过程中体现了什么?

  • A)The root cause for misinterpretations of scientific findings.科学发现被误读的根本原因。
  • B)The difficulty in understanding what's actually happening.理解实际发生情况的难度。
  • C)The steps to be taken in arriving at any conclusion with certainty.确定无疑地得出任何结论所需采取的步骤。
  • D)The necessity of continually reexamining and challenging findings.不断重新审查和质疑研究发现的必要性。
