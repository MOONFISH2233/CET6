【参考范文 · 164 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

With the trend of Urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for Nature. As concrete forests expand, many urban dwellers rarely feel the breeze, hear birds, or touch soil. A stronger bond with nature is not a luxury; it is essential for mental and physical well-being in an increasingly artificial environment.

One practical way to cultivate this love is to make nature part of daily routines. For example, residents can walk in nearby parks, grow plants on balconies, or join community gardening projects. These activities reconnect people with the living world. Last year, I started visiting a small riverbank park every weekend. Watching seasons change there reduced my stress and made me more aware of environmental protection.

Therefore, city governments and individuals should work together to create more green spaces and spend time in them. Only by integrating nature into urban life can residents truly develop a stronger love for it and build healthier cities for future generations.
