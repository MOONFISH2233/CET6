【参考范文 · 178 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

People are now increasingly aware of the challenges in making a decision when faced with too many choices. Ironically, the abundance of options that was meant to empower us often leaves us hesitant, anxious and exhausted instead.

A personal experience makes this clear. Last semester, when I tried to pick an elective, the online system listed over sixty courses. I spent three whole evenings comparing descriptions, reading reviews and switching between tabs, yet I still felt uncertain and finally enrolled at random. The endless comparison drained my energy long before the real learning began, and it left me doubting whether I had chosen wisely at all.

The lesson I have drawn is not to fear choices but to tame them. Before browsing, I now fix two or three criteria that truly matter to me and ignore the rest. By narrowing the field in advance, I turn an overwhelming list into a manageable shortlist, and I make decisions faster and with far less regret. When choices multiply, a clear standard, rather than more comparison, is what sets us free.
