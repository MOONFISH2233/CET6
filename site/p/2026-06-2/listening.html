【题目】 What is the man planning to do?

这个男人计划做什么？

  • A)Celebrate his 10th wedding anniversary.庆祝他的结婚十周年纪念日。
  • B)Celebrate his promotion in Barcelona.在巴塞罗那庆祝他的升职。
  • C)Give his wife a pleasant surprise.给他的妻子一个惊喜。
  • D)Ask for leave to visit Barcelona.请假去巴塞罗那。
解析
A（细节题）
B) 原词陷阱 Celebrate his promotion in Barcelona. 盗用了原文 in Barcelona，但录音说的是去 Barcelona 庆祝周年纪念，而非庆祝升职，promotion 未提及。
C) 过度推断 Give his wife a pleasant surprise. 把原文 I promised Jane 的浪漫氛围曲解为 surprise，录音中男士是答应订旅行，并未说是惊喜。
D) 原词陷阱 Ask for leave to visit Barcelona. 盗用原文 as I have annual leave to take，但录音说'有年假要休'，并非'申请请假'，动作被偷换。

It's my 10th wedding anniversary next week, and I promised Jane that I would book a city break for us, as I have annual leave to take, and it would be nice to celebrate somewhere warm.

下周是我结婚十周年纪念日，我答应 Jane 会给我们订一次城市短途旅行，因为我有年假要休，而且去个暖和的地方庆祝会很好。

信号承诺句+原因铺陈。男士用 I promised Jane that I would book a city break for us 直接说明自己的计划，后面 as 引出原因；这是典型的'被问者应答'，直接承载答案。
替换It's my 10th wedding anniversary next week↔Celebrate his 10th wedding anniversary.事件同义概括 I promised Jane that I would book a city break for us↔Celebrate his 10th wedding anniversary.计划句→事件目的
排除B)原词陷阱Celebrate his promotion in Barcelona. 盗用了原文 in Barcelona，但录音说的是去 Barcelona 庆祝周年纪念，而非庆祝升职，promotion 未提及。 借用原文：in Barcelona C)过度推断Give his wife a pleasant surprise. 把原文 I promised Jane 的浪漫氛围曲解为 surprise，录音中男士是答应订旅行，并未说是惊喜。 D)原词陷阱Ask for leave to visit Barcelona. 盗用原文 as I have annual leave to take，但录音说'有年假要休'，并非'申请请假'，动作被偷换。 借用原文：as I have annual leave to take
定位P2 It's my 10th wedding anniversary next week, and I promised Jane that I would book a city break for us, as I have annual leave to take, and it would be nice to celebrate somewhere warm. 下周是我结婚十周年纪念日，我答应 Jane 会给我们订一次城市短途旅行，因为我有年假要休，而且去个暖和的地方庆祝会很好。

【题目】 Why is the man starting to feel a bit frantic?

这个男人为什么开始感到有些慌乱？

  • A)He is unable to find some inexpensive accommodation.他找不到价格便宜的住宿。
  • B)There are few warm places to go at this time of the year.每年的这个时候，适合去的温暖地方很少。
  • C)There is so much for him to do before going on vacation.在度假之前，他有太多事情要做。
  • D)He is afraid Barcelona may not be so fabulous as expected.他担心巴塞罗那可能没有预期的那么棒。
解析
A（细节题）
B) 原词陷阱 There are few warm places to go at this time of the year. 盗用原文 somewhere warm，但原文说想找个暖和地方庆祝，没说暖和地方少，属原词陷阱。
C) 过度推断 There is so much for him to do before going on vacation. 把找住宿的困难扩大为'很多事要做'，录音未提及其他事务，属过度推断。
D) 与原文相反 He is afraid Barcelona may not be so fabulous as expected. 与女士说的 fabulous cosmopolitan city 方向相反，且男士慌乱原因是住宿而非担心城市不好。

But the anniversary is looming, and I'm still scrambling to find accommodation. Everything is either wildly expensive or already fully booked. I'm starting to feel a bit frantic.

但纪念日越来越近，我还在拼命找住宿。要么贵得离谱，要么已经订满。我开始有点慌了。

信号问题铺陈+情绪词。I'm still scrambling to find accommodation 点明困难，Everything is either wildly expensive or already fully booked 具体说明，I'm starting to feel a bit frantic 直接回应题干情绪。

D) 偷换概念 Stay in a hotel away from the coast where the accommodation is less expensive. 盗用原文 a bit further up the coast，但原文是'沿海岸更往北'，不是'远离海岸'，方向被改动。

Well, if you are going for a whole week, why don't you do a couple of nights in somewhere really glamorous, and then do the rest of the week a bit further up the coast, where the accommodation is cheaper, and that will offset the cost of the expensive place?

嗯，如果你要去一整周，为什么不先找家很华丽的地方住两晚，然后剩下的时间住到海岸更北一点的地方，那里住宿更便宜，可以抵消贵地方的费用。

It was a lazy, hazy, crazy day on the dwarf planet Pluto when New Horizons, a spacecraft from Earth, flew by at a speed of 31,000 mph (miles per hour) two years ago. One of the more bizarre things they found was that the haze in Pluto's atmosphere was much thicker than previously thought. The planet also had an atmosphere much cooler than earlier estimates, topping out at −333.4°F.

Now, a study published in Nature links those two atmospheric observations. A computer model developed by planetary scientist Xi Zhang and colleagues shows the haze of tiny droplets in the upper atmosphere is likely scattering light from the Sun, preventing heat from reaching the planet below. We see hazy skies on Earth too, from time to time, whenever solid particles get suspended in the air—from water-based fogs, to ash from fires, to the toxic droplets that make up a thick smog. 如果冥王星的大气层与地球以及其他邻近行星的大气层截然不同，这将有助于科学家拓展对行星大气层的认识。但目前，这只是基于新视野号获得的数据和一个复杂计算机模型提出的合理推测，而不是对霾的成分进行直接观测所得出的结论。
