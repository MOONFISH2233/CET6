原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Smartphones have always posed challenges for parents of teens. From social media apps to excessive screen time, the digital world is as threatening as the physical.

智能手机一直给青少年父母带来挑战。从社交媒体应用到过度使用屏幕，数字世界和现实世界一样具有威胁性。

P2

A new study shows that about half of parents of teens are worried about their teens being exposed to explicit content online. This is a valid concern, of course. But there are ways to alleviate the spread of explicit content, from changing the settings in their kids' phone to monitoring such content with apps like Bark.

一项新的研究显示，大约一半的青少年父母担心自己的孩子会在网上接触到露骨内容。当然，这种担忧是合理的。但是，有一些方法可以减缓露骨内容的传播，比如更改孩子手机中的设置，或使用 Bark 等应用监控这类内容。

P3

Just as alarming, if not more so, is that the amount of time teens spend on social media can lead to anxiety and depression.

同样令人担忧，甚至更加令人担忧的是，青少年花在社交媒体上的时间可能会导致焦虑和抑郁。

P4

Excessive screen time has detrimental effects, for a lot of obvious reasons. Constant media consumption inhibits healthy activities associated with childhood, like spending time outside, exercising, talking to friends and family. If it interferes with sleep, that's even worse.

过度使用屏幕会产生不利影响，原因有很多，而且显而易见。持续接触媒体会妨碍童年时期与健康相关的活动，例如待在户外、锻炼以及与朋友和家人交谈。如果它还干扰睡眠，那就更糟糕了。

P5

Ample research shows too much smartphone use produces significant anxiety, depression, and feelings of isolation in teens. Teens log on to social media or spend time online trying to make connections, but end up feeling more alone and sad than ever. Faceless communication not only produces feelings of isolation and loneliness but increases instances of online bullying and susceptibility to criminal conducts.

大量研究表明，过度使用智能手机会使青少年产生严重的焦虑、抑郁和孤立感。青少年登录社交媒体或上网，试图建立联系，但最终却比以往任何时候都感到更加孤独和悲伤。无面对面的交流不仅会产生孤立感和孤独感，还会增加网络欺凌事件以及受犯罪行为影响的可能性。

P6

Though I've banned most social media, I've still battled smartphone time with my own kids. A lot of the time I wish I could throw my kids' smartphones into the nearest lake. The convenience of communication—when they need a ride home from school after practice, for instance—often feels like it isn't worth the battle to keep their screen time low and productive, and continue to encourage "real-life" activities outside and with friends and family. I understand the addiction and often tell them I'm not sure how I would have handled having a smartphone as a teen. As an adult, I too, struggle with overuse.

虽然我已经禁止使用大多数社交媒体，但我仍然一直在和自己的孩子争论使用智能手机的时间。很多时候，我真希望能把孩子们的智能手机扔进最近的湖里。通信的便利性——比如他们课外活动结束后需要搭车回家时——常常让人觉得，为了控制他们的屏幕使用时间，使其保持较低水平并发挥积极作用，同时继续鼓励他们在户外以及与朋友和家人进行“现实生活”活动，并不值得这样争斗。我理解这种成瘾，而且常常告诉他们，我不知道自己在青少年时期拥有一部智能手机会如何应对。作为一个成年人，我也在努力应对过度使用的问题。

P7

In a recent TV interview to promote a new film about a mother and daughter navigating social media use, actress Kate Winslet expressed her view on teens and social media—and her concern mirrors the trends. "I do definitely feel that the world of social media is frightening to parents because we don't really know what's there," she said. "We don't know how our children are using it. We don't know the effects that it's really having on them."

在最近一次为宣传一部讲述母女共同应对社交媒体使用问题的新电影而进行的电视采访中，女演员凯特·温斯莱特表达了她对青少年和社交媒体的看法，而她的担忧与相关趋势相一致。她说：“我确实觉得，社交媒体的世界令父母感到害怕，因为我们并不真正知道那里有什么。我们不知道自己的孩子是如何使用它的。我们不知道它究竟会对他们产生什么影响。”

P8

I don't think the U.S. government should regulate social media and teen smartphone use, but it is a vital topic for parents to understand and take seriously. Unfortunately, now, parents don't just have to worry about real-world dangers, but all the ones sneaking online and the effects they have on teenagers and their mental health.

我认为美国政府不应监管社交媒体和青少年使用智能手机的问题，但这是一个父母必须了解并认真对待的重要话题。遗憾的是，如今父母不仅要担心现实世界中的危险，还要担心所有悄悄潜入网络的危险，以及这些危险对青少年及其心理健康造成的影响。

解析逐题详解

51

What do around half of parents of teens do according to a new study?

根据一项新研究，大约一半有青少年的父母会做什么？

  • A)They set their kids' phones to filter out obscene information.他们将孩子的手机设置为过滤淫秽信息。
  • B)They try to alleviate the spread of explicit scenes on the internet.他们试图减缓互联网上露骨场景的传播。
  • C)They feel concerned about their kids' viewing indecent content online.他们担心孩子在网上观看不雅内容。
  • D)They strive to prevent their kids from spending excessive screen time.他们努力防止孩子花费过多时间看屏幕。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 偷换概念：措施≠已实施行为A项They set their kids' phones to filter out obscene information.把第二段中changing the settings这类可能的缓解措施，说成是父母已经采取的行动，而原文并未说半数父母已这样做。
B) 偷换概念：可能性≠实际行为B项They try to alleviate the spread of explicit scenes on the internet.把原文there are ways to alleviate the spread（存在缓解途径）替换为父母try to alleviate（正在尝试缓解），改变了行为主体与事实状态。
C) ✓ 正确C项They feel concerned about their kids' viewing indecent content online.准确对应定位句中父母worried about孩子being exposed to explicit content online。
  • worried↔concerned
  • being exposed to explicit content online↔viewing indecent content online
D) 答非所问：跨段混用话题D项They strive to prevent their kids from spending excessive screen time.将第三段以后才出现的excessive screen time话题嫁接到第二段关于explicit content的研究结论上，答非所问。
P2

A new study shows that about half of parents of teens are worried about their teens being exposed to explicit content online.

一项新研究显示，约半数青少年父母担心自己的孩子在网上接触露骨内容。

52

What does a large amount of research show about teens' excessive smartphone use?

大量研究显示，青少年过度使用智能手机会造成什么影响？

  • A)It allows them less time for meaningful activities.这使他们用于有意义活动的时间减少。
  • B)It renders them more lonely and low-spirited.这使他们更加孤独和情绪低落。
  • C)It gives them less time for social interactions.这使他们用于社交活动的时间减少。
  • D)It makes them more alert to online bullying.这使他们更加警觉网络欺凌。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念：上位/下位词替换失当A项It allows them less time for meaningful activities.把第四段healthy activities associated with childhood, like spending time outside, exercising, talking to friends and family中的healthy activities替换为meaningful activities，缩小并改变了概念范围。
B) ✓ 正确B项It renders them more lonely and low-spirited.准确概括了定位句中more alone and sad than ever及feelings of isolation的含义。
  • feeling more alone↔lonely
  • sad↔low-spirited
  • feelings of isolation↔lonely
C) 与原文相反：行为方向矛盾C项It gives them less time for social interactions.说减少了社交时间，但原文Teens log on to social media or spend time online trying to make connections说明青少年反而花更多时间在网上进行社交尝试。
D) 偷换概念：结果→主体态度D项It makes them more alert to online bullying.把原文increases instances of online bullying（增加网络欺凌案例）改为more alert to online bullying（对网络欺凌更警觉），改变了语义关系。
P5

Ample research shows too much smartphone use produces significant anxiety, depression, and feelings of isolation in teens. Teens log on to social media or spend time online trying to make connections, but end up feeling more alone and sad than ever.

大量研究表明，过度使用智能手机会给青少年带来严重的焦虑、抑郁和孤独感。青少年登录社交媒体或上网试图建立联系，但最终感到比以往更加孤独和悲伤。

53

What do we learn about the author's feelings regarding smartphones and screen time?

我们从作者对智能手机和屏幕使用时间的看法中了解到什么？

  • A)They are deep-seated.这种看法根深蒂固。
  • B)They are instinctive.这种看法出于本能。
  • C)They are unique.这种看法与众不同。
  • D)They are mixed.这种看法很复杂，兼具正反两面。
解析
D（态度题）
A) 以偏概全：只取单一态度信号A项They are deep-seated.（根深蒂固的）只呼应了作者对智能手机问题的强烈反感，但忽略了作者承认便利和理解上瘾的部分。
B) 偷换概念：情感强烈≠本能B项They are instinctive.（本能的）将作者对智能手机的厌恶理解为本能反应，但原文明确给出了理性原因（屏幕时间、通讯便利等），并非单纯本能。
C) 无中生有C项They are unique.（独特的）在原文中没有任何对应信息，作者反而说I understand the addiction，暗示这种感受具有普遍性。
D) ✓ 正确D项They are mixed.用一词概括了第六段中作者既反感又理解、既看到弊端又承认便利的复杂情感。
  • I wish I could throw my kids' smartphones into the nearest lake. The convenience of communication...often feels like it isn't worth the battle...I understand the addiction...I too, struggle with overuse.↔mixed
54

What does actress Kate Winslet think about the world of social media?

女演员凯特·温斯莱特如何看待社交媒体的世界？

  • A)It poses increasing challenges to parents trying to ensure internet safety.对于试图确保网络安全的父母而言，它带来的挑战日益增多。
  • B)It scares parents because they don't really know what is on the screen.它让父母感到害怕，因为他们并不真正知道屏幕上有什么。
  • C)It is a sphere difficult for parents and their teens to navigate.这是一个父母和他们的青少年子女都难以应对的领域。
  • D)It is full of uncertainties frightful to parents and their teens.其中充满了令父母和他们的青少年子女感到恐惧的不确定性。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 无中生有：添加原文未出现概念A项It poses increasing challenges to parents trying to ensure internet safety.将原文frightening扩大为increasing challenges，并加入原文未提的ensure internet safety。
B) ✓ 正确B项It scares parents because they don't really know what is on the screen.与定位句frightening to parents because we don't really know what's there完全对应。
  • the world of social media is frightening to parents↔It scares parents
  • we don't really know what's there↔because they don't really know what is on the screen
C) 张冠李戴：背景信息≠人物观点C项It is a sphere difficult for parents and their teens to navigate.源自第七段首句a new film about a mother and daughter navigating social media use，这是电影内容，不是Kate对社交媒体的看法。
D) 偷换概念：扩大态度对象范围D项It is full of uncertainties frightful to parents and their teens.把原文frightening to parents扩大为frightful to parents and their teens，增加了青少年作为恐惧主体。
P7

"I do definitely feel that the world of social media is frightening to parents because we don't really know what's there," she said.

她（Kate Winslet）说：我确实觉得社交媒体世界对父母来说很可怕，因为我们真的不知道那里面有什么。

55

What message does the author try to convey in the last paragraph?

作者试图在最后一段传达什么信息？

  • A)Parents have to be concerned about dangers to their teens in both real and virtual worlds.父母必须关注现实世界和虚拟世界中对青少年子女构成的危险。
  • B)Governments have responsibilities in regulating social media and teens' smartphone use.政府有责任监管社交媒体以及青少年对智能手机的使用。
  • C)Parents should provide guidance to their teens in the use of social media.父母应当指导青少年子女使用社交媒体。
  • D)Online dangers are as serious as problems with teenagers' mental health.网络危险与青少年心理健康问题同样严重。
解析
A（主旨题）
A) ✓ 正确A项Parents have to be concerned about dangers to their teens in both real and virtual worlds.与末段parents don't just have to worry about real-world dangers, but all the ones sneaking online完全对应。
  • parents don't just have to worry about real-world dangers, but all the ones sneaking online↔Parents have to be concerned about dangers to their teens in both real and virtual worlds.
B) 与原文相反B项Governments have responsibilities in regulating social media and teens' smartphone use.认为政府有责任监管，但原文I don't think the U.S. government should regulate social media and teen smartphone use明确反对政府监管。
C) 偷换概念：态度重视≠具体指导C项Parents should provide guidance to their teens in the use of social media.把原文parents to understand and take seriously（父母应理解并认真对待）改为provide guidance（提供指导），改变了具体行动。
D) 偷换概念：影响关系→比较关系D项Online dangers are as serious as problems with teenagers' mental health.把原文the effects they have on teenagers and their mental health（网络危险对青少年心理健康的影响）改为online dangers与mental health problems之间的严重程度比较，改变了逻辑关系。
