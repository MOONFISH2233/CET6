范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 161 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

In an era of rapid social development, it is vitally important to enhance young people's sense of responsibility. As society changes at an unprecedented pace, young people often face distractions and pressures that weaken their commitment to family, community, and the future. Without a strong sense of responsibility, they may become self-centered and short-sighted.

The decline of responsibility among youth is partly caused by the instant gratification culture brought by technology and social media. Many young people are used to receiving immediate rewards and may avoid duties that require long-term effort. To address this problem, schools and families should work together to cultivate accountability through real tasks and role models. For example, involving students in community service can help them understand the value of contributing to society.

In conclusion, enhancing young people's sense of responsibility is not only a personal virtue but also a social necessity. Only when the younger generation takes responsibility seriously can society achieve sustainable development and harmony.
