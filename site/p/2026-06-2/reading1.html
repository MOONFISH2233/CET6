原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

What does self-discipline look like at work? Sometimes, it's the ability to resist temptation, such as distractions to avoid a particular task. Other times it's the ability to persist with a task when your energy and motivation seem low.

在工作中，自律是什么样的表现？有时，它是抵制诱惑的能力，比如抵制那些让你逃避某项特定任务的干扰。还有些时候，它是在精力和动力似乎都很低的时候，仍然坚持完成一项任务的能力。

P2

What do you believe about the nature of your own self-discipline? Is it something that runs on internal energy that is spent with use? Or is it something that is bolstered as you apply it and persist?

你如何看待自身自律的本质？它是否依靠一种会随着使用而消耗的内在能量运行？还是说，它会在你运用自律并坚持下去的过程中得到增强？

P3

Your answer matters, as it determines how much self-discipline you experience. For years, research in psychology pointed to self-control as a resource that is depleted with use and needs to be restored through breaks, rest, or sleep. More recently, however, research points to the importance of mindset. If you view self-discipline as an expendable resource, your willpower tends to fade with use. If you believe that exercising your self-control strengthens and fuels it, that is more likely to be your experience as you resist temptation or push yourself to get something done that is not appealing.

你的答案很重要，因为它决定了你能体验到多少自律。多年来，心理学研究认为，自我控制是一种会因使用而消耗、需要通过休息、放松或睡眠来恢复的资源。然而，近来的研究更加重视思维方式的重要性。如果你把自律看作一种会耗尽的资源，你的意志力往往会随着使用而减弱。如果你相信运用自我控制能够增强并补充它，那么当你抵制诱惑，或迫使自己完成一件并不吸引人的事情时，你更有可能获得这样的体验。

P4

If you don't already view self-discipline as a force that feeds itself, what might you do? Reflect on times that you had initial difficulty resisting temptation, getting started on something, or sticking with it, but pushed ahead and accomplished the task or met the goal. That's the kind of experience we're talking about with this particular belief about self-discipline, and you've already had such an experience, so you've shown that it's possible. When you feel your willpower and motivation fading, remind yourself of those previous experiences and imagine the same happening now.

如果你还没有把自律看作一种能够自我补充的力量，可以做些什么呢？回想一下这样的时刻：起初你很难抵制诱惑、开始做某件事，或者坚持做下去，但后来你还是继续努力，完成了任务或实现了目标。这正是我们所说的关于自律的这种特定信念所对应的经历，而你已经有过这样的经历，所以你已经证明了这是可能的。当你感到意志力和动力逐渐消退时，提醒自己想起以往的这些经历，并想象现在也会发生同样的情况。

P5

What if your experiences have always seemed to indicate that your self-control diminishes with use, and you need breaks to recharge? There's nothing wrong with that as long as you can afford to take those breaks. If not, remind yourself that not everyone experiences self-discipline as a depletable resource and that your experience might be different if you focus on gathering more energy at this point rather than focusing on how difficult it is to continue or the need for a break.

如果你的经历一直表明，你的自我控制会随着使用而减弱，需要通过休息来充电，那该怎么办？只要你有条件休息，这本身没有什么问题。如果没有条件，那么就提醒自己，并非每个人都会把自律体验为一种会耗尽的资源；如果此时你把注意力放在积蓄更多能量上，而不是关注继续下去有多么困难或认为自己需要休息，你的体验可能会有所不同。

P6

Also, if you know someone who is a role model of self-discipline, picture that person in your situation, persisting in the task. Regardless of how you do it, as you push forward to continue to accomplish progress, be sure to mentally reinforce yourself by taking pride in your ability to exert more willpower as you persist.

此外，如果你认识一位自律方面的榜样，可以想象那个人处在你的情境中，坚持完成这项任务。无论你采用什么方式，当你继续向前、不断取得进展时，一定要通过为自己能够在坚持过程中发挥出更强的意志力而感到自豪，在心理上不断强化自己。

P7

The benefits of exerting willpower are cumulative. Your self-discipline is akin to a muscle, and each time you resist temptation or persist in a task despite a lack of motivation, you strengthen that muscle, leading to less effort and resistance in similar instances in the future.

发挥意志力所带来的益处会不断累积。你的自律就像一块肌肉，每当你抵制诱惑，或在缺乏动力的情况下仍坚持完成一项任务时，你都会锻炼并强化这块肌肉，从而使自己在今后遇到类似情况时付出更少的努力，也能减少阻力。

46

What plays an important role in determining how much self-discipline people experience?

什么在决定人们所能体验到的自律程度方面发挥着重要作用？

  • A)Their belief about the nature of motivation.他们对动机本质的看法。
  • B)Their use of internal energy for persisting.他们为坚持下去而使用内在能量的方式。
  • C)Their conception of self-discipline.他们对自律的理解。
  • D)Their ability to resist temptation.他们抵制诱惑的能力。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 偷换概念选项把原文关于self-discipline本性的信念，偷换成了about the nature of motivation，属于概念替换。
B) 偷换概念'internal energy'只是人们可能持有的一种信念内容，不是决定自律体验的因素本身。
C) ✓ 正确Their conception of self-discipline对应'你对self-discipline本性的回答/信念'，与定位句完全贴合。
  • Your answer matters↔Their conception of self-discipline
  • determines how much self-discipline you experience↔plays an important role in determining how much self-discipline people experience
D) 答非所问抵抗诱惑是self-discipline的一种表现形式，而不是决定人们体验到多少自律的因素。
P3

Your answer matters, as it determines how much self-discipline you experience.

你的答案很重要，因为它决定了你体验到多少自律。

47

What did years of research suggest about self-discipline?

多年的研究表明，自律具有什么特点？

  • A)It is a depletable resource.它是一种可以耗竭的资源。
  • B)It is bolstered through use.它会通过使用得到增强。
  • C)Its strength is dependent on willpower.它的强度取决于意志力。
  • D)Its exercise is an exhausting experience.运用它是一种令人精疲力竭的经历。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确It is a depletable resource准确对应a resource that is depleted with use。
  • a resource that is depleted with use↔It is a depletable resource
B) 与原文相反这是较新研究/积极信念的观点，不是'years of research'早期研究的结论。
C) 无中生有原文并未说self-control的强度dependent on willpower，二者在这里几乎是同义循环。
D) 过度推断原文说需要休息恢复，不等于说使用体验是'exhausting experience'（令人筋疲力尽的经历）。
P3

For years, research in psychology pointed to self-control as a resource that is depleted with use and needs to be restored through breaks, rest, or sleep.

多年来，心理学研究把self-control视为一种会随使用而耗尽、需要通过休息、睡眠来恢复的资源。

Why does the author ask us to reflect on our experience of meeting the goal despite initial difficulty?

尽管一开始困难重重却最终实现目标，作者为什么要求我们回想这样的经历？

  • A)To show the urgency of abandoning outdated beliefs about self-control.说明摒弃关于自我控制的过时观念的紧迫性。
  • B)To show the feasibility of viewing self-discipline as a force fueling itself.说明将自律视为一种能够自我提供动力的力量是可行的。
  • C)To support the argument for persistence in workplace accomplishments.支持在工作成就方面坚持不懈的观点。
  • D)To add evidence to consistent findings in previous psychological research.为以往心理学研究中的一致发现增添证据。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 过度推断原文并未说要'abandon outdated beliefs'，只是建议补充一种积极信念，没有 urgency 或彻底抛弃旧信念的意思。
B) ✓ 正确viewing self-discipline as a force fueling itself对应this particular belief，the feasibility of对应you've shown that it's possible。
  • That's the kind of experience we're talking about with this particular belief about self-discipline↔viewing self-discipline as a force fueling itself
  • you've shown that it's possible↔the feasibility of
C) 答非所问虽然文中提到workplace，但本段建议的目的不是支持'职场成就中的坚持'，而是证明自律信念的可行性。
D) 张冠李戴定位句所支持的是'自律可自我强化'的新信念，而非previous psychological research中'self-control会耗尽'的一致发现。
P4

Reflect on times that you had initial difficulty resisting temptation, getting started on something, or sticking with it, but pushed ahead and accomplished the task or met the goal. That's the kind of experience we're talking about with this particular belief about self-discipline, and you've already had such an experience, so you've shown that it's possible.

回想那些你起初难以抵制诱惑、难以开始或难以坚持，但最终推进并完成任务或达成目标的时刻。这正是我们所说的关于自律的那种特定信念所对应的体验，而且你已经有过这样的体验，所以你已证明这是可能的。

49

What are we advised to do if we cannot afford to take breaks to recharge?

如果我们无法抽出时间休息来恢复精力，建议我们怎么做？

  • A)Figure out what has gone wrong.弄清楚哪里出了问题。
  • B)Focus on the hardest part of our task.专注于任务中最困难的部分。
  • C)Exert ourselves to go on with the work.使出全力继续工作。
  • D)Gather new energy from people around.从身边的人那里汲取新的能量。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 无中生有原文并未建议去'figure out what has gone wrong'，而是直接给出调整心态的建议。
B) 与原文相反原文明确说不要focus on how difficult it is to continue，而该选项说focus on the hardest part，与作者建议相反。
C) ✓ 正确Exert ourselves to go on with the work对应定位句中'focus on gathering more energy...continue'的核心动作。
  • focus on gathering more energy at this point rather than focusing on how difficult it is to continue↔Exert ourselves to go on with the work
D) 偷换概念原文说'focus on gathering more energy'，并未说从'people around'那里获取能量；第六段提到role model，但也不是本题条件建议的内容。
P5

If not, remind yourself that not everyone experiences self-discipline as a depletable resource and that your experience might be different if you focus on gathering more energy at this point rather than focusing on how difficult it is to continue or the need for a break.

如果不能休息，提醒自己并非所有人都把自律体验为会耗尽资源；如果你此刻专注于积聚更多能量，而不是专注于继续有多难或需要休息，你的体验可能会不同。

50

Why does the author compare our self-discipline to a muscle?

作者为什么把我们的自律比作肌肉？

  • A)The more it is applied, the more resistance it encounters.运用得越多，遇到的阻力就越大。
  • B)The more it is exerted, the more proud we become.越是运用它，我们就越感到自豪。
  • C)The more it is enforced, the stronger our willpower proves.越是强行运用它，我们的意志力就越能得到证明。
  • D)The more it is exercised, the more strengthened it gets.锻炼得越多，它就越强大。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反原文说强化肌肉后'leading to less effort and resistance'，即阻力更少，而不是encounters more resistance。
B) 张冠李戴proud是第六段mental reinforce的内容，不是肌肉比喻要说明的要点。
C) 偷换概念原文说strengthen that muscle（强化自律本身），而选项说stronger our willpower proves，把主语从self-discipline换成了willpower，且语义上二者在此文中近于同义反复。
D) ✓ 正确The more it is exercised, the more strengthened it gets完整对应each time...you strengthen that muscle的解释。
  • each time you resist temptation or persist in a task despite a lack of motivation, you strengthen that muscle↔The more it is exercised, the more strengthened it gets
P7

Your self-discipline is akin to a muscle, and each time you resist temptation or persist in a task despite a lack of motivation, you strengthen that muscle, leading to less effort and resistance in similar instances in the future.

你的自律 akin to 肌肉，每次你抵制诱惑或在缺乏动力时坚持任务，你都在强化那块肌肉，从而在未来类似情况下减少努力和阻力。
