原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

An awakening has been taking place in the physical world against the beauty model that has been dictated to us for years. But in the digital arena, social media determines what is considered beautiful.

在现实世界中，一场反对多年来强加给我们的美丽标准的觉醒正在发生。然而，在数字领域，社交媒体决定着什么才被视为美丽。

P2

The two opposing struggles are taking place in parallel. In the physical world, the struggle goes against the underlying pressure exerted on women to conform to an unrealistic beauty ideal. As part of the struggle, various media outlets have presented women whose bodies don't correspond to the so-called ideal. All those women who had previously been excluded from the covers of magazines, television series and the public agenda, have become "legitimate". At the same time, a group of influencers have begun to upload to social media photos of themselves without makeup, and even photos in which they highlight supposed flaws.

这两种相互对立的斗争正在并行展开。在现实世界中，这场斗争反对的是施加在女性身上的、要求她们符合不切实际的美丽理想的潜在压力。作为这场斗争的一部分，各种媒体纷纷呈现身材不符合所谓理想标准的女性。所有那些过去被排除在杂志封面、电视连续剧和公共议题之外的女性，如今都变得“名正言顺”了。与此同时，一群有影响力的人开始在社交媒体上上传自己不化妆的照片，甚至上传突出所谓缺陷的照片。

P3

Technology has reshaped our beauty ideal and is doing a great job communicating that message to the masses. One of the bizarre legacies of the past decade is the popularity of the "cyborg look", which illustrates what Americans will look like in 2050.

科技重塑了我们的美丽理想，并且非常有效地将这一信息传递给大众。过去十年留下的奇怪遗产之一，就是“赛博格外貌”的流行；这种外貌展现了美国人在2050年可能呈现的样子。

P4

The cyborg look spread rapidly. Today, however, the Instagram face has become the new beauty ideal.

赛博格外貌迅速传播开来。然而，如今Instagram脸已经成为新的美丽理想。

P5

The internalization of accepted beauty norms is much more effective when there is active involvement in the learning process. The active involvement of users is reflected in the gamified(游戏化的) interaction offered by the social media platforms—the ability to like, write a comment, compare, share.

当人们积极参与学习过程时，对公认美丽规范的内化会更加有效。用户的积极参与体现在社交媒体平台所提供的游戏化互动中，即点赞、发表评论、比较和分享的能力。

P6

Once the desired beauty ideal has been internalized, users are given tools or features to change their appearance to suit the accepted beauty ideal such as editing the image, choosing the ideal filter, the right background.

一旦人们内化了理想的美丽标准，用户就会获得各种工具或功能，用来改变自己的外貌，使其符合公认的美丽标准，例如编辑图像、选择理想的滤镜和合适的背景。

P7

A survey conducted in the United States revealed that more than 50% filter the images before posting them. And you will not be surprised to hear that the majority of them are women. One of the significant consequences of obsessive filtering is the emerging tendency to treat oneself as an object to be observed and valued, in the same way another person observes and judges from the side.

一项在美国开展的调查显示，超过50%的人会在发布图像前对其进行滤镜处理。听到其中大多数是女性，你不会感到惊讶。过度使用滤镜的一个重要后果，是人们越来越倾向于把自己当作一个供人观察和评价的对象，就像另一个人从旁观察和评判自己一样。

P8

The effect of the filters is already far beyond amiable amusement. The filters and the entire game played on the networks affect the mental health of the users. According to a study, apps like Instagram, Snapchat and FaceTune allow users to achieve a level of perfection that was previously only observed in beauty magazines.

滤镜所产生的影响已经远远超出了友善的消遣范畴。滤镜以及网络上进行的整个游戏都会影响用户的心理健康。一项研究表明，Instagram、Snapchat和FaceTune等应用程序能让用户达到一种此前只有在美容杂志中才能看到的完美程度。

P9

Even though humanity has always cherished beauty, in the last decade our obsession with looks has reached an unprecedented peak. The time spent on social media creates an urge to achieve an impossible beauty ideal so powerful that the only thing that can fix it is not cosmetic intervention, but mental health care.

尽管人类一直珍视美丽，但在过去十年里，我们对外表的痴迷已经达到前所未有的高峰。花在社交媒体上的时间催生出一种强烈的冲动，促使人们追求不可能实现的美丽理想；唯一能够解决这一问题的，不是整容干预，而是心理健康护理。

解析逐题详解

51

What do we learn about beauty in the digital arena?

关于数字领域的美,我们了解到什么?

  • A)It dictates the taste of digital media.它主宰着数字媒体的品味。
  • B)It has been in the making for years.它已经酝酿多年。
  • C)It has ushered in a new awakening.它开启了一种新的觉醒。
  • D)It is defined by social media.它由社交媒体定义。
D（细节题）
A) 与原文相反It dictates the taste of digital media 让"美"反过来去 dictate digital media,原文是 social media 在 determine beauty,主客被掉换。
B) 张冠李戴It has been in the making for years 谈的是"已经酝酿多年",原文只说 dictated to us for years 的是"physical world 的旧美标准",并非 beauty in the digital arena 本身。
C) 张冠李戴It has ushered in a new awakening 说"数字领域的美开启了新觉醒",但原文 An awakening has been taking place in the physical world 明确把觉醒放在 physical world,而非 digital arena。
D) ✓ 正确It is defined by social media 几乎就是预判句"美由社交媒体决定"的英文被动版,正中定位句 social media determines what is considered beautiful 的核心信息。
  • social media determines what is considered beautiful↔It is defined by social media
P1

But in the digital arena, social media determines what is considered beautiful.

但在数字领域,是社交媒体决定了什么才被认为是美的。

52

What does the passage say about beauty in the physical world?

文章关于现实世界中的美说了什么?

  • A)Women are under constant pressure to keep up with beauty models.女性承受着持续的压力以跟上美的模板。
  • B)Women are encouraged to pursue a beauty ideal that has never existed.女性被鼓励追求一种从未存在过的美的理想。
  • C)A fight is going on to remove pressure on women to conform to an absurd beauty ideal.一场斗争正在进行,旨在消除女性顺应荒谬美的理想的压力。
  • D)Media outlets have begun to present as beauty models trendy women without any makeup.媒体机构已开始将不化妆的时尚女性作为美的模板呈现。
C（细节题）
A) 与原文相反Women are under constant pressure to keep up with beauty models 强调女性"持续被施压",方向恰好与原文的"对抗这种压力"相反,文章重点在抗争,而非顺从。
B) 无中生有Women are encouraged to pursue a beauty ideal that has never existed 中的 encouraged to pursue 在原文找不到对应——原文谈的是被施压,而非被鼓励;"never existed"也并非原文 unrealistic 的等值表达。
C) ✓ 正确A fight is going on to remove pressure on women to conform to an absurd beauty ideal 把"正在进行抗争"+"对象是施加在女性身上的、要她们顺从荒谬美标准的压力"两层全保留,正是预判内容。
  • the struggle goes against the underlying pressure exerted on women to conform to an unrealistic beauty ideal↔A fight is going on to remove pressure on women to conform to an absurd beauty ideal
D) 偷换概念Media outlets have begun to present as beauty models trendy women without any makeup 把媒体改变形象人选(那些"不符合所谓理想身材"的女性)与 influencers 不化妆自拍混在一起,且原文是 influencers 上传素颜照,不是 media outlets 把"时尚的不化妆女性"包装成 beauty models。
P2

In the physical world, the struggle goes against the underlying pressure exerted on women to conform to an unrealistic beauty ideal.

在现实世界里,这场斗争所反对的是施加在女性身上、要求她们顺从一种不切实际的美标准的潜在压力。

53

What do we learn from the passage about the Instagram face?

从文章中我们对Instagram脸了解到什么?

  • A)It is now regarded as the new beauty ideal.它现在被视为新的美的理想。
  • B)It is what most women will go after in 2050.它是2050年大多数女性将追求的目标。
  • C)It is being much talked about on social media.它在社交媒体上被广泛讨论。
  • D)It is a perfect illustration of the ultimate beauty.它是终极美的完美例证。
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确It is now regarded as the new beauty ideal 几乎是定位句的字面同义改写,锁定即可。
  • the Instagram face has become the new beauty ideal↔It is now regarded as the new beauty ideal
B) 张冠李戴It is what most women will go after in 2050 把"2050"这个时间安在 Instagram face 头上,而 the popularity of the cyborg look, which illustrates what Americans will look like in 2050 里 2050 描述的是 cyborg look,而非 Instagram face。
C) 无中生有It is being much talked about on social media 加入了"正在被大量讨论"这层意思,原文只说它"成了新美标准",并未提及讨论度。
D) 过度推断It is a perfect illustration of the ultimate beauty 用 perfect 与 ultimate 两个最高程度词,原文只说 the new beauty ideal,既无"完美"也无"终极"。
P4

Today, however, the Instagram face has become the new beauty ideal.

然而,如今 Instagram face 已经成为新的美标准。

54

What has obsessive filtering resulted in?

过度滤镜导致了什么?

  • A)A good many women striving to reach an impossible level of perfection.许多女性努力达到一种不可能的完美水平。
  • B)An urge to turn the entire game played on the network to one's advantage.一种将网络上进行的整个游戏转化为对自己有利的冲动。
  • C)A tendency to regard one's body as an object of observation and judgment.一种将自己的身体视为观察和评判对象的倾向。
  • D)An increasing number of women filtering their images before uploading them.越来越多的女性在上传图像前对其进行滤镜处理。
C（细节题）
A) 张冠李戴A good many women striving to reach an impossible level of perfection 中"impossible level of perfection"是 P8 与 P9 关于滤镜带来的整体后果,不是 P7 这句关于 obsessive filtering 的 consequence。
B) 无中生有An urge to turn the entire game played on the network to one's advantage 的"为己所用"是原文完全没有的概念,只是借用了 the entire game played on the networks 的字面壳。
C) ✓ 正确A tendency to regard one's body as an object of observation and judgment 把"被观察与被评价的客体"这层意思以名词化方式呈现,与预判完全一致。
  • the emerging tendency to treat oneself as an object to be observed and valued↔A tendency to regard one's body as an object of observation and judgment
D) 答非所问An increasing number of women filtering their images before uploading them 描述的就是 obsessive filtering 这一行为本身(原因),而非 resulted in 所问的后果。
P7

One of the significant consequences of obsessive filtering is the emerging tendency to treat oneself as an object to be observed and valued, in the same way another person observes and judges from the side.

过度滤镜化的一个显著后果是,人们正在出现一种倾向:把自己当作一个被观察、被评价的客体,就好像旁人从一旁观察并评判一样。

What does the author want to emphasize at the end of the passage regarding Americans' obsession with looks?

作者在文章结尾想强调关于美国人对外表痴迷的什么?

  • A)Cosmetic surgery should be made more accessible to the masses.整容手术应该让大众更容易获得。
  • B)Psychological intervention should be introduced to alleviate it.应该引入心理干预以减轻这种痴迷。
  • C)Their time spent on social media should be strictly controlled.他们在社交媒体上花费的时间应该受到严格控制。
  • D)Its root cause should be meticulously examined and analyzed.其根本原因应该被细致地检查和分析。
B（态度题）
A) 与原文相反Cosmetic surgery should be made more accessible to the masses 主张更易获得整容,但原文 not cosmetic intervention 已经明确排除"美容干预"。
B) ✓ 正确Psychological intervention should be introduced to alleviate it 把作者真正强调的 mental health care 改成同义表达 psychological intervention,正中作者立场。
  • the only thing that can fix it is not cosmetic intervention, but mental health care↔Psychological intervention should be introduced to alleviate it
  • cosmetic intervention↔Psychological intervention
C) 过度推断Their time spent on social media should be strictly controlled 提出严格控制使用时间,而原文 The time spent on social media creates an urge… 只把"花在社媒的时间"作为成因列出,并未呼吁去 strictly control 它。
D) 无中生有Its root cause should be meticulously examined and analyzed 主张"细致检查与分析其根本原因",但原文只给方案(mental health care),并未呼吁去"分析根因"。
