原文逐段精读

每段英文下是中译。遇到生词查出来写旁边，再回头读生词所在的句子。

P1

Imagine you're an alien sent to Earth to document the behaviour of the mammals inhabiting the planet. You stumble into a movie theatre that's showing the latest Hollywood horror film.

想象一下，你是一名被派往地球、负责记录这个星球上哺乳动物行为的外星人。你偶然走进一家正在放映最新好莱坞恐怖电影的电影院。

P2

Several dozen humans are gathered together in a dark, undecorated room. They're all staring at a rectangular area on which patterns of light change rapidly.

几十个人类聚集在一间昏暗而没有任何装饰的房间里。他们全都盯着一块矩形区域，那里光线的图案正在快速变化。

P3

They are clearly in a state of high arousal. Their heart rate is elevated, they occasionally glance around nervously, and they sometimes jump collectively in their seats, and emit high-pitched warning calls.

他们显然处于高度兴奋的状态。他们的心率加快，偶尔紧张地环顾四周，有时还会集体在座位上跳起来，并发出尖锐的警告声。

P4

Eventually, the lights come up and the rectangular screen goes black. The humans stand up and leave the room, chatting and laughing, and showing signs of pleasure.

最后，灯亮了，矩形屏幕变成了黑色。人类站起来离开房间，一边聊天大笑，一边流露出愉悦的迹象。

P5

Why do these humans voluntarily expose themselves to what appears to be a deeply unpleasant experience? And why do they react so strongly to those patterns of light on a screen?

为什么这些人类会自愿让自己经历这种看起来极其令人不快的体验？他们为什么会对屏幕上那些光线图案产生如此强烈的反应？

P6

I am fascinated with the paradox of horror—the strange fact that many people seek out scary entertainment.

我对恐怖的悖论着迷——许多人会主动寻求恐怖娱乐，这一事实十分奇怪。

P7

I think the answer to the puzzle lies in human nature.

我认为，这个谜题的答案在于人性。

P8

My research suggests that we humans evolved to find pleasure in situations that allow us to experience negative emotions in a safe context. You can see these elements of horror in children's games. Take hide-and-seek for example, which is a simulation of a predator-prey interaction. The kid hides and the adult pretends to be a predator, searching for the child while howling like a dangerous beast.

我的研究表明，我们人类经过进化，能够在安全的情境中体验负面情绪，并从中获得愉悦。你可以在儿童游戏中看到恐怖的这些要素。以捉迷藏为例，它是对捕食者与猎物互动的一种模拟。孩子躲起来，成年人假装成捕食者，一边像危险的野兽那样嚎叫，一边寻找孩子。

P9

This simulation gives the child crucial information about how to avoid becoming prey, and children tend to find that kind of activity deeply satisfying, presumably because it gives them a safe experience of a potentially catastrophic scenario.

这种模拟为孩子提供了关于如何避免成为猎物的关键信息，而孩子往往会觉得这类活动非常令人满足，大概是因为它让孩子在安全的情况下体验了一个可能造成灾难的场景。

P10

They find it pleasurable, and pleasure is evolution's way of motivating us toward adaptive behaviour.

他们会从中感到愉悦，而愉悦正是进化促使我们采取适应性行为的方式。

P11

Horror is pleasurable to many people because it lets us play with negative emotions and develop coping strategies. We learn what it feels like to be truly afraid, and we learn how to handle negative emotion.

恐怖之所以能给许多人带来愉悦，是因为它让我们得以玩味负面情绪，并培养应对策略。我们会了解真正感到害怕是什么感觉，也会学会如何处理负面情绪。

P12

How, then, does horror work? My research suggests that horror works by exploiting an ancient set of biological defence mechanisms—an evolved fear system, which we share with other animals. But humans are uniquely imaginative, and we use our evolved imagination to travel into virtual worlds that are full of danger.

那么，恐怖是如何起作用的呢？我的研究表明，恐怖是通过利用一套古老的生物防御机制发挥作用的——这是一种经过进化形成的恐惧系统，我们与其他动物共同拥有这种系统。但人类具有独特的想象力，我们利用经过进化形成的想象力，进入充满危险的虚拟世界。

P13

There are good reasons for watching a horror film, even if you're not a loyal horror fan. If you make it through the film in one piece, you'll probably experience a strong sense of mastery, a sense that you were able to make it through an appalling experience. Anyway, watching a horror film makes you better at handling your own fear, and who knows when that will become critically relevant?

即使你不是忠实的恐怖片爱好者，观看恐怖电影也有充分的理由。如果你能完好无损地看完整部电影，你可能会产生强烈的掌控感，觉得自己成功熬过了一段骇人的经历。无论如何，观看恐怖电影会让你更善于应对自己的恐惧，而谁知道这种能力什么时候会变得至关重要呢？

解析逐题详解

46

The alien finds the audience in the movie theatre clearly in a state of ______.

外星人发现电影院里的观众明显处于______状态。

  • A)total mindfulness完全专注
  • B)extreme excitement极度兴奋
  • C)spiritual elevation精神升华
  • D)intense curiosity强烈好奇
解析
B（细节题）
A) 与原文相反total mindfulness 指「完全的正念/平静专注」，与 high arousal 描绘的生理躁动完全相反。
B) ✓ 正确预判语义「高度兴奋」与 extreme excitement 在程度词 extreme=high、情绪词 excitement=arousal 两个维度上完全对位。
  • high arousal↔extreme excitement
  • Their heart rate is elevated ... jump collectively ... emit high-pitched warning calls↔extreme excitement
C) 无中生有spiritual elevation 指「精神升华」，原文只描写心率 elevated 这种生理升高，未提及任何精神层面的升华。
D) 偷换概念intense curiosity 把状态偷换成「强烈好奇」；但原文是因恐怖剧情引发的恐惧式高唤起，不是认知性好奇。
P3

They are clearly in a state of high arousal. Their heart rate is elevated, they occasionally glance around nervously, and they sometimes jump collectively in their seats, and emit high-pitched warning calls.

他们显然处于一种高度唤起的状态。心率加快，偶尔紧张地四下张望，时不时在座位上一齐惊跳，并发出尖叫般的警示声。

47

Why do many people seek out scary entertainment, according to the author's research?

根据作者的研究,为什么许多人会寻求恐怖娱乐?

  • A)They gain experience in overcoming horror in real life.他们获得了克服现实生活中恐怖的经验。
  • B)They find joy in going through simulated horrible experiences.他们在经历模拟的恐怖体验中找到乐趣。
  • C)They have learned from hide-and-seek as kids the thrill involved.他们从儿时的捉迷藏游戏中学到了其中的刺激感。
  • D)They have evolved to gain pleasure in escaping life-threatening situations.他们进化出了在逃离危及生命的情境中获得快乐的能力。
解析
B（细节题）
A) 偷换概念They gain experience in overcoming horror in real life 把「安全语境的模拟体验」偷换成「现实生活中克服恐怖」，与原文 safe context 矛盾。
B) ✓ 正确They find joy in going through simulated horrible experiences 两端都吻合预判：joy=pleasure、simulated horrible experiences=negative emotions in a safe context。
  • find pleasure↔find joy
  • experience negative emotions in a safe context↔going through simulated horrible experiences
C) 以偏概全They have learned from hide-and-seek as kids the thrill involved 拿一个例证 hide-and-seek 当原因，又把 pleasure 改成 thrill；hide-and-seek 是论据而非原因，本身不能解释成年人为何看恐怖片。
D) 偷换概念They have evolved to gain pleasure in escaping life-threatening situations 把「在安全语境下体验负面情绪」偷换成「在逃离致命威胁中获得快感」——原文是模拟、非真威胁；evolved、pleasure 借词制造伪等值。
P8

My research suggests that we humans evolved to find pleasure in situations that allow us to experience negative emotions in a safe context.

我的研究表明，人类在进化中学会了在能让我们于安全语境下体验负面情绪的情景中获得快感。

48

What do children learn from hide-and-seek?

孩子们从捉迷藏中学到了什么?

  • A)How to avoid falling prey to an attacker.如何避免成为攻击者的猎物。
  • B)How to simulate a predator-prey interaction.如何模拟捕食者与猎物的互动。
  • C)How to keep themselves from catastrophic errors.如何防止自己犯下灾难性错误。
  • D)How to turn a dangerous scenario into a safe one.如何将危险场景转变为安全场景。
解析
A（细节题）
A) ✓ 正确How to avoid falling prey to an attacker 是预判的近乎逐字改写，avoid 对 avoid、falling prey 对 becoming prey、attacker 对 predator。
  • how to avoid becoming prey↔How to avoid falling prey to an attacker
  • The kid hides and the adult pretends to be a predator↔an attacker
B) 答非所问How to simulate a predator-prey interaction 学的是「如何模拟捕食情境」，但原文说「这场模拟（this simulation）」是大人创造的，孩子从中学到的是避险知识，而不是模拟技巧本身。
C) 无中生有How to keep themselves from catastrophic errors 凭空造出「灾难性错误」一说；原文只有 catastrophic scenario「灾难性情境」，且讨论的是体验该情境而非「避免错误」。
D) 偷换概念How to turn a dangerous scenario into a safe one 把原文「在安全条件下体验潜在的灾难性情境」偷换成「把危险情境变安全」——原文的 safe 是体验时的环境条件，并非孩子把危险情境改造成了安全情境。
P9

This simulation gives the child crucial information about how to avoid becoming prey, and children tend to find that kind of activity deeply satisfying, presumably because it gives them a safe experience of a potentially catastrophic scenario.

这种模拟向孩子提供了关于「如何避免成为猎物」的关键信息，孩子往往觉得这种活动极其令人满足，大概是因为它让他们在安全条件下体验了一种潜在的灾难性情境。

49

Why is horror gratifying to many people?

为什么恐怖对许多人来说是令人满足的?

  • A)It reminds them of an ancient set of biological defence mechanisms.它让他们想起一套古老的生物防御机制。
  • B)It triggers their imagination to travel into dangerous virtual worlds.它激发他们的想象力进入危险的虚拟世界。
  • C)It allows them to learn what fear feels like and how to tackle it.它让他们学习恐惧的感觉以及如何应对恐惧。
  • D)It activates their evolved fear system and their unique fantasy.它激活了他们进化而来的恐惧系统和独特的幻想能力。
解析
C（细节题）
A) 偷换概念It reminds them of an ancient set of biological defence mechanisms 把「让我们演练负面情绪」偷换成「让我们想起远古防御机制」——原文是「利用」(exploiting) 防御机制，而不是「使我们回忆起」。
B) 答非所问It triggers their imagination to travel into dangerous virtual worlds 描述的是恐怖如何作用（机制），而非「为什么令人满足」（原因）；机制 vs 原因不同问，且 P12 的话题是 how，不是 why。
C) ✓ 正确learn what fear feels like and how to tackle it 用并列结构同时覆盖预判的两层「体验」和「应对」，是原文最完整的同义改写。
  • We learn what it feels like to be truly afraid↔learn what fear feels like
  • develop coping strategies ... learn how to handle negative emotion↔how to tackle it
D) 答非所问It activates their evolved fear system and their unique fantasy 是把 P12 机制段的两个核心要素「evolved fear system」与「imagination」拼起来，依旧回答的是「机制」而非「为什么令人满足」。
P11

Horror is pleasurable to many people because it lets us play with negative emotions and develop coping strategies. We learn what it feels like to be truly afraid, and we learn how to handle negative emotion.

对许多人而言，恐怖之所以令人愉悦，是因为它让我们演练负面情绪并发展出应对策略。我们由此了解真正害怕的感觉，也学会如何处理负面情绪。

50

What will one experience if they watch a horror film through without being hurt?

如果一个人毫发无损地看完一部恐怖电影,他们会体验到什么?

  • A)A strong sense of clear relevance.强烈的明确相关感。
  • B)A profound sense of good fortune.深刻的幸运感。
  • C)A profound sense of intense relief.深刻的强烈解脱感。
  • D)A strong sense of being in control.强烈的掌控感。
解析
D（细节题）
A) 偷换概念A strong sense of clear relevance 摘取原文末句 critically relevant 中 relevant 的衍生形态，把后置的 critically relevant 偷换为 clear relevance；原文是说「处理恐惧的能力未来可能会变得至关重要」，并非「看完后体验到清晰的相关感」。
B) 无中生有A profound sense of good fortune 描述「幸运感」，原文从未提及；作者强调的是因撑过而获得的胜任感，而非「庆幸自己运气好」。
C) 无中生有A profound sense of intense relief 描述「释然感」，原文没有任何「relief/放松」语义；mastery 强调的是控场，而非紧张消退。
D) ✓ 正确strong sense of 对原文原词，mastery 与 being in control 是同义场最佳对应，D 字面命中预判。
  • a strong sense of mastery↔A strong sense of being in control
  • a sense that you were able to make it through an appalling experience↔being in control
P13

If you make it through the film in one piece, you'll probably experience a strong sense of mastery, a sense that you were able to make it through an appalling experience.

如果你能毫发无损地撑过这部电影，你大概会体验到一种强烈的掌控感——一种自己能挺过令人毛骨悚然之体验的胜任感。
