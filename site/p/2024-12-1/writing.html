范文参考范文

【参考范文 · 170 词】写作无唯一标准答案，以下为达标示范

To increase the likelihood of success, one should set realistic goals and work persistently towards them. This statement reveals a simple truth: ambition alone means little unless it is anchored to attainable targets and sustained effort.

A realistic goal keeps us moving instead of despairing. If a beginner runner vows to finish a marathon next week, failure is almost certain, and disappointment soon kills motivation. However, by aiming first at three kilometres, then five, and finally the full distance, the runner sets goals within reach and steadily raises the bar. Each small victory fuels the next.

Yet reasonable goals mean nothing without persistence. My cousin, who dreamed of passing CET-6, did not expect overnight fluency. She fixed a modest daily plan of thirty new words and one reading passage, and she stuck to it for months. As a result, she cleared the exam comfortably.

In this way, pairing sensible targets with steady work transforms a distant dream into a series of achievable steps. That is the surest road to success.
